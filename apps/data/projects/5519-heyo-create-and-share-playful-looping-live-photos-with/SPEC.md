# SPEC.md — Heyo – Create and share playful looping live photos with friends

## Problem

Heyo lets you capture short, lively photo loops and share them in themed galleries that feel fun and immediate. The site showcases collections like Sari Exposure, Misty Figures, and Lake Daydream, inviting you to browse and post your own motion-filled moments. With a simple login and a focus on visual expression, Heyo turns everyday scenes into dynamic loops you can explore and remix with a creative community. View startup
