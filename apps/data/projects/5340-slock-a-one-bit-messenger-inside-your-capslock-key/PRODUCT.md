_Based on source brief:_ Two computers, one bit.<p>Hi — I built slock, a small macOS menu bar app that connects two people through their capslock lights.<p>Hold capslock on your Mac, and the other person’s capslock light turns on. Let go, and it turns off.<p>That’s all. A tiny way to interact someone without opening a chat window. Invent a blink language, try Morse code, or just bother someone who agreed to install this with you.
