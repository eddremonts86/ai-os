# SPEC.md — Ponito – Compare prices, track price history, and read independent reviews

## Problem

Ponito is a shopping platform that helps you compare prices across shops, track price history, and evaluate products with independent reviews. Browse deals, categories, brands, and promotions to find the best offer and shop with confidence. The site publishes a price index, methodology, and safety alerts to support transparent decisions. Merchants and developers can integrate via the Ponito Platform, Product Intelligence API, and Trust Engine for data-driven commerce. View startup
