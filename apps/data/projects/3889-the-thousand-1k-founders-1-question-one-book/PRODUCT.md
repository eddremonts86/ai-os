# The Thousand – 1k founders, 1 question, one book

## Value Proposition

A public time capsule of the people building technology today. The Thousand gives 1,000 founders and brands one page each — logo, website, prediction — all answering the same question: what will look completely different in 2030? Pages are claimed in a live auction where founders bid for position, and when all 1,000 are filled the predictions become a book: free eBook, with a printed edition available.

**One-liner:** 1,000 founders, one question about 2030, an auction for page position, and one book at the end.

## Target Users

| Stakeholder | Why they care |
|---|---|
| Founders and brands | A permanent page with their logo, website and 2030 prediction among 999 peers. |
| Auction participants | Bidding for page position turns participation into a public game. |
| Future readers | A free eBook compiling 1,000 predictions from people building technology today. |

The author's comment defines the participants (founders, brands, bidders) and the readers of the eventual book.

## Jobs To Be Done

1. **Functional job** — Claim one of 1,000 pages under a brand name and publish a prediction.

2. **Functional job** — Bid for page positions in a live auction.

3. **Functional job** — Compile all 1,000 predictions into a free eBook and a printed edition.

4. **Emotional job** — Leave a dated, public record of what a founder believes the future looks like.

## Success Metrics

- **Pages claimed:** progress toward all 1,000 pages filled — the precondition for the book.
- **Auction activity:** bids placed per page, proving the mechanic engages rather than repels.
- **Prediction quality:** submissions are real predictions, not filler, so the book stays interesting (the author's stated worry).
- **Book delivery:** the free eBook ships and a printed edition is available once pages fill.

## Pricing & Monetization

The eBook will be free, with a printed edition available as well (price not stated). Page claiming and bidding are the monetization mechanism implied by the auction format, but no bid pricing or fees are stated.

## Competitive Landscape

The post names no competitors, though one HN commenter dismissed the concept as something posted nearly every day. The category is collective-publication experiments and auction-driven directories — million-dollar-homepage-style claimable grids and founder directories. The stated differentiator is the combination: one shared question, 1,000 branded pages, live bidding for position, and a compiled book at the end.

## Risks & Open Questions

- [ ] Cold-start problem: the author is at the very beginning, and the book only exists when all 1,000 pages are filled.
- [ ] The auction mechanic is unproven — the author explicitly asks whether it can produce an interesting book.
- [ ] One HN commenter already called the concept derivative; differentiation beyond the mechanic is thin.
- [ ] The printed edition's economics are unstated; the free eBook is a stated cost with no revenue attached.
- [ ] The single question limits content to one answer per founder, which constrains depth.
