# The Thousand – 1k founders, 1 question, one book

## Tech Stack

- **Public page claiming:** each founder claims one of 1,000 pages under their brand.
- **Live auction bidding:** bids compete for page positions in real time.
- **Founder directory:** logos, websites and predictions organized across all pages.
- **Book compilation pipeline:** predictions compile into the eBook and printed edition.
- **Static site publishing:** the claiming and bidding site at thethousand.co.

## Architecture

- **Claim layer:** 1,000 pages, each claimed once by a founder or brand.
- **Auction layer:** bids change page positions; positions are the scarce good.
- **Content layer:** logo, website and the 2030 prediction per page.
- **Compilation layer:** filled pages flow into the free eBook and the printed edition.

## Milestones

1. **M0 — Claimable pages.** Founders can claim a page and publish logo, website and prediction.

2. **M1 — Live auction.** Bidding for page positions works and settles correctly.

3. **M2 — Full grid.** All 1,000 pages are filled with real predictions.

4. **M3 — The book.** The free eBook compiles and a printed edition becomes available.

## Risks

- **Fill rate:** the entire premise needs 1,000 founders to act; nothing guarantees they arrive.
- **Auction fairness:** bidding mechanics need to feel fair or founders will not participate.
- **Content quality:** if predictions are marketing filler, the book fails the author's own interestingness test.
- **Perception risk:** the concept reads as derivative to at least one early commenter.
