# The Thousand – 1k founders, 1 question, one book

## Problem

The capture is a URL-only Show HN post (thethousand.co), and the author's own comment on the thread carries the pitch: The Thousand is an experiment to bring together 1,000 founders and brands to answer the same question — What will look completely different in 2030? Each founder can claim one of 1,000 pages under their brand name, and the page includes their logo, website and prediction. The unique part is a live auction format: pages can be claimed and founders can compete for positions through bids, turning the building of the book into a public experiment. When all 1,000 pages are filled, the predictions will be compiled into a book; the eBook will be free, with a printed edition available as well. The goal is a time capsule of what 1,000 people building technology today think the future will look like. The author is at the very beginning and wants feedback on the concept, the auction mechanics and whether this can produce an interesting book.

## Objective

Fill all 1,000 pages of The Thousand: a public, auction-driven collection where founders claim a page under their brand and publish their answer to one question — what will look completely different in 2030 — then compile the predictions into a free eBook with a printed edition. The MVP is the live claiming and bidding site at thethousand.co with the book compilation pipeline defined.

## Target Users

- Founders who want their brand and prediction recorded next to 999 others for a 2030 time capsule.
- Builders who enjoy the auction mechanic — claiming pages and outbidding for position as a public experiment.
- Readers who want a compiled book of 1,000 predictions from people building technology today.

## MVP Scope

- 1,000 claimable pages, one per founder or brand, each with logo, website and prediction.
- Live auction format: bids compete for page positions.
- Book compilation when all pages are filled: free eBook plus a printed edition.
- The single question framing the whole experiment: what will look completely different in 2030.

## Constraints

- The experiment needs 1,000 filled pages before the book exists; the author is at the very beginning.
- The eBook is stated free; the printed edition's price is not stated.
- The auction mechanic is the experiment itself — feedback on it is explicitly requested, meaning it is unproven.
- One HN commenter dismissed the concept as derivative; the author's response is not part of the capture.
