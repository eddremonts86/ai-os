# SPEC.md — My Own Homepage – Track the fastest-rising search trends on YouTube, TikTok, and Instagram

## Problem

My Own Homepage curates a free daily snapshot of the fastest-rising search terms across YouTube, TikTok, and Instagram. It highlights themes, products, recipes, and ideas by velocity, offering a Google Trends-like view that's handpicked for relevance. Browse the top 50 surging queries with platform tags and growth percentages to spot opportunities, plan content, and understand what audiences care about right now. View startup
