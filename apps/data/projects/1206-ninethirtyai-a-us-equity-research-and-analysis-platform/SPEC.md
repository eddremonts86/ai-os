# NinethirtyAI – A US equity research and analysis platform

## Problem

Hi HN - I'm Yashu and I've been building Ninethirty for a while.The problem: I do my own research, and a variety of tabs open for screening, charts, financials and so on; and then a chat window where I'd paste numbers for an LLM to reason and analyse them.We built NineThirty to solve a bunch of these problems -* A screener that goes deep and is genuinely customizable - fundamental, technical, news, and event criteria in the same query, not three separate tools. Besides, the problem does not end with screening - (almost) instantly backtest to understand historically what stocks have come up on the screen and their performance.* Company financials, sector views, heatmaps, events, seasonality, etc. the usual stuff* An AI assistant (Dr Market) that sits on top of this curated, processed data layer. Use it to validate a setup and it's working from the actual real time technical indicators, not a plausible-sounding reconstruction. This doesn't eliminate hallucinations. The hope is that when Dr. Market gets something wrong, it's interpreting the data badly rather than making up the data itself (or citing the wrong sources).Where it's early: coverage is US equities, and there are rough edges I know about and probably a few I don't. It analyzes; it doesn't give recommendations, and it's not advice. Free while I figure out what it costs to run and what's actually worth paying for.What I'd most like from HN is to try to break Dr. Market? Ask it something where you already know the answer and tell me where it's wrong. And if the screener can't express a screen you actually want to run, that's the feedback I want most.Thanks!
