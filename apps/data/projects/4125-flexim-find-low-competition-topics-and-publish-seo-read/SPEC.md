# Flexim – Find low-competition topics and publish SEO-ready content fast

## Problem

Flexim helps SaaS teams grow organic traffic without hiring marketers. It analyzes your site, validates demand and difficulty, and suggests low-competition topics with smart SEO-ready titles and briefs. Use templates for blogs, comparisons, and knowledge bases, then draft with your preferred AI and refine in the built-in editor and checklist. Flexim also works as a headless CMS and backend: manage content in a visual builder, publish with one click, and access a generated API and LLM docs to integrate with Next.js and more. View startup
