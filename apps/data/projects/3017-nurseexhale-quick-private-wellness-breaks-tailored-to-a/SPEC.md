# NurseExhale – Quick, private wellness breaks tailored to a nurse's shift

## Problem

NurseExhale is a wellness app built for nurses to decompress during and after demanding shifts. It offers 60-second check-ins for energy, emotional load, and meaning, plus 2 to 10 minute breathwork, decompression, and boundary sessions that fit between patients or on the commute home. The app runs locally with no employer login and stores your history on your device. Use it before, during, or after a shift to reset, sleep better, and separate work from home with practical nurse-focused tools. View startup
