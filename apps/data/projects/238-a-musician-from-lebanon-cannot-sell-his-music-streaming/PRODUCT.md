# A musician from Lebanon cannot sell his music: streaming pays pennies, and Bandcamp doesn't accept payments in his country. Needs a fair radio-platform with direct sales

> Auto-generated product brief. Review and refine before MVP scoping.

## Value Proposition

Pays artists in countries that mainstream platforms structurally exclude, at a rate above streaming pennies, and gives listeners a direct-purchase path that the artist actually receives.

## Target Users

Independent musicians in Lebanon and similar payout-restricted countries; listeners who want a curated independent-music experience and are willing to pay per-track or per-album; small radio curators.

## Jobs To Be Done

When I make music, I want a platform that pays me in a currency I can actually receive, at a rate that respects the work. When I discover a musician I like, I want to buy the track or album directly so the artist gets most of the money.

## Success Metrics

Active independent artists onboarded per quarter (Lebanon focus in M1). Average per-play payout to artists vs. industry-streaming benchmark. Per-month listener sessions. Direct-sales conversion rate.
