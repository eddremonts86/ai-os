## Value Proposition

_Based on source brief:_ Taste Library is a web app that lets you manage and show off your collection. It's like Delicious Library, but for the web.Here is the link: https://tastelibrary.appI missed Delicious Library enough that I wrote about trying to recreate it last year (which was also on HN: https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=42860141). I love its design, and miss the “Delicious Generation” movement that embraced pixel-perfect, personal and fun designs. I wanted Taste Library to carry some of that spirit. I don’t want it to feel like another generic database app. I want it to feel visual, playful, and enjoyable to browse.Taste Library is currently a UI prototype. There's no backend yet, but the demo library is nearly fully interactive. You can add things, search, reorder what you have, and manage shelves. All pages work on both desktop and mobile. I'm deliberately working out the interaction and visual design first, and I'd especially like feedback on that before building the rest.Please give it a try at https://tastelibrary.app and let me know what you think in the comments.
