# SPEC.md — Video to Text – Turn videos and audio into accurate, timestamped transcripts fast

## Problem

Video to Text converts videos and audio into accurate, searchable transcripts with speaker labels and timestamps. It supports 99 languages and handles mixed-language recordings, making it useful for subtitles, meetings, interviews, courses, and podcasts. You can upload files or paste public links from YouTube, TikTok, Instagram, X, and Facebook, then export results as TXT, SRT, VTT, or CSV. New users get 30 free credits, and pay-as-you-go pricing keeps costs predictable. View startup
