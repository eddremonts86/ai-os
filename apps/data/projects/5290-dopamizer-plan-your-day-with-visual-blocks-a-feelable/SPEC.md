# SPEC.md — Dopamizer – Plan your day with visual blocks, a feelable timer, and gentle XP rewards

## Problem

Dopamizer helps ADHD brains plan and move through the day with less friction. It replaces overwhelming lists with colored time blocks you can scan at a glance, and a draining-arc timer that makes time feel real. Capture thoughts instantly without breaking focus, track mood and routines, and earn honest XP for showing up. Sync stays encrypted, works across iPhone, Android, macOS, Windows, and Linux, and mirrors sessions between devices so your focus follows you. View startup
