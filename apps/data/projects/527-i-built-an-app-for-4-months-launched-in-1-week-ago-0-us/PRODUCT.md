# "I built an app for 4 months, launched in 1 week ago. 0 users, super polished and proven demand."

> Auto-generated product brief. Review and refine before MVP scoping.

## Value Proposition

A founder who just launched with zero users can run through a 15-minute intake and get a ranked diagnosis of the 3 most likely root causes, plus a 14-day experiment plan sized to a solo founder's working hours, instead of guessing for another 4 months.

## Target Users

| Stakeholder | Why they care |
|---|---|
| Solo founder (1 week post-launch) | Has zero users; needs a diagnosis, not motivation. |
| Small team (2-3, same situation) | Cannot tell whether the problem is audience, channel, onboarding, or price. |
| Pre-launch founder | Wants to know what to watch for in the first 14 days. |

## Jobs To Be Done

1. **Functional job** — Diagnose "why no users" without spending another 4 months guessing.
2. **Emotional job** — Stop feeling stuck at zero.
3. **Social job** — Be able to defend the diagnosis to a co-founder with a clear ranked list, not vibes.

## Success Metrics

- **Diagnostic accuracy:** ≥50% of users report the top-ranked root cause matched their situation within 14 days.
- **Plan completion:** ≥40% of users finish the 14-day experiment plan.
- **Movement:** ≥30% of users have at least one signup by day 14.

## Pricing & Monetization

Free for the first 14-day plan. $29 one-time for a second 14-day plan with an updated diagnosis.

## Competitive Landscape

- **IndieHackers threads** — anecdotal, contradictory.
- **Launch checklists (ProductHunt, Hacker News)** — channel-focused, no diagnosis.
- **Coaches / advisors** — expensive, slow, not tailored.

## Risks & Open Questions

- [ ] Validate the diagnosis heuristics against 30 actual "zero users" launches before launch.
- [ ] Decide whether to add a paid "founder office hours" tier in v2.
