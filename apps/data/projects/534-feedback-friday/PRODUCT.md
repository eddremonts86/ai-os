# Feedback Friday

> Auto-generated product brief. Review and refine before MVP scoping.

## Value Proposition

An indie founder can post their landing page on Friday and get 3-5 structured critiques (first impression, what's missing, what I'd change) by the following Wednesday, with the most useful ones highlighted by the founder's thanks.

## Target Users

| Stakeholder | Why they care |
|---|---|
| Indie founder with a landing page | Needs 3-5 fresh pairs of eyes without a $200 usability test. |
| Community regular | Likes giving feedback; wants a structured format to do it well. |
| Marketing consultant | Wants a stream of public landing-page critiques to learn from. |

## Jobs To Be Done

1. **Functional job** — Get actionable feedback on a landing page without paying for it.
2. **Emotional job** — Feel that the critiques are honest, not just "looks great!".
3. **Social job** — Be able to thank the critic who helped, publicly.

## Success Metrics

- **Thread volume:** ≥10 landing pages posted per Friday thread within 60 days.
- **Critique density:** median 3+ critiques per posted landing page within 7 days.
- **Thanks rate:** ≥40% of posted landing pages receive at least one "thanks".

## Pricing & Monetization

Free. No upsell.

## Competitive Landscape

- **Reddit r/SaaS Feedback Friday** — the baseline; works but critiques are unstructured.
- **Paid usability tests (UserTesting, Maze)** — $200+/test; overkill for a landing page.
- **Twitter / LinkedIn feedback threads** — useful, low signal-to-noise.

## Risks & Open Questions

- [ ] Validate the critique templates actually produce better feedback than unstructured comments.
- [ ] Decide whether the scoreboard should be public or opt-in.
