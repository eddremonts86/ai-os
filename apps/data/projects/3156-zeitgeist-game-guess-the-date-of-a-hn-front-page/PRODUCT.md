# Zeitgeist Game – guess the date of a HN front page

## Value Proposition

Guess the date of the HN front page you're shown.

## Target Users

HN readers who want a nostalgia/zeitgeist guessing game based on what was trending on the site years ago.

## Jobs To Be Done

HN readers who want a nostalgia/zeitgeist guessing game based on what was trending on the site years ago.

## Success Metrics

Average absolute error (days) of the player's guess versus the true date.
Daily active guessers, and share who return the next day.
