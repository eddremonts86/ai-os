# SPEC.md — viso.ai – Build, deploy, and scale AI vision from any camera in minutes

## Problem

Viso Now helps businesses and technical teams quickly turn computer vision ideas into working applications. Operations, quality, safety, and maintenance teams can identify bottlenecks, automate visual inspections, detect hazards, and spot equipment issues earlier. Engineers and AI teams can build, test, and integrate applications without long cycles of data collection, model testing, or script writing. Innovation teams and integrators can turn ideas into solutions, demonstrate value, and deliver projects faster. Users can upload existing video, describe what they want to detect, and create their first application in minutes. View startup
