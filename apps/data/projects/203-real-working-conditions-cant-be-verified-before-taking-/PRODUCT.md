# Real working conditions can't be verified before taking a job — reviews lie. Need a service for direct, anonymous, verified reports from current employees.

> Auto-generated product brief. Review and refine before MVP scoping.

## Value Proposition

Stop wasting six months of a notice period on a job whose reality you did not know. The first 1000 paid reports pay for the marketing budget twice over.

## Target Users

UK mid-career professionals considering a job change. Senior engineers, finance professionals, lawyers, and anyone whose offer includes a 3-6 month notice period and therefore a high cost of a bad move.

## Jobs To Be Done

Functional: know what a job is actually like before signing. Emotional: stop the anxiety of trusting a stranger's review. Social: be the candidate who arrives at the new job already knowing the unwritten rules.

## Success Metrics

At least 50% of paid reports come from a return user. Dispute rate from companies below 5%. Verification failure rate below 10% (indicating the flow is realistic).

## Competitive Landscape

Glassdoor and Indeed are the obvious competitors but they do not verify employment. Blind (US) verifies but is US-only. No UK service combines employment verification with structured report data and a public dispute mechanism.

## Risks & Open Questions

Risk of legal pressure from a UK employer if a report is identifiable. Risk of an employer punishing an employee whose verification token is spotted. Risk of bad-faith reports from competitors. Risk of being classified as a defamation intermediary.
