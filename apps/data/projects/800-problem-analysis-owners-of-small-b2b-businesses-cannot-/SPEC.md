# Problem Analysis: Owners of small B2B businesses cannot build a growth system. Do they need a fractional CMO, or can a SaaS be built?

## Problem

The poster observes that owners of small B2B businesses cannot build a growth system on their own and asks whether the answer is a fractional CMO service or a SaaS product. The source page on ProblemHunt gives only this framing — no quoted numbers, no interview evidence, no named user beyond the role of "owner of a small B2B business", and no country-specific market sizing. This plan treats the title as the entire problem statement.

## Objective

Frame the question for the poster: under what conditions is the right answer a fractional-CMO service, and under what conditions is it a SaaS? Deliver a written problem-analysis page that lays out the trade-offs the post raises.

## Target Users

Owners of small B2B businesses (per the title) who are weighing a fractional CMO engagement against buying or building a marketing SaaS.

## MVP Scope

A short problem-analysis write-up published as a single web page: the question, the conditions that favour each answer, and the open evidence needed to pick one. No product, no marketplace, no SaaS app — the MVP is the analysis itself.
