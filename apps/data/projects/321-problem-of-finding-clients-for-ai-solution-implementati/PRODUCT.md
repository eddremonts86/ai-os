# Problem of finding clients for AI solution implementation in Europe and Eastern

> Auto-generated product brief. Review and refine before MVP scoping.

## Value Proposition

A UK or Western European AI consultancy fills its bench with 2–3 new European client engagements per quarter, with the go-to-market engine running on the platform and proposals drafted by AI.

## Target Users

| Stakeholder | Why they care |
|---|---|
| UK boutique AI consultancy founder | Has the technical team; needs the GTM engine. |
| Western European AI consultancy sales lead | Wants repeatable outbound, not cold-call heroics. |
| Eastern European AI delivery shop | Wants a UK-side partner desk sourced from the platform. |

## Jobs To Be Done

1. **Functional job** — Fill the bench with mid-market European clients ready to buy AI implementation.
2. **Emotional job** — Stop the 'where is the next client' worry that boutique AI founders carry.
3. **Social job** — Have a CRM with a clean pipeline instead of a spreadsheet of 'leads from last quarter'.

## Success Metrics

- Engagements closed per consultancy per quarter — ≥ 2 median.
- Pipeline value per consultancy per quarter — track median.
- Outbound-to-meeting conversion rate ≥ 5%.
- Sales-lead NPS ≥ 50 at month 3.

## Pricing & Monetization

Per-seat pricing: EUR 149/seat/month for the GTM engine (discovery + outreach + CRM sync). Proposal-drafting add-on: EUR 99/seat/month. Annual discount 20%.

## Competitive Landscape

- Apollo / Lemlist / Reply.io — strong Western tools; no AI-vertical signal; no Eastern European coverage.
- LinkedIn Sales Navigator alone — strong on intent, weak on automation.
- Outbound agencies — bespoke, expensive, slow.

## Risks & Open Questions

- [ ] GDPR risk on prospect data — Mitigation: lawful basis per prospect; data minimisation; per-prospect erasure.
- [ ] Outbound spam pattern — Mitigation: per-prospect cap; warm-up sequence; explicit opt-out handling.
- [ ] AI-vertical signal accuracy — Mitigation: explicit signal definitions; quarterly retraining; manual review of high-value prospects.
