# Fans of paper planners lack an iPad app that would allow importing PDF layout templates and using them as a foundation for digital notes

## Value Proposition

Take any PDF planner template and use it as the background of a writable iPad note.

## Target Users

People who like paper planners (per the title) and already own an iPad with Apple Pencil.

## Jobs To Be Done

When the user has a PDF planner layout they already like, turn it into a digital surface they can write on with a Pencil.

## Success Metrics

Whether users keep a note open across multiple sessions, and how many distinct PDF templates they import per account.

## Competitive Landscape

Other iPad note apps exist (Goodnotes, Notability), but the source does not name any direct competitor that imports third-party PDF planner templates.

## Risks & Open Questions

The source gives no signal on whether users want sync, sharing, or search; those must be deferred or validated in interviews before adding them.
