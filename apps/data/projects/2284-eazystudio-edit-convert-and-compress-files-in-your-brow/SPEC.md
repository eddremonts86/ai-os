# EazyStudio – Edit, convert, and compress files in your browser with 80+ tools

## Problem

EazyStudio is a browser-based toolkit with over 80 free utilities for creators, developers, and teams. It lets you compress, convert, edit, and generate images, PDFs, audio, video, code, and game assets without uploads or sign-ups. Most tools run locally to keep files private and fast.
