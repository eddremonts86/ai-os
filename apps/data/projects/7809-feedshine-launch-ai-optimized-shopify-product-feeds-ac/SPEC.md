# SPEC.md — Feedshine – Launch AI-optimized Shopify product feeds across major channels

## Problem

Feedshine helps Shopify stores publish high-quality product feeds to Google, Meta, Microsoft, Pinterest, TikTok, Snapchat, and more. It builds a working feed in minutes from your catalog, fills all 87 Google attributes, and uses AI to improve titles, categories, labels, and other fields. Manage one catalog with separate feeds per channel and market, including local pricing and translations. Automate updates, catch bad data before it reaches channels, and scale to 100k products.
