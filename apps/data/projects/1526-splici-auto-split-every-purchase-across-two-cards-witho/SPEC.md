# Splici – Auto-split every purchase across two cards without hassle

## Problem

Have you ever thought, "I wish I could put half of this on my credit card and the other half on my debit card" for a big purchase? What if you could do that with any purchase you want? That's what we built Splici for: add any cards you want, choose how to split a purchase, and generate a virtual card that does all the work for you. Budget the right way, with your money. Try it out today; it's free! View startup
