# SourceGPU – Buy or rent enterprise GPU infrastructure from vetted providers

## Problem

SourceGPU operates a marketplace for AI hardware and compute. It lets you buy enterprise GPU servers, clusters, workstations, and standalone GPUs from verified suppliers and rent high-performance GPU capacity for training, inference, and fine-tuning. The platform supports secure escrow transactions, offers diverse inventory including H100, H200, and Blackwell systems, and connects global buyers with qualified sellers and compute partners to match demand with available infrastructure.
