# Camera for OBS Studio – Turn your iPhone into a low-latency pro camera for OBS Studio

## Problem

Camera for OBS Studio lets you stream high-quality video and audio from your iPhone or iPad directly into OBS Studio. Use a low-latency wired USB connection for reliable sync or go wireless via NDI when you need freedom of movement. Control focus, exposure, white balance, and more with DSLR-like manual settings, lens selection, grids, and focus peaking. Set up multiple devices, manage them remotely from a browser, and run on macOS or Windows with the companion OBS plugin. View startup
