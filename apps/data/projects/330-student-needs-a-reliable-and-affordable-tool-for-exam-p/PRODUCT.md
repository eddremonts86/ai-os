# Student needs a reliable and affordable tool for exam preparation

> Product brief authored from the source title and category. The poster's text was not available (source.name: manual); sections below re-state the problem and infer only what the title and category support.

## Value Proposition

An Indian student preparing for a competitive exam gets reliable daily practice, a topic weakness signal the student actually trusts, and a weekly summary a parent can read - for less than the cost of one private coaching session.

## Target Users

- Indian students preparing for JEE / NEET / UPSC / SSC / banking exams on a tight family budget.
- Tier-2 / tier-3 city students with patchy connectivity who need offline-capable revision.
- Parents who want a weekly-readable progress summary without sitting in on coaching.

## Jobs To Be Done

1. **Functional job** - Cover the syllabus in a structured way each week without paying for full coaching.
2. **Emotional job** - Stop feeling that the gap between self-study and coaching is insurmountable.
3. **Social job** - Give the family a visible signal that preparation is on track, not a vague promise.

## Success Metrics

- **Activation:** student answers the first daily set on the day of signup.
- **Adherence:** >= 4 sessions per week for at least 6 weeks (cohort retention).
- **Outcome proxy:** mean accuracy on flagged weak topics improves by >= 15 percentage points over 4 weeks.

## Competitive Landscape

- **Paid coaching (Unacademy, PW, BYJU's)** - effective but expensive and not adaptive per student.
- **Free YouTube channels** - inconsistent depth and no tracking.
- **Mock-test apps (Embibe, Oliveboard)** - good for tests, weaker for daily adaptive revision.
