# Traders lose money due to emotional decisions (fear, greed) and lack a tool for real-time control of their psychological state during trading

> Auto-generated product brief. Review and refine before MVP scoping.

## Value Proposition

Replaces the post-trade regret journal with a real-time state monitor that flags emotional-risk moments before a trade is placed and enforces a cool-down.

## Target Users

Indian retail traders (equity, F&O, crypto); quant-minded traders who want state monitoring on top of their existing setup.

## Jobs To Be Done

When I am about to place a trade, I want to see my current emotional state and a cool-down suggestion so I do not act on fear or greed. When I finish a session, I want to review the moments where my state correlated with bad trades so I can learn the pattern.

## Success Metrics

Active traders in monthly subscription. Cool-down acceptance rate. Self-reported reduction in emotional-trade frequency. Broker API integration count.

## Competitive Landscape

TraderSync and Edgewonk are post-trade journals, not real-time. Broker-native tools (Zerodha's Streak, Angel One's SmartAPI) are strategy-focused, not state-focused. Mindfulness apps (Calm, Headspace) are not trading-specific. No source-named direct competitor combining biometric state with cool-down enforcement.

## Risks & Open Questions

Biometric-data reliability varies by device. Cool-down enforcement can be circumvented (users can just close the app). No false-claims about returns — must be careful in copy. Need 15 trader interviews before claims about behavioural improvement are credible.
