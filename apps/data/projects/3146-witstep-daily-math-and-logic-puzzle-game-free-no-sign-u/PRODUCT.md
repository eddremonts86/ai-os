# Witstep – daily math and logic puzzle game (Free No Sign-up)

## Value Proposition

A daily math and logic puzzle you can play immediately: free, no sign-up, with a patterns mode alongside the daily.

## Target Users

Casual puzzle players who want a short daily warm-up and will leave rather than create an account.

## Jobs To Be Done

- Play today's puzzle without registering
- Come back tomorrow for a new one
- Work through patterns puzzles when I want more than the daily

## Success Metrics

- Day-over-day return rate on the daily puzzle
- Puzzle completion rate versus abandonment
- Whether players move from the daily into the patterns mode

## Competitive Landscape

Math puzzle apps (Brilliant, Mathway) exist, but the source does not name any direct competitor that ships a daily math and logic puzzle with no sign-up.

## Risks & Open Questions

- The poster says they will expand the pattern library over time; cadence is the open commitment
- With no sign-up there is no account to hang a streak on, so retention has to work anonymously
- Difficulty calibration is unaddressed in the source and decides whether a daily is fun or annoying
