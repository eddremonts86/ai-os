# SPEC.md — How do you build organic traction as an indie dev?

## Problem

I have recently become a fulltime indie gamedev.<p>While I&#x27;m willing to trust myself on technical and creative output, the topic of getting the word out that I might release something interesting and worth people&#x27;s time seems to have become increasingly daunting. Most popular technical channels like HN &#x2F; specific sub-reddits  require active community participation and &quot;karma-farming&quot; just to be able to post something, which is hard for me to spend time on (I&#x27;d rather work on my stuff). Twitter(X) &#x2F; Bluesky have low visibility for gamedev topics. All text-focused websites seem to have become really hard organic marketing channels especially after AI slop came in.<p>IG &#x2F; Tiktok &#x2F; Twitch would be channels I&#x27;d rather use when I get into the actual spending-marketing-dollars mode (i.e., inorganic; which is also when I&#x27;d get into Steam &#x2F; App Store level marketing as well).<p>So far, Youtube seems to be the one place where the algorithm might not be quite as punitive as the gates that other places have put up, but it&#x27;s not a place for getting high-quality feedback (surprisingly videos on LinkedIn do rather well - on engagement but not sure that&#x27;s the right channel).<p>What do folks here in a similar situation do?
