## Value Proposition

_Based on source brief:_ Nuatus brings your day into a single, private view you can open on any device. It combines tasks, notes, checklists, bookmarks, birthdays, local weather, and optional read-only Google and Microsoft Calendar and Contacts, then syncs everything securely across phone, tablet, and laptop. Capture with one box, organize with folders and groups, and find anything instantly, even offline. It runs in any browser, adds to your home screen with a tap, and never sells or mines your data.
