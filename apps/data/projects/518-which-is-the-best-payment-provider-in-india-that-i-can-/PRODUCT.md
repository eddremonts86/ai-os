## Value Proposition

A non-Indian SaaS founder can pick an Indian-friendly payment provider in under 5 minutes by filtering the comparison on their actual country, customer country, and monthly volume, instead of reading four different pricing pages and a Reddit thread.

## Target Users

| Stakeholder | Why they care |
|---|---|
| Non-IN SaaS founder with IN customers | Cannot get Razorpay directly; needs to know the realistic wiring options. |
| Indian founder selling internationally | Needs multi-currency settlement and a path to USD/EUR payouts. |
| Marketplace / agency handling IN payments | Wants to compare UPI vs. card success rates. |

1. **Functional job** — Pick a payment provider that actually settles to a non-IN bank.
