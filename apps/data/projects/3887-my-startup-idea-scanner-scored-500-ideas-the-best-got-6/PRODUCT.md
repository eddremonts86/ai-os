# My startup-idea scanner scored 500 ideas; the best got 6.3/10

## Value Proposition

A reality check for business ideas. The scanner takes your input, your market and your background and returns a handful of ideas scored on winnability — can a solo founder with your specific background win against what already exists? The author publishes the unflattering results: 500+ ideas scored since June, best 6.3, median 1.3, none above 7. One free scan without an account; further scans cost because each one burns Vertex tokens.

**One-liner:** A winnability-scored startup idea scanner: can a solo founder with your background win against what exists.

## Target Users

| Stakeholder | Why they care |
|---|---|
| Solo founders | Test an idea against their own background before committing months to it. |
| Indie hackers | Compare a set of ideas honestly instead of chasing generic top-10 lists. |
| Idea-curious browsers | A free no-account scan, with the author's own score distribution as the demo. |

The author built the tool for himself first; the audiences follow from the free-scan offer and the posted score distribution.

## Jobs To Be Done

1. **Functional job** — Turn input, market and background into a small set of candidate ideas.

2. **Functional job** — Score each idea on winnability for a solo founder with that background.

3. **Functional job** — Avoid false confidence: the score must reflect winnability, not on-demand optimism.

4. **Emotional job** — Know which ideas are actually worth building before investing months.

## Success Metrics

- **Score honesty:** the distribution (best 6.3, median 1.3, none above 7) is published rather than hidden.
- **Winnability basis:** scoring measures whether a solo founder with the user's background can win against existing alternatives.
- **Funnel:** a free no-account scan leads to paid scans.
- **Token cost:** each scan's Vertex token cost is metered and covered by pricing.

## Pricing & Monetization

A free scan without an account is included. The tool is otherwise paid — the author states each scan costs Vertex tokens, which is why it is not free — but no price for paid scans is stated.

## Competitive Landscape

The post names no competitors. The category is business-idea evaluation and founder-fit tools — idea generators, market-validator services and LLM-based business advisors. The stated differentiator is the winnability framing: scoring whether a solo founder with a specific background can win against what already exists, with a published, brutally low score distribution as proof of honesty.

## Risks & Open Questions

- [ ] The median score of 1.3 suggests the tool mostly says no; whether that is truth or an overly harsh model is the open question the author himself is testing.
- [ ] Winnability is the author's own model; no independent validation of its calibration exists.
- [ ] Vertex token cost per scan sets a floor on pricing the author has not disclosed.
- [ ] The pivot from 1 million ideas to 10 is recent; product-market fit is unproven.
- [ ] The post is an invitation to try the scanner, which reads as pre-adoption, not traction.
