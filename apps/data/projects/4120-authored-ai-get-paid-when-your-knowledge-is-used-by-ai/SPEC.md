# Authored AI – Get paid when your knowledge is used by AI

## Problem

For years, AI has been trained on authors' work without compensation. Authored AI changes that by creating a marketplace for AI agents built by real experts, trained on private knowledge not found on the internet. Package your knowledge as an agent and earn every time someone chats with it. Set it up once and earn 24/7, with no audience needed. For users, get one-on-one access to rare specialists who know their field, and every answer pays the expert behind it. View startup
