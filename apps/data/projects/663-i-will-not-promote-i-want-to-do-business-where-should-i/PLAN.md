# I will not promote: I want to do business. Where should I start?

## Tech Stack

Not applicable. No product, no tech choices. The only tangential reference is "books, courses, resources" that the author wants recommended.

## Architecture

None. There is no product.

The structure of the post is a seven-question funnel, in roughly the order a pre-founder would ask them: idea selection → importance of the idea → field-switching → copy vs. unique → differentiation → mistakes → resources.

## Milestones

The post has no milestones. The author is pre-decision, not pre-execution. If a "do the work" plan were extracted, the steps would be:

1. Read or watch the resources recommended in the comments.
2. Pick one or two of the seven questions and pressure-test the answer with someone the author respects.
3. Choose a starting idea (existing-model-improved vs. unique) only after the previous step is done.

## Risks

- **The author is asking too many questions at once.** Seven broad questions risk shallow replies. The post acknowledges this ("I know its too many points") but does not prioritise.
- **Generic answers.** A long open-ended list tends to attract generic advice ("follow your passion", "talk to customers") that does not help with the actual decision.
- **Premature confidence.** The author has a "business background" but no business yet; the most useful signal is which advice they should ignore, and that is not asked.
