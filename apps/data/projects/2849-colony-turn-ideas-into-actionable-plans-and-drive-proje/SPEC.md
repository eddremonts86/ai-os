# Colony – Turn ideas into actionable plans and drive projects to done

## Problem

Colony is an AI project platform that turns rough ideas, briefs, or overloaded weeks into clear, actionable plans. It includes an agent for planning, assignment, and review to keep every step visible and accountable. Teams get a daily workflow that highlights what needs attention, capacity insights to prevent burnout, and client and CRM tools in one workspace. With core project management features, integrations, and scalable tiers from solo to enterprise, it keeps work moving until finished. View startup
