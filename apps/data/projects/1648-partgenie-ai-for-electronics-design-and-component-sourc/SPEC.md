# PartGenie – AI for electronics design and component sourcing

## Problem

PartGenie is your AI hardware companion for electronics design and component sourcing. It helps hardware engineers, designers, and procurement teams quickly find the right electronic components and make better sourcing decisions. Search across 22M+ electronic components with detailed specifications, datasheets, and verified alternatives. Features include component search and datasheet retrieval, BOM risk assessment, verified alternative suggestions, AI-powered datasheet analysis, and hardware design and sourcing assistance. Design faster, source smarter, and manage your hardware development workflow more efficiently. View startup
