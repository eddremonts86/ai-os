# Business setup and launch challenges in India

> Auto-generated product brief. Review and refine before MVP scoping.

## Value Proposition

An Indian first-time founder goes from 'I want to start a company' to 'GST registered, current account open, first invoice sent' in under 14 days, at one flat price below the typical CA-led path.

## Target Users

| Stakeholder | Why they care |
|---|---|
| Indian first-time founder | Has the idea; does not have the CA relationship; needs the paperwork handled. |
| Indian 2-cofounder team | Needs shareholding + founder agreement + the registrations in one flow. |
| Indian CA / CS firm | Wants a white-label setup flow for their clients without building the UI themselves. |

## Jobs To Be Done

1. **Functional job** — Set up and launch the business in days, not weeks, without a CA retainer.
2. **Emotional job** — Stop the dread of 'I should have started last year' that founders carry.
3. **Social job** — Tell a co-founder or investor 'the company is registered, here is the CIN' on day one.

## Success Metrics

- Time-to-CIN median ≤ 7 days for Pvt Ltd.
- Time-to-GST-registration median ≤ 10 days.
- Customer-reported savings vs CA-led setup ≥ ₹15,000 median.
- Founder NPS ≥ 50 at month 3.

## Pricing & Monetization

Flat ₹9,999 for the setup bundle (entity + PAN + TAN + GST + current account + Udyam + first month of compliance). Premium ₹24,999 adds: founder agreement, ESOP setup, first 3 months of compliance, and a CA partner on WhatsApp.

## Competitive Landscape

- Local CA firms — strong but bespoke, slow, no online tracking.
- IndiaFilings / ClearTax — broad but no end-to-end setup flow; user still coordinates the steps.
- Zoho / Tally sign-up flows — cover accounting, not the registration gauntlet.

## Risks & Open Questions

- [ ] MCA / GSTN API instability — Mitigation: CA-partner hand-off for filings; status page per step; fallback to manual e-filing if APIs are down.
- [ ] Founder KYC failure — Aadhaar/PAN mismatch or name mismatch is the most common delay. Mitigation: pre-flight check at signup; explicit re-submit flow.
- [ ] Bank-account opening delays — Mitigation: parallel-track with two bank partners; status visibility per partner.
