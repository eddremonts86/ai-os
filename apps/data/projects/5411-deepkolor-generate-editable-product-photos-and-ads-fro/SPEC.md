# SPEC.md — DeepKolor – Generate editable product photos and ads from a simple description

## Problem

DeepKolor is an AI design agent that creates editable visuals for marketing and ecommerce. Describe what you need, upload a product photo if you have one, and chat to refine it until it fits. The platform produces studio-quality product images, ad creatives, posters, virtual try-ons, background removals, and more, all inside one canvas with image and video workflows. Teams use DeepKolor to iterate faster, export watermark-free assets, and create campaign-ready visuals with commercial rights. View startup
