# SPEC.md — AlignMe – Track progress toward goals with AI that scores your daily efforts

## Problem

AlignMe helps you focus on goals instead of endless to-dos. Enter your goals, then jot down what you did during the day. AlignMe’s AI parses your notes, maps activities to goals, and awards points based on impact so you see real progress. The app visualizes where your time goes, highlights distractions and unknowns, and encourages compassionate reflection. Use the insights to adjust your week and keep moving projects forward without busywork. View startup
