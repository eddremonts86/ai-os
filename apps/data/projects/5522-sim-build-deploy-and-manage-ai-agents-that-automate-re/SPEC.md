# SPEC.md — Sim – Build, deploy, and manage AI agents that automate real work

## Problem

Sim is an open-source AI workspace for building, deploying, and managing AI agents. Teams connect over 1,000 integrations and major LLMs, provide agents context with knowledge bases, tables, and files, and design logic visually, conversationally, or with code. Sim offers end-to-end observability with traces, logs, and real costs, plus enterprise features like SOC 2 compliance and self-hosting. It helps automate workflows across sales, support, IT, compliance, finance, and HR at production scale. View startup
