# Starting outreach at a local AI law startup, I will not promote

> Auto-generated product brief. Review and refine before MVP scoping.

## Value Proposition

A product that shipped too early burns its launch on a version that did not work. The audience it
reached is still the cheapest one it will ever have, and it is unreachable in practice because
nobody knows who saw what, and because the story that earned the attention is no longer true.
This keeps the earned audience, splits it by what each person actually saw, and makes the second
announcement about what changed.

**One-liner:** Ask the people who already tried it to look again, and say something different to
each group depending on what they saw the first time.

| Team with a decayed press hit | Traffic from one article has died; the people it reached are still identifiable and are the cheapest audience they will ever have. |
| Operator emailing lapsed users by hand | Already doing this in waves without a record of which wave worked. |

## Jobs To Be Done

1. **Functional** — "tell the people who already tried this that it works now, and say it
 differently to each group depending on what they saw."
2. **Emotional** — stop postponing the post. The source names avoidance, not ignorance, as the
 blocker.
3. **Social** — sound like the company they are now without disowning the story that got them
 their first users.

## Success Metrics

- **Reachability:** share of the first launch's audience that can be contacted at all. If this is
 low, there is no product here, and it is the first thing to measure.
- **Response by segment:** replies and reactivations per segment, which is the comparison the
 poster cannot make while sending waves by hand.
- **Reactivation:** lapsed users who return and then use the product a second time. A single
 return visit is the message working; the second is the product working.
