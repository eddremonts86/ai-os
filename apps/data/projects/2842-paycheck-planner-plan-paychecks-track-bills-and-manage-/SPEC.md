# Paycheck Planner – Plan paychecks, track bills, and manage debt with AI budgeting insights

## Problem

Paycheck Planner helps you plan each paycheck so you always know which bills to cover, how much to save, and how fast you can pay down debt. It connects to your bank accounts, organizes upcoming expenses, and builds payoff plans you can follow. Use AI-driven insights to spot trends, adjust budgets, and make confident decisions. Stay on top of due dates, reduce interest, and align your spending with your goals without juggling spreadsheets or guesswork. View startup
