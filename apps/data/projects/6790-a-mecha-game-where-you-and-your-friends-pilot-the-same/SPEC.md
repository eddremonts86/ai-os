# SPEC.md — A mecha game where you and your friends pilot the SAME giant robot

## Problem

2 months and 3,000 prompts later, we have Mecha Royale (mecharoyale.com)<p>It’s a browser-based battle royale with 100 mechas fighting across one massive map.
But the part I’ve been having the most fun with is squad mode where you and your friends climb into the same mecha.<p>One person can pilot it while your teammates control different weapons. So you’re basically yelling at your friends while trying to operate one giant war machine together as 99 other mechas are trying to kill you.<p>I built most of the game with Claude Code, including a lot of the multiplayer infrastructure behind it.<p>Still polishing it, but I think it&#x27;s finally at the point where I&#x27;m ready to share. You can play it now at mecharoyale.com<p>I&#x27;d love to hear your feedback! Thank you in advance.
