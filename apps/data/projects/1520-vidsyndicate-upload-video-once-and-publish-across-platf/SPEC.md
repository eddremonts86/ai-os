# VidSyndicate – Upload video once and publish across platforms from one dashboard

## Problem

VidSyndicate helps creators publish content across Facebook, YouTube, Pinterest, Twitch, Mastodon, Bluesky, and more from one dashboard. Upload once, use AI to generate titles, descriptions, and hashtags, then schedule and publish platform-ready posts without re-uploading files. Every asset is securely stored in the cloud, and the publishing calendar keeps everything organized. VidSyndicate also includes a free mobile-friendly Start Page to showcase your content and social links. View startup
