# Procurement data quality suffers — supplier contacts outdated, prices stale, history lost. Existing tools are slow, expensive, or built for the wrong region.

> Auto-generated product brief. Review and refine before MVP scoping.

## Value Proposition

Stop losing the institutional memory of why you chose each supplier. The first 100 procurement teams pay for the service from the recovered time auditing a stale contact that no longer needs chasing.

## Target Users

SMB procurement teams in Brazil and Latin America (5-50 people) whose supplier data lives in spreadsheets and whose procurement decisions are losing auditability as the team grows.

## Jobs To Be Done

Functional: have a current, structured supplier database that does not require a daily audit. Emotional: stop the embarrassment of contacting a dead email or quoting a year-old price. Social: be the procurement team that wins the audit because the history is intact.

## Success Metrics

At least 80% of supplier contacts current after 90 days. Refresh failure rate below 5%. At least 50% of users actively adding decision history entries. Renewal rate above 70% after 6 months.

## Competitive Landscape

Coupa and Ariba are aimed at large enterprises and do not ingest from spreadsheets. TOTVS is large-enterprise too. Airtable and Notion are generic CRUD. No mainstream service combines spreadsheet ingest with enrichment and decision history for the Brazilian SMB procurement team.

## Risks & Open Questions

Enrichment may guess wrong on a small supplier with no public footprint. Pricing data may be misleading if scraped without consent. Competitor scaring tactics if the service is seen as a supplier-relationship database. Costs of repeated enrichment can grow.
