# SPEC.md — FOSS browser-based visual Kegel trainer for men

## Problem

Hey, HN. Long time reader; first submission.<p>I read HN daily and greatly enjoy looking at the projects that people are working on. I find the single-page websites particularly fun and decided to make one of my own.<p>KegelTrainer.net is a visual Kegel trainer for men with configurable settings based on clinically recommended protocols. It’s ready to go instantly with no login.<p>I made this tool because I do Kegels myself as a man of a certain age and wanted an easy way to track hold times and reps.
