# How to build something from the ground up? i will not promote

## Problem

A Reddit meet-other-builders post from a software engineer with a stable job who is more excited by side projects, startup ideas, figuring out how products could work, shipping things, and seeing if people care. The poster does not need to be a founder — they would be happy becoming one of the first people who helps turn someone else's idea into something real. They acknowledge startups are stressful, uncertain, and messy, but want that experience while still young enough to take the risk. They explicitly do not have a polished pitch and are not asking for a "founding engineer" offer — they want to meet people who are actually building.

## Objective

Capture the poster's meet-builders request and the framing (they want to be an early contributor, not necessarily the founder), without inventing a specific startup, stack, or joinability signal.

## Target Users

- Primary: the poster, a software engineer with a stable job who wants to join an early-stage startup as an early contributor.
- Secondary: founders looking for an early engineer, who might find the post useful.

## MVP Scope

- The poster's "I want to build" framing.
- The framing that they are open to being an early contributor, not necessarily the founder.

## Design Direction

See `DESIGN.md` for this project's design tokens.

## Constraints

- The poster explicitly says they are not asking for a "founding engineer" offer.
- No specific stack, startup idea, or geography is named.
- The poster has a stable engineering job — they are not quitting to build yet.
- No willingness-to-pay signal.
