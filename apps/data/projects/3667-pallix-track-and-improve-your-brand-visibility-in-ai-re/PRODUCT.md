# Pallix – Track and improve your brand visibility in AI recommendations

> Auto-generated product brief. Review and refine before MVP scoping.

## Value Proposition

Pallix is a product that gives marketing teams visibility into how AI engines recommend their brand, with citations and community signals mapped, sentiment and competitor share tracked across named channels, and prioritized fixes for content, authority and technical gaps surfaced from the evidence. The product is offered as a free audit and a guided demo, consistent with the BetaList listing.

The source is vendor copy rather than a technical write-up, so every claim about what Pallix does is attributed to the listing, and the methodology behind any metric — visibility score, competitor share, sentiment, citation graph, prioritization, impact measurement — is treated as a design choice to be documented in the product rather than a number from the listing. The three named AI engines (ChatGPT, Perplexity, Gemini) and the four named signal sources (Reddit, YouTube, marketplaces, editorial sites) are the scope; expansion beyond them is open but is not promised.

**One-liner:** Pallix shows marketing teams how AI engines recommend their brand, with citations and community signals mapped, competitor share and sentiment tracked, and prioritized fixes for content, authority and technical gaps surfaced from the evidence.

## Jobs To Be Done

1. **Functional job** — Know which brands appear for buyer prompts across ChatGPT, Perplexity and Gemini, with citations mapped.
2. **Functional job** — Track competitor share and sentiment over time, with the methodology auditable.
3. **Functional job** — Get prioritized fixes for content, authority and technical gaps surfaced from the evidence.
4. **Emotional job** — Trust the numbers because the methodology is published, not because the vendor copy sounds confident.
5. **Social job** — Share a visibility view with a colleague or client in a form they can audit, not a screenshot of vendor copy.

## Success Metrics

- **Methodology coverage** — share of surfaced metrics (visibility score, competitor share, sentiment, prioritization, impact measurement) with a published methodology page, since methodology coverage is the credibility claim.
- **Prompt-set rationale coverage** — share of monitored brands with a documented prompt set and rationale, since generic prompts miss the buyer's actual questions.
- **Citation completeness** — share of brand-presence entries with a citation chain attached, since unattributed presence is a trust failure.
- **Sentiment failure-mode disclosure** — share of sentiment outputs that name their failure modes, since sentiment in AI answers is noisy.
- **Fix traceability** — share of prioritized fixes that trace back to the evidence (a prompt, a citation, a signal), since ungrounded fixes are not actionable.
- **Impact-measurement methodology adherence** — share of impact views that use the published methodology rather than an ad-hoc comparison.

## Pricing & Monetization

The architecture fixes only the cost shape: cost scales with the volume of monitored prompts, the number of monitored brands and the depth of the citation graph, not with seats, so any paid shape would have to be priced around monitoring volume or per monitored brand rather than per seat.

## Competitive Landscape

- **In-house AI-visibility tracking** — what marketing teams do today when they do not have a product; the cost is the engineering and analyst time, which Pallix is meant to replace.
- **Traditional brand-monitoring tools** — focused on social listening and news; the differentiator is the AI-engine-first surface (ChatGPT, Perplexity, Gemini), not the social-listening breadth.
- **AI-search and SEO tools** — overlap on the SEO-adjacent angle; the differentiator is the buyer-prompt monitoring and the citation graph across AI engines specifically.

The listing names no specific competitor, so the comparison stops here.

## Risks & Open Questions

- [ ] Publish the methodology for every surfaced metric (visibility score, competitor share, sentiment, prioritization, impact measurement) before the metric is shown.
- [ ] Document the per-monitored-brand prompt set with rationale, since generic prompts miss the buyer's actual questions.
- [ ] Name the failure modes of sentiment in AI answers explicitly, rather than presenting a sentiment number as authoritative.
- [ ] Publish the data-source coverage: which subreddits, which YouTube channels, which marketplaces, which editorial sites, and the rationale.
