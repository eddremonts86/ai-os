# SPEC.md — My21Reset – Get weekly meals, movement, and shopping lists in your calendar

## Problem

My21Reset plans meals and gentle movement sessions each week and delivers them to your existing calendar. Set your food preferences and schedule, then get breakfast, lunch, and dinner with recipes and ingredients in each event, plus home sessions, walks, and water prompts added to your routine. Swap meals and your shopping list updates by aisle. Start with a free week, then choose monthly, yearly, or a 21-day one-off plan you can pause or cancel anytime. View startup
