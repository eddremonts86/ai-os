# Free economic nexus checker for online sellers (all 50 states)

## Value Proposition

A seller can check economic nexus in all 50 US states for free, instead of subscribing to a sales-tax service to learn the same yes/no.

## Target Users

US-based online sellers, mostly small and indie, who sell into several states and do not know which ones now expect them to register for sales tax.

## Jobs To Be Done

- Enter my sales per state and see, per state, whether I have crossed the threshold
- Know which states I should register in before a filing deadline, not after
- Re-check after a growth month without paying for a subscription

## Success Metrics

- Sellers who complete a full 50-state check in one visit
- Return visits after a sales period closes, since the check is periodic by nature
- How current the per-state threshold table is when a check is run

## Competitive Landscape

Sales-tax-nexus services (TaxJar, Avalara) exist, but the source does not name any direct competitor that gives a free economic-nexus checker for all 50 states.

## Risks & Open Questions

- The source does not say where the per-state thresholds are sourced from or how often they are refreshed
- A wrong yes/no here has tax consequences; the disclaimer and update cadence carry the product
- Whether sellers know their per-state sales figures well enough to enter them
