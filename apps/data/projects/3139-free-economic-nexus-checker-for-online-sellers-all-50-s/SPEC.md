# Free economic nexus checker for online sellers (all 50 states)

## Problem

The poster shipped a free economic-nexus checker at nexussnapshot.com that covers all 50 US states, intended for online sellers who need to know whether they have crossed a state's sales-tax threshold.

## Objective

Tell an online seller, in one click, whether they have crossed the economic-nexus threshold in any US state.

## Target Users

US-based online sellers (often small/indie) who need to know whether they owe sales-tax registration in any state.

## MVP Scope

Free web tool where a seller enters sales per state and gets a yes/no nexus answer for each of the 50 states, based on the published threshold.

## Design Direction

See `DESIGN.md` for this project's design tokens.

## Constraints

Threshold rules change; stale data could mislead.
