# SPEC.md — PitchMonster – Practice sales calls with AI buyers and get instant coaching

## Problem

PitchMonster helps sales teams practice real conversations, analyze live calls, and get AI coaching before and after every deal. The platform simulates buyers that match your ICP in 29 languages, challenges reps with objections, and scores performance against your playbooks. Teams create scenarios from documents, calls, and CRM data, integrate with Salesforce and Gong, and track improvement with clear analytics. Enterprises use it to cut ramp time and standardize coaching at scale. View startup
