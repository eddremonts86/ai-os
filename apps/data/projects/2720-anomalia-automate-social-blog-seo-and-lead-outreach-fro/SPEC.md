# Anomalia – Automate social, blog, SEO, and lead outreach from one dashboard

## Problem

Anomalia automates distribution for small businesses by planning, creating, and publishing social posts, blog articles, and SEO content across channels. It learns your brand voice and visuals from your site and uploads, proposes an editorial plan, and lets you approve it with one tap. The built-in radar scans news and social to draft timely posts and surface warm leads from Reddit, Instagram, and Threads, then tracks replies and conversions. Use it to grow organic reach, rank on Google, and get cited by AI assistants. View startup
