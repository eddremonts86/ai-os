# ToolScope – Compare AI tools and chat with custom AI characters

## Problem

ToolScope combines a verified AI tools directory with an AI character chat platform. It helps you discover, compare, and evaluate real AI products across categories, while also letting you explore and converse with persistent AI personalities that retain backstory, mood, and memory. You can create your own AI characters for others to chat with and use features like pricing comparisons, stacks, and recommendations to choose the right tools with confidence. View startup
