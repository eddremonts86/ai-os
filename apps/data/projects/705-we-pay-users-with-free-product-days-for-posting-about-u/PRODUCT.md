# We pay users with free product days for posting about us - the experiment

> Auto-generated product brief. Review and refine before MVP scoping.

## Value Proposition

_Based on source brief:_ Two-person bootstrapped product, no ad budget. So the marketing budget lives inside the product: any user who posts publicly about the extension sends us the link and gets one free day of the top plan per post. Uncapped - 30 posts is a free month. [preview.redd.it/nqmx3kyo9rjh1.png…](https://preview.redd.it/nqmx3kyo9rjh1.png?width=473&format=png&auto=webp&s=e8dbec70ee5b90e86944c13f7e6a10edab18823f) Design decisions that mattered: - Social posts ONLY. Nothing touches store reviews, ever - review incentives are policy violations and trust poison. - Uncapped on purpose: the person doing 20 posts is exactly the evangelist we want to super-serve. - Manual fulfillment by email - the personal thank-you drives repeat posting more than the reward does. - The reward is paid-tier access, so the reward itself demos the upgrade. User posts convert better than anything we write, because they sound like humans. Launch is Sunday; this flywheel is a big part of the plan. Curious if others have paid users in product instead of ads. submitted by /u/Ok_Negotiation_2587 [link] [comments]

**One-liner:** Pay users in paid-tier access for each public post they make about the product, uncapped, fulfilled by personal email — turning the marketing budget into a flywheel that demos the upgrade.

## Target Users

| Stakeholder | Why they care |
|---|---|
| Two-person bootstrapped SaaS founders | Have a paid tier but no ad budget; need a launch flywheel that runs on existing user goodwill. |
| Power users who already evangelise | Want a tangible reward (paid-tier access) that proves the upgrade is worth the price. |
| Indie hackers running paid extensions | Want a trackable, manually-fulfillable loop without giving up margin to ads. |

## Jobs To Be Done

1. **Functional job** — Acquire paying users at launch without paying for ads.
2. **Emotional job** — Feel that growth is being earned by real users, not bought.
3. **Social job** — Public posts read as authentic peer-to-peer recommendations rather than founder copy.

## Success Metrics

The post does not publish specific numbers. Implied metrics from the founder's reasoning:

- Posts submitted per week — the loop's only input.
- Repeat posters per user — the founder's claim that the personal thank-you, not the reward, drives repeat posting is the testable hypothesis.
- Net new paid conversions attributable to a known post (track the email → upgrade path).

## Pricing & Monetization

Reward is paid-tier product access, priced at one day per verified public post. Uncapped: 30 posts = 30 free days = roughly a free month. Cost of the reward is the per-day top-plan margin (the founder treats this as marketing spend). The reward is structured to demo the upgrade rather than to subsidise non-payers.

## Risks & Open Questions

- [ ] Manual fulfilment does not scale past ~50 active posters / week; what happens at 500?
- [ ] The reward is paid-tier access; abusive users could post filler content to harvest days. How is post quality verified?
