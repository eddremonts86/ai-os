# SPEC.md — Thumbly – Generate high-CTR thumbnails from your video in 30 seconds

## Problem

Thumbly is an AI thumbnail generator designed for YouTube and TikTok creators. Paste a video URL or upload a file, choose from over 20 style presets, and produce multiple formats in one batch. You can fine-tune results with the Style Tuner, custom prompts, and a Face Library that preserves your real likeness. Edit elements with AI, swap faces for A/B tests, and score designs with the Thumbly Score across virality, clarity, idea, curiosity, and emotion to boost click-through rates. View startup
