# Did anyone here A/B test a free demo of your software vs a free trial?

## Problem

A Reddit pricing-research post. The poster is researching whether to offer free trials for their SaaS and is considering free demos as an alternative. They note that stats show free trials often convert better, but a free demo (a gated, pre-defined version a prospect can play with — used SalesForce as an example with a pre-defined project they can add and remove numbers from) reduces the "I want to know what I'm signing up for" anxiety. They are open to offering both and are asking if anyone here has A/B tested a free demo vs. a free trial.

## Objective

Capture the A/B-test question (free demo vs. free trial conversion) so the poster can pick a pricing-experiment design, without inventing conversion rates, lift numbers, or named A/B-test winners.

## Target Users

- Primary: the poster, a SaaS founder researching pricing / conversion experiments.
- Secondary: other SaaS founders weighing free-demo vs. free-trial as a top-of-funnel choice.

## MVP Scope

- Document the A/B-test question.
- Document the framing difference (free trial = time-boxed full access; free demo = gated, pre-defined scenario a prospect plays with).
- Capture any community replies that name a tested lift / drop in either arm.

See `DESIGN.md` for this project's design tokens.

## Constraints

- The poster does not name their product or category — only "my SaaS".
- The poster is in the research phase, not running an A/B test yet.
- The free-demo framing is explicitly the "SalesForce pre-defined project" pattern, not a guided tour.
- No conversion lift, sample size, or significance threshold is in the source.
