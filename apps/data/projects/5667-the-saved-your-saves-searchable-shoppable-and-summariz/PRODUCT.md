## Value Proposition

_Based on source brief:_ The Saved helps you capture links, videos, posts, and screenshots from anywhere and actually use them. It auto-transcribes and summarizes content, pulls out steps and recipes, adds tags and categories, and surfaces direct shopping links and maps locations. Search works across all platforms and formats so you can find what you saved in seconds and jump to key moments. Use the web app, mobile apps, and browser extension, bulk import old saves, and share collections to manage your data on your terms.
