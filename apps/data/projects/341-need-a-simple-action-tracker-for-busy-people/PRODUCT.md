# Need a simple action tracker for busy people

> Product brief authored from the source title and category. The poster's text was not available (source.name: manual); sections below re-state the problem and infer only what the title and category support.

## Value Proposition

A UAE professional taps once on their Watch or Lock Screen, the action lands in a private timeline on the phone, and the day ends with a 30-second review - no project set-up, no sub-tasks, no team invite.

## Target Users

- UAE working professionals (consultants, PMs, founders) who already own an Apple Watch and use Shortcuts.
- Travelling executives in the GCC who need to capture ideas between meetings without unlocking a phone.
- Personal-KM enthusiasts in Dubai/Abu Dhabi who want a private timeline, not a project management suite.

## Jobs To Be Done

1. **Functional job** - Capture the next action in 5 seconds from any wearable surface.
2. **Emotional job** - Stop feeling that the productivity app is bigger than the work it tracks.
3. **Social job** - Have a private record the user can review at 6pm without sharing with anyone.

## Success Metrics

- **Speed:** median time from Watch tap to action saved = 5 actions / week for at least 60% of weekly actives by month 2.
- **Conversion:** >= 4% of installs convert to one-time purchase within 30 days.

## Competitive Landscape

- **Todoist / Things 3 / OmniFocus** - powerful; deep project hierarchies exceed the 5-second brief.
- **Apple Notes + Reminders** - native; capture works but lacks the single-screen review ritual.
- **Bear / Day One** - note-shaped, not action-shaped; review surfaces are heavier.
