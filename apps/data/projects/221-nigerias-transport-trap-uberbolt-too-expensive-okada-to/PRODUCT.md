# Nigeria's transport trap: Uber/Bolt too expensive, okada too deadly, Danfo buses a nightmare. Millions stuck without a safe, affordable option.

> Auto-generated product brief. Review and refine before MVP scoping.

## Value Proposition

Replace the daily transport trap with a real option. The first 100,000 active riders pay for the service from the recovered time and the avoided safety incidents.

## Target Users

Nigerian commuters in Lagos, Abuja, Port Harcourt, and Ibadan who currently use okada or Danfo for daily trips and want a safer, more predictable alternative. Also Nigerian businesses that need to move staff mid-day.

## Jobs To Be Done

Functional: get a reliable, mid-cost ride with a real driver. Emotional: stop the dread of the morning commute. Social: be the commuter who can show up on time without the cost of Uber or the risk of okada.

## Success Metrics

At least 100,000 active riders within 12 months. Driver response time median below 8 minutes. Rider safety incident rate below major ride-hailing baseline. Less than 5% of trips result in a payment dispute.

## Competitive Landscape

Uber and Bolt are expensive for daily use. Gokada (pre-ban) was the motorcycle option but local regulatory changes have left a gap. Lagos Bus Rapid Transit (BRT) is fixed-route only. No mainstream service combines the three vehicle classes with cash-or-transfer payment and explicit helmet enforcement.

## Risks & Open Questions

Regulatory risk if state transport authorities change the rules. Driver-side fraud (fake registrations, dual apps). Rider-side fraud (fake disputes). Cash handling has safety risks for drivers. Motorcycle safety incidents can damage the brand even when the company is enforcing the rules.
