# Kipper – Ask finance questions and get real numbers in Slack, Teams, or SMS

## Problem

Kipper lets your team ask finance questions in plain English and get live numbers from NetSuite, QuickBooks, and Xero inside Slack, Teams, and SMS. It converts questions into SQL against your read-only data, so answers are accurate and audit-logged without risk of changes. Admins control access by team, account, or role. Setup takes minutes—connect your systems, add Slack or Teams, set permissions, and start asking. View startup
