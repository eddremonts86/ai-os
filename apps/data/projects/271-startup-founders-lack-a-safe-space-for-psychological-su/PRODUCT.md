# Startup founders lack a safe space for psychological support during moments of burnout and loneliness

> Auto-generated product brief. Review and refine before MVP scoping.

## Value Proposition

Replaces the unstructured "vent to a co-founder who is also burning out" with a facilitated, confidential, peer-group space that founders can stay in for as long as they need.

## Target Users

Serbian startup founders experiencing burnout or loneliness; facilitators who are ex-founders with counselling training.

## Jobs To Be Done

When I am burning out, I want a confidential group of peers who get it so I can say the things I cannot say in my company Slack. When I am between sessions, I want a private check-in channel so I do not have to wait a week to be heard.

## Success Metrics

Active founders in weekly groups. Group retention at 3 / 6 months. Facilitator NPS. Self-reported burnout-score improvement (validated scale, not invented).

## Competitive Landscape

Traditional therapy is expensive and not founder-fluent. Peer-support apps (e.g. Wisdo, Koko) are not founder-specific. Founder communities (e.g. YC's) are competitive, not therapeutic. No source-named direct competitor combining facilitated peer support with founder context.

## Risks & Open Questions

Facilitator recruiting is the gating item — good facilitators are scarce and expensive. Confidentiality breach destroys the product. Burnout-scale use must be licensed and validated. Need 15 founder interviews before claims about burnout reduction are credible.
