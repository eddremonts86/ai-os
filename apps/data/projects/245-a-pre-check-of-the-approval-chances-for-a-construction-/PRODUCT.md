# A pre-check of the approval chances for a construction project with the Australian council before making significant investments

> Auto-generated product brief. Review and refine before MVP scoping.

## Value Proposition

Replaces a $5,000-15,000 pre-lodgement meeting cost with a $200-500 pre-check report that gives the developer a realistic read on approval probability before they commit.

## Target Users

Australian property developers, architects, homeowners planning construction; small architecture and planning firms.

## Jobs To Be Done

When I am about to commit to design fees for a project, I want a structured read on approval likelihood so I can decide whether to proceed. When I work with multiple clients, I want to triage which projects are worth pitching for.

## Success Metrics

Report generation time (target: under 4 hours). Customer-reported calibration accuracy at 90 days. Repeat-customer rate. Stripe revenue per month.

Legal risk if the report is treated as advice; clear disclaimer and "this is not legal advice" required. Need 5 architect / developer interviews to calibrate the report's usefulness before claims about accuracy.
