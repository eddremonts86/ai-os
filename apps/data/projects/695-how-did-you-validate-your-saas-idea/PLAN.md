## Risks

- **AI-validation false confidence.** Replies that say "the AI said it was a good idea, so I built it" are a known failure mode; the thread is only useful if it surfaces the cases where AI got it wrong.
- **Politeness-as-signal.** "Yes I'd use that" replies are not validation. The author did not ask the thread to define validation, but the thread's most useful replies will.
- **Channel-of-the-replier bias.** "Where" answers will cluster around wherever the replier hangs out. The author should not treat any single channel recommendation as universal.
- **Single-method bias.** Replies that champion one method (only AI, only direct) overstate their own usefulness. The author's two-branch framing suggests he is open to combining them.
