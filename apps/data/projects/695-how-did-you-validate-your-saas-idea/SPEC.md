# How did you Validate your Saas Idea?

## Problem
 I'll be Direct with the Question because there isn't much to talk about: How did you Validate your Saas Idea? Like, did you ask AI? Is it a good way to ask Ai? And what are the results? Or did you ask directly to your Target Costumers? And where? submitted by /u/Betauser98 [link] [comments]

---

## Objective

This is a short methods question to r/SaaS, not a product spec. The author is asking how other founders validated their SaaS idea — specifically, whether they used AI for the validation step and how that went, or whether they asked target customers directly, and if so, where. The post is brief and explicitly framed as direct ("there isn't much to talk about"). No product, vertical, or ICP is named by the author.

## Target Users

- Primary: r/SaaS readers who have actually validated (or tried to validate) a SaaS idea and can describe the method they used and what came of it.
- Secondary: any founder at the "is this real" stage who is choosing between AI-driven validation and direct-customer validation, and is reading the thread to decide which to spend time on.
- Tertiary: people building AI-validation tools who want to know whether founders actually use them and what the failure modes are.

## MVP Scope

No product is described. The closest thing to a deliverable is the open question the post puts to the community, which has two parallel branches:

1. **AI-based validation.** Did you ask an AI (LLM, persona simulation, market-research prompt)? What did you ask? What were the results — useful signal, hallucinated confidence, or somewhere in between?
2. **Direct customer validation.** Did you ask your target customers? Where — Reddit, LinkedIn, Twitter, a landing page, a personal network, cold email? What was the response rate? Did you treat a positive reply as evidence, or did you require commitment (waitlist signup, paid pre-order)?

If anything were to be built from this post, it would be a validation-pipeline tool. That is out of scope here.

## Constraints
