# The monthly headache of being remote tech support for elderly relatives who constantly need help with the same computer problems

> Auto-generated product brief. Review and refine before MVP scoping.

## Value Proposition

Turns the recurring monthly call into a one-click session that often resolves the issue before the human helper even joins, because the AI triage has already identified the likely cause.

## Target Users

Adult children supporting elderly parents; elderly parents themselves; small-team admins supporting non-technical colleagues.

## Jobs To Be Done

When my mother calls about her computer again, I want her to click one button and have me see her screen so I can fix it in five minutes. When the issue is one of the 30 common ones, I want the AI to suggest the fix before I join so the call is even shorter.

## Success Metrics

Average session duration (target: under 8 minutes). Triage-suggestion acceptance rate. Elderly user abandonment rate (target: under 10%). Helper-side monthly retention.

## Competitive Landscape

TeamViewer and AnyDesk are remote-control tools with no triage or knowledge base. GoToResolve and ConnectWise are IT-helpdesk tools for SMBs, not for individuals. No source-named consumer competitor for elderly tech support specifically.

## Risks & Open Questions

Tauri + WebRTC cross-platform reliability on slow connections is the long pole. Elderly users may not click the icon at all if the visual design is wrong. Need to validate with 10 helper + elderly pairs before claims about triage quality are credible.
