# MealSwap – Swap homemade meals with neighbors and discover local home cooking

## Problem

MealSwap is a Bay Area peer-to-peer meal sharing platform connecting people through homemade food. Users can discover nearby cooks, share their dishes, and exchange or sell meals within their community. The site offers categories like breakfast, dinner, vegan, and dessert, along with messaging, profiles, and sales tracking. MealSwap supports community farming, food donations, and partnerships with local nonprofits and farms to expand access to fresh, local meals. View startup
