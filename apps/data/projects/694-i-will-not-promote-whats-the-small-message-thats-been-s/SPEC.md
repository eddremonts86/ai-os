## Objective

This is a behavioural / emotional reflection post, not a product spec. A founder is asking r/startups about a very specific pattern: short replies to high-stakes recipients (investor who said yes, first customer who said yes) sit in drafts all afternoon, getting rewritten until the words stop sounding like his, while low-stakes internal chat goes out in seconds. He used to attribute it to English being his second language and is no longer sure that is the cause. The post asks four reflective questions and explicitly invites the opposite-experience replies — people who just send it and it works out — to share what they do differently. No product, tool, or workflow is proposed.

## Target Users

- Primary: r/startups readers who recognise the same pattern — the high-stakes-recipient draft that gets rewritten until it sounds like no one.
- Secondary: founders whose default mode is to just send, and who can articulate what makes that work for them (the author explicitly says he wants to hear from this group most).
- Tertiary: anyone whose freeze-on-yes pattern has ever been mis-attributed to a non-native language; the author is half-jokingly walking back that explanation and inviting others to do the same.

## MVP Scope

No product is described. The closest thing to a deliverable is the four questions the post puts to the community:

1. Which message is it for you, and who is it usually to?
2. When was the last one, and how long did it sit?
3. Did anything actually happen because it sat, or was it fine in the end?
4. What do you do while it is sitting — do you work on other stuff, or does the whole afternoon go?

A fifth implicit ask: replies from people who just send it in ninety seconds and it works out fine, with a description of what they are doing differently.

If anything were to be built from this post, it would be a writing-flow or commit-button tool that helps founders close the gap on high-stakes messages. That is out of scope here.

## Constraints

- No product, no tech, no pricing, no go-to-market. The post is a behavioural observation.
- The author does not name a domain, vertical, geography, or specific recipients — only "investor or first customer, doesn't matter which." Replies that assume a specific recipient type (only VCs, only enterprise buyers) over-fit.
- The "English is my second language" line is offered as a hypothesis the author is walking back, not as a fact. Replies that take it at face value will misread the post.
- The pattern described is internal and self-reported. There is no measurement, no log, no counter — replies that try to quantify this without data are guessing.
