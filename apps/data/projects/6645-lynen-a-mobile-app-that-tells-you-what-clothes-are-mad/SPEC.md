# SPEC.md — Lynen – A mobile app that tells you what clothes are made from

## Problem

Hi HN,<p>I&#x27;m Chinedu, the creator of Lynen<p>I built this because I got tired of buying clothes and having the seams pull out after the first wash or purchasing garments that said 100% cotton and turns out there is some polyester or toxic dye in the composition.<p>Key Features<p>1. Mobile App - You can take this with you anywhere.<p>2. Scan or Search - Scan or manually look garment barcode and get lab verified fabric and chemical composition of your garment, as well as a durability result.<p>3.
