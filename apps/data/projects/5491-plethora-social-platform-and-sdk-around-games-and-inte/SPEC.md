# SPEC.md — Plethora, Social platform and SDK around games and interactive content

## Problem

Plethora is a native social platform for interactive content (like games, puzzles, art, and any other user creations), where AI makes creating games and experiences as simple as a prompt.<p>This addresses both the consumer problem with passive feeds and the creator problem of interactive work being flattened into videos, images, or links.
