# Anyone here in a meteorology startup? I will not promote

> Auto-enriched product brief.

## Value Proposition

Replaces "ask a forum and hope someone in the niche replies" with a structured, searchable directory of meteorology-adjacent employers and the skills they hire for.

## Target Users

- Physics undergraduates with an offer for a meteorology masters who want to know what jobs exist
- Meteorology MSc students exploring non-academic paths
- Small-climate-startup founders who want one place to mention an open role

## Jobs To Be Done

When I am deciding whether a meteorology startup is a real career path, I want to see who hires, what skills they want, and whether anyone has already made the jump, so I can pick a masters specialism with employment in mind.

## Success Metrics

- Directory covers at least 3 of 5 archetypes (forecasting vendor, climate-risk SaaS, atmospheric data, energy-meteorology, drone / sensor weather)
- At least 10 moderated hiring-signal posts in the first month
- Returning-visitor rate above 20% week-over-week
