# World Gold – Track live gold prices and global flows on an interactive globe

## Problem

World Gold is an interactive globe that tracks live gold prices and market flows by country. It maps exchange hubs, trade corridors, and central bank reserves, adding timely news with each figure linked to a primary data source. Use it to watch intraday moves, compare countries, and explore drivers like silver, the dollar index, yields, oil, and bitcoin. View startup
