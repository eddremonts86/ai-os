# FoxDesk – Client requests, recorded work, and clear reports in one workspace

## Problem

FoxDesk is a client work hub for freelancers, agencies, and service teams. Email and portal requests become tickets with an owner, priority, and full history. Employees, freelancers, and connected coding agents can record completed work and time against the right client. At month end, FoxDesk turns approved entries into a clear report explaining what was done and how long it took. The cloud version includes hosting, updates, and backups with no per-user fee. A self-hosted open-source edition is also available. View startup
