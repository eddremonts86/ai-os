# Bark – Meet people by hunting for your dog across a playful dating feed

## Problem

Bark turns dating into a playful rescue mission centered on your dog. You sign up, upload your pup, and another user temporarily "keeps" it, sending your dog on an adventure. You then browse a local feed, searching for your dog and declaring "That's my dog!" when you find it. Matching connects you with the keeper to chat about the dog, life, and more, with photos withheld until the conversation deepens. Bark encourages authentic connections sparked by a shared love of dogs. View startup
