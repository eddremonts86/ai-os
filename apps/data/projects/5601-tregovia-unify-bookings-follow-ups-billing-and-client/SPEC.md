# SPEC.md — Tregovia – Unify bookings, follow-ups, billing, and client messaging in one platform

## Problem

Tregovia centralizes bookings, follow-ups, billing, client conversations, and data into one platform for service businesses. It replaces multiple tools with online booking, automated SMS and email reminders, a multi-channel inbox, quotes and invoices with Stripe payments, and a sales pipeline. Teams can track no-shows, manage calendars, and run nurture sequences without switching apps. Tenant-isolated data, role-based access, and GDPR compliance help you operate securely at scale.
