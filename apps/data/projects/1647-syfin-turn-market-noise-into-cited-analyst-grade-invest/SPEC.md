# SyFin – Turn market noise into cited, analyst-grade investment insights

## Problem

SyFin reads tens of thousands of articles, filings, and earnings calls so you don't have to and turns them into the story behind every market move. See which narratives are driving your holdings with portfolio heatmaps, structured company briefs, supply-chain context, and real-time alerts. Ask the analyst Q&A anything and get answers with citations. Institutional-style research, built for self-directed investors and small teams. View startup
