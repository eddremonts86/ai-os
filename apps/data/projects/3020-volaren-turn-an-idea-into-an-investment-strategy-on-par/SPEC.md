# Volaren – Turn an idea into an investment strategy on par with professionals

## Problem

Volaren is an investing platform for experienced and everyday investors alike. One side works like a personal team of analysts: you share what you believe about a company or market and why, and it gathers data, tests the idea against history, and finds the best way to hold that view across stocks, options, bonds, and hedges rather than just buying an obvious stock. The other side is a marketplace where you can browse strategies and portfolios built on Volaren, see exactly what's inside each one, and follow the ones that fit even if you don’t have your own view.
