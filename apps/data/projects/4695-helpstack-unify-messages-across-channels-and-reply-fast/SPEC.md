# HelpStack – Unify messages across channels and reply faster with AI and translation

## Problem

HelpStack centralizes customer messages from email, Facebook Messenger, Instagram DM, and website chat into a single real-time inbox. It helps teams respond faster with AI-suggested replies that agents approve in one click and auto-translation across 40+ languages. HelpStack offers search, threading, assignments, analytics, and sets up in minutes via OAuth and IMAP. Built and hosted in the EU, it encrypts data and supports GDPR compliance to keep customer conversations secure. View startup
