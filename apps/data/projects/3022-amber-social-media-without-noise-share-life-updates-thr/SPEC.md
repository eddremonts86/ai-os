# Amber – Social media without noise, share life updates through newsletters

## Problem

Tired of seeing ads on Instagram? Miss content from your real friends? Want a break from social media without missing their updates? Meet Amber, social media without the noise. Just upload your photos, and Amber automatically selects the best moments, lets you add real context and storytelling instead of just cheeky captions, and turns them into shareable albums anyone can open via email newsletter or text, no account required.
