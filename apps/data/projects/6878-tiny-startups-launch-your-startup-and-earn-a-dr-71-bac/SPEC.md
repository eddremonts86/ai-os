# SPEC.md — Tiny Startups – Launch your startup and earn a DR 71 backlink

## Problem

Tiny Startups is a pay-what-you-want leaderboard where founders bid to claim ranked spots and instantly list their startup. The higher you pay, the higher you rank, and every listing includes a DR 71 do-follow backlink with no account required and a secure Stripe checkout. The board updates live, showing new launches and activity while driving clicks to featured sites. Use it to quickly promote a project, test demand, and capture early traffic from a targeted audience.
