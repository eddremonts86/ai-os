# Startup teams have nowhere to find relevant custdev participants (people for interviews) to test early ideas and prototypes without overpaying for biased feedback

> Auto-generated product brief. Review and refine before MVP scoping.

## Value Proposition

A startup team posts a screener for a target persona and gets vetted, non-professional respondents who actually match — paid only on completed interviews that pass a basic consistency check, so the team gets real signal instead of professional-incentive-hunter noise.

## Target Users

| Stakeholder | Why they care |
|---|---|
| Early-stage startup teams (pre-seed → Series A) | Running 5–30 custdev interviews per validation cycle and need real-persona signal. |
| UX research teams at later-stage companies | Running concept tests on tight timelines and need vetted respondents fast. |

## Jobs To Be Done

1. **Functional job** — Find custdev participants who actually match the persona and aren't professional-incentive hunters.
2. **Emotional job** — Stop the recurring frustration of interviews that turn out to be unusable.
3. **Social job** — Be the team that ships the product because the validation was real, not because they talked to 30 paid respondents.

## Success Metrics

- **Activation:** first screener live + 5 vetted respondents matched in under 7 days for 80% of new teams.
- **Match precision:** ≥ 70% of booked interviews result in usable signal (rated by the team).
- **Inconsistency catch-rate:** ≥ 80% of professional-respondent gaming attempts flagged by the consistency check.
- **Repeat rate:** ≥ 50% of teams run a second interview cycle within 90 days.

## Pricing & Monetization

Team-side: free to post screeners + browse the respondent pool; $25–$150 per completed interview (tiered by persona seniority), platform takes 30%. Respondent-side: free to apply and complete interviews, paid out per completed + consistency-passed interview. No subscription, no per-seat fee.

## Competitive Landscape

- **Respondent.io + User Interviews + Prolific** — generic respondent pools, no startup-specific screening, professional-respondent gaming is the documented industry problem.
- **Facebook / LinkedIn / Slack communities** — works once, doesn't scale, no consistency check.
- **Personal-network recruiting** — biased by definition; works for warm intros only.
- **UserTesting + TryMyUI** — UX testing focused, not custdev, pricing $50–$100+ per session.

## Risks & Open Questions

- [ ] Validate whether the consistency check catches the dominant gaming patterns or whether a manual reviewer layer is needed for v1.
- [ ] Confirm the 30% platform take funds the consistency-check review queue without squeezing respondent payouts below an attractive floor.
- [ ] Decide whether the platform should expand to other interview types (concept tests, diary studies) in v2 or stay custdev-only through 2026.
