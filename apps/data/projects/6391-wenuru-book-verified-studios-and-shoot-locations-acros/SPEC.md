# SPEC.md — wenuru – Book verified studios and shoot locations across India with ease

## Problem

wenuru is a marketplace for production spaces in India. It lets you discover, compare, and book photo, video, podcast, and music studios, along with film city sets, homes, cafés, offices, and vehicles for shoots. Listings show verified details, real-time availability, and transparent hourly rates so you can plan with confidence. Hosts list their spaces, set their own pricing, and manage bookings while keeping earnings, making it simple to match the right space to any brief. View startup
