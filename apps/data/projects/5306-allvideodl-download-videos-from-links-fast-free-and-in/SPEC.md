# SPEC.md — AllVideoDL – Download videos from links fast, free, and in HD with no signup

## Problem

AllVideoDL lets you download videos from shareable links across social networks and video sites in seconds. Paste a URL from Instagram, Twitter/X, Facebook, TikTok, Reddit, and more, then get a direct HD file without watermarks. The service runs in your browser, requires no account, and doesn’t store personal data or downloaded content. Use it on any device or grab the Android app for auto-detection and one-tap saves while you browse. View startup
