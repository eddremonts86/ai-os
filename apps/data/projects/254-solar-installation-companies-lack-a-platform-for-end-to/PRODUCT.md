# Solar installation companies lack a platform for end-to-end tracking of a customer's project — from sale to official approval, causing complaints and dissatisfaction

> Auto-generated product brief. Review and refine before MVP scoping.

## Value Proposition

A customer-facing status surface that turns the installer's workflow into a per-project timeline the Brazilian customer can read at any time: where the project is, what is blocked, and what the next milestone is. The installer reduces inbound "where is my installation?" calls; the customer gets the visibility the current process denies them.

## Target Users

- Brazilian residential and commercial customers waiting on a solar installation.
- Solar installation companies in Brazil that want fewer inbound status calls and better post-sale satisfaction.
- Project managers and field engineers at those companies currently tracking projects in spreadsheets and WhatsApp groups.
- Solar-install associations or cooperatives wanting a shared customer-comms template across member installers.

## Jobs To Be Done

- When I have just signed a solar contract, I want a page I can open any time to see where the project stands, so I stop calling the installer to ask.
- When I am the installer and a permit is sitting at the distributor for three weeks, I want to record that fact once and have it show up on the customer's page, so the customer understands the delay without me re-explaining it on the phone.
- When I am a project manager, I want a weekly digest email going out to every active customer, so I stop being the bottleneck for "what changed this week?"
- When I am a solar association, I want a status template my members can adopt, so customers across the industry see a similar surface.

## Success Metrics

- Number of active projects on the platform at month six (proxy for installer adoption).
- Reduction in inbound "where is my project?" contacts per active customer per month (the installer's own measure; the source does not state a baseline).
- Weekly digest email open rate (proxy for the customer actually reading the status).
- Customer satisfaction score post-installation, compared between projects using the platform and projects not using it (the installer's own measure; the source does not state a baseline).

## Risks & Open Questions

- Validate problem with 5 installer interviews before MVP: confirm that the missing-visibility framing matches what Brazilian installers actually face and that they would record milestones against each project.
- Installer-side friction: a milestone-entry flow longer than one minute will not be used. The MVP must measure the time-to-record per milestone and stay under that ceiling.
- Stale timelines are worse than no timeline. The MVP needs an "installer hasn't updated in 14 days" alert to the installer's project manager, not just a quietly stale customer page.
- Distributor integration is tempting but out of scope. The MVP cannot scrape distributor portals or guess at permit status; it must rely on the installer's recording.
- Localisation matters: Brazilian Portuguese (pt-BR) is the primary language, but terminology (homologação, art, vistoria) is specific. The MVP needs a Brazilian installer to vet the milestone labels.
