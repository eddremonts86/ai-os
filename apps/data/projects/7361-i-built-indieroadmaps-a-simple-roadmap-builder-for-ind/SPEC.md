# SPEC.md — I built IndieRoadmaps, a simple roadmap builder for indie hackers

## Problem

I built a place where you can create your public roadmap and let your community vote on its features. When you ship something, everyone who voted gets an email notification automatically.<p>It also works before you&#x27;ve launched anything, good for validating ideas without writing a line of code.<p>Free to use, roadmap is live in 1 minute.
