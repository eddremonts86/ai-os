# Micro-influencer cannot monetize a loyal audience: there is no safe and effective platform for deals with small brands and those willing to work with small influencers in India.

## Value Proposition

A deal-brokering platform built for micro-influencers in India and the small brands that want to work with them, sized for modest budgets and modest audiences. Matches do not depend on follower count; the match scoring weighs relevance, engagement, and fit between the brand's brief and the creator's actual content. Payments settle to the creator's domestic bank or UPI on approval, so the deal ends the way both sides agreed it would.

## Jobs To Be Done

1. **Functional job** — Find and accept a paid brand collaboration without negotiating tools or eligibility thresholds built for larger creators.
2. **Functional job** — Brief, agree price, and receive a deliverable from a small creator without leaving the platform to do it in chat or email.
3. **Functional job** — Receive the payment in a domestic Indian rail on the day the work is approved.
4. **Emotional job** — Stop feeling that a real, engaged audience is being wasted because of a missing piece between deal and payout.
5. **Social job** — Show, on a small creator's profile, that they have a record of completed brand deals in INR they can reference.

## Success Metrics

- **Deal completion rate** — share of accepted deals that reach the approved-and-paid state, since the platform's value collapses if deals stall at agreement.
- **Median time from acceptance to payout** — the source pain is the missing transaction, so this is the metric the product exists for.
- **Match-to-deal conversion** — briefs posted versus briefs that turn into a paid deal, to see whether the relevance scoring actually works for modest audiences.
- **Creator repeat rate** — creators who close more than one deal on the platform, since the value compounds only if creators come back.
- **In-rail payout share** — share of payouts settled through the domestic Indian rail versus any fallback path, because the cross-border fallback is the friction the platform removes.

## Competitive Landscape

- **Global creator-marketplace platforms** — built for creators who already produce media kits at scale and for mid-to-large brands running larger budgets; their eligibility and tooling leave modest Indian creators and small brands outside the loop.
- **Manual negotiation via DM and email** — how most of these deals happen today, with no shared deal workspace, no escrow, and no platform-side record-keeping.
- **Indian influencer-marketing agencies** — provide the deal-brokering function but at minimum monthly retainers that small creators and small brands cannot afford for a single deal.
