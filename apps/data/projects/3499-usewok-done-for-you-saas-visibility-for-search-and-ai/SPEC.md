# UseWok – Done-for-you SaaS visibility for search and AI

## Problem

UseWok runs tailored 14-day public presence campaigns for SaaS companies. It handles relevant directory and public-profile submissions, original articles, targeted social publishing, campaign tracking, and verified live URLs as placements go public, helping buyers, search engines, and AI assistants find, understand, and evaluate your SaaS. The service costs $59 once, with no subscription. View startup
