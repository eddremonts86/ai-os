Plus, Pro, Enterprise, Business and Education plans do not have ads. Ad-free options remain available.[^2]To begin, ads will not be personalised. We select them using the current topic of your ChatGPT conversation and limited contextual information, such as your general location and device type. We do not use your past chats or memories to select these ads.Before we begin to personalise ads, we will first ask you to choose whether you would like to see more relevant and personalised ads. If you choose to participate, you’ll get relevant and personalised ads using information that stays only on ChatGPT, such as ads you've interacted with, or context from your chats. You will be able to manage ad personalisation in settings.[^3]For ads shown in ChatGPT, we use information about ads you view or interact with to measure and improve their effectiveness. Advertisers only receive overall information about how their ads perform, such as total views or clicks.## What’s not changingAds do not influence the answers ChatGPT gives you. Ads are always clearly labeled and are visually separated from ChatGPT responses.Your personal details and conversations with ChatGPT remain private and are not shared with advertisers.
