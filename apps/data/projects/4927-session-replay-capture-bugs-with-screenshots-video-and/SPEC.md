# SPEC.md — Session Replay – Capture bugs with screenshots, video, and console details in one click

## Problem

Session Replay streamlines bug reporting by combining screenshots or video with automatic technical context. Reporters use a Chrome extension to capture issues and share a single link that includes console logs, browser and system details, and notes. Teams get all reports for their domains in a centralized dashboard with filters and collaboration features. It reduces back-and-forth and helps developers reproduce and fix issues faster.
