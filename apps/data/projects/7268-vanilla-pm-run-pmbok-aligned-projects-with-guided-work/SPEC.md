# SPEC.md — Vanilla PM – Run PMBOK-aligned projects with guided workflows and analytics

## Problem

Vanilla PM is a project management platform based on PMBOK 8th edition. It guides you from initiation to closing with governed documents, a WBS-centered model, and step-by-step coaching. Manage scope, schedule, risks, budget, stakeholders, and governance in one system, with Earned Value, Monte Carlo forecasting, and change control. Draft documents with optional AI using your data, and keep an auditable trail for construction, IT, marketing, and NGO projects. View startup
