# SPEC.md — I graded 200 AI tools/apps and track any changes in their policies

## Problem

Hi all,<p>Given the recent OpenAI saga with the Navier–Stokes problem, I thought it would be a good time to show my latest project.<p>The site pulls policies from 200+ websites, keeps track of them and compares them to any changes. I then grade the companies (A-F + Unclear) and I have emailed all of the companies that have been graded D or below.<p>Where applicable, such as with OpenAI, I&#x27;ve provided instructions on how users can opt-out of their data being used for training.<p>Findings of note so far:<p>- 80 tools train on you by default. 55 don&#x27;t let you opt-in and four do, but you have to pay.
- 106 tools don&#x27;t say if they do or don&#x27;t train on your data&#x2F;usage.
- Only 36 (16%) say that they do not train on your data.
- One company, FastMail, made a change to their policy based on my feedback&#x2F;grading.<p>If you have any feedback, suggestions or concerns please let me know! I&#x27;m trying to improve this as and when I can. Also let me know if you have a specific app&#x2F;tool in mind you&#x27;d like for me to grade&#x2F;track.
