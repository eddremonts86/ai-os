## Value Proposition

_Based on source brief:_ I often see things that I&#x27;d like to remember for later - movies, books, travel ideas etc. I know there are a load of &quot;Save for later&quot; services out there.
I also like to have a few things to read when I get a little more time on a Sunday morning with a coffee. Bring these together and you have The Rewind.
The idea is you save things through the week, and then on the Sunday, you get a nice, personalised newsletter delivered by email of your saved items, but fleshed out to give you all the info about them. And I wanted it to be a good read, with useful info and nicely designed.<p>I have it up and running and it recognises a lot of categories of things. The enrichment is decent for all the core areas as well but I&#x27;m still working on that.
If that sounds like your kind of thing, I&#x27;d love some feedback on it and to see what things people actually save and whether I&#x27;m doing a good job of fulfilling them.
You can get started by visiting the website.
Couple of notes though - you save things through a Telegram bot for now. I know this limits things for now, but it was the easiest way to get started. And, since it&#x27;s a newsletter, you&#x27;ll need to verify your email address to actually receive the newsletter. Thanks!
