# The Rewind, a personalised weekly newsletter

> Auto-generated product brief. Review and refine before MVP scoping.

## Value Proposition

_Based on source brief:_ I often see things that I'd like to remember for later - movies, books, travel ideas etc. I know there are a load of "Save for later" services out there.
I also like to have a few things to read when I get a little more time on a Sunday morning with a coffee. Bring these together and you have The Rewind.
The idea is you save things through the week, and then on the Sunday, you get a nice, personalised newsletter delivered by email of your saved items, but fleshed out to give you all the info about them. And I wanted it to be a good read, with useful info and nicely designed.I have it up and running and it recognises a lot of categories of things. The enrichment is decent for all the core areas as well but I'm still working on that.
If that sounds like your kind of thing, I'd love some feedback on it and to see what things people actually save and whether I'm doing a good job of fulfilling them.
You can get started by visiting the website.
Couple of notes though - you save things through a Telegram bot for now. I know this limits things for now, but it was the easiest way to get started. And, since it's a newsletter, you'll need to verify your email address to actually receive the newsletter. Thanks!
