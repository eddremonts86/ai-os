## Risks

- **Optimism bias.** The author frames the worst case ("even if it fails I can still gain valuable experience and jump over to another company") as low-risk. Replies that treat this as the floor — not the median — are doing him a favour.
- **Validation theatre.** "I'm really liking how it's turning out so far" is an internal feeling, not a market signal. If "validated" turns out to mean "I built it and I like it," the runway is being burned on a non-evidence.
- **Identity and lifestyle.** Quitting changes daily structure, social context, and the way the author explains himself to family. The post does not name this; replies that ignore it will not help.
- **Insurance gap.** The author has flagged health insurance but not the timing — losing employer coverage can take effect immediately or at month-end depending on jurisdiction and plan. Replies that ignore this could let him go uninsured for a window he didn't budget for.
- **Opportunity cost of stock.** If the author is leaving unvested RSUs/options on the table, the actual cost of the leap is runway plus the forfeit. The post treats the trigger (RTO policy) and the cost (vesting) as separate things; they are not.
