# What should I know ahead of time before going in full time on a startup idea? [I will not promote]

## Problem
 I'm a software engineer at a big tech company and I've been wanting to quit for a long time due to the return to office policy. I've got an app idea that I'm currently in the middle of validating and getting a prototype developed for and I'm really liking how it's turning out so far. I'm planning on tentatively quitting my job by end of year and going all in on this app for at least 6 months or so. I'm thinking that even if it fails I can still gain valuable experience and jump over to another company. Before I take the leap I'm giving myself lots of time to think this through. So far I'm planning out how my monthly expenses and health insurance will look like while I tap into my savings during those months. What else should I be considering carefully before I leave my job to go in on this idea? submitted by /u/ihatecoreclasses [link] [comments]

---

## Objective

This is a "checklist before I quit" post, not a product spec. A software engineer at a large tech company is planning to quit by end of year to go full-time on a validated app idea for ~6 months, funded by savings, and is asking the r/startups community what else he should be considering beyond monthly expenses and health insurance. The author states the runway, the trigger (return-to-office policy), and his fallback ("even if it fails I can still gain valuable experience and jump over to another company"). No product is proposed.

## Target Users

- Primary: r/startups readers who have made the leap from a big-tech job to a startup and can speak to what they wish they had sorted out first.
- Secondary: employed engineers in their planning window — anyone currently weighing a quit date against a runway number.
- Tertiary: anyone who has bounced back from a failed indie attempt into another job and can speak to whether the "valuable experience / easy bounce-back" framing holds.

## MVP Scope

No product is described. The closest thing to a deliverable is the open-ended "what else should I be considering" question. The post itself names two buckets the author has already covered (monthly expenses, health insurance). Replies are expected to fill in the rest:

1. Financial runway and burn — beyond bare monthly expenses, what about taxes (self-employment / quarterly estimated), emergency buffer, and the opportunity cost of stock vesting forfeit at his current employer.
2. Legal and entity — sole proprietor vs. LLC vs. delaying incorporation; what that does to his ability to sign contracts or accept payment.
3. Equity and vesting — unvested RSUs/options at the big-tech employer; the actual cost of leaving in month X vs. month Y.
4. Health insurance — gap coverage between employer plan and the new plan (COBRA, ACA, spouse plan).
5. Customer pipeline — what does "validated" mean concretely; how many paying users or LOIs does he have before quitting.
6. Bounce-back plan — whether the assumption "I can still jump over to another company" still holds in his market at his tenure level.
7. Founder psychology — what changes about decision-making, motivation, and identity once the job is gone.
