## Value Proposition

_Based on source brief:_ RenewalWatch helps you track contract renewals without using spreadsheets or manual calendars. Upload a PDF or import a contract URL and it extracts renewal dates, notice periods, auto-renewal status, and vendor details, then deletes the source file after processing. You get email alerts at 90, 60, and 30 days so you can renegotiate, cancel, or budget in time. Use it for SaaS subscriptions, vendor agreements, leases, insurance, domains, and certificates, with CSV export for portability. View startup
