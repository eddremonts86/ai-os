# SPEC.md — Ember – a strategy game where everyone moves at once

## Problem

Hey y’all!<p>Back in 2019, inspired by my love of board games and what’s possible with digital games, I created a little strategy game built around simultaneous moves and shifting pressure across a grid. I thought there was a really interesting game hiding in there, but eventually stopped digging as life happened. Work, pandemic, kid, the usual...<p>Then all of this AI nonsense pulled me back in and made me want to see how far I could run with the original idea. That turned into Ember, which somehow also became my first app ever on the App Store and Google Play.<p>I haven’t had this much fun building something since I was a kid. Hope some of you enjoy it!
