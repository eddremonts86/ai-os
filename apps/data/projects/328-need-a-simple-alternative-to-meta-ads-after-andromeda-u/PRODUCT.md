# Need a simple alternative to Meta Ads after Andromeda update

> Product brief authored from the source title and category. The poster's text was not available (source.name: manual); sections below re-state the problem and infer only what the title and category support.

## Value Proposition

A US small-business operator gets back the simplicity they lost after the Andromeda update: one brief, three ad platforms, one dashboard, daily spend pacing, and reporting that names the platform that is wasting the budget instead of hiding it inside Meta.

## Target Users

- US small-business owners running under $5k/month in paid spend who lost performance after the Andromeda update.
- Solo marketers and agency operators serving 3-10 small US clients who want one dashboard instead of three.
- DTC brand founders who need cross-platform creative A/B without re-uploading the same asset four times.

## Jobs To Be Done

1. **Functional job** - Run paid campaigns on Meta, Google and TikTok without opening three different ad managers.
2. **Emotional job** - Stop feeling that every platform change undoes weeks of optimization.
3. **Social job** - Show the founder's team a single dashboard with named KPIs, not screenshots of three tools.

## Success Metrics

- **Activation:** first cross-platform campaign live within 30 minutes of signup.
- **Adoption:** weekly active users check the dashboard at least 4x per week.
- **Efficiency:** cost per acquired customer across the managed portfolio is within +/-10% of pre-Andromeda baseline after 60 days.

## Competitive Landscape

- **Meta Ads Manager** - familiar but increasingly complex after Andromeda; single-platform.
- **AdEspresso / Hootsuite Ads** - older cross-platform dashboards that did not keep pace with the TikTok Ads API.
- **Smartly / Madgicx** - DTC-focused, but priced for agencies handling $20k+/month spend.
