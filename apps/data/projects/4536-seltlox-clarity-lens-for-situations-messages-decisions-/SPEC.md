# SPEC.md — Seltlox – Clarity lens for situations, messages, decisions, questions, and patterns

## Problem

Seltlox is built for moments when something feels hard to read. Describe what's going on in your own words or paste the text you're trying to understand. Seltlox provides an analysis to help you see what matters, what may be shaping the moment, and what might be missed. Choose different modes depending on the angle you want, while keeping the final judgment in your hands. View startup
