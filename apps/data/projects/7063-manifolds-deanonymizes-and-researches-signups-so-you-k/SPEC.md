# SPEC.md — Manifolds – Deanonymizes and researches signups so you know who they are

## Problem

Manifolds is a deanonymization tool built for companies with high signup volume. You provide an email, and we return detailed person and company data. Manifolds automatically identifies your signups via API, turning a large stream of anonymous signups into a smaller, manageable stream of valuable opportunities. We are used by sales teams to identify high-value prospects, product teams for better segmentation and user understanding, and growth marketers for targeted ad campaigns and attribution. We work with mature and hypergrowth companies with 1,000 to 1M signups per month. View startup
