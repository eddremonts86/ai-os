# Startup founders get lost in legal, accounting, and administrative tasks after incorporation, leading to stress and risks due to the lack of a clear, step-by-step plan.

> Auto-generated product brief. Review and refine before MVP scoping.

## Value Proposition

A US founder gets a single ordered, deadline-aware roadmap of every legal, accounting, and administrative step their company actually needs in the first 18 months after incorporation — so they stop finding out about the 83(b) deadline, the Delaware franchise tax, and the state annual report after the fact.

## Target Users

| Stakeholder | Why they care |
|---|---|
| First-time US founders (0–6 months post-incorporation) | Already have a lawyer and an accountant, but no one is handing them the merged checklist with deadlines. |
| Repeat founders with a new entity | Don't want to relearn the compliance calendar from scratch for each new venture. |
| Fractional CFOs and startup lawyers | Want a structured roadmap to give clients instead of a hand-written PDF. |

## Jobs To Be Done

1. **Functional job** — Know every filing, deadline, and form the company owes between incorporation and the 18-month mark.
2. **Emotional job** — Stop the recurring anxiety that something was missed and will cost the company in penalties or cap-table mistakes.
3. **Social job** — Look organized to investors and a future head of finance during diligence.

## Success Metrics

- **Activation:** roadmap seeded from the onboarding quiz within 10 minutes for 80% of signups.
- **Retention:** ≥ 60% of founders return at least once per 14-day window during the first 6 months.
- **Deadline reliability:** zero missed IRS or state deadlines for founders who marked a step "done" inside the app.
- **Cap-table tie-out:** ≥ 90% of cap-table imports reconcile against the steps the founder is told to do.

## Pricing & Monetization

Free for the first 3 months post-incorporation (covers the highest-risk deadline window). $19/month per entity after that, or $190/year. Cap-table import + multi-entity view on the paid plan. No per-step upsell, no referral fee to law firms.

## Competitive Landscape

- **Clerky + Stripe Atlas + Firstbase** — each covers its own silo (incorporation, banking, registered agent) but none ties them into an ordered post-incorporation plan with deadlines.
- **Carta + Pulley** — own the cap-table side but not the rest of the compliance calendar (franchise tax, state reports, payroll).
- **Bonsai + Wave** — small-business accounting tools that surface tax deadlines but do not cover the founder-specific items (83(b), 409A, R&D credit).
- **Law firm onboarding PDFs** — bespoke per engagement, not maintainable across multiple clients.

## Risks & Open Questions

- [ ] Confirm whether IRS deadlines alone are enough to seed the roadmap or whether state-level data ingestion is required from day one.
- [ ] Validate the 18-month scope with at least 3 startup lawyers and 3 fractional CFOs before launch.
- [ ] Decide whether the product includes a money-handling feature (escrow for filing fees) or stays strictly advisory.
