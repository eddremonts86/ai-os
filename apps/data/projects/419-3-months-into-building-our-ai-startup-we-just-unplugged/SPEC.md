## Problem

The poster and their friend have been building a SaaS AI startup for 3 months. They had a problem: they wanted an AI with their own specialist in the same context windows they were doing. They had some small validation from passive validation (looking from some posts who faced the same problem). They operated the work like the poster dealt with backend and the friend dealt with frontend. 3 months later they were still underdeveloped and the poster felt it really took so long. They read The Lean Startup by Eric Ries, got a ton of concepts on the foundation of building a startup — the main idea is stay within each step. The post is a reflection, not a question. The poster is sharing that they unplugged their own backend system. No country, no specific stack, no revenue was stated.
