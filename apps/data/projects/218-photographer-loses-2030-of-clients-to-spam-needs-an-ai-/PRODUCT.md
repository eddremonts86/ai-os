# Photographer loses 20–30% of clients to spam — needs an AI clone with a copy of her voice to answer and book or deflect.

> Auto-generated product brief. Review and refine before MVP scoping.

## Value Proposition

Recover 20–30% of lost bookings from spam and missed calls. The first 100 photographers pay for the service from the recovered bookings in the first quarter.

## Target Users

Solo wedding and event photographers in Russia and the CIS who lose leads to missed calls and spam. Secondarily: solo videographers, makeup artists, and small studio owners with the same pattern.

## Jobs To Be Done

Functional: have every call answered with a real-feeling voice in the photographer's style. Emotional: stop the dread of the missed-call list after a wedding. Social: be the photographer who never misses a real client, even mid-shoot.

## Success Metrics

At least 20% of inbound calls converted to a discovery call where the human photographer was previously missing them. Caller satisfaction rating above 4 out of 5 in a follow-up survey. Spam deflection rate above 70% of total calls.

## Competitive Landscape

Smith.ai and Ruby are human services, not voice clones. Generic AI voice agents (Vocode, Retell) require the user to set up their own telephony and integrations. No mainstream service combines a clone-from-real-voice training flow with a photographer-specific qualification script and a Russian-language UX.

## Risks & Open Questions

Voice clone may sound robotic on certain callers. Spam pattern detection may misclassify a real client. Russian-language fine-tuning of the voice clone may be limited. Pricing discussion must not auto-commit to a number the photographer has not set.
