# BritishAccent – Generate natural UK English voiceovers with 148 regional voices

## Problem

BritishAccent lets you generate natural UK English audio in one workspace. Choose from 148 RP, Scottish, Welsh, and Northern Irish voices, preview deliveries, paste your script, then create and download the finished read. Use it for videos, lessons, podcasts, and business narration, or upload a reference clip in Voice Clone to match a specific delivery. Test names, places, and numbers in context and keep the strongest take. View startup
