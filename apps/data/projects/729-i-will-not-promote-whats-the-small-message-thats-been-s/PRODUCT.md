# I will not promote: whats the small message thats been sitting in your drafts all afternoon?

## Value Proposition

**One-liner:** A community-shared inventory of the "small message that sits in drafts all afternoon" — the 4-sentence reply to a person who already said yes (investor or first customer), and the contrast with messages that go out in seconds — so founders recognise the pattern and learn how others break the freeze.

## Target Users

- Primary: the poster, a founder who freezes on short high-stakes messages to investors / first customers.
- Secondary: other founders who recognise the same freeze pattern.

## Jobs To Be Done

1. Functional — name the pattern so it is not mistaken for a personal failing.
2. Functional — hear from people who just send the message in ninety seconds what they do differently.
3. Emotional — feel less alone in the freeze.
4. Social — compare notes with peers on what actually breaks the freeze (vs. what does not).

## Success Metrics

- Replies that name the specific message and recipient for the responder.
- Replies that name a "what works differently" pattern the responder uses.
- Concrete app / tool metrics are not in the source.
