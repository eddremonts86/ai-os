# How did you get your first real users when nobody knew your product existed?

> Auto-generated product brief.

## Value Proposition

A channel catalog for solo developers building developer tools who are stuck on the discovery part and want a realistic map of which channels actually delivered the first 50-100 users for comparable projects. Generic launch content in this space is mostly Product Hunt top-10 lists. This one is grounded in the post: Reddit, GitHub, communities, content, direct outreach — with the named signal each one produces.

## Target Users

Primary: a solo developer or very small team building a developer tool (CLI, desktop app, library, framework) who has shipped v1 and is stuck on the discovery part. Secondary: a first-time technical founder who is weighing whether to hire a growth person or do the discovery work themselves.

## Jobs To Be Done

When I (a solo developer or small team) am stuck on the discovery part after shipping v1 of a developer tool, give me a channel catalog with the named signal each channel produces and the named time cost, so I can pick the one that matches my temperament and my time budget instead of trying all of them at once.

## Success Metrics

1. The poster (or a comparable developer) can name, after one read, the channel that delivered the first 50-100 users for their category and the one that did not.
2. A developer can defend the channel-pick in the next planning meeting with a specific signal they're tracking.
3. The founder's launch-research time after reading the output is shorter than the time they would have spent cold.

## Risks & Open Questions
