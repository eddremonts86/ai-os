# SPEC.md — On the Contest of Meat and Machine

## Problem

I have worked in both production and R&amp;D in my career. My experience provides the general sense that there is an inherent tension between <i>exploration</i> vs <i>exploitation</i>. My production managers were interested purely on delivering product A on time T, for the good reason of putting food on the table. It mattered not <i>how</i>, it mattered <i>what</i> and <i>when</i>. Finish it, ship it, forget it. In research (aka exploration) the how mattered as much if not more than the what. Often the <i>what</i> was a ready observable, already there, needing no manufacturing, but really an assessment or explanation.It was easy to see the groups mutually loathed each other, despite their self-evident complementary characters.<p>Is this what is happening currently in math&#x2F;basic sciences&#x2F;SWE?
