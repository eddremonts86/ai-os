## Value Proposition

_Based on source brief:_ Meridian is an open-source, local-first work journal for developers. It reconstructs your day from on-screen activity and turns it into daily summaries, standups, time reports, and draft project ticket updates.The goal is to reduce the time spent manually reconstructing unplanned work and writing status updates. Activity is stored in an encrypted database on your machine, and generated updates remain drafts until you review them.Meridian is MIT-licensed and currently supports integrations with Jira, Linear, GitHub, and Azure DevOps.
