# Problem of automating culturally relevant content creation

> Auto-generated product brief. Review and refine before MVP scoping.

## Value Proposition

A Jamaican creator or brand produces a culturally-tuned weekly content calendar in hours, with copy and visuals that read as Jamaican to a Jamaican, not as US-default with a flag emoji.

## Target Users

| Stakeholder | Why they care |
|---|---|
| Jamaican creator | Produces content weekly; needs culturally-resonant copy and visuals without hiring a team. |
| Jamaican small/mid brand | Wants a weekly social calendar that does not sound like a US ad with a flag. |
| Jamaica Tourist Board / Caribbean tourism body | Wants diaspora-targeted content in English and Patois at scale. |

## Jobs To Be Done

1. **Functional job** — Produce a week's content calendar in hours, not weeks.
2. **Emotional job** — Stop the embarrassment of generic AI copy that 'reads wrong' to a Jamaican audience.
3. **Social job** — Have a feed that a Jamaican scrolls past and nods at, not past and grimaces at.

## Success Metrics

- Time-to-weekly-content-calendar ≤ 3 hours median.
- Cultural-authenticity panel review ≥ 4/5 from 5 Jamaican creators per quarter.
- Engagement rate delta vs prior baseline — target +25% relative.
- Creator NPS ≥ 50 at month 3.

## Pricing & Monetization

Free tier: 1 brand, 4 posts/month, no video. Creator Pro (USD 24/month): unlimited posts, short-video scripts, 4 video outputs/month, calendar scheduling. Brand Pro (USD 99/month): multi-creator roster, custom brand voice, Patois opt-in, Canva integration.

## Competitive Landscape

- Generic AI tools (Jasper, Copy.ai) — strong on US English; Patois register is caricature at best.
- Caribbean creator agencies — high quality, slow, expensive.
- Manual creator + Canva — works at low volume, breaks at weekly cadence.

## Risks & Open Questions

- [ ] Cultural-authenticity drift — Mitigation: explicit prompt guardrails; cultural advisor on retainer; Jamaican creator review before publish.
- [ ] Stereotype backlash — Mitigation: explicit 'do not stereotype' prompt set; review queue for high-risk content.
- [ ] Voice-cloning consent — Mitigation: explicit per-voice consent flow; no likeness generation without documented consent.
