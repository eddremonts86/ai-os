# SPEC.md — Giraffe – Keep AI-derived decisions and current facts organized by project

## Problem

Giraffe keeps the decisions, current facts, and open questions from your AI-assisted work organized by project. It captures only what you explicitly save, preserves the history of changes, and lets you search concise, labeled items instead of chat transcripts. Connect ChatGPT or Gemini to file insights as they happen, then revisit a project to see what's true now, what changed, and why. Export everything as Markdown or JSON, and use it for planning, strategy, meetings, and research. View startup
