# Coming soon: validated problems for startups with a high chance of success

> Auto-generated product brief. Review and refine before MVP scoping.

## Value Proposition

A weekly published "Validated Problem" that compresses 20+ in-person interviews into the seven sections a founder needs to decide whether to start building: target user, verbatim quotes, workarounds used today, willingness-to-pay, competitive landscape, and the gap a new startup could close. The editorial bar — at least 20 real conversations before anything ships — converts the noisy raw-problem pool into a shortlist a solo founder or venture studio can act on, and the published gap section gives every subscriber a defensible thesis for free.

## Target Users

| Stakeholder | Why they care |
|---|---|
| Solo founder / small team "looking for the idea" | Replaces doom-scrolling forums with a curated shortlist of research-backed opportunities. |
| Indie hacker / internal venture studio | Steady stream of pre-validated problems to pick from each week. |
| Investor / accelerator | Spots emerging demand patterns from the underlying research before the founder community notices. |
| Corporate innovation team | Uses the gap section to brief execs on where a new product could land. |

## Jobs To Be Done

1. **Functional job** — Each week, publish one new problem dossier that a founder can read in 15 minutes and use to decide whether to spend the next 6 months building it.
2. **Emotional job** — Stop the "is this idea real?" anxiety by handing founders verbatim quotes from 20 people who already feel the same pain.
3. **Social job** — Be able to point an investor or co-founder at a published dossier instead of a one-paragraph idea note.

## Success Metrics

- **Publication cadence:** ship ≥ 1 validated problem per week with the existing 2-editor team, sustained over a 12-week pilot.
- **Editorial gate:** 100% of published problems pass the "≥ 20 in-person interviews" threshold; 0 problems publish under threshold.
- **Newsletter growth:** ≥ 5,000 free newsletter subscribers within 90 days of first publication.
- **Paid conversion:** ≥ 4% of free subscribers convert to the paid dossier tier within 60 days of a relevant problem being published (target ≥ 200 paying readers).
- **Founder signal:** ≥ 10 published dossiers result in a publicly announced startup, hackathon project, or paid research request within 6 months.

## Pricing & Monetization

- **Paid tier** — full dossier (all 7 sections + interview transcripts) and the historical archive. €19/month or €149/year (ProblemHunt is RU/EU-priced; € figures match the rest of the catalog).
- **Team / studio tier** — €99/month for up to 10 seats, includes the interview raw notes and a Slack channel for thesis questions.
- **Sponsored problem** — a partner pays €4,000–€8,000 to be the named sponsor of one week's edition (editorial content stays independent; sponsor is a footer / logo placement only).

## Competitive Landscape

- **Reddit, Quora, niche forums** — high volume, low signal; no editorial bar, no willingness-to-pay work, no founder-ready summary.
- **"Idea databases" (IdeaBrowser, MicroConf resources, Starter Story)** — curated but usually built around a successful founder's retrospective, not pre-build research.
- **ProblemHunt's own problem feed** — the unvalidated pool this product filters; Validated Problems is the premium tier above it.
- **Consulting / venture studios** — produce similar research for clients at €20k+ per engagement; not accessible to a solo founder.
- **"Customer development" books / courses** — teach the method, do not deliver the output. Validated Problems is the output.
- **Y Combinator's "Request for Startups"** — broad thesis areas (e.g. "new fintech primitives"), not per-problem dossiers with 20 interviews.

## Risks & Open Questions

- [ ] Sustaining the "20 real interviews" bar weekly — recruiting and screening interviewees for a new topic every week is the editorial choke point. Mitigation: a standing pool of opt-in interviewees (newsletter subscribers willing to be interviewed) and per-topic outreach templates.
- [ ] Editorial bias — the editors' selection of which problems to pursue shapes the publication's coverage. Mitigation: a public "intake → research → interview → draft → publish" status board so subscribers see what's in flight.
- [ ] Willingness-to-pay drift — quotes from interviewees can read as "I would pay €100" but never convert when a paid product lands. Mitigation: include a "what they actually pay today" field per interview so the dossier captures evidence, not just stated intent.
- [ ] Anonymity vs. depth — verbatim quotes are most useful with context (role, sector), but every additional field raises re-identification risk. Mitigation: a redaction checklist the editor ticks before publishing; default strip of city, employer, and date.
- [ ] Cannibalisation of free submissions — if Validated Problems becomes the marquee product, the free submission flow loses attention. Mitigation: explicitly frame the free feed as the intake for Validated Problems, so submissions are valued, not sidelined.
- [ ] Channel concentration — Telegram + newsletter is the only distribution. Mitigation: every dossier is markdown + JSON, exportable for syndication; add an RSS feed and a Substack mirror in v1.x.
