# Problem with choosing a business niche in the local market

> Product brief authored from the source title and category. The poster's text was not available (source.name: manual); sections below re-state the problem and infer only what the title and category support.

## Value Proposition

An Indian entrepreneur who is stuck between two or three niches gets a side-by-side, evidence-grounded decision document for their specific city in under 30 minutes, instead of relying on the loudest WhatsApp opinion.

## Target Users

- First-time Indian founders in tier-2 / tier-3 cities weighing 2-3 candidate niches.
- Returning founders who left a previous venture and want a structured re-evaluation, not a gut call.
- Indian MBA / EdTech graduates with a 6-12 month runway who want a decision document they can show a co-founder or family investor.

## Jobs To Be Done

1. **Functional job** - Pick a business niche the founder can actually start in their city this quarter.
2. **Emotional job** - Reduce the dread of choosing wrong and losing the runway.
3. **Social job** - Bring a defensible document to the family or co-founder who will sign off.

## Success Metrics

- **Activation:** brief intake and first comparison saved in under 30 minutes.
- **Action rate:** >= 50% of users who finish the comparison download the one-page memo.
- **Behavioral:** at least 60% of users pick the top-ranked niche on their own within 7 days, regardless of whether they tell us.

## Competitive Landscape

- **WhatsApp / Reddit / Quora threads** - anecdotes that are useful but unstructured.
- **IndiaMART / TradeIndia listings** - useful for competition density but not for demand or fit.
- **Generic business coaches** - expensive, slow, not specific to a city.
