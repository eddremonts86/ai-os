# RinseLead – Cold email platform that helps sales teams and founders land in inbox

## Value Proposition

Run outbound without the tool juggling. RinseLead puts list verification, domain warmup and cold email campaigns in one platform, built around a single outcome: landing in the inbox instead of spam. Sales teams and founders get the three steps that normally live in separate products, in one place.

**One-liner:** One cold email platform for verification, warmup and sending, built to land in the inbox.

## Target Users

| Stakeholder | Why they care |
|---|---|
| Sales teams doing outbound | Fewer tools; one place to run the whole send lifecycle. |
| Founders self-running outbound | Deliverability handled without specialist knowledge. |
| Multi-domain operators | Warmup state visible across every sending domain. |

## Jobs To Be Done

1. **Functional job** — Verify an email list so bad addresses never reach the send step.
2. **Functional job** — Warm up sending domains automatically until reputation supports volume.
3. **Functional job** — Launch cold email campaigns from the same platform.
4. **Functional job** — See deliverability health per domain and campaign instead of guessing why mail landed in spam.

## Success Metrics

- **Verified-list health:** share of addresses cleared or flagged before first send.
- **Domain readiness:** median days from connecting a domain to warmup-complete state.
- **Inbox placement:** tracked via opens and replies, plus any available placement signals per campaign.
- **Consolidation stickiness:** share of new accounts that use verification, warmup and sending together within the first weeks.

## Risks & Open Questions

- [ ] Deliverability is a moving target: sender reputation, spam filter changes and recipient behavior sit outside the product's control.
- [ ] Warming and sending are abuse-adjacent; the platform must police its own users or shared IP reputation collapses.
- [ ] The capture states no sending-infrastructure detail (dedicated vs shared IPs, providers), which is the riskiest part of the product.
- [ ] Cold email faces tightening inboxing rules; the product's viability tracks that regulatory and platform pressure.
- [ ] Unstated pricing means unknown positioning against per-tool budgets.
