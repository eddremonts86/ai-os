# POPSESH – Find films to watch tonight with swipe-and-match picks

## Value Proposition

A swipe deck tuned to tonight — your time, your mood, your taste, and the other person's if you're picking together — so you go from "I don't know what to watch" to a film in five swipes.

**One-liner:** Tonight's film, in five swipes.

## Target Users

| Stakeholder | Why they care |
|---|---|
| Couples and pairs who watch together | The "you pick" deadlock is the worst part of movie night; the Match feature turns it into a game. |
| Solo viewers at decision time | A 90-minute window and an unfiltered streaming catalogue is a recipe for 30 minutes of menu-scrolling; the time-budget filter cuts the catalogue to what fits. |
| Casual film fans | They don't keep watchlists, don't rate films, and resent onboarding questionnaires; three posters they loved is the entire interview. |

## Jobs To Be Done

1. **Functional job** — Pick a film to watch in under a minute, on the device you already have open, optionally with the person sitting next to you.
2. **Emotional job** — Stop the "I don't know, what do you want to watch?" loop and the low-grade frustration that comes with a catalogue you can't narrow.
3. **Social job** — Open a Match session, hand a phone (or a link) to the other person, and turn picking into a small shared game with a payoff.

## Success Metrics

- **Activation:** % of TestFlight installers who finish the three-poster seed and complete at least one swipe within the first session.
- **Retention:** Match sessions started per week / Match sessions completed per week; the funnel the founder cares about is "did the partner join via the link". Sticky retention is a function of repeat Match usage by the same pair, not solo swipes.
- **Revenue:** BetaList does not state pricing; the beta is open via TestFlight. The revenue shape will be defined later — the post does not commit to a model.

## Pricing & Monetization

BetaList does not state pricing. The landing page closes with "the beta is open tonight" and a TestFlight join link. No pricing tiers, no subscription, no IAP.

## Competitive Landscape

The BetaList post does not name competitors. The category sits between streaming-service recommendation surfaces (Netflix / Prime / Apple TV built-in rows) and discovery apps (Letterboxd, Mubi, Reelgood, JustWatch). POPSESH's stated wedge is the in-the-moment filter (time + mood + taste) and the multi-user Match mechanic, neither of which is the surface any streaming service makes easy.

## Risks & Open Questions

- **Catalogue source.** The landing page does not name the metadata provider (TMDB is the obvious choice; the post does not confirm). Without a clean licence posture and a backfill strategy, the deck cannot ship.
- **Onboarding honesty.** Three posters you loved is fast, but it biases toward mainstream. The re-deal mechanic (down = come back in two weeks) is the founder's hedge against this — it has to actually behave that way or trust breaks.
- **Match funnel.** The product hinges on the second person joining from a link. If that link takes more than one tap to open the deck, the loop dies. The MVP has to test the link-to-first-swipe time, not just the in-app flow.
- **No-account persistence.** A solo user with no account and no history loses their taste seed if they switch devices. Whether the MVP ships with optional iCloud / anonymous-account sync, or accepts the churn, is a decision the post does not address.
