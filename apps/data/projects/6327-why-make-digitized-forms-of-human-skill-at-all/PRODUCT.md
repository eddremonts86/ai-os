_Based on source brief:_ Why do people still make digitized forms of their skills when digitized forms are doomed to be replicated by AI?<p>I don’t get it. In 10 years time, when every human skill will be replaced by specialized models that do it better, where would they get data to train new models?<p>Okay. Let’s say they use post training based on human reactions to the AI generated data to train better models. Sure, that would work for a while. But what happens in a generation? Because everything that would be happening would be done by AI, optimized based on the reactions of humans to it. Wouldn’t it get pretty boring at some point because no one would be doing anything interesting at all?<p>So as predicted by Dostoyevsky and noted by Nolans in Mememto, humans have a tendency to hate their own utopia because it’s not interesting enough.<p>I guess that’s how humans would conceive the idea of waging war against AI. So if that’s where we’re all headed, why not just nip it in the bud?
