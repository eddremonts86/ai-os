# Aristra, your life OS

## Problem

Most AI cares only about the outcome users want. Aristra cares about your process. It works alongside and for you across the things you already use, learning about you and from you, day and night. What works becomes a playbook it reaches for next time. What stops being true quietly fades. How you actually get things done is the most valuable thing about you, and it's the one thing no AI understands. One AI. One memory. Your life OS.
