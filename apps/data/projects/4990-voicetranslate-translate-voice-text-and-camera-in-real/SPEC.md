# SPEC.md — VoiceTranslate – Translate voice, text, and camera in real time across 500+ languages

## Problem

VoiceTranslate delivers real-time translation for voice, text, and camera across 500+ languages. Use it in your mobile browser without downloads, add it to your home screen, and start live conversations with auto-speak and replay. The service offers group sessions, full history with PDF/CSV export, and an earpiece mode for private listening. A Free plan includes daily translations, while Pro removes limits for a low monthly or annual price and eliminates ads on yearly plans. View startup
