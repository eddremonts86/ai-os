# SPEC.md — subba – Answer enquiries, book appointments, and take payments 24/7

## Problem

subba is an AI receptionist for service businesses that handles enquiries, bookings, and payments around the clock. It reads messages from web chat, email, phone, and SMS, replies in your tone, checks availability, and books customers straight into your diary while collecting deposits or full payment via Stripe. You can start with human approval and switch to full automation once confident. subba also sends reminders to reduce no-shows and asks happy customers for Google reviews the next day. View startup
