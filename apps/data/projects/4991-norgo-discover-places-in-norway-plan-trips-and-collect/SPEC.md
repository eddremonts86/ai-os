# SPEC.md — NorGo – Discover places in Norway, plan trips, and collect memories

## Problem

NorGo is a travel and experience platform that helps you discover places across Norway, plan day trips, and collect memories on the go. It shows attractions near you with location-based matches, curated recommendations, and seasonal tips. You can create routes, share plans with family, and check in to get a stamp in a digital travel passport. NorGo also includes AR experiences at selected locations to bring destination stories to life while you're there. View startup
