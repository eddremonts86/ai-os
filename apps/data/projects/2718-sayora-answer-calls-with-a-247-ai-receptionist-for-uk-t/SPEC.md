# Sayora – Answer calls with a 24/7 AI receptionist for UK trades

## Problem

Sayora is an AI receptionist for UK trades and small businesses. It answers diverted calls in a natural British voice, screens sales calls, captures job details, books appointments on your calendar, and texts you a summary after each call. You get a dedicated UK number, transcripts and recordings in an inbox, and 24/7 coverage for a flat monthly price. Set it up by letting it learn from your website, then forward calls when you can't pick up. View startup
