# Systemic problem of unenforceable contracts and slow dispute resolution

> Product brief authored from the source title and category. The poster's text was not available (source.name: manual); sections below re-state the problem and infer only what the title and category support.

## Value Proposition

A Russian SME drafts a contract from a Civil-Code-aware template, signs it with a CryptoPro e-signature, and on breach routes the dispute to an arbitration panel with a 30-60 day SLA - instead of waiting 12+ months for a Russian commercial court date.

## Target Users

- Russian SMEs signing B2B contracts with other Russian counterparties.
- Russian SMEs signing cross-border contracts that will accept Russian-jurisdiction arbitration.
- Russian in-house legal leads at growth-stage companies standardising a contract library.

## Jobs To Be Done

1. **Functional job** - Sign an enforceable contract and resolve a breach in weeks.
2. **Emotional job** - Stop worrying that a small commercial dispute is too expensive to litigate.
3. **Social job** - Be the SME partner that has a real contract, not a PDF signed by a handshake.

## Success Metrics

- **Activation:** first contract drafted and signed within 30 minutes of signup.
- **Dispute resolution time:** P50 of arbitration awards under 60 days for claims = 80% of dispute complainants rate the process as 'fair' on a 5-point scale.

## Competitive Landscape

- **Russian commercial courts (arbitrazh)** - capable but slow; 6-18 month dockets.
- **Contract templates on free Russian legal portals** - generic; no arbitration path.
- **Generic mediation providers** - good on small claims but weak on enforceable awards.
