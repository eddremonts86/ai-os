# SPEC.md — Risume – An AI-native resume builder that tailors your resume to every job

## Problem

Risume provides a resume builder that keeps writing, layout, and tailoring in one place. It lets you import your existing resume, rebuild it into editable sections, and uses an agent that reads job posts to suggest focused, truthful edits you can accept or undo. You can design directly on the page, choose ATS-friendly templates, and export pixel-perfect PDFs. Risume helps you customize resumes for each role while keeping every change visible and under your control. View startup
