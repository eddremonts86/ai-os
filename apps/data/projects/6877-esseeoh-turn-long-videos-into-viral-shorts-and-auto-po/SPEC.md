# SPEC.md — Esseeoh – Turn long videos into viral Shorts and auto-post them for you

## Problem

Esseeoh turns your long-form videos into high-performing Shorts without manual work. It monitors your channels, detects new uploads, and uses AI to find the most engaging moments, cutting 30–60 second vertical clips. It writes SEO-friendly titles, descriptions, and hashtags, then auto-publishes to YouTube Shorts and Instagram Reels.
