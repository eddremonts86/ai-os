# Hekate Dreams – Record dreams and get personal readings, images, and a private star map

## Problem

Hekate is a private dream journal that reads back what you write and paints what it sees. Capture dreams by typing or speaking half-asleep, then receive a reading in your own words and an image of the dream. As entries grow, Hekate maps recurring people, places, and symbols into a starry sky so you can trace patterns over time. It keeps your data safe with no ads, no tracking, and zero-retention processing. It will launch on iOS and Android. View startup
