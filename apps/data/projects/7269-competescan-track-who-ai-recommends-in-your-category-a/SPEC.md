# SPEC.md — CompeteScan – Track who AI recommends in your category and get a plan to win

## Problem

CompeteScan helps teams see whether AI answers recommend them or a competitor and shows how to change it. It queries ChatGPT, Gemini, and Perplexity with buyer-style prompts, measures share of voice against chosen rivals, and shows cited sources. You get a clear visibility report and a prioritized playbook with fixes to close citation gaps and overtake competitors. Start with a free scan, then scale tracking, prompts, and alerts with paid plans designed for brands and agencies. View startup
