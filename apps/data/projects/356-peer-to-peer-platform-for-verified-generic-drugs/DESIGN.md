# Peer-to-peer platform for verified generic drugs — design direction

Calm and clinical, but not cold. The buyer is mobile-first, often looking at a competitor brand while comparing; the page needs to look honest, not promotional. Use a neutral white background with a single brand colour (deep teal) for verified-lot badges and CTAs. Show the per-batch provenance as a visible chain (manufacturer → distributor → seller → buyer) rather than a logo wall. Typography: a humanist sans for body, slightly tighter for batch numbers and expiry dates so the buyer can scan them. The seller dashboard is utilitarian — body-only, no marketing flourishes; the verifier queues are deliberately plain so the reviewer can focus on the license fields. The "snap a brand strip" flow uses a single large drop zone with a thick border and the OCR result written in plain text, never as a recommendation — the system explains what it read and asks the buyer to confirm.
