# Search for an affordable way to get PR in Forbes and other top media outlets

> Product brief authored from the source title and category. The poster's text was not available (source.name: manual); sections below re-state the problem and infer only what the title and category support.

## Value Proposition

A Russian founder with a real story gets a ranked short list of tier-1 journalists, a beat-matched pitch, and a 3-touch follow-up sequence - without paying a $15k placement agency to do the same mechanical work.

## Target Users

- Russian founders of post-Series-A startups who want tier-1 coverage and have a $500-2k media budget, not a $15k retainer.
- In-house marketing leads at Russian tech companies who would otherwise brief an agency.
- Solo PR freelancers in Russia serving 3-5 clients who want a tool that automates the research step.

## Jobs To Be Done

1. **Functional job** - Get a foot in the door at Forbes / TechCrunch / Inc without paying an agency.
2. **Emotional job** - Stop treating PR as a black box that requires insider connections.
3. **Social job** - Show investors the resulting coverage as proof, not as a slide claim.

## Success Metrics

- **Activation:** brief completed and first pitch sent within 60 minutes of signup.
- **Pitch quality:** >= 10% reply rate from tier-1 journalists across the first 50 sent pitches.
- **Outcome:** at least 1 tier-1 coverage hit per 25 pitches in the first 90 days.

## Competitive Landscape

- **PR agencies (5W, Edelman, etc.)** - effective for companies with $15k/month retainers.
- **Generic PR SaaS (Muckrack, Prowly)** - journalist databases; no beat-matched drafting or follow-up sequence.
- **Cold-email tools (Lemlist, Instantly)** - useful but built for sales, not PR cadence and reply-context.
