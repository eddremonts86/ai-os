# Problem of finding a workout partner in a new gym

> Auto-generated product brief. Review and refine before MVP scoping.

## Value Proposition

A new joiner at an Indian gym can fill in a 2-minute profile and get 3–5 vetted partner candidates from the same gym in under a week, without ever cold-approaching a stranger at the squat rack.

## Target Users

| Stakeholder | Why they care |
|---|---|
| New joiner at a metro gym | Moved cities or gyms; needs a partner at the same level and schedule. |
| Returning lifter | Trained alone too long; wants accountability. |
| Gym manager | Member retention suffers in the first 30 days; a partner-finder is a low-cost perk. |

## Jobs To Be Done

1. **Functional job** — Find a partner at the same gym who trains at the same level on compatible days.
2. **Emotional job** — Replace the awkwardness of cold-approaching with a structured introduction.
3. **Social job** — Have a workout buddy to talk to in a new city.

## Success Metrics

- **Time to first match:** median ≤ 7 days from profile complete to a mutual-opt-in chat open.
- **Match success:** ≥ 40% of mutual-opt-in chats lead to at least one logged workout together in 14 days.
- **Retention:** ≥ 30% of matched users are still training at the same gym after 60 days.
- **Safety:** zero harassment reports unaddressed within 24 hours in the pilot.

## Pricing & Monetization

Free for users. Gym-side paid tier for a verified gym page, weekly partner-finder post, and a monthly retention report (INR/month per gym).

## Competitive Landscape

- **Bumble BFF / Tinder for friends** — too broad; no gym-specific context, no schedule alignment.
- **WhatsApp / Instagram DMs at the gym** — works for regulars, opaque for newcomers.
- **Gym in-house social events** — helpful but infrequent and not partner-specific.

## Risks & Open Questions

- [ ] Confirm gym directory data sourcing — partnerships with chains vs self-serve signup by gym managers.
- [ ] Decide the level taxonomy (5 buckets, 3 buckets) with 20 user interviews before launch.
- [ ] Define the safety response SLA — harassment reports need a 24-hour human review path.
