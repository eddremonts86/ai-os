# Spreeflo – Automate marketing journeys that convert and retain customers with AI

## Problem

Spreeflo is a marketing automation platform that helps you convert, retain, and grow customers through behavior-based, personalized messaging. It unifies email campaigns, journeys, forms, popups, web analytics, and event tracking to capture intent, segment audiences, and send the right message at the right time. Built-in AI drafts campaigns, segments, and copy in your brand voice, while playbooks and templates help you launch quickly at a lower cost than legacy tools. View startup
