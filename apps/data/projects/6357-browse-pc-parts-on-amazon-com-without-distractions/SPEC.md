# SPEC.md — Browse PC Parts on Amazon.com without distractions

## Problem

I&#x27;ve built a website where you can browse PC parts from Amazon without distractions and clutter. Most people, like myself, value more information and a clear way to find products than being bombarded with irrelevant stuff like &quot;hey buy this item too&quot; or &quot;people also buy this&quot; Sometimes you need to scroll too far down just to see actual specs. So I decided to make it simple, just a list of products and some filters to find what I need.
If you value a distraction free, no clutter way to find what you need, check it out: <a href="https:&#x2F;&#x2F;findpcpart.com" rel="nofollow">https:&#x2F;&#x2F;findpcpart.com</a><p>In the future, I plan to extend it and provide more info for each product.<p>If you have any questions or suggestions, feel free to comment below.
