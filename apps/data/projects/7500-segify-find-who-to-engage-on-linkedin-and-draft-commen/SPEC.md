# SPEC.md — Segify – Find who to engage on LinkedIn and draft comments in your voice

## Problem

Segify helps personal brands and B2B creators gain visibility on LinkedIn by showing who is close to their brand and where to engage today. It maps your audience with an Orbit model, turns your ICP into actionable trays of posts and conversations, and suggests first-draft comments in your voice for you to review and publish. Segify also classifies your posts to show which formats activate customers and your ICP, fitting into a 10–15 minute daily routine on desktop or web. View startup
