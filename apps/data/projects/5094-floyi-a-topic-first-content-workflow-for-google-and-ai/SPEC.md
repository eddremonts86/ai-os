# SPEC.md — Floyi, a topic-first content workflow for Google and AI search

## Problem

Hi HN!<p>I&#x27;m an SEO and AI search consultant. I&#x27;ve spent years creating topical maps and content strategies for brands and that led me to building Floyi.<p>I’ve seen over the years, long before AI-generated content, how content strategies started with individual keywords and disconnected article ideas. Teams published more, but organic traffic stalled and sometimes declined because the content focus was fragmented.<p>That’s why I always start by defining the brand, what it wants to be known for, and their buyers’ needs. Then I turn that context into topical research, a topical map, and a content publishing plan.<p>The problem I kept having was keeping the brand’s context across the separate tools, documents, and spreadsheets throughout the workflow. By the time you were ready to start a brief or writing, you had to remember to find the brand and writing guidelines first to ensure the brand’s voice was intact.<p>I built Floyi to keep the process in one workflow, carrying brand&#x2F;audience context from research to mapping to content briefs to articles.<p>You can try it here for free: <a href="https:&#x2F;&#x2F;app.floyi.com&#x2F;" rel="nofollow">https:&#x2F;&#x2F;app.floyi.com&#x2F;</a><p>I would appreciate feedback on improvements, especially where the workflow feels fragmented.
