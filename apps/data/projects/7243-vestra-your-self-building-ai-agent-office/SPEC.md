# SPEC.md — Vestra – Your self-building AI agent office

## Problem

Vestra is your self-building AI native office. Delegate outcomes, not prompts: Vestra's AI agents plan steps, browse the live web, run code, create real files, and work in your tools like Gmail, Sheets, Notion, and Slack on their own cloud computer, then deliver finished work. Run one-off tasks or scheduled routines, watch progress live, step in anytime, and pay only for what your agents use. Built for founders, agencies, and operators who want execution, not conversation.
