# SPEC.md — WhoGoes – Turn public LinkedIn posts into outreach-ready event attendee lists

## Problem

WhoGoes converts public LinkedIn posts into verified, outreach-ready attendee lists for over 1,200 trade shows and conferences. Sales and marketing teams can browse events, preview five real contacts per event, then filter by ideal customer profile to unlock names, titles, companies, LinkedIn profiles with post proof, and verified work emails. Pricing is pay-as-you-go with credits that never expire, starting with 20 free credits so you can target attendees before events without costly databases or manual scraping. View startup
