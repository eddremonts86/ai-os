# YC may have already peaked, and the data is starting to show their stumble (I will not promote)

## Problem

A Reddit analysis post citing the NBER paper "BEYOND DEMO DAY: SORTING AND VALUE ADDED IN STARTUP ACCELERATORS" (studying ~750,000 American startups across 329 accelerators) argues that YC's estimated value-add had fallen dramatically by 2022, with 60–80% of accelerators appearing worse than simply building without one. The poster's three-cause hypothesis: (1) batches grew while partner/investor attention did not scale proportionally, (2) YC selected younger founders with less domain context, (3) AI as a paradigm shift undermines YC's accumulated pattern recognition from Stripe/Airbnb/Dropbox/Coinbase-era SaaS. The poster warns that YC's network moat (Bookface) can compound downward — fewer defining AI companies → weaker network → less value for future AI founders.
