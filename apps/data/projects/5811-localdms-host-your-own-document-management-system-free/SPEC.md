# SPEC.md — LocalDMS – Host your own Document Management System free for beta testers

## Problem

LocalDMS is a Document Management System that doesn’t phone home. Legal, finance, and healthcare teams face challenges with cloud DMS like subscriptions, data sovereignty, and security concerns. LocalDMS runs on your infrastructure, sets up in under 5 minutes, and offers version control, browser previews, and a full audit trail. It's free for up to 10 users with no subscription required. In beta, 50 compliance teams can test approval workflows and get the Enterprise edition free forever. No cloud uploads or credit card required. View startup
