# Embercard – Generate flashcards and schedule reviews to peak before exams

## Problem

Embercard helps students study with spaced repetition that aligns with real exam dates. Upload slides, PDFs, or text, and its AI extracts key concepts to create clean, high-quality flashcards tailored to your major. Each deck ties to a class with a semester end date, so reviews increase as finals approach and peak when you need them most. Every generated card links back to the exact passage it came from. After reviewing a card, tap the source link to jump to the original slide or paragraph and study the surrounding context. You can also create manual cards, import existing decks, and use FSRS scheduling to keep learning on track without busywork. View startup
