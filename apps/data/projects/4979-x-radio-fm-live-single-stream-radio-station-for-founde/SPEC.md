# X-Radio.FM – Live single-stream radio station for founders building in public

## Problem

X Radio runs a single live internet radio station designed for founders building in public. The stream follows one schedule so everyone hears the same host reads, music beds, and breaks without chat or feeds to distract you. Come listen live, catch archived shows, and explore sponsor and advertising options aimed at the startup community. The station curates tracks and segments that keep builders company while they work and share their journey. View startup
