# eBay scraper — accidental capability, looking for a product shape

## Problem

I accidentally made a powerful eBay scrapper that gets thousands of results in seconds I first wanted to get eBay prices for my app turns out I can get anything I want So i made a quick live bidding extractor that you’ll notice in the video and some other extractors the question is to the experts can I make a powerful saas or micro saas out of that ? ## Objective

Determine whether an eBay scraper built 'by accident' (capable of pulling thousands of results per second, plus a live-bidding extractor and other extractors) can be packaged into a sustainable micro-SaaS — and what the wedge use case is.

## Target Users

eBay power-sellers, drop-shippers, and price-intelligence researchers who currently do this work with manual scripts or expensive tools. Secondary: indie hackers evaluating scraper-shaped micro-SaaS opportunities.

## MVP Scope

A single-user MVP: one vertical (e.g. live bidding feed) shipped to one customer persona (eBay power-sellers), with a paid plan once the wedge is validated. The full multi-extractor suite is the eventual product; the MVP is one extractor + one persona.

## Design Direction

See `DESIGN.md` for this project's design tokens.

## Constraints

eBay's TOS restricts scraping — the product has to be defensible. The author has the capability but no persona yet; 'what to charge and who to sell to' is the open question. No WTP signal in the source.
