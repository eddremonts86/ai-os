# I've built an app that runs real spoken mock job interviews with an AI — it researches the company first, then grills you for 30 minutes — Design direction

Like a focused interview room, not a marketing site.

- Surface: pre-interview setup, interview screen, post-interview scorecard.
- Color: ink + accent for "score ready."
- Typography: sans for setup, monospace for timestamps.
- No gamification in MVP.
