## Value Proposition

A spoken mock-job-interview app that conducts real-time conversations and returns a structured scorecard with a recording for self-review.

- Job seekers preparing for technical or behavioural interviews
- Career switchers running practice loops
- Bootcamp graduates doing interview warm-ups

When I am preparing for a job interview, I want a tool that runs a real spoken mock interview and gives me a structured scorecard, so I can rehearse without scheduling a human.
