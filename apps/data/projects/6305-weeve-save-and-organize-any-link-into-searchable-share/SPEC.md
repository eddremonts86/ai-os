# SPEC.md — Weeve – Save and organize any link into searchable, shareable lists

## Problem

Weeve helps you save products, recipes, articles, social content, and any link in one organized place. Create lists and collections, search everything instantly, and share lists with friends. Save from your phone via the share sheet, from Chrome with a one-click extension, or by pasting links on the web. Weeve reduces tab overload and scattered bookmarks so you can compare options, plan trips and projects, and quickly find what you discovered later. View startup
