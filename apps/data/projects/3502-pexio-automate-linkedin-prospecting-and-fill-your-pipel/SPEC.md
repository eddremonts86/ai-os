# Pexio – Automate LinkedIn prospecting and fill your pipeline with leads

## Problem

Pexio is a B2B prospecting platform that automates lead generation on LinkedIn and centralizes the sales cycle with CRM and ERP features. Connect your account, define your ideal client, and it will send personalized messages with AI and follow up automatically until you get a response. It offers reports, dashboards, and an inbox, with plans for teams and agencies in Latin America, supporting Spanish and local pricing. It helps manage the process from first contact to invoice payment. View startup
