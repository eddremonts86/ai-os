# Startups at the monetization validation stage have nowhere to quickly start accepting payments without company registration to test demand for their MVP.

> Auto-generated product brief. Review and refine before MVP scoping.

## Value Proposition

A payment-collection surface a Moroccan founder can switch on in days, not weeks: link a payment page to an MVP, take real card payments, and have the funds held by the service until the founder has a registered entity to receive them. The founder validates willingness to pay without first incorporating a company, and the buyer sees a real, trustworthy merchant on the statement.

## Target Users

- Moroccan founders with a working MVP who want to validate willingness to pay before incorporating.
- Solo developers and small teams shipping a product and wanting to charge in 7–14 days, not 6–10 weeks.
- Bootstrapped founders who would rather route early revenue through a fiscal sponsor than incorporate on day one.
- International founders selling into Morocco who cannot easily open a Moroccan entity.

## Jobs To Be Done

- When I have an MVP and one early signal of demand, I want a payment page I can link to today, so I can put a price in front of real users this week.
- When a buyer pays, I want the funds held safely until I have a registered entity, so I do not have to incorporate before validating demand.
- When a buyer asks for a refund, I want to process it from my dashboard, so I do not lose trust on day one of monetisation.
- When I finally incorporate, I want the held funds transferred to my Moroccan business bank account, so I close the bridge cleanly and move on.

## Success Metrics

- Number of Moroccan founders who create a payment page in the first 30 days (proxy for onboarding friction).
- Total Moroccan-dirham volume processed per month (proxy for validation activity).
- Share of processed volume that is held for more than 90 days (proxy for founders who never incorporate; high share = bridge failing to close).
- Refund rate per founder (proxy for product–market fit signal at the early stage).

## Risks & Open Questions

- Validate problem with 5 Moroccan founder interviews before MVP: confirm that the no-entity gap matches what founders actually face and that they would route payments through a holding account.
- Regulatory path: holding customer funds in Morocco is regulated. The MVP must confirm whether the chosen structure (fiscal sponsor, regulated payment institution, or licensed acquirer partnership) is permissible before taking real money.
- Merchant-of-record clarity: the buyer must see who they are paying. Naming the service as merchant is honest; hiding it is fraud-prone. The MVP's payment-page disclosure must be unmissable.
- Bridge-to-incorporation risk: if founders stay on the holding-account model indefinitely, the service becomes a permanent shadow banking layer it is not licensed to be. The MVP must define a clear bridge-closure path.
- Tax and VAT scope: the MVP does not handle Moroccan VAT. If the founder crosses thresholds, they must register; the MVP must surface that reminder rather than hide it.
