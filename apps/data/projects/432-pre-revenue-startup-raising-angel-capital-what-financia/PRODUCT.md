# Pre-revenue startup raising angel capital - what financials need to be in a pitch deck/data room? (I will not promote)

> Auto-enriched product brief.

## Value Proposition

A pitch-deck financial-section generator that outputs the four slides angel investors actually open, without the founder having to hire a CFO or learn Excel finance.

## Target Users

- Pre-revenue founders raising between $250K and $2M from angels
- First-time fundraisers who do not yet have a CFO
- Angel mentors running pitch-practice cohorts

## Jobs To Be Done

When I am raising angel capital pre-revenue, I want the financial slides an angel actually opens, so I do not lose the meeting to a missing cash-bridge or an unsupported burn number.

## Success Metrics

- At least 200 decks generated in first 90 days
- Investor-rejection survey self-reports "deck quality" as the cause less often after using the tool

## Pricing & Monetization

Pricing & Monetization is intentionally left as TODO in this plan because the source post at `https://www.reddit.com/r/startups/comments/1vma20k/prerevenue_startup_raising_an` did not name a price, a billing model, or a comparable benchmark. ## Competitive Landscape

Pitch and Visible.vc exist in the broader startup world; not mentioned in source. Plan is a pre-revenue-only wedge.

## Risks & Open Questions

- If the tool invents revenue projections the founder cannot defend, it makes the deck worse
- Plan mandates "explicitly blank if unknown" everywhere
