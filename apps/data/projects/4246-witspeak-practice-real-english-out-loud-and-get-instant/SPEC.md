# SPEC.md — WitSpeak – Practice real English out loud and get instant AI speaking feedback

## Problem

WitSpeak is an AI English speaking coach for iPhone and Android that helps you build confident, natural speech. You talk out loud to roleplay everyday scenarios, run IELTS-style mock interviews, and drill pronunciation while the app gives instant feedback on fluency, vocabulary, grammar, and pronunciation. WitSpeak guides you with stronger sample answers, a personalized study plan, and progress tracking so you can practice anytime and improve faster with focused coaching. View startup
