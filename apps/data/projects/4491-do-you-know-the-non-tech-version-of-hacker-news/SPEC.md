# SPEC.md — Do you know the non-tech version of Hacker News?

## Problem

Hacker News is a community where mostly technical people collaborate, discuss, share about the things they are building or working on. People also post about jobs and those who are searching for jobs. As a community, we can connect with each other very easily through direct message or exchanging through email.<p>I wonder, is there a direct equivalent to Hacker News, with the same level of discussion, project sharing, and job networking built specifically for non-technical people?
