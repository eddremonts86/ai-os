# HostPDF – Upload and share your PDFs online for free

## Problem

HostPDF lets you upload PDF files and instantly get short, shareable links. It stores documents securely and lets you remove access anytime by deleting a link. Use it for event flyers, menus, resumes, and more, and share on social media or with customers and recruiters. HostPDF offers a free plan with limited uploads and storage, and a Pro plan with higher limits and permanent storage so you can scale as your needs grow. View startup
