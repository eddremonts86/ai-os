# YouEx.ai – Capture, qualify, and manage every inbound lead automatically

## Problem

YouEx combines an AI Web Agent, an AI-native CRM, and revenue automation to turn inbound interest into revenue. It talks to website visitors, answers questions, captures details, and books meetings 24/7. Inside the CRM, every lead is researched, enriched, scored, and routed so your team knows who to contact and why. Automated outreach and follow-ups keep opportunities moving. Integrations with email, calendar, Slack, Zapier, and MCP let you operate it from assistants like ChatGPT and Claude. View startup
