# SPEC.md — Get U Hired – Find matching tech jobs fast, track applications, and apply first

## Problem

Get U Hired runs your entire tech job search from one native app for iPhone, iPad, and Mac. When a matching role is posted, you get a push notification to apply early instead of being applicant number 300. Every job includes a plain-language AI summary and a brief on the company. You can filter by role, stack, seniority, location, and remote, tailor your resume with one tap, build your CV in the app, and message recruiters with shortcuts. Apply on the company's careers page and track applications from applied to offer, with response rates and notes. View startup
