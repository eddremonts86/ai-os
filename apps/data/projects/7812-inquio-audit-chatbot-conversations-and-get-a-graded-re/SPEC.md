# SPEC.md — Inquio – Audit chatbot conversations and get a graded report with fixes

## Problem

Inquio analyzes your chatbot conversations and produces a clear report card showing how the bot is performing. It calculates an Inquio Score, pinpoints issues ranked by severity, benchmarks results, and offers clear recommendations so you know what to fix. Teams can detect safety incidents with Defender, surface sales signals with Sales Radar, and share or export reports. Upload CSVs or connect via API to see results within minutes and iterate confidently. View startup
