# SPEC.md — Weezpr – Track your trades, discipline, and patterns driving your results

## Problem

Weezpr is a trading journal that helps traders understand more than just profit and loss. You can import trades, review entries and exits, add notes and snapshots, create day and trade plans, and analyze performance over time. The Calendar and Analytics highlight patterns in results and behavior, while the Discipline Tracker lets traders define their own rules and review how consistently they followed them. Weezpr is currently in a free private beta and is being improved based on feedback from active traders. View startup
