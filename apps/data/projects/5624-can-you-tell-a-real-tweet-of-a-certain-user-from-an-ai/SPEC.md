# SPEC.md — Can you tell a real tweet of a certain user from an AI one?

## Problem

Just a small game I coded up for research.<p>After some limited tests, people tend to pick the AI-written post as the
real one more than 55% of the time, almost 60%.<p>So apparently LLMs kinda pass the Turing test when it comes to social media
posts and not even general social media posts, but posts of a specific user!?
