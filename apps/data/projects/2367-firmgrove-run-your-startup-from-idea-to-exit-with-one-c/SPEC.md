# Firmgrove – Run your startup from idea to exit with one company brain

## Problem

Most founders spend their week as the integration layer, using eight tools that don't communicate and a deck, a model, and an investor update that quietly disagree. Firmgrove is the company brain that runs your startup. Finance, sales, marketing, people, operations, fundraising, and governance sit on one brain, so every number agrees wherever it appears and nothing reaches an investor until it has passed an audit. There is no tool to learn. You explain your company once, then you talk and decide while Firmgrove does the groundwork: research, drafts, pipeline, deck, and board pack. Its memory compounds so the company gets sharper every week, from the first idea to the exit. View startup
