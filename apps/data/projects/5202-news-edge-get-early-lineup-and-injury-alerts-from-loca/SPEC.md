# News Edge – Get early lineup and injury alerts from local sports media worldwide

## Problem

News Edge tracks local sports media across 100+ countries to surface facts that can affect match outcomes. It focuses on concrete updates like lineup changes, injuries, suspensions, crises, and motivation, tying each item to a specific fixture and timestamp. Subscribers receive signals on Telegram before kickoff, while the website hosts a public archive with match results for transparency. Use it to spot weakening squads and react before the line moves.
