# SPEC.md — ScholarSail – Study, map ideas, and schedule recall in one connected workspace

## Problem

ScholarSail brings your study process into a single connected workspace. Create AI-generated flashcards, map concepts with online mind maps, and place decks in 3D memory palaces to move from understanding to recall without switching tools. Organize drafts, sources, and ideas, then plan focused work with Kanban boards, calendars, Gantt views, and timeboxed day plans. Built-in Pomodoro, ambient noise, and a circadian rhythm tracker help you study when it counts and stay on track. View startup
