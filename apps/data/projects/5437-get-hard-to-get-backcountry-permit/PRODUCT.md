_Based on source brief:_ I got tired of trying to go backpacking and losing out on permits either through lottery or someone effectively bid-sniping cancellations. So, I built a real-time alert system across every state and federal booking system in the US. This covers ~700 permitted trails and locations as well as campsites.<p>It&#x27;s the first real end-to-end product I&#x27;ve ever built myself and I gotta say, it&#x27;s a lot of fun.
