# How do you get real GTM experience before you have a GTM job?

## Problem
 I'm a 3rd-year BTech student currently trying to move toward product marketing/GTM. I've done some product-focused internships/volunteer work involving market research, competitive research, LLM/product research, feature development and working with engineering teams. More recently, I've been teaching myself PMM/GTM through practical projects on real SaaS products, looking at ICPs, positioning, messaging, competitive landscapes, customer lifecycle, time-to-value and potential acquisition/growth problems. But I've hit a point where I'm realizing the limitation of doing everything as a personal project: I can analyze a product from the outside, but I can't replicate actually working inside a startup. I don't have access to things like customer conversations, sales pipelines, conversion data, retention data, experiments, or the day-to-day constraints behind GTM decisions. For people who started in GTM without much prior experience: How did you get your first opportunity to work on a real product? Did you start through internships, volunteering, helping an early-stage founder, freelancing, personal projects, or something completely different? I'm particularly interested in hearing from people who started with very little formal GTM experience and eventually got their hands on real work. I'm trying to figure out the most realistic next step rather than collecting another dozen courses or theoretical frameworks. submitted by /u/Stunning-Extension80 [link] [comments]

## Objective

Capture a Reddit career-advice post from a 3rd-year BTech student asking how to break into product marketing/GTM without a formal GTM role. The post is a question, not a product. It frames the structural gap between personal-project GTM work and in-house GTM work, and asks the community how others crossed it (internships, volunteering, helping early-stage founders, freelancing).

## Target Users

- Primary: students or career-changers who have done the public-data version of GTM (ICP research, positioning analysis, lifecycle mapping on real SaaS products) and hit the ceiling where outside analysis cannot replicate inside work.
- Secondary: career-changers and junior PMs asking the same 'how do I get the first one' question.

## MVP Scope

The post is a career question, not a product brief. There is no MVP. What is captured here is the gap between outside analysis (which the author already does well) and inside work (which they cannot access).
