# SPEC.md — Rapid Cart – Turn DM orders into a single link that collects complete requests

## Problem

Rapid Cart helps small businesses that sell through social media move from messy DMs to clear, actionable orders. Create a personal order link in under a minute, share it in your bio or messages, and receive structured requests with product details, budget, timing, and delivery info all in one place. Accept, decline, or update orders while automatic emails keep customers informed. Start simple and scale with branded pages, QR codes, embeds, API access, and webhooks as your business grows.
