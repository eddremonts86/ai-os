# SPEC.md — GetPlus AI – ChatGPT Plus and Pro recharge service with invoicing and corporate payments

## Problem

GetPlus AI offers ChatGPT Plus and Pro recharge services for individuals and businesses. After ordering online, users receive an activation code to activate on a specified page, usually within 3–5 minutes. The platform supports Alipay, WeChat Pay, and international cards via Stripe. It provides invoices, supports corporate transfers and contracts, and offers customer support for bulk purchases and compliant reimbursements. It also offers a 30-day warranty and full refund if recharge fails. View startup
