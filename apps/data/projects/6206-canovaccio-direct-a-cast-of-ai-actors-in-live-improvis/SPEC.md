# SPEC.md — Canovaccio – Direct a cast of AI actors in live, improvised performances

## Problem

Bring stories to life with you as the director. Canovaccio lets you build a troupe of AI characters with distinct personalities and voices, then guide them through improvised scenes or scripts. Shape the tone, steer the action, and hear your cast perform. Give private directions, try another take, and hear how the same cast responds differently. Invite friends into private Watch Parties. Share finished Script performances by link or publish them to the public Showcase. Test dialogue, rehearse NPC encounters, or enjoy unexpected story turns. Keep refining your production, or export the performance as a printable script. View startup
