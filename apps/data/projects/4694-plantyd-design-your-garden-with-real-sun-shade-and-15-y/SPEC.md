# Plantyd – Design your garden with real sun, shade, and 15 years of growth modeling

## Problem

Two things decide whether a garden works: light and time. Plantyd is a garden design tool that models both. It uses a satellite view of your property to map where sun and shade fall throughout the year, shows each plant at its 15-year size, and dates your vegetable sowings based on local frost records. You bring the yard. Plantyd knows the plants. You get a garden that looks good the first year and still works in the tenth. View startup
