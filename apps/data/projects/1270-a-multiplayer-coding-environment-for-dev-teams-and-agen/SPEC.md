# A multiplayer coding environment for dev teams and agents

## Problem

As a hardcore programmer, I used to attend lots of hackathons, and collaboration was always a huge headache. Especially when everyone on the team was "vibe coding" their own parts. Agents would lose context, and individual solutions would end up out of sync and eventually fail to come together at the end.To fix this exact problem, I built Forklane.Forklane is a multiplayer coding platform with a full, built-in agent and orchestration stack. It’s designed to help dev teams and AI agents collaborate in live real time sessions seamlessly without stepping on each other's toes and keeping all teammates agents synced.Check it out at https://forklane.aiWould love to hear community feedback and thoughts about the tool. Try it out in your teams and let me know
