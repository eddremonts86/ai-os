# Phaserr – Build training programs and share them with athletes

## Problem

Phaserr is a program builder for strength and conditioning coaches. If you write your programs in Google Sheets, this replaces the spreadsheet. You build the block, and Phaserr handles the numbers—1RM loads, warm-up ramps, rep schemes, and progressions. When you log a new max, every percentage updates itself with no formulas to fix. Describe the block you want, and AI writes a first draft using your exercise library and your athlete's real numbers. You then adjust it. It never sends anything on its own—your programming stays yours. Send it with one link, and your athlete opens it on their phone with no account or app store needed. You can also export a PDF if preferred. View startup
