# Micro-influencer cannot monetize a loyal audience: there is no safe and effective platform for deals, direct sales, or tip jars that works in India.

> Auto-generated product brief. Review and refine before MVP scoping.

## Value Proposition

Start earning from the audience you already have. The first 10,000 active creators pay for the service from the recovered friction of the existing Indian creator-stack patchwork.

## Target Users

Indian micro-influencers (10-100k followers, primarily on Instagram, YouTube, and short-form) who want a working monetization layer. Secondary: Indian small businesses that want to deal with creators but cannot navigate the existing marketplaces.

## Jobs To Be Done

Functional: have a working tip jar, product store, and brand-deal workflow. Emotional: stop the embarrassment of asking for support when the platform doesn't support the local payment. Social: be the creator who can finally say yes, I take UPI in their bio.

## Success Metrics

At least 10,000 active creators within 12 months. Median monthly creator earnings above the 5000 rupee mark. Brand-deal completion rate above 70%. Zero UPI webhook disputes that result in a creator loss.

## Competitive Landscape

Patreon and Ko-fi do not support UPI reliably. Local clones are unreliable. Brand-deal marketplaces (Plixxo, Pepper Content) require a larger follower count. No mainstream service combines UPI tips, direct sales, and brand-deal workflows with an Indian-micro-influencer-tuned discovery layer.

## Risks & Open Questions

UPI webhook reliability is a real risk. GST compliance requires ongoing work. Brand-deal escrow must be defensible. Platform-fee regulation in India is in flux. Creator churn is high if the first 30 days do not deliver visible earnings.
