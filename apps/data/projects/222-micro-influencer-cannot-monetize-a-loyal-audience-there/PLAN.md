# Micro-influencer cannot monetize a loyal audience: there is no safe and effective platform for deals, direct sales, or tip jars that works in India.

## Tech Stack

Next.js for the creator web profile. Flutter for the mobile companion. PostgreSQL for the creator and brand data. Razorpay and Cashfree for the payment rails. Cloudflare R2 for the product media.

## Architecture

Profile → tip jar (UPI) → product catalog → brand-deal workflow. Per-creator analytics. Brand-side discovery feed with audience-quality signals. GST-compliant invoicing per product sale.

## Milestones

M0 — creator profile with UPI tip jar. M1 — product sales with GST invoicing. M2 — brand-deal workflow. M3 — 1000 active creators in pilot. M4 — public launch with the brand discovery feed.

## Risks

UPI webhook reliability is a real risk. GST compliance requires ongoing work. Brand-deal escrow must be defensible. Platform-fee regulation in India is in flux. Creator churn is high if the first 30 days do not deliver visible earnings.
