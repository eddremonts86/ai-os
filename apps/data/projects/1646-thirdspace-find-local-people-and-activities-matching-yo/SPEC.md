# ThirdSpace – Find local people and activities matching your interests and time

## Problem

ThirdSpace helps you move beyond the screen to meet people and do things you love. The app matches you with others who share your interests, availability, and activity style, then shows local events and groups that fit your schedule and energy level. Organize or join workouts, casual meetups, and niche hobbies all in one place. Use it to discover both mainstream and hard-to-find activities and turn online interests into real-world experiences.
