# SPEC.md — What if we funded math research like we fund AI labs?

## Problem

The annual U.S. federal budget for basic math research is &lt;$1B.<p>OpenAI alone just raised ~$122B, and frontier labs are increasingly concentrating enormous amounts of compute and human talent on open math problems.<p>We seem to be entering a strange situation where a handful of private labs have vastly more resources for attacking these problems than the academics traditionally responsible for doing so.<p>Have we had the capability all along, just not the hype? Please discuss :)
