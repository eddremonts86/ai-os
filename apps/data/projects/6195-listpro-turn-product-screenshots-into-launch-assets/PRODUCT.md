## Value Proposition

_Based on source brief:_ I am a true believer in &quot;throwing things at the wall and seeing what sticks&quot; ideology so I&#x27;ve built products for multiple marketplaces- Shopify, Notion, mondaydotcom &amp; also SaaS. While AI coding made building faster, launching was my big problem. Marketplaces and launch platforms (Product hunt, appsumo etc) are a sea of products and it&#x27;s really tough to pass them with average looking launch assets.<p>I&#x27;m not a designer, nor do I find it sensible to pay for a monthly subscription when the launch is not a weekly thing (not yet atleast). So I kept facing this issue again and again when I&#x27;d spend hours and hours just researching good visual trends, references, then struggling to get a decent looking outcome right on canva. My goal was not to make my product look like a shiny bright thing - but to make sure people understand what it&#x27;s meant to do in one glance, rather than scrolling through busy screenshots. By my 10th product, I&#x27;d landed on a system that produced high quality gallery assets that I was confident to use - designed specific to the product I’d launch.<p>So I shared the workflow with a few friends and they found it quite nice. It helped their products gain more visibility - visitors could actually understand what your product does without the mental load of looking at a screenshot.<p>So I made this into product and made it available to everyone. Upload the most basic resources : product URL and raw screenshots to generate the whole kit - cover images, launch video, feature cards to explain each functionality.<p>Still in beta. Curious how people use it and what results it brings for them.
