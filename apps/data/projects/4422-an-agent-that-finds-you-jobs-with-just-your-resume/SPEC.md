# An agent that finds you jobs with just your resume

## Problem

All you do is upload your resume, and give the site your preferences on location, job titles, pay range, etc, and for free the site finds you a batch of jobs (usually around 5-15 per batch).It takes 10-30 seconds to generate a batch of jobs.The site is free and will always be free for people looking for jobs. It uses agents to check job boards all across the internet to find new / relevant jobs for people. I don't plan to ever make the site cost money for job seekers.For employers, I plan to charge a monthly fee for every live job they want delivered to job seekers.I think this experience is significantly better than using LinkedIn, Indeed, or other websites, even HN. But I believe most people will use multiple job search tools not just one.
