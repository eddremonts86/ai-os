## Value Proposition

_Based on source brief:_ All you do is upload your resume, and give the site your preferences on location, job titles, pay range, etc, and for free the site finds you a batch of jobs (usually around 5-15 per batch).<p>It takes 10-30 seconds to generate a batch of jobs.<p>The site is free and will always be free for people looking for jobs. It uses agents to check job boards all across the internet to find new &#x2F; relevant jobs for people. I don&#x27;t plan to ever make the site cost money for job seekers.<p>For employers, I plan to charge a monthly fee for every live job they want delivered to job seekers.<p>I think this experience is significantly better than using LinkedIn, Indeed, or other websites, even HN.
