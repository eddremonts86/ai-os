## Value Proposition

_Based on source brief:_ All you do is upload your resume, and give the site your preferences on location, job titles, pay range, etc, and for free the site finds you a batch of jobs (usually around 5-15 per batch).It takes 10-30 seconds to generate a batch of jobs.The site is free and will always be free for people looking for jobs.
