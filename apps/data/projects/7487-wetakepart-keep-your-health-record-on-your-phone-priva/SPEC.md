# SPEC.md — WeTakePart – Keep your health record on your phone private, clear, and shareable

## Problem

WeTakePart brings your medical history, medications, lab results, and fitness data together in a single private health record on your phone. It processes scanning, storage, and its AI health assistant locally, so nothing is uploaded or sold. You can capture letters, track vitals and meds, and see results explained in plain language. Connect wearables to view trends and context and share only what you choose with clinicians via time-limited access you can revoke at any moment. View startup
