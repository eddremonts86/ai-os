# VibeLing – Learn words from real content using AI and spaced repetition

## Problem

VibeLing helps language learners remember words and phrases from movies, TV shows, books, podcasts, songs, and everyday conversations. Add any word or phrase, and VibeLing automatically creates a translation, contextual examples, pronunciation audio, and personalized spaced-repetition reviews. Instead of studying generic lists or making flashcards, you build a personal vocabulary from content you enjoy. VibeLing helps you understand new expressions, practice spelling and meaning, and review them for long-term retention. The app is available on iOS and Android with unlimited free words. View startup
