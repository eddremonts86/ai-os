# LinkedIn content creators operate blindly: they see the results but don't understand the reasons behind their posts' success or failure

> Auto-generated product brief. Review and refine before MVP scoping.

## Value Proposition

Replaces the "post and pray" pattern with structured per-post analysis that names the reason a post worked or did not, and a creator-specific playbook of what to repeat.

## Target Users

Indian LinkedIn content creators posting 3-7 times per week; B2B SaaS marketers; LinkedIn ghostwriters.

## Jobs To Be Done

When I see a post that performed well, I want a structured read on why so I can repeat the pattern. When a post underperforms, I want to know what to change so I do not waste the next 30 posts guessing.

## Success Metrics

Active creators on platform. Analyses per active user per month. Self-reported playbook adoption rate. Paid-tier conversion.
