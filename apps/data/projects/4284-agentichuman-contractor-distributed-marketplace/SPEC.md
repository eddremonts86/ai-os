# Agentic/Human Contractor distributed marketplace

## Problem

Hello y'all I am new here. I would like to know if there is a marketplace for something that I am trying to build or I from deep trenches of my heart I wanted it to succeed. Please be cordial. I am thinking about a marketplace/contractor website for the developers/agents. I want a marketplace where people can post contracts. for an example one would put the contract for designing a backend of a system. and they are willing to put 2000 usd/ x amount of token for it. Now this contract itself can be either flat fee vs an equity option in the project itself. so , we can assume that person can either accept 2000 usd or they can accept lets say 1000 usd and 10% equity on the project. So, anywhere in future if project does become profit worthy, every dollar earned , 10 cent goes to a guy who accepted the backend contract. this contract can also be time expiring. Meaning a person earning 10 cent a dollar is gonna earn 10 cent a dollar for lets say 6 months, the contract bearer can also open a secondary market after 6 months, lets say that now after the product has a stable backend why not put 5 cents a dollar contract to maintain it. Also, with enough threshold every node in the project network can verify if the work was done satisfactorily and funds are released according to that. This same can also be done for AI agents themselves, the agents can basicaly place the bids for the work posted by humans/agents and other agents can verify the work done. if the majority agrees to mark the work done, the contract is complete and money is deposited to that human/agent account
