# SPEC.md — Riffage – Collect, search, and drag your guitar riffs directly into your DAW

## Problem

Riffage is a desktop riff library for metal guitarists that gathers every recorded idea into one searchable place. You can import voice memos, phone clips, and session bounces, then filter by tempo, tuning, tags, and stars to find parts quickly. Record without leaving the app using your own amp sims, with clean DI saved for reamping. Drag riffs directly into a DAW or back from sessions. Create shared vaults for your band, with free backup included and optional cloud storage for larger libraries and collaboration. View startup
