# SPEC.md — WIGGWIGG – Keep work, dating, and selling on separate phone numbers

## Problem

Keep work, dating, and selling separate. Each identity has contacts and a password vault encrypted on your device. Add a Canadian phone number with a plan and control call-routing settings. Free worldwide: account, vault, contacts, two identities, and a 30-day Vault Premium trial. In Early Access, phone numbers, the 7-day Canadian-number trial, and purchases require a Canadian province confirmed for taxes. The cryptographic core is MIT-licensed; the rest is currently closed source. Early Access is a real, paid service but a product under construction: core features can break, screens may change often, and an announced feature can be pulled while it is rebuilt. View startup
