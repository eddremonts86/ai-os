# SPEC.md — Local Personal Financial Assistant

## Problem

I built an IOS personal financial app to help user analyze their financial situation. User can easily import their bank statement from their banking app, CFO(this app) will analyze and show  charts by categories, find potential wrong items like duplicated charges, inactive subscription, higher loan rate than the market. Your can easily ask AI to explain the result or give you a review.<p>You can ask your CFO:
1. &quot;Hi Ling, why my loan rate is been highlighted? Can I get a better one from bank? 
2. &quot;Can you draft an email to Air Canada for the duplicated charge?&quot;<p>CFO is a sub app under Linggen, find more on its .dev website.
