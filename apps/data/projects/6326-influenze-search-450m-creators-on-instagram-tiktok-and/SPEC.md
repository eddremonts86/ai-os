# SPEC.md — Influenze – Search 450M+ creators on Instagram, TikTok, and YouTube with audience info

## Problem

Influenze helps teams discover and evaluate creators across Instagram, YouTube, and TikTok. It offers AI-driven search over 450M+ profiles with real-time audience insights, engagement metrics, growth trends, brand affinities, and estimated pricing. Users can build and share shortlists, compare influencers side by side, and reach out via verified contact details. Agencies, brands, and talent managers use Influenze to replace spreadsheets with fast, accurate creator intelligence. View startup
