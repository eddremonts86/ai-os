# SPEC.md — NutriFit – Manage nutrition clients and health data in one workspace

## Problem

NutriFit is a cloud workspace for nutritionists, dietitians, wellness coaches, fitness professionals, and individuals. It brings together client profiles, food and activity logs, body measurements, habits, lab results, supplement plans, messaging, reminders, and progress analytics into one connected system. Professionals can manage multiple clients and personalize plans, while individuals can track meals, calories, macros, biomarkers, and progress alone or with a specialist. NutriFit offers a free Lite plan and PRO plans starting at $20 per month. View startup
