# AI agent for automatic SEO promotion of websites on Wordpress and Tilda

> Product brief authored from the source title and category. The poster's text was not available (source.name: manual); sections below re-state the problem and infer only what the title and category support.

## Value Proposition

A Serbian SEO operator connects a WordPress or Tilda site, picks the weekly routines, and gets an approval queue of SEO drafts and metadata fixes every Monday - without having to open Search Console and the CMS in two tabs.

## Target Users

- Serbian and wider Balkan SEO operators managing 10-30 small-business WordPress and Tilda sites.
- In-house marketers at SMBs in the region who want SEO to run on autopilot without a full-time hire.
- Freelance web designers who maintain sites they built and want a recurring revenue stream from each one.

## Jobs To Be Done

1. **Functional job** - Keep 10-30 sites ranking without running the SEO checklist manually.
2. **Emotional job** - Stop dreading Monday morning's SERP audit.
3. **Social job** - Hand the client a weekly SEO report that names the change and the keyword, not the time spent.

## Success Metrics

- **Adoption:** operator connects >= 5 sites within 30 days of signup.
- **Approval rate:** >= 70% of agent-generated changes approved without edits.
- **Outcome proxy:** >= 50% of connected sites move up at least 2 positions on tracked keywords over 90 days.

## Competitive Landscape

- **Yoast / RankMath** - on-page guidance, not an agent that runs a routine.
- **SE Ranking / Ahrefs** - rank tracking + audits; no CMS publish path.
- **Generic AI writers** - produce content, do not handle metadata, internal links, sitemap, GSC triage.
