# BeartIMAGE – Edit, compress, and convert photos privately in your browser

## Problem

BeartIMAGE is a browser-based suite of photo tools that runs entirely on your device with zero uploads. It lets you resize, crop, compress, convert, and adjust light and color with live previews and batch processing. You can use on-device AI to remove backgrounds, add watermarks, wrap screenshots in mockups, build print-ready photo albums, view and strip EXIF data, and draw on a canvas. It's free to use with no accounts or watermarks and supports JPG, PNG, WebP, BMP, and PDF exports. View startup
