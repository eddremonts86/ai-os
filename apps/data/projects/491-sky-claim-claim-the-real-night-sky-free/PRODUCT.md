# Sky Claim - Claim the real night sky - Free

> Auto-enriched product brief.

## Value Proposition

A free AR web app where you point your phone at the night sky and claim a region of stars as yours, with an optional public map of claims.

## Target Users

- Casual stargazers looking for a playful AR experience
- Parents wanting a free, low-pressure educational activity
- Astronomy enthusiasts who want a personal sky record

## Jobs To Be Done

When I am looking at the night sky, I want a tool that lets me claim a region of stars as mine, so I have a playful personal record of the sky.

## Success Metrics

- At least 10,000 claims in pilot
- Median session length above 2 minutes
