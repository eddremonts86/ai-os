# Free, Fast Screenshot App for macOS with One-Click Screenshot Sharing

> Auto-generated product brief. Review and refine before MVP scoping.

## Value Proposition

A macOS screenshot tool that captures, annotates and shares in one click — for humans in chat and for AI agents with comments attached.

**One-liner:** Capture, annotate and send a screenshot in one click — for humans and AI agents.

## Target Users

Anyone on macOS who sends many screenshots daily, especially developers giving feedback to AI coding agents. Adjacent: support and QA teams that annotate screenshots for tickets.

## Jobs To Be Done

- When I want to send a screenshot, I want one click to capture, annotate and share so it takes seconds, not minutes.
- When I want feedback from an AI agent, I want annotations and comments attached so the agent sees the same intent as a human reviewer.
- When I need a specific region or window, I want the same one-click flow as the default macOS app.

## Success Metrics

- Average time from 'I want to send a screenshot' to 'screenshot is in chat'.
- Share-to-chat vs share-to-agent ratio.
- Daily active users.
- App Store rating (if distributed via App Store).

## Pricing & Monetization

Source does not state pricing or monetisation. Treat as free until the author publishes a model.

## Competitive Landscape

Default macOS screenshot tool, CleanShot X, Shottr, and Snagit all overlap. The new app differentiates on the one-click share flow for both human chat and AI agents, and on the explicit AI-agent feedback path.

## Risks & Open Questions

- macOS sandbox and screen-capture permissions; mitigation is to ship a clear permissions guide.
- App Store review for a system-level capture tool; mitigation is to ship outside the App Store if needed.
- 'One click' UX is subjective; mitigation is to test with real users.
