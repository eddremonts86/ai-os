# Proofavo – Send images and PDFs for client approval without accounts

## Problem

Proofavo replaces email threads where artwork approvals often get lost. Upload a PDF, JPG, or PNG, send one link, and your client can comment directly on the artwork and approve it without an account or login, even on a phone. Approval locks to the exact file with a SHA-256 fingerprint. Flat workspace pricing is designed for freelancers, small studios, and print shops. Live sandbox demo is available for both sides without sign-up at proofavo.com/demo. View startup
