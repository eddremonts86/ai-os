# The Mahj Report – Keep casual American mahjong score and crown winners your way

## Problem

The Mahj Report is a scoring and season-tracking app for casual American mahjong groups. Set house rules once, then tap to log each hand while standings update automatically. Poll the group for a date (no app needed to answer), lock in the winner, and RSVPs open on their own. Track nightly and season winners with Grand Champion and Hot Hand boards, then share results to your group chat. Switch between custom scoring and official NMJL mode. View startup
