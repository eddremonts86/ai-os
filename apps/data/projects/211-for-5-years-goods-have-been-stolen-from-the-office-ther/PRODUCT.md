# For 5 years, goods have been stolen from the office. There is no available service that automatically distinguishes visitors from staff and alerts on suspicious patterns.

> Auto-generated product brief. Review and refine before MVP scoping.

## Value Proposition

Stop the long, slow bleed of office theft without paying for an enterprise access-control system. The first 100 offices pay for the install from the goods they recover in the first quarter.

## Target Users

Small and mid-size offices in India and South-East Asia (10-100 people) with existing CCTV but no enterprise access control. Also warehouse lobbies, clinics, and small co-working spaces with the same problem.

## Jobs To Be Done

Functional: know who is in the building and whether their pattern is normal. Emotional: stop the constant low-grade anxiety of leaving the office. Social: be the office manager who finally has a real answer, not a shrug.

## Success Metrics

Visitor classification accuracy above 90% on the office's own camera angles. At least 3 of the first 10 offices report a successful identification of a real incident within the first 6 months. Alert false-positive rate below 10% during business hours.

## Competitive Landscape

Enterprise access control (Honeywell, Siemens) is too expensive and requires badges. Generic AI cameras (Hikvision, Dahua) detect people but not staff vs visitor. Cloud video services (Google Nest, Ring) upload all footage to the cloud, which is unacceptable for an office. No mainstream self-hosted service combines staff enrolment with real-time visitor classification on existing CCTV.

## Risks & Open Questions

Risk of misidentifying a staff member as a visitor with severe consequences. Risk of running afoul of India's DPDP Act on biometric data. Staff enrolment may be socially awkward. Lighting and camera angles may make classification brittle.
