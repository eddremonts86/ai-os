I remember how any hype creating or mystic news were rejected at the level of basic engineering intuition: that&#x27;s bull, because that&#x27;s not how this works.<p>This is totally not the case with LLMs. We get links like &quot;Why are AI agents lying, cheating and coordinating?&quot; going viral, we have discussions in every second thread about who gets replaced and in what exact way, we get fear mongering posts upvoted that preach AGI and call for AI regulations. Which is the same exact content we had during Web etc times about &quot;dangers of the machine&quot; from people who have no idea how &quot;the machine&quot; works.<p>But <i>we</i> do.<p>What is happening, HN?
