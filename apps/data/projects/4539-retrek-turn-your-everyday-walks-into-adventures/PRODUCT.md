## Value Proposition

_Based on source brief:_ reTrek turns the walking you already do into progress along a famous trail. Pick the Camino de Santiago, Everest Base Camp, the Inca Trail, Hadrian's Wall, or a story-driven quest, then walk in real life: the evening loop, the dog walk, the trip to the shop. Every step moves you along the route. Reach milestones to unlock content like short films or the next chapter of your quest, where your choices shape the ending. You can team up and pool your steps to finish together, or race to see who gets there first. Follow friends, cheer each other on, and collect a shareable card for every trail you finish.
