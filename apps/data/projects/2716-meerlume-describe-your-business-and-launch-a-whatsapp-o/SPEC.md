# Meerlume – Describe your business and launch a WhatsApp or Telegram bot in minutes

## Problem

Meerlume turns a plain-language description of your business into a working bot on WhatsApp or Telegram. It captures leads, answers FAQs, takes bookings with live availability, and manages handoffs so you can step in. Preview and edit flows by asking in natural language, then go live in minutes. Use 120+ templates and connect to Google Calendar, Sheets, Zapier, Stripe, Slack, and more. Manage conversations from your dashboard or inside your messenger, keep one calendar across channels, and start free on Telegram with paid plans including WhatsApp. View startup
