# SeaWingman – Meet friends nearby across Southeast Asia without the dating vibe

## Problem

SeaWingman helps locals, expats, and travelers in Southeast Asia meet real friends quickly. The app focuses on friendship over dating, letting you find people nearby, join group chats, and attend or host events in cities like Bangkok, Pattaya, and Chiang Mai. Create a profile around your interests and say when you're free, then message, match, and meet up safely with built-in verification and controls. Start free with core features, or upgrade for unlimited friends and ad-free browsing. View startup
