# Guide to AI Brand Visibility Tracking Tools in 2026

> Auto-generated product brief. Review and refine before MVP scoping.

## Value Proposition

An agency or brand can monitor prompt visibility across the six major AI engines for the underlying inference cost alone, instead of paying $300-2,000/month per seat to a hosted visibility SaaS, while still getting citation, share-of-voice, and "which competitors are winning these prompts" output.

## Target Users

| Stakeholder | Why they care |
|---|---|
| AEO/GEO agency | Running 500+ prompts/month for clients; hosted pricing breaks the unit economics; needs BYOK. |
| In-house growth marketer (SMB) | Wants visibility without an enterprise contract; willing to wire up their own keys. |
| Solo founder/indie brand | Wants to know when ChatGPT starts citing competitors instead of them, cheaply. |

## Jobs To Be Done

1. **Functional job** — Run visibility checks across AI engines without paying SaaS markup on top of inference costs.
2. **Emotional job** — Stop treating the AI Visibility Score as a vanity metric; surface the prompts that actually moved.
3. **Social job** — Bring the team evidence ("we lost these 12 prompts to Reddit threads last month") instead of a one-number report.

## Success Metrics

- **Activation:** user completes brand + first 20 prompts within 30 minutes of signup.
- **Throughput:** ≥500 prompts/week tracked across the active user base within 60 days.
- **Retention:** ≥60% of agencies with ≥1,000 prompts/week are still active after 90 days.
- **Citation coverage:** ≥85% of tracked prompts return at least one cited source per engine, on average.

## Pricing & Monetization

Free tier: 20 prompts/week across 2 engines, 1 brand. Pro $49/month: 500 prompts/week, 6 engines, 3 brands, citation export. Agency $199/month: 5,000 prompts/week, 10 brands, team seats, white-label PDF reports. BYOK keys required on all paid tiers.

## Competitive Landscape

- **Profound, Scrunch, Evertune** — enterprise visibility SaaS at $1,000+/month per brand; comprehensive but expensive.
- **Peec AI, Otterly AI, Promptwatch** — hosted SMB tools at $50-300/month; markup on inference is real.
- **Writesonic** — bundles GEO tracking inside a content platform; useful only if the user already writes there.
- **Llumo** — BYOK approach the source explicitly highlights; closest free/cheap alternative.

## Risks & Open Questions

- [ ] Validate the BYOK assumption with 10 agencies — many will not wire keys for compliance reasons.
- [ ] Confirm the citation extraction works on Google AI Overviews (the format keeps changing).
- [ ] Decide whether to add a hosted fallback tier for users who can't BYOK.
