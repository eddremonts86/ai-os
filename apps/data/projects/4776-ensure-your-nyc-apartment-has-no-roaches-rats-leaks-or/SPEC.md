# Ensure your NYC apartment has no roaches, rats, leaks, or problems

## Problem

We got tired of spending hours per apartment digging through HPD, DOB, 311, and housing records researching issues, so we built Apartment Fax to make our lives easier.For $1, Apartment Fax digs through HPD, DOB, 311, and housing records to figure out whether the unit/building has any rats, roaches, leaks, noise, etc.Let us know if you have any questions.
