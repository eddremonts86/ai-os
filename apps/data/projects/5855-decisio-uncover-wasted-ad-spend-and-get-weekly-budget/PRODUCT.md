## Value Proposition

_Based on source brief:_ Decisio decides where your marketing budget should go. It analyzes performance across paid channels and identifies inefficient budgets, then recommends what to scale, stop, or reallocate. Teams receive a weekly Monday brief with clear verdicts, forecasts, and a CFO-ready report, plus a full audit trail of approvals and impacts. As results are logged, Decisio learns and improves confidence scores so you cut waste faster and back the campaigns that truly perform. Start free with no account needed. Paste a screenshot of your Meta Ads account and get the monthly report you'd send your director — spend, results, cost per lead, campaigns ranked — in about 20 seconds. No login, no OAuth, no CSVs.
