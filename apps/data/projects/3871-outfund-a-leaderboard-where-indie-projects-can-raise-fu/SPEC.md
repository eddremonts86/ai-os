## Objective

Build the claimed product: a leaderboard where indie projects raise funds, with project pages, a public ranking and a working contribution flow. The MVP is listing, ranking display and a single, transparent way for the public to contribute funds.

- Indie makers seeking early funding and visibility.

- Project browsers who follow indie scenes.

## MVP Scope

- Project listing with pitch and funding state.
- Public leaderboard with a stated ranking rule.
- Contribution flow (the capture does not state the mechanism; the MVP picks one and documents it).
- Transparency: who raised what, visible on the leaderboard.
