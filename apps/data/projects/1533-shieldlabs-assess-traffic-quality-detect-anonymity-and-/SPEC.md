# ShieldLabs – Assess traffic quality, detect anonymity, and stop multi-accounting

## Problem

ShieldLabs helps companies detect anonymous traffic, assess its quality, and prevent abuse and fraud. It identifies visitors behind VPNs, proxies, Tor, and anti-detect browsers with up to 99% accuracy, then returns a risk score for every visit. Traffic quality is scored by channel, referrer, and campaign. It is used to prevent multi-accounting, free-trial abuse, bonus and promo abuse, new-account fraud, account takeover, account sharing, ban evasion, ad fraud, and location spoofing. It takes five minutes to install and offers 5,000 free identifications. View startup
