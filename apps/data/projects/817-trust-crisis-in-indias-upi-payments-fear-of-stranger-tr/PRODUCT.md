# Trust crisis in India's UPI payments: fear of stranger transfers hurts business

## Value Proposition

A trust signal for Indian merchants who receive UPI payments from people they do not know, so they can decide quickly whether to fulfil the order.

## Target Users

Small Indian merchants and freelancers who accept UPI from unfamiliar customers and currently refuse or delay orders out of fraud fear.

## Jobs To Be Done

When a stranger pays me by UPI, I want a quick risk score and the reason behind it, so I can decide whether to ship the order without spending half an hour researching the payer.

## Success Metrics

Whether the trust signal reduces failed UPI transactions between strangers; the source does not quantify this.
