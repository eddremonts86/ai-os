# Need help with outreach at a "local" AI law SaaS

## Problem

A founder runs a central European AI lawyer platform. All leads come from Google SEO, people searching "lawyer AI" (in local language). When the SaaS was built, it was very basic, almost like a PoC, unusable for real lawyers, but the founder did not care because the goal was to validate SEO demand. The platform now works for real lawyers and the founder needs help with outreach beyond SEO. The implicit product: an AI-lawyer SaaS that is functional but under-distributed; the founder needs outreach playbook, not product work.

## Objective

Define the MVP scope for the AI-lawyer platform (already exists) plus the outreach playbook (the founder's actual ask). The plan re-documents the product surface and gives the founder a structured outreach workflow.

## Target Users

- **Primary:** lawyers in central Europe who want AI-assisted contract review, document summarisation, and case research.
- **Secondary:** small law firms that cannot afford a junior associate for document review.
- **Tertiary:** in-house legal teams at SMBs in the region.

## MVP Scope

- The AI-lawyer platform (already exists; the plan re-documents it).
- Outreach playbook: 5 channels beyond SEO (LinkedIn, industry events, lawyer-association partnerships, content marketing, referral programme).
- Per-channel playbook: weekly outreach volume, expected response rate, expected conversion.
- A weekly outreach tracker.
- Free tier: the playbook + the weekly tracker. Pro at $49/month: 1:1 outreach coaching.
- Excluded in v1: PR-firm partnership, paid advertising for legal services (heavily regulated).

## Design Direction

See `DESIGN.md` for this project's design tokens. Default visual: a single outreach-workflow surface — the 5 channels on the left, the per-channel playbook in the centre, the weekly tracker on the right. No marketing-site chrome; the product is the playbook.

## Constraints

- Legal-services advertising is heavily regulated in the EU; paid acquisition is excluded.
- The founder's existing SEO traction is the wedge; outreach must amplify, not replace.
- The outreach playbook must respect the lawyer-association rules in each jurisdiction.
