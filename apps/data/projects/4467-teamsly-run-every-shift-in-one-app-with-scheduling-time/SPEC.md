# SPEC.md — Teamsly – Run every shift in one app with scheduling, time tracking, tasks, and chat

## Problem

Teamsly is workforce management software for hourly, shift-based, deskless, and field teams. It combines employee scheduling, time tracking, team communication, tasks, forms, audits, and training into one web and mobile app. Managers build schedules manually, from templates, or with AI. They manage availability, time off, open shifts, swaps, jobs, and crews; verify clock-ins with geofencing; and track timesheets, overtime, and labor costs. Teams can assign recurring tasks with photo proof, collect forms and audits with signatures, deliver courses and quizzes, and use chat and announcements. Schedules, timesheets, and completed work stay in sync. Teamsly supports English and Spanish. View startup
