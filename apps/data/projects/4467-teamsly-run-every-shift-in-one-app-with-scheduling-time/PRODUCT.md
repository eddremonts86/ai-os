_Based on source brief:_ Teamsly is workforce management software for hourly, shift-based, deskless, and field teams. It combines employee scheduling, time tracking, team communication, tasks, forms, audits, and training into one web and mobile app. Managers build schedules manually, from templates, or with AI.
