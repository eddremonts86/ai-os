# No device allows a 4-year-old child to independently stream music to a speaker over Wi-Fi without using a phone, tablet, or increasing screen time

> Auto-generated product brief. Review and refine before MVP scoping.

## Value Proposition

Replaces the phone-in-the-child's-hand pattern with a physical, tactile device that gives the child genuine independence over music while keeping the phone in the parent's pocket.

## Target Users

Norwegian parents of small children (3-6 years); grandparents; daycares and preschools.

## Jobs To Be Done

When my 4-year-old wants a song, I want them to push a button on a physical device and hear it play so I do not have to hand them my phone. When I want to change which songs the buttons play, I want a simple parent app that does not require a tech tutorial.

## Success Metrics

Devices shipped per quarter. Parent-app setup completion rate (target: 95%). NPS among parents. Repeat-purchase rate (hand-me-downs and gifts).
