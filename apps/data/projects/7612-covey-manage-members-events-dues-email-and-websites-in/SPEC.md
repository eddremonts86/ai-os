# SPEC.md — Covey – Manage members, events, dues, email, and websites in one platform

## Problem

Covey combines member management, events, dues, email, websites, and community into one platform for associations, nonprofits, clubs, and alumni groups. It supports households, custom fields, renewals, multi-event registration with carts, and integrated Stripe and PayPal payments with a reconciled ledger and QuickBooks export. Teams can design newsletters, segment sends, and host a member portal and forums. Human-led migration and support help you switch with confidence. View startup
