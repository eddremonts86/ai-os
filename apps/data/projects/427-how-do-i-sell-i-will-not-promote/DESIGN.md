# How Do I Sell? (I will not promote) — Design direction

Deliberately un-marketing. The poster is exhausted; the design should feel like a quiet worksheet.

- Surface: a single-page scroll, one question per step, progress bar in plain text.
- Output: a 4-quadrant diagnostic card with quadrant names ("ICP not yet believable," "Demo collapses at technical objections," etc.) and one-line remediation per card.
- Color: monochrome with a single warm accent for the "blocked" quadrant.
- Typography: editorial serif for quadrant headings, monospace for the numeric scores.
- No stock imagery of businesspeople shaking hands.
