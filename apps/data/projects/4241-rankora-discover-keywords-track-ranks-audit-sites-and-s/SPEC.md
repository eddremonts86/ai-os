# SPEC.md — Rankora – Discover keywords, track ranks, audit sites, and ship clear SEO fixes

## Problem

Rankora is an AI-powered SEO platform that combines keyword research, rank tracking, backlink analysis, technical audits, and content optimization into one toolkit. Enter any URL, and it crawls pages, pulls live Google data, and runs 36+ technical checks to reveal issues and opportunities. It generates a prioritized action plan in plain English, helps benchmark competitors, and tracks progress over time. Use it to diagnose, fix, and grow organic visibility with less manual effort.
