# Search for an effective cleaning business management solution

> Auto-generated product brief. Review and refine before MVP scoping.

## Value Proposition

A small US cleaning business books the week, bills recurring customers automatically, plans the route, and stops missing calls — for one flat monthly fee that does not punish growth.

## Target Users

| Stakeholder | Why they care |
|---|---|
| US solo cleaning operator | Books 15–30 jobs a week by phone; wants a website that handles the recurring cycle. |
| US husband-wife cleaning team | Two schedules to coordinate; needs shared calendar + route planner. |
| Small US janitorial team (2–10 cleaners) | Wants the same scheduling surface without the per-seat fee of enterprise field-service software. |

## Jobs To Be Done

1. **Functional job** — Book, schedule, route, bill, and pay for a week's cleanings without five different tools.
2. **Emotional job** — Stop answering the phone at 6am because customers can self-book and self-reschedule.
3. **Social job** — Look professional to customers with a real website, real SMS reminders, and a credit card on file.

## Success Metrics

- Time-to-schedule-the-week ≤ 30 minutes for an operator with 20+ recurring customers.
- Customer no-show rate reduction — measure delta vs baseline after 90 days.
- Recurring-billing failure rate ≤ 3% (cards declined + ACH returns).
- Operator NPS ≥ 50 at month 3.

## Pricing & Monetization

Flat $79/month per operator account (unlimited cleaners, unlimited customers). Annual $790 (saves $158). Premium tier ($149/month) adds customer self-booking landing page + Google Ads integration.

## Competitive Landscape

- Jobber / ServiceTitan — strong but per-seat; price floor $200+/month for a 3-cleaner team.
- Calendly + Stripe + Wave — duct-taped stack; works but is 4 tools and 4 bills.
- Cleaning-specific SaaS (ZenMaid, Launch27) — closer fit but still per-user pricing and limited mobile UX.

## Risks & Open Questions

- [ ] Cleaner mobile-web UX — Mitigation: design and test on low-end Android browsers; weekly usability sessions with real cleaners.
- [ ] Stripe ACH returns — Mitigation: automatic retry on day 3, customer notification on day 1, fallback to card if ACH fails.
- [ ] TCPA compliance — Mitigation: express-consent opt-in flow at booking; opt-out handled via Twilio keyword.
