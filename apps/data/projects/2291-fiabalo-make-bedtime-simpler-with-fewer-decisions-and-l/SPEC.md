# Fiabalo – Make bedtime simpler with fewer decisions and less searching

## Problem

Fiabalo helps parents make bedtime easier with less searching, fewer decisions, and stories meant for bedtime. You can read aloud when you want to share the story yourself or listen together when you're too tired to read and just want to press play. Soft ambient sounds keep the quiet going after the story ends. The experience stays simple, child-friendly, and free from distractions, letting you enjoy the moment together. View startup
