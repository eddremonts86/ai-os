# There is no simple and secure way to pass bitcoin to heirs in the event of sudden death. Without complex multisig, heirs lose access forever.

> Auto-generated product brief. Review and refine before MVP scoping.

## Value Proposition

Make sure your bitcoin reaches your family, not a black hole. The first 1000 subscribers self-fund the engineering cost out of the one wallet they were going to lose.

## Target Users

Bitcoin holders in Latin America and the US with self-custody who have at least one non-technical heir and want a recovery path that does not require ongoing family education about keys.

## Jobs To Be Done

Functional: ensure heirs can recover bitcoin without learning crypto. Emotional: stop the anxiety of 'what if I die and my family is locked out'. Social: be the bitcoin holder who treated inheritance as a real planning problem, not a meme.

## Success Metrics

At least 200 active users in the first year. Invocations of the recovery flow (whether ultimately successful or not) less than 5 per year. False-positive release incidents zero.

## Competitive Landscape

Casa, Unchained, and Nunchuk offer multisig with recovery but require the heir to be technically literate. Traditional estate lawyers do not write bitcoin recovery scripts. No mainstream service combines sharded, encrypted recovery packages with a deadman-switch release flow that does not custody the bitcoin.

## Risks & Open Questions

Risk of routing attacks on the heart-beat signal. Risk of a fraudulent heir claim. Legal risk if the service is interpreted as a custodian. Operational risk if the service is shut down — the recovery package must remain decryptable by the user independently.
