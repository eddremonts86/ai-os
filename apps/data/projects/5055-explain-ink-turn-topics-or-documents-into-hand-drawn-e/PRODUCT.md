## Value Proposition

_Based on source brief:_ explain.ink creates hand-drawn explainer videos from a topic, script, or uploaded document. It writes the story, generates scene-by-scene drawings in one of 18 consistent styles, records a matching voiceover, and renders an HD MP4 with full commercial rights. You can start from a line of text, paste your own script, or upload PDFs and docs, then pick a style and language. The service delivers ready-to-use videos without any drawing or editing needed.
