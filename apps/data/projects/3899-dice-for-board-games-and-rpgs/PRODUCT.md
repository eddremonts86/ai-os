# Dice for Board Games and RPGs

## Value Proposition

Every table has a moment where someone asks if anyone has a d8. A web dice roller answers that question permanently: the full polyhedral set, pools and modifiers, animated rolls that land visibly, and a shared link so a remote table rolls together. The product is utility first — fast, fair, and always available — with enough physical feel to not feel like a calculator.

**One-liner:** Polyhedral dice for board games and RPGs, in the browser.

## Target Users

| Stakeholder | Why they care |
|---|---|
| TTRPG players | Dice pools with modifiers are the core mechanic of most systems. |
| Game masters | Shared rolls keep remote sessions honest and visible. |
| Board gamers | A lost die no longer cancels a game night. |
| Minimalists | One less box of plastic to carry to the table. |

## Jobs To Be Done

1. Roll any standard polyhedral die instantly, including pools like 3d6+2.
2. Trust that rolls are fair and not rigged.
3. Share a roll with everyone at the table, in person or remote.
4. Keep a history of the session's rolls for disputes and recaps.

## Success Metrics

- Rolls per session and sessions per week.
- Share of rolls using pools or modifiers, the power-user signal.
- Shared-session usage: tables that roll together versus solo rollers.
- Return rate: how many sessions come back within a week.
