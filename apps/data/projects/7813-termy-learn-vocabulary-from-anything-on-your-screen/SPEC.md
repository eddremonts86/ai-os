# SPEC.md — Termy – Learn vocabulary from anything on your screen

## Problem

Termy turns the content you already enjoy into language practice. Press a shortcut in any game, video, website, or app to get an in-context explanation of a word or phrase. Save it with its original sentence, screenshot, translation, and pronunciation, then review it through adaptive spaced-repetition exercises. Termy supports 30 languages on Windows and macOS, with a free plan available. View startup
