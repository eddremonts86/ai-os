# SPEC.md — MySocialSaver – Download social media videos and photos fast with no watermark

## Problem

MySocialSaver lets you save videos and photos from Instagram, TikTok, Facebook, X (Twitter), Threads, YouTube Shorts, and Pinterest right in your browser. Paste any post, reel, video, or pin link and it detects the platform and media type, then delivers a clean, high-quality file with no watermark. It requires no account, app, or extension and works on desktop and mobile. The service focuses on public content, keeps downloads direct when possible, and is actively maintained to keep pace with platform changes. View startup
