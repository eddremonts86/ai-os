# Inability to get a response to complaints from companies

> Auto-generated product brief. Review and refine before MVP scoping.

## Value Proposition

An Argentine consumer files a complaint that gets a response within the legal window — or has the regulator's form pre-filled and ready to escalate within one tap.

## Target Users

| Stakeholder | Why they care |
|---|---|
| Argentine consumer with a telecom/bank/retail complaint | Has the case; needs the channel and the persistence. |
| Argentine consumer ready to escalate to PRO Argentina | Knows the company has failed; needs the right superintendo and the form. |
| PRO Argentina case officer | Wants pre-validated, well-documented cases, not malformed submissions. |

## Jobs To Be Done

1. **Functional job** — File a complaint that the company actually has to answer in the legal window.
2. **Emotional job** — Stop the powerless feeling when an auto-reply replaces an answer.
3. **Social job** — Tell a friend with the same problem 'here is how you file it properly'.

## Success Metrics

- Complaint-to-substantive-response rate — ≥ 60% within 10 business days.
- Escalation-to-PRO success rate — ≥ 80% of escalated cases accepted by the superintendo.
- User-reported recovered value (pesos) — tracked via self-report at complaint close.
- WhatsApp bot weekly active retention ≥ 50%.

## Pricing & Monetization

Free for individual consumers. B2B tier: companies paying for in-platform SLA monitoring and consumer-feedback aggregation. Government/NGO partnerships: free, capped at agreed volume.

## Competitive Landscape

- PRO Argentina web portal — exists but capacity-bound; not a consumer-side workflow.
- Consumer-protection law firms (Defensa del Consumidor) — strong but expensive.
- WhatsApp advocacy groups — high reach, low individual case throughput.

## Risks & Open Questions

- [ ] False escalation — consumers escalate too early. Mitigation: enforcement of the legal 10-business-day window; reminder before escalation.
- [ ] Company retaliation — a complaint can affect service. Mitigation: pseudonymous submission via the platform email relay; identity revealed only on regulator escalation.
- [ ] Regulator backlog — a flood of well-formed cases can outpace capacity. Mitigation: tiered routing; only high-confidence cases go to the regulator.
