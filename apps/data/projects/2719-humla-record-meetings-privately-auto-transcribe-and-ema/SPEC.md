# Humla – Record meetings privately, auto-transcribe, and email summaries

## Problem

Humla is a free, open-source Mac app that records both sides of your calls without a meeting bot, transcribes live on device, and turns notes into ready-to-send recaps. Pick presets for client emails or internal action lists, regenerate in one click, and copy straight to email. Humla lets you chat across your meeting history with offline models via Ollama, providing answers with citations that jump to the source. Keep everything on your Mac with local Whisper, or sync workspaces with Humla Cloud or a self-hosted server, while keys stay in the macOS Keychain. View startup
