# SPEC.md — Kobra – a Shadcn alternative focused on design quality

## Problem

I feel like especially post-AI, most agents default to the same Next.js &#x2F; Tailwind &#x2F; shadcn stack. It’s great for getting something working fast, but the end result often looks and feels the same and has almost become synonymous with AI slop atp. And when your landing page looks like it was one-shotted at 2am, ppl will assume that quality translates over to your product too.<p>Kobra is my attempt at fixing that, it&#x27;s a 1:1 replacement for shadcn&#x2F;ui and also uses the new shadcn&#x2F;lint library to help your agents maintain consistency. Heavy emphasis on stronger visual defaults and delightful interactions w&#x2F; motion.
