# Evant – Boost RSVPs and attendance with two-way SMS for your community

## Problem

Evant is a community SMS platform that helps organizers drive RSVPs, manage events, and keep members engaged. You can send two-way, targeted messages, schedule reminders, and let people RSVP by text or link with no app required. Use a dedicated 10-digit number, build segmented lists, and check people in at the door. Evant connects to your CRM and provides analytics on signups, attendance, and deliverability so you can act on data. Run text-to-donate campaigns, automate onboarding flows, and coordinate teams from a web dashboard built for nonprofits, schools, and community groups. View startup
