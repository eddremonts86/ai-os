# Radial Roots – Build archival circular family trees with rich data and print-ready exports

## Problem

Radial Roots lets you create circular family trees spanning up to ten generations with detailed context. You can map life dates, migration routes, professions, and DNA haplogroups directly onto elegant concentric rings. Generate large, print-ready PDFs, pure vector files, or high-resolution images that keep clarity at any size. The app runs fully in your browser to keep your genealogy private, with a simple flat fee to unlock premium export formats and optional theme upgrades. View startup
