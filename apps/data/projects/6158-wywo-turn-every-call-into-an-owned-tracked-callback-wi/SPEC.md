# SPEC.md — WYWO – Turn every call into an owned, tracked callback with AI catch-up

## Problem

WYWO helps phone-driven small teams capture every message, assign callbacks, and follow through without the bloat of legacy CRMs. It logs calls in seconds or ingests messages from missed calls, web forms, and AI receptionists, then routes ownership with SLAs and reminders until done. Teams get an AI catch-up each morning that summarizes what happened, flags overdue items, and answers questions. Flat pricing and optional add-ons keep it simple for growing businesses. View startup
