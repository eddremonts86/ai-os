# Unrush – Unrush is a context-aware adaptive sound app for easier mindful living

## Problem

Unrush offers adaptive sound journeys and continuous music flows for focus, calm, recovery, movement, and sleep. It doesn't focus on searching for music but on serving your intention. By reading signals such as heart rate, HRV, weather, light, and daily rhythms, Unrush recommends sounds that better match your body, mind, and environment. Available on iOS, you can listen hands-free as your day evolves. View startup
