# SPEC.md — Churn Solution – Cancel flows, payment recovery, and win-backs that protect your MRR

## Problem

Churn Solution helps subscription businesses reduce churn and grow recurring revenue. It offers cancellation flows with segmentation and dynamic offers, dunning email sequences to recover failed payments, and win-back campaigns via email and SMS. Teams can track churn, cohorts, and revenue forecasts and use AI to categorize cancellation feedback and reveal patterns. It also monitors chargebacks and speeds resolution to help retain more customers and protect MRR. View startup
