# Free lifetime pro access for limited time to crunchbase alternative

> Auto-generated product brief. Review and refine before MVP scoping.

## Value Proposition

Deep-tech funding and competitor research that an AI pipeline assembles from public sources, browsable by sector, with the early cohort holding lifetime Pro access that is grandfathered into whatever Pro becomes. The pitch is not "cheaper than Crunchbase" — it is that the research is community-corrected and the price of being early is permanent.

## Target Users

| Stakeholder | Why they care |
|---|---|
| Founder or operator in a deep-tech sector | Needs funding history and competitor sets on AI, biotech, cleantech or quantum companies without a per-seat research subscription. |
| Indie analyst / newsletter writer | Publishes about startup sectors weekly and needs a citable profile they can link to, not a paywalled record. |
| Niche contributor | Knows one sector better than the AI does; correcting a profile earns standing and the lifetime Pro grant. |
| The directory itself | Every accepted correction raises the profile quality that the next visitor arrives to — the flywheel the giveaway is trying to start. |

## Jobs To Be Done

1. **Functional job** — Look up a startup's funding, financials, competitors and team without hitting a paywall or a sales call.
2. **Emotional job** — Stop being the person who cannot check a claim because the source costs more than the answer is worth.
3. **Social job** — Cite a public profile in a post or a memo and have the reader able to open it.

## Success Metrics

- **Grant redemption:** share of the capped lifetime-Pro codes actually redeemed during the limited-time window — the campaign's only honest measure of reach.
- **Retention past the grant:** share of lifetime-Pro accounts that return in month two and month three. A redeemed grant nobody uses proves the giveaway travelled, not that the product works.
- **Contribution rate:** accepted profile edits per hundred active accounts. Below a floor, "community-driven" is a claim, not a mechanism.
- **Profile depth:** share of listed companies whose profile carries a funding round, a competitor set and a team entry, each with a source link.
- **Paid conversion after the window:** Pro subscriptions started by accounts that arrived after the giveaway closed. This is the number that says whether the free cohort seeded a business or replaced it.

## Pricing & Monetization

The source names no price for Pro. It states only that lifetime Pro subscriptions are being given away free for a limited time, with grandfathered access to future Pro features. Any subscription figure would be invented, so the pricing model is an open question, constrained by the grandfathering promise: post-campaign Pro pricing has to stand on its own against a cohort that will never pay it.

## Competitive Landscape

- **Crunchbase** — the named comparison. Established dataset and API, priced per seat, which is exactly the barrier this launch is built against.
- **PitchBook / CB Insights** — enterprise research subscriptions; deeper private-market data, entirely out of reach at indie prices.
- **Wikipedia and company sites** — free and citable, but not comparable across a sector and never structured as funding records.
- **Sector newsletters and hand-kept lists** — where the target users actually get this today; current, opinionated, unqueryable.

## Risks & Open Questions

- [ ] Establish whether AI-assembled funding figures are accurate enough to publish without human review, and what the correction latency is when they are not.
- [ ] Cap the lifetime-grant count and cost out serving a Pro account for years before the campaign runs.
- [ ] Confirm which public sources permit derived company profiles, since re-hosting a competitor's dataset would end the project rather than launch it.
- [ ] The Show HN post had 1 point and no comments at capture; demand is unvalidated. Decide what signal, other than redemptions, would justify continuing.
- [ ] Decide how a Pro feature can ever be priced separately without breaking the grandfathering promise made in public.
