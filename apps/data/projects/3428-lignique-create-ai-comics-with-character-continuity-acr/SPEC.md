# Lignique – Create AI comics with character continuity across panels

## Problem

Lignique is a continuity-first AI studio for comics, storyboards, and graphic novels. Creators lock reusable characters, styles, backgrounds, and props, then carry them across frames without rebuilding context in every prompt. Targeted reference-based AI editing lets them revise areas instead of regenerating entire images. From concept to final export, Lignique turns one-off AI generation into a controlled sequential workflow, reducing repetition, rework, and production time while keeping creators in control.
