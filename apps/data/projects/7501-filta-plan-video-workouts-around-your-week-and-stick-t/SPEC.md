# SPEC.md — Filta – Plan video workouts around your week and stick to them

## Problem

Filta helps you turn the workout videos you already love into a plan you'll follow. It connects exercise content to your calendar, sets weekly targets based on your goals, and schedules sessions around your availability. As you complete or skip workouts, Filta learns and adjusts to make the plan more realistic. Ask for quick sessions like "I have 25 minutes" and get something that fits. Start with a free week, then continue for a low monthly price. View startup
