# SPEC.md — JobTune – Score and tailor your resume to any job posting in seconds

## Problem

JobTune helps job seekers get past ATS filters by scoring a resume against any job posting and pinpointing missing keywords. It works as a Chrome extension alongside LinkedIn, Indeed, Glassdoor, and career pages, extracting the role and requirements with one click. The tool rewrites bullets using only your real experience, highlights relevant skills to add, and exports tailored resumes and cover letters. Start free with five tunes and keep your data on your device. View startup
