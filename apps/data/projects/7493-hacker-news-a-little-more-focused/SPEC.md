# SPEC.md — Hacker News, a little more focused

## Problem

I love AI and LLMs as much as the next person, but sometimes I just want to browse stories about, say programming. So I built this for myself and figured I will share it here as well.<p>Each story gets up to three tags, and I can use them to filter down the top stories to just the topics I am interested in. The tagging isn’t perfect. For example, some tags have a lot of overlap, like AI&#x2F;ML and LLMs. But it works well enough for me and since it’s a side project, it doesn’t have to be perfect :)
