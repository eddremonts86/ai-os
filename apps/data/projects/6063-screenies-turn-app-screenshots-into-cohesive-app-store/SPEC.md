# SPEC.md — Screenies – Turn app screenshots into cohesive App Store sets in minutes

## Problem

Screenies helps iOS teams create polished, on-brand App Store screenshot sets from their existing screenshots and a short app description. It restyles each image into a visual story aligned with your features and brand, then exports the full set at the exact 6.9-inch iPhone resolution required by App Store Connect. Use credits via subscription or top-ups, make low-cost edits and localizations, and prepare launches faster without design templates or manual resizing. View startup
