## Value Proposition

_Based on source brief:_ I play chess casually and usually struggle to figure out why Lichess analysis say my move is a blunder. Almost all the analysis tools will just suggest the best variant for a position, and you will have to follow the suggested engine lines, or play them out manually to analyze it better.<p>So I built this tool to make that process easier for beginner players. Behind the scene: This app run Stockfish in your browser, at a position, the app will follow the engine&#x27;s suggested lines, search for any meaningful situation like a check, a fork, a capture, material change, positional disadvantages,... and point that out with an explanation.<p>You can see the app here: <a href="https:&#x2F;&#x2F;whyblunder.vercel.app" rel="nofollow">https:&#x2F;&#x2F;whyblunder.vercel.app</a> and paste the PGN of your game.<p>If you play on Lichess, you can also replace the lichess.org part in the browser URl with https:&#x2F;&#x2F;whyblunder.vercel.app&#x2F;#&lt;game-id&gt; to quickly analyze, for example: <a href="https:&#x2F;&#x2F;whyblunder.vercel.app&#x2F;#cBVQFX8l" rel="nofollow">https:&#x2F;&#x2F;whyblunder.vercel.app&#x2F;#cBVQFX8l</a>
