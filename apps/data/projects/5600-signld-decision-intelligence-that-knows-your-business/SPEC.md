# SPEC.md — SIGNLD – Decision intelligence that knows your business with 800+ data connections

## Problem

Point AI at your business data and it hallucinates. SIGNLD builds a Knowledge Graph across everything you connect, with 800+ integrations from QuickBooks and Salesforce to SAP, Snowflake, Gmail, and Slack. Every answer reads from real data instead of guessing, traceable to the exact row and system. It runs in your own AWS environment, so your data never leaves your tenant. View startup
