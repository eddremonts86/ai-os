# Automating Tilda landing page creation for webinars — design direction

Quiet, professional, programme-first. The marketer is here to publish a webinar, not to design a brand. The brief form is a single column with clear section headings and a "Save draft" button at the bottom. The speaker library is a grid of cards with a placeholder photo and a "Replace" button. The preview pane is a side-by-side render of the Tilda page as it will appear, with a "Publish" button at the top right. The dashboard is a list of upcoming webinars with a colour-coded status (draft, ready, published). The published page itself is the Tilda template — the platform does not add its own branding on top of the published page.
