## Value Proposition

_Based on source brief:_ MindVault is a desktop app that connects Muse 2, Muse S, and Muse S Athena headsets to Windows, macOS, and Linux over Bluetooth. It streams four EEG channels at 256 Hz with millisecond timestamps, shows live band power and spectrograms, and records locally. You can export CSV or EDF for Python, MNE, or EEGLAB, or pipe data via LSL and OSC with an HTTP API for experiments. Researchers, builders, and educators can run real-time sessions without a phone in the loop. View startup
