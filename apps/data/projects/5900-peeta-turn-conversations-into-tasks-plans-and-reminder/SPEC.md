# SPEC.md — Peeta – Turn conversations into tasks, plans, and reminders across your life

## Problem

Less to handle, more time for you. Peeta is your everyday AI assistant working with you, your family, and friends together. It can automatically turn emails into calendar events and tasks, organize your day, and help keep important things from slipping through. It also supports multiple people working together inside Peeta’s native group chats, where the AI helps coordinate plans, tasks, and follow-ups for everyone. View startup
