# ego lite – Let your AI agents drive your logged-in browser to automate the web

## Problem

ego lite is a Chromium-based browser designed for AI agents to run web automation using your real, logged-in sessions. It shares your Chrome data in one click and provides Spaces so agents work in separate workspaces without touching your tabs. Through the ego-browser skill, any agent that can run code—like Claude Code, Codex, or Cursor—can snapshot, click, fill, and navigate pages in one pass, finishing complex tasks faster and with fewer tokens while keeping data local on your machine.
