# SPEC.md — Nearbase – Affordable managed PostgreSQL hosting in Asia

## Problem

Nearbase provides fully managed PostgreSQL hosting across local data centers in Asia and the Middle East. It delivers low latency, in-country data residency, and transparent pricing while handling provisioning, backups, patching, and failover. Teams can scale CPU, memory, and storage independently with zero downtime and benefit from encryption, enterprise-grade isolation, and a 99.99% SLA. Choose from 10+ regions to keep your database close to users and meet regional compliance requirements. View startup
