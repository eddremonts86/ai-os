# toca.la - song writer's companion app — Design direction

Like a songwriter's notebook, not a DAW.

- Surface: lyric editor + section list + audio attachments.
- Color: ink + accent for "saved."
- Typography: serif for lyrics, monospace for sections.
- No "AI lyrics" copy.
