# The systemic crisis of athlete transition

> Auto-generated product brief. Review and refine before MVP scoping.

## Value Proposition

A US athlete lands a meaningful post-playing role within 12 months of retirement, with a civilian-ready resume, an interview narrative, an employer marketplace that values athlete traits, and a peer cohort that understands the transition.

## Target Users

| Stakeholder | Why they care |
|---|---|
| US professional athlete retiring in 6–24 months | Has the savings question and the identity question; needs the plan and the network. |
| US collegiate athlete in senior year | Wants a head start on the civilian career before the playing career ends. |
| US employer who wants to hire athletes | Has the role and the culture fit; needs a structured way to find and onboard athlete hires. |

## Jobs To Be Done

1. **Functional job** — Land a meaningful post-playing role with sustained income.
2. **Emotional job** — Process the identity shift from athlete to civilian without it being a crisis.
3. **Social job** — Be part of a peer community that gets it, instead of isolating after the locker room empties.

## Success Metrics

- Placed-in-meaningful-role rate ≥ 70% within 12 months of retirement for engaged athletes.
- Cohort completion rate ≥ 80%.
- Employer NPS ≥ 50 from companies that hired through the marketplace.
- Athlete NPS ≥ 60 at month 6.

## Pricing & Monetization

Free tier: skills translation + resume builder. Athlete Pro ($29/month or $290/year): interview narrative builder, cohort community, financial runway planner. Employer Pro ($499/role): marketplace listing + structured athlete-fit scoring.

## Competitive Landscape

- NFL Player Engagement / NBA Cares / MLB Players Alumni — league-run, capacity-bound, sport-specific.
- General career platforms (LinkedIn, Indeed) — no athlete-specific translation.
- Athletes-for-Hire type agencies — bespoke, expensive, no peer community.

## Risks & Open Questions

- [ ] Mental-health exposure — Mitigation: licensed facilitator in every cohort; referral flow; explicit crisis resources.
- [ ] NCAA compliance — Mitigation: no payment to athletes; NIL-neutral curriculum; legal review of cohort content.
- [ ] Placement claims risk — Mitigation: explicit 'outcomes depend on engagement' disclaimer; aggregate stats, no individual promises.
