# No access to mentors from real experts to start in the real estate field

> Auto-generated product brief. Review and refine before MVP scoping.

## Value Proposition

Replaces the expensive 1:1 advisory or low-signal free content with affordable, vetted, topic-routed 1:1 mentorship sessions with real Indian real-estate experts.

## Target Users

Indians starting in real estate (first-home buyers, aspiring agents, first-time investors); Indian real-estate experts wanting paid mentorship.

## Jobs To Be Done

When I am about to make my first real-estate decision, I want a 1:1 session with a vetted expert in that specific topic so I do not have to learn by making mistakes. When I finish the session, I want structured notes I can refer back to.

## Success Metrics

Active mentees per month. Sessions completed per month. Expert-side retention at 3 / 6 months. Mentee-side repeat-session rate.

## Competitive Landscape

Topmate and Maven are general mentorship platforms, not India-real-estate-tuned. Local Indian real-estate advisory is relationship-based, not platform-based. No source-named direct competitor combining Indian-real-estate expertise with affordable 1:1 mentorship.

## Risks & Open Questions

Expert vetting is the gating item — false experts destroy trust quickly. Mentee-side churn is high for educational services. Expert-side supply is bounded. Need 15 expert and 15 mentee interviews before claims about session quality are credible.
