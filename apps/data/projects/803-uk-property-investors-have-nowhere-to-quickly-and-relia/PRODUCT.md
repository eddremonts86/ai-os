# UK property investors have nowhere to quickly and reliably assess refurbishment costs and find verified contractors, leading to weeks of delays and financial risks.

> Auto-generated product brief. Review and refine before MVP scoping.

## Value Proposition

A UK property investor enters a property's specs and gets a defensible refurbishment cost range plus a vetted 3–5 contractor shortlist — in one workflow, before they commit to a deal or pay for a Quantity Surveyor.

## Target Users

| Stakeholder | Why they care |
|---|---|
| Individual UK property investors (1–10 refurbs/yr) | Need a fast cost band + contractor shortlist per deal without paying a QS every time. |
| Buying agents / sourcing teams | Screen 20–50 deals/month and want a consistent first-pass cost signal. |
| Small-portfolio landlords | Already pay for a QS but want a structured spec to hand contractors so quotes come back apples-to-apples. |

## Jobs To Be Done

1. **Functional job** — Convert a property's specs into a defensible refurbishment cost range and a vetted contractor shortlist.
2. **Emotional job** — Stop the recurring worry that an under-scoped refurb will blow up the deal's margin.
3. **Social job** — Show a buying agent or JV partner that a deal has been costed, not just sourced.

## Success Metrics

- **Activation:** estimator returns a cost range for ≥ 80% of property-spec inputs without manual fallback.
- **Conversion:** ≥ 1 contractor shortlist generated per active investor per month.
- **Quote quality:** ≥ 60% of contractor RFQs sent through the platform return quotes within 7 days.
- **Vetting precision:** < 10% of contractors on a shortlist flagged "did not pursue" by the investor within 90 days.

## Pricing & Monetization

Free for 3 estimator runs per month. £29/month for unlimited estimator runs + 5 vetted contractor RFQs. £99/month for unlimited RFQs + saved-deal workspace. No referral fee from contractors, no lead-gen mark-up.

## Competitive Landscape

- **Checkatrade + TrustATrader + Rated People** — general trade directories, not investor-specific, no cost estimator attached.
- **BCIS + Spons** — building-cost data feeds but not investor-facing products; investor has to interpret the numbers.
- **Quantity Surveyor firms** — bespoke, expensive, not viable for first-pass screening on a non-acquired deal.
- **Spreadsheets from mentors / podcasts** — work for one-off deals but not repeatable across a portfolio.

## Risks & Open Questions

- [ ] Validate whether the BCIS regional adjustment factor is the right baseline or whether investor-specific trade bands diverge materially.
- [ ] Confirm NICEIC / Gas Safe / FMB credential verification can be done from a public lookup or requires direct trade-body integration.
- [ ] Decide whether to charge contractors a placement fee (the marketplace temptation) and whether that compromises the "verified, not sponsored" stance.
