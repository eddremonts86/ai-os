# AirScore – Check any U.S. address for a 0–100 air quality and pollution score

## Problem

AirScore provides a simple 0–100 environmental score for any U.S. address by combining current air quality with long-term pollution and nearby environmental risks. It factors in PM2.5 levels, proximity to Superfund sites, EPA TRI facilities, airports, and highways, helping homebuyers, renters, and homeowners uncover environmental context they might otherwise miss before choosing where to live. AirScore Pro adds shareable reports, side-by-side address comparisons, and full nearby site lists. View startup
