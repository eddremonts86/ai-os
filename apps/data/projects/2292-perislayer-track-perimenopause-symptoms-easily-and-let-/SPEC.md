# PeriSlayer – Track perimenopause symptoms easily and let AI find patterns

## Problem

Perimenopause can bring many symptoms at once, like hot flashes, broken sleep, mood swings, brain fog, and irregular cycles. It can be hard to know if they're connected. PeriSlayer helps you figure that out. You log symptoms in a few taps, and the app spots patterns over time, such as bad sleep showing up two days before mood dips. It turns "am I losing it?" into data you can share with your doctor. It also includes a library of evidence-based articles, a 60-second symptom quiz, an anonymous community, and Pilates sessions for this life stage. Built by women in their 40s who were tired of being told it was just stress. The iOS beta launches the week of August 23. View startup
