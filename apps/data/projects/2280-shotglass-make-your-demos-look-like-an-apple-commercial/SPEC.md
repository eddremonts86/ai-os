# ShotGlass – Make your demos look like an Apple commercial

## Problem

ShotGlass is a screen recorder for Mac that makes cinematic demos automatically. You just click record, do your thing, and then you can make it 3D in the editor or enjoy smooth auto zoom. It records your screen or multiple windows, takes and annotates screenshots, and lets you drop recordings onto a virtual 3D MacBook with a simulated camera lens. It is a one-time purchase and runs completely locally. View startup
