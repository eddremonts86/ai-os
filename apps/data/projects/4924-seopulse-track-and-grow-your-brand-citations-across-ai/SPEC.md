# SEOPulse – Track and grow your brand citations across AI search engines

## Problem

SEOPulse is an AI search visibility platform that shows where your brand is cited across engines like ChatGPT, Gemini, and Perplexity. It crawls live AI responses, maps entity-level authority, and monitors citation share by region and language so you can spot gaps and win recommendations. Marketing teams use its capture-to-optimize workflow to find missing mentions, simulate prompts, and strengthen external signals to drive more AI citations and qualified demand. View startup
