# SPEC.md — Vibestocks – Discover stocks through your interests with personalized AI summaries

## Problem

Vibestocks helps you discover and follow companies through your interests. Build a watchlist, get a personalized market feed, and read AI-driven summaries that match your investing level. Instead of just showing prices, Vibestocks explains what’s moving stocks and why, highlighting key topics like AI, clean energy, and rates. The platform adapts to you across desktop and mobile, keeping your daily brief focused on the companies and themes you care about. View startup
