# SPEC.md — Rush Loop – Play a one-tap rhythm reflex game and chase global high scores

## Problem

Rush Loop is a minimalist one-tap reflex game that challenges you to lock a marker in the green zone as the speed ramps up. Build streaks, climb global leaderboards, and complete daily challenges to earn coins and unlock themes, badges, and season pass rewards. Push your best run to 500 points and beyond, track achievements, and compete in streak ranks. Jump into quick sessions or grind levels to perfect your timing and dominate the charts. View startup
