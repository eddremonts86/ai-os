# Search for personal business niche considering psychological barriers

> Product brief authored from the source title and category. The poster's text was not available (source.name: manual); sections below re-state the problem and infer only what the title and category support.

## Value Proposition

A Russian-speaking founder who has stalled between two or three niches gets a profile + niche pairing that names the specific psychological barrier and offers one small, verifiable action per day for the first 7 days - instead of another scorecard.

## Target Users

- Russian-speaking solo founders who have considered 2-4 niches and not started any.
- Career-changers in Russia/CIS in their 30s who want a niche that fits their life, not a YC deck.
- Russian-speaking creators and consultants who have moved from idea to 'almost started' repeatedly.

## Jobs To Be Done

1. **Functional job** - Pick a niche this month and start, not 'eventually'.
2. **Emotional job** - Stop feeling that the barrier is invisible and nameless.
3. **Social job** - Have a small, named commitment to share with a partner, not a 30-page plan.

## Success Metrics

- **Activation:** profile + first niche pair saved within 45 minutes of signup.
- **Adherence:** >= 50% of users complete 5+ of the first 7 micro-actions.
- **Self-reported start:** >= 30% of users self-report having 'taken the first concrete step' on the chosen niche within 30 days.

## Competitive Landscape

- **Generic business-niche tools** - operate on demand and capital, not on psychological profile.
- **Career coaches (Russian market)** - expensive, slow, not data-driven.
- **Mindset / CBT apps (Youper, Woebot)** - clinical-feeling; do not adapt to a business-niche context.
