# Holp – Customer service AI agent that answers instantly across channels and in any

## Problem

Holp is a customer service AI agent that answers customer questions instantly across channels and languages. It learns from your website and uploaded documents, builds a knowledge base in minutes, and replies in your tone of voice via a shareable Holp link or embed. Teams reduce repetitive inquiries while customers get 24/7 accurate answers. Configure branding, add content, and approve responses to keep control. View startup
