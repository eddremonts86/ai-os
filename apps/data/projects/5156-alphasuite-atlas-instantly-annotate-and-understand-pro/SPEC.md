# AlphaSuite Atlas – Instantly annotate and understand protein structures

## Problem

AlphaSuite Atlas turns any protein structure into a fully annotated, interactive model in about 15 seconds. Domains, motifs, secondary structures, active and binding sites, ligands, and cofactors are labeled automatically, with a plain-language function summary and image/file exports. You can choose from over 11 million structures searchable by name, UniProt ID, PDB code, disease, pathway, and plain language. View startup
