# Looking to Sell my AI SaaS: $1.8K revenue in 3.5 months, <$45/month in costs

> Product brief for the BYOK X/Twitter growth tool documented in the source acquisition listing. The poster is selling; the plan here is the underlying product spec.

## Value Proposition

A solo founder or creator can run X/Twitter as a primary lead-generation channel without paying for AI inference on every reply or post they draft. The customer's own API key covers inference; the operator's only cost is hosting, email, payments, and the domain.

## Target Users

| Stakeholder | Why they care |
|---|---|
| Indie founders | Spend 1-2 hours/day on X replies; cannot afford a $300/month VA to do it. |
| Agency operators | Manage 5-15 client accounts but want their clients to each bring their own AI key. |
| Personal-brand creators | Want a writing-style profile that learns their voice across replies and posts. |
| Growth consultants | Need to monitor keywords and watchlists without paying per-inference to a vendor. |

## Jobs To Be Done

1. **Functional job** — Draft a contextual reply to a tweet in the customer's voice in under 30 seconds.
2. **Functional job** — Generate a weekly queue of original post drafts on a topic.
3. **Emotional job** — Stop feeling like the X grind is the only channel that works.
4. **Social job** — Look responsive and on-brand without spending all day in the app.

## Success Metrics

- **Activation:** first reply drafted and pasted into the X composer within 7 days of install.
- **Retention:** at least 3 replies or posts drafted per active user per week.
- **Unit economics:** operator cost ≤ $45/month for ≤ 1,000 active users (BYOK means inference cost does not scale with customers).
- **Customer satisfaction:** the source reports "overwhelmingly positive" feedback; the plan commits to NPS ≥ 40.

## Pricing & Monetization

Lifetime-deal pricing in the source: $80-100 average order value. No recurring revenue yet. A new operator launching a fresh build should default to $19/month recurring or $99 lifetime with a $39/month upgrade for advanced features (custom style training, watchlist digests).

## Competitive Landscape

- **TweetHunter / Hypefury** — subscription-based, they pay inference; higher customer LTV but higher operator cost.
- **Typefully** — editorial workflow, weaker on reply drafting.
- **Manual + ChatGPT** — what most founders do today; the BYOK positioning wins against the workflow tax of copying a tweet into ChatGPT.
- **Taplio / TweetStorm** — agency-tier, multi-account, more expensive.

## Risks & Open Questions

- [ ] BYOK means the customer can churn by canceling their AI subscription; a usage-metered add-on (the operator pays for inference on a credit-pack basis) is the obvious hedge but the source rejected it.
- [ ] X platform policy shifts (rate limits, API pricing) can break the reply-drafting UX overnight; a "browser-extension fallback" is the mitigation.
- [ ] Acquisition price ($6,000 in the source) is below the 12-month gross margin implied by $1,800 / 3.5 months — a rebuild has a steeper payback than the existing product.
