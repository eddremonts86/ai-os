# TaqFlow – Move B2B funds across Central Asia with live FX and next-day settlement

## Problem

TaqFlow enables businesses to send, receive, and settle cross-border B2B payments across Central Asia and the Caucasus with live FX, transparent fees, and next-day settlement. Every transaction settles on a distributed ledger for real-time verification and lower costs. Built-in KYB, dual approvals, and audit trails support compliance. You can hold multi-currency balances, lock rates, and manage treasury across 12 currencies and eight markets. Bank-grade security, two-factor authentication, and segregated client accounts protect every payment. View startup
