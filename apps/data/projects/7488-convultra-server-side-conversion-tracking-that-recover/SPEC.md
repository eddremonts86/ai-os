# SPEC.md — Convultra – Server-side conversion tracking that recovers sales lost by pixels

## Problem

Convultra is a server-side conversion tracking platform for marketing agencies and in-house teams. It records conversions on the server instead of the browser, so ad blockers, cookie loss, and iOS restrictions don't affect your data. It forwards conversions back to Google Ads with real order values attached. The dashboard combines ad spend and revenue in one place, showing revenue by source and campaign, ROAS and CPA per channel, and markers on charts to highlight performance changes. Convultra also tracks referrals from ChatGPT, Claude, and Perplexity, letting you see the value of AI-driven traffic. View startup
