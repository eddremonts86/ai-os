# Time tracking for solo consultants that ends in a PDF invoice

## Value Proposition

Time tracking that ends where the consultant actually gets paid. Most timers stop at a total; this one's stated difference is that the tracked hours terminate in a PDF invoice, so the loop from clock to billable document closes inside one tool. For a solo consultant that loop is the whole business rhythm — and the capture's demo URL says the product can show it working.

**One-liner:** Time tracking for solo consultants that ends in a PDF invoice.

## Target Users

| Stakeholder | Why they care |
|---|---|
| Hourly solo consultants | The invoice is the deliverable; a tracker that stops before it doubles their work. |
| Freelance developers | Deep-focus sessions need a frictionless timer, and clients expect a PDF. |
| Coaches and designers | Non-technical pros who want the invoice to look clean without tooling overhead. |
| Month-end billers | People reconstructing timesheets from memory need the ledger, not just a stopwatch. |

## Jobs To Be Done

1. Start a timer when work begins without breaking flow.
2. End the month with a complete, trustworthy hours ledger.
3. Generate a PDF invoice from tracked time in one step.
4. Keep each client's rate and history in one place.

## Success Metrics

- Timers started per active user per week: the core habit signal.
- Share of tracked sessions that end up in an exported invoice.
- Time from opening the tool to a generated PDF invoice on a fresh demo run.
- Invoices generated per user per month.
