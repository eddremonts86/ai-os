# Founder POV

> Auto-enriched product brief.

## Value Proposition

A 30-question security self-audit for small SaaS founders, returning a prioritised remediation list without an enterprise price tag.

## Target Users

- Solo SaaS founders with no security background
- Small SaaS teams of 2-5 without a dedicated security person
- Founders worried about being "poked at" but unsure what to fix first

## Jobs To Be Done

When I am running a small SaaS and worried about being "poked at," I want a self-audit that tells me what to fix today vs. this quarter, so I do not either ignore security or panic-buy an enterprise tool.

## Success Metrics

- At least 200 audits completed in 90 days
- Self-reported "fixed at least one item this week" rate above 50%
