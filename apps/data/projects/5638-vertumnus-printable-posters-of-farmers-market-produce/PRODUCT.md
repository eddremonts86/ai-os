## Value Proposition

_Based on source brief:_ The origin of this project was me, as a casual farmers&#x27; market attendee, grossly misremembering the start date of early girl tomatoes. Ironically, I still haven&#x27;t achieved that goal, because I couldn&#x27;t find a suitable source with varietal level granularity online. I&#x27;m half dreading a &quot;Why didn&#x27;t you check X?&quot; comment and discover there&#x27;s some perfect local varietal data source, but that would really be the best case scenario.<p>The poster is customizable per crop, and the pictures reposition heuristically. There&#x27;s only two regions, SF and NY, with very different source data for each.
