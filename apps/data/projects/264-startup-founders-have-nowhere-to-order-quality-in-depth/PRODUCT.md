# Startup founders have nowhere to order quality, in-depth research on specific projects or niches — existing services provide superficial and unreliable reports

> Auto-generated product brief. Review and refine before MVP scoping.

## Value Proposition

Replaces the shallow AI-only report with a human-led, deep, source-cited report that founders can actually cite in a board meeting.

## Target Users

USA startup founders making a key decision; VC associates doing thesis work; corporate-strategy teams at mid-stage startups.

## Jobs To Be Done

When I am about to commit engineering time to a niche, I want a 15-30 page report that names the actual players, the actual numbers, and the actual risks so I can decide. When the report arrives, I want to be able to email the researcher with follow-up questions for 30 days.

## Success Metrics

Report delivery on time at 5 business days (target: 90%). Customer-reported depth rating (target: 8/10 average). Repeat-customer rate at 6 months. Stripe revenue per month.

## Competitive Landscape

CB Insights, PitchBook are data aggregators, not custom reports. AlphaSights and GLG are expert networks. Generic research agencies (e.g. EDC) are expensive and slow. No source-named competitor that combines AI-assisted research with human delivery at this price point.

## Risks & Open Questions
