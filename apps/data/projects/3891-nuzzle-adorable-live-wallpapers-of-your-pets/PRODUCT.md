# Nuzzle – adorable live wallpapers of your pets

## Value Proposition

A phone lock screen that makes you smile every time you pick it up. Nuzzle's premise is a pet photo transformed into an animated live wallpaper, built because the author judged the existing iOS live wallpaper apps bad. The value is emotional and personal: your pet, alive on the screen, with as little friction as a photo app can manage.

**One-liner:** Adorable live wallpapers of your pets, on iOS.

## Target Users

| Stakeholder | Why they care |
|---|---|
| Pet owners | Their pet is the most personal image on the phone; a static photo underuses it. |
| Gift-givers | A personalized animated wallpaper is a cheap, charming present. |
| iOS personalization fans | They exhausted themes and icon packs and want the lock screen to feel alive. |
| The author's own benchmark | Existing live wallpaper apps were, in his words, not good — quality itself is the draw. |

## Jobs To Be Done

1. Turn one favorite pet photo into an animated wallpaper in a few taps.
2. Get the result onto the lock screen without fighting iOS wallpaper mechanics.
3. Feel the phone is personal, not off-the-shelf.
4. Re-make wallpapers easily as new pet photos pile up.

## Success Metrics

- Downloads and installs per week, the first honest signal for a solo iOS app.
- Wallpapers created per install: how often the core loop actually completes.
- Share rate of finished wallpapers, the strongest word-of-mouth lever.
- App Store rating and the share of reviews mentioning live wallpaper quality.

## Pricing & Monetization

None stated. The capture says nothing about price, trials or subscriptions.

## Competitive Landscape

The capture names no competitors, but the author states his motivation as the low quality of existing live wallpaper iOS apps, so the competitive frame is the whole category of iOS live wallpaper and personalization apps. Nuzzle's stated edge is quality, applied to the pet-photo angle.

## Risks & Open Questions

- [ ] The source is a single sentence; the MVP may not match what the author actually built.
- [ ] iOS restricts how live wallpapers behave (battery, length, triggers), and the app must work inside those limits.
- [ ] Animation effects can drain battery and invite uninstalls if not measured.
- [ ] Adorable is subjective; without a feedback loop the polish effort may miss.
- [ ] No stated monetization means no evidence the market will pay.
