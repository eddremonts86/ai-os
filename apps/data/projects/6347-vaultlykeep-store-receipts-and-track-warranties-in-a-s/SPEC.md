# SPEC.md — VaultlyKeep – Store receipts and track warranties in a secure, reminder-driven vault

## Problem

VaultlyKeep lets you capture, organize, and protect purchase records in a secure browser-based vault. Upload paper or digital receipts, set warranty durations, and get automatic reminders 30, 7, and 1 day before expiry. Search instantly by item, store, or category, track return windows, and attach manuals or service documents to each record. Share a household vault with up to five users, export clean PDFs, and generate insurance or resale reports to claim or sell with confidence. View startup
