# SPEC.md — 1Presence – The personal AI assistant that learns who you are and never forgets

## Problem

1Presence is an AI assistant that learns who you are, connects your entire digital life, and never forgets. It remembers your people, projects, and decisions, and you can open the memory to see what it knows. It accesses Gmail, Calendar, Notion, GitHub, and Microsoft 365, joins meetings, drafts in your voice, and lives in Slack as a teammate you can message. Your documents sit in a private vault that you control. It offers specialist agents, routines that run automatically, dashboards that stay current, and ready-made Kits that set up parts of your life. Nothing is sent or posted without your approval. View startup
