## Value Proposition

_Based on source brief:_ Prinn helps small teams use AI safely by catching sensitive data before it leaves their computers. It runs on each device, scans prompts to tools like ChatGPT, Claude, Copilot, Gemini, and Grok in milliseconds, and flags API keys, tokens, bank details, IDs, and more. Users can choose to redact, stop, or send, and can remember their choices. No prompts are stored or sent; records stay encrypted on your devices with minimal reporting for owners. Prinn targets teams without dedicated IT security. View startup
