# SPEC.md — SympathIQ – Book a verified therapist, coach, dietitian, or trainer online

## Problem

SympathIQ is a UK marketplace connecting people with verified therapists, coaches, dietitians, and fitness specialists in six categories under one account. Every specialist is credential-checked against a real professional body before taking clients. Pricing is pay-per-session with a flat platform fee and no subscription, so there's nothing owed in weeks you don't book. Browse verified profiles and book directly online, usually in under two minutes. View startup
