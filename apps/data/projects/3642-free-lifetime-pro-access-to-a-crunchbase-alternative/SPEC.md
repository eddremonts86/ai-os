# Free lifetime pro access to a crunchbase alternative

## Problem

The capture is a promotion, not a product description. The author's full statement is: a month ago we launched on Hacker News and reached the front page; as a thank you we are now offering free lifetime pro subscriptions to the HN community.

That is the entire capture. There is no product name beyond the front-page reference, no description of the "Crunchbase alternative" claim beyond the one word "alternative", no list of features, no stated audience, no pricing structure for the non-HN community, no link to the original launch, no screenshot, and no roadmap. The most that can be said about the product is that it is a paid product with a "pro" tier, positioned against Crunchbase, and that the team is offering lifetime pro access for free to Hacker News accounts as a thank-you for a previous front-page launch.

This plan refuses to invent the product. There is no honest way to write a Target Users section, an MVP Scope, a Tech Stack, an Architecture or a Success Metrics section without manufacturing a company-data product the source does not describe. The plan therefore stays as a draft. The Problem section above is the only section that can be written without fabrication. The Objective, Target Users, MVP Scope and Constraints sections below state explicitly what the source lacks and what would be needed to enrich them, so a future author can fill them in without re-reading the capture.

## Objective

_Cannot be written honestly from the capture._ The source does not name the product, does not describe what makes it a Crunchbase alternative rather than a directory or a CRM, does not state which data sources it uses (Crunchbase itself, public filings, scraping, paid data providers) and does not state who the paying customer is outside Hacker News. Any Objective written here would be inventing a company-data product that the post does not describe. This section is left as a draft, with the gap noted in the Constraints section.

_If enriched later_, the Objective will be a single paragraph stating the product's claim, the action it enables for the named customer, and the boundary at which the source's silence stops. The author should re-read the original launch thread and fill this section with what they actually built, not with what a Crunchbase alternative might look like in general.

_If enriched later_, the author should list each paying-customer segment with a one-line reason they care, and should be explicit about which segments the source already supports versus which segments are inferred from the Crunchbase alternative positioning. The inference must be marked as inference.
