# SPEC.md — Browse last quarter's Supreme Court cases, linked and cited as you read

## Problem

I made this as a means to browse through documents that are highly linked and referential in general, and thought it would be interesting to apply it to court cases. So this has the opinion, the arguments per person, the dissents, and concurrences all mapped out.<p>It also is linked to an LLM you can chat about them with if you click a linkage and it has a DAG of the knowledge of all the content of both pieces and their premises, and can discuss, debate, or do deeper research and opining.
