# SPEC.md — Breakdown: a daily game about guessing data distributions

## Problem

Breakdown is a daily game where you guess how some data is distributed. Today&#x27;s puzzle is &quot;what is the world&#x27;s habitable land used for.&quot; Make your best guess and then reveal the answer. You might be surprised by how far off you are (or how close).<p>There&#x27;s a new question every day, and you can also play all past questions in the archive.<p>No account required and no ads.<p>Inspired by the Ipsos Perils of Perception surveys, it&#x27;s also an attempt to gamify testing our knowledge and biases.<p>If this is interesting, you should also check out Perils of Perception:
