# Vocalify – Get live, cents-accurate pitch feedback while you sing in your browser

## Problem

Vocalify is a browser-based studio for singers that listens as you sing and shows your pitch against target notes in real time, measured in cents. Use built-in warm-ups, scales, sirens, and interval drills, or create your own melodies and practice with backing tracks. It scores every note, tracks progress over time, and adapts to your range. Train your ear with recognition and sing-back tests, replay takes, and sync history across devices. The tool is free to use, with an optional Pro plan for stem splitting and more storage.
