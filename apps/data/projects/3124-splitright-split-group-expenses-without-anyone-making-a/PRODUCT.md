# Splitright – split group expenses without anyone making an account

> Auto-generated product brief. Review and refine before MVP scoping.

## Value Proposition

Split group expenses where every participant sees their share via a link, with no signup required for anyone.

## Target Users

Friend groups, roommates, and travellers who abandon Splitwise-style apps because getting everyone to sign up is the friction.

## Jobs To Be Done

Track who owes what across a group, settle up, and share the result without onboarding every participant.

## Success Metrics

No metric is implied by the source.

## Pricing & Monetization

Not stated in the source.

## Competitive Landscape

Splitwise dominates the "everyone signs up" version of this idea. Splitright's stated differentiator is removing the signup step.

## Risks & Open Questions

The hard part is usually reminders and settlement; without accounts, the app needs a different model for chasing people who have not opened the link.
