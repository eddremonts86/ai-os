# Splitright – split group expenses without anyone making an account

## Problem

The Show HN post links to the App Store listing for Splitright but the scraper captured only the link. From the title alone, Splitright splits group expenses with no sign-up step for any participant.

## Objective

Let a group settle shared expenses (trips, shared flats, dinners) without forcing every participant to create an account.

## Target Users

Groups of friends, roommates, and travellers who want to settle up quickly without everyone installing and signing into yet another app.

## MVP Scope

A mobile app where one person creates an expense, adds others by name or contact, and shares a link each participant can tap to see what they owe — no signup required for the recipients.
