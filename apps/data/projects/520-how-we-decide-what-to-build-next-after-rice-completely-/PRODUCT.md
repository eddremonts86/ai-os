# How we decide what to build next after RICE completely fell apart for us

> Auto-generated product brief. Review and refine before MVP scoping.

## Value Proposition

A product team can rank initiatives against each other using inputs the team actually has (customer evidence, build cost confidence, strategic fit), see the reasoning per item, and produce an auditable backlog that survives a leadership challenge — without a 30-line spreadsheet.

## Target Users

| Stakeholder | Why they care |
|---|---|
| Product team (5-15) | RICE became guesswork; needs a framework that reflects what they actually know. |
| PM at a startup | Needs to defend the roadmap without theatrics. |
| Engineering lead | Wants to know the cost-confidence band before committing a sprint. |

## Jobs To Be Done

1. **Functional job** — Replace RICE without rebuilding the spreadsheet.
2. **Emotional job** — Stop dreading prioritization meetings because inputs were always contested.
3. **Social job** — Be able to send a link to a stakeholder instead of a deck.

## Success Metrics

- **Adoption:** ≥80% of initiatives in the next quarter's roadmap entered through the tool.
- **Audit trail:** 100% of weight or score changes show a reason in the audit log.
- **Stakeholder trust:** ≥3 stakeholder reviews of the public board with no "what about X?" follow-ups.

## Pricing & Monetization

Free for teams up to 10. $49/month per workspace for 11+ users. $199/month for SSO + audit-log export.

## Competitive Landscape

- **RICE / ICE / WSJF spreadsheets** — the thing the source explicitly says broke.
- **Productboard, Roadmunk** — powerful, expensive, built for enterprise roadmapping.
- **Trello / Linear / Jira** — flexible but no prioritization framework baked in.

## Risks & Open Questions

- [ ] Validate the three-dimension model against 3 product teams before launch.
- [ ] Decide whether weights should be PM-only or team-voted.
- [ ] Confirm whether the public board needs commenting in v1.
