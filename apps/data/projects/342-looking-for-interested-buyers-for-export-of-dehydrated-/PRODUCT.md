# Looking for interested buyers for export of dehydrated products from India

> Product brief authored from the source title and category. The poster's text was not available (source.name: manual); sections below re-state the problem and infer only what the title and category support.

## Value Proposition

An Indian dehydrator-exporter publishes a COA-backed listing once and starts receiving qualified importer inquiries in WhatsApp, with sample shipment and USD-escrow contract support, instead of depending on personal networks and trade fair calendars.

## Target Users

- Small and mid-sized Indian dehydrator-exporters with annual revenue $0.5-10M looking for global buyers.
- Food importers in the Middle East, Africa, EU and Southeast Asia who already buy dehydrated produce from non-Indian sources.
- Indian food-export trade bodies (APEDA, MPEDA) who want a single listing of vetted exporters.

## Jobs To Be Done

1. **Functional job** - Land a 20-foot container order from a verified importer without flying to a trade fair.
2. **Emotional job** - Stop worrying that the buyer profile is fake or speculative.
3. **Social job** - Be presentable as an Indian exporter to a buyer in Cairo or Dubai who already sources elsewhere.

## Success Metrics

- **Activation:** exporter publishes a verified listing within 14 days of signup.
- **Inquiry quality:** >= 30% of importers who send an inquiry move to a sample shipment within 30 days.
- **Conversion:** >= 5% of inquiries convert to a first contract within 90 days.

## Competitive Landscape

- **IndiaMART / TradeIndia** - domestic and B2C-heavy; not built for export sample/contract flows.
- **Alibaba** - global but Chinese-default; Indian-origin exporters compete with origin bias.
- **Personal networks + trade fairs** - what exporters use today; works but does not scale.
