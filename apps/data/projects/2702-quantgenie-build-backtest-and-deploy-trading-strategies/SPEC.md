# QuantGenie – Build, backtest, and deploy trading strategies from plain English

## Problem

QuantGenie lets you describe a trading idea in plain English and turns it into a deterministic, validated strategy you can backtest and deploy. It includes institutional-grade tick data for US equities and major futures, visual logic editing, and a validation suite with Monte Carlo, in/out-of-sample, walk-forward, and parameter surface analysis. Connect your broker with one click and run strategies server-side while monitoring performance and risk in a unified dashboard. View startup
