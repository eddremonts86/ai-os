# Obidos – self-hosted secrets storage and sharing with fine-grained ACLs

## Problem

Obidos is a self-hosted, open source web app for storing and sharing digital artifacts securely: passwords, 2FA QR Codes, documents, contracts, audio, video, or anything else that needs controlled access.Share with a colleague or a group, with fine-grained control over exactly who can access what.Integrates with AD/LDAP, SMTP, SMS, and SNMP for enterprise environments.
