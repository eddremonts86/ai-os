# Sowiks – Capture, annotate, and share screenshots as instant links

## Problem

Sowiks is a Mac screenshot and screen recording app that turns every capture into a shareable link. Take area, window, full-screen, and scrolling shots, then annotate with arrows, text, blur, step numbers, and more. Record video or GIF with camera, mic, and system audio, and trim before sharing. Use OCR to extract text, organize captures into collections, and sync to the cloud with optional passwords and expirations. Keyboard shortcuts keep you fast, while compression preserves quality and reduces file size. View startup
