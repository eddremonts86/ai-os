# SPEC.md — Signal Studio – Upload your brand kit and get 100+ on-brand ad banners in every size

## Problem

Most ad banners don't fail in design; they fail in production. Resizing one layout into 11 Meta placements, translating it for 5 markets, and rebuilding it every 39 days when the creative burns out uses hours better spent on offers and testing. Signal Studio takes your brand kit—logo, colors, fonts, product shots—and generates 100+ banners that look like your brand, not a template. Every placement size in one click. Same layout, new language, same day. Enough variants to run real creative tests instead of betting on three. Try it before signing up: the free generator on our site makes 3 banners from your brand kit, no account needed. Launching September 1. View startup
