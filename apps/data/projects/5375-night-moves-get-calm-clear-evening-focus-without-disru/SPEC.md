# SPEC.md — Night Moves – Get calm, clear evening focus without disrupting your sleep

## Problem

Night Moves is a caffeine-free focus supplement designed for afternoon and late-night work that sharpens clarity while protecting sleep. It uses a precise blend of L-theanine and L-tyrosine to promote steady focus, stress resilience, and alpha brainwave calm. Take one capsule about 20 minutes before you start, and enjoy productive flow without a jittery crash. The SleepSafe approach prioritizes ingredients that aid attention yet preserve sleep quality, so you can work late and still wake up rested. View startup
