# SPEC.md — SparkVid – Create AI videos with text and images using top models in one place

## Problem

SparkVid is an AI video maker that lets you turn text descriptions and images into polished videos using top models like Seedance, Kling, and Veo. It helps you compare outputs and keep faces, products, and styles consistent. You can direct motion with reference clips, control camera moves, and edit scenes by describing changes. SparkVid also generates and edits images, then sends frames into video, helping creators and marketers produce content faster with less guesswork. View startup
