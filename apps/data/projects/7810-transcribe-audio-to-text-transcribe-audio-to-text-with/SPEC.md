## Problem

Audio Converter AI is an AI-powered tool that helps you transcribe audio to text quickly and accurately. Upload an audio or video file and turn spoken content into editable text in minutes. It supports over 100 languages, speaker recognition, timestamps, and popular audio and video formats. Whether you need to transcribe meetings, interviews, podcasts, lectures, webinars, calls, or voice recordings, Audio Converter AI makes it easy to turn speech into searchable, readable, and reusable text.
