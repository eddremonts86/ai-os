# Republished: There is no app for Nigerian passengers that shows the real-time flight status and helps with transit, hotel, and the embassy line.

> Auto-generated product brief. Review and refine before MVP scoping.

## Value Proposition

Stop the 5-app patchwork for every Nigerian international trip. The first 100,000 active users pay for the service from the recovered time and the avoided trip mishaps.

## Target Users

Nigerian international passengers flying to or from Lagos, Abuja, and Port Harcourt. Secondary: Nigerian diaspora flying home and the diaspora-adjacent travellers (work, family, study).

## Jobs To Be Done

Functional: have a single app for the whole journey, not the patchwork. Emotional: stop the dread of the Lagos airport and the embassy line. Social: be the family whose members all use the same app for the trip.

## Success Metrics

At least 100,000 active users in 18 months. Real-time flight status accuracy above the airline's own app. Offline section usage above 60% of sessions. Affiliate-driven revenue per user above the regional baseline.

## Competitive Landscape

Google Flights, TripIt, and Hopper are global and not Nigeria-specific. Airline apps are single-airline. No mainstream service combines real-time flight status with destination airport transit, hotel, and visa/embassy rules for the Nigerian international passenger.

## Risks & Open Questions

AviationStack latency can produce stale status. Visa/embassy rules change frequently and must be human-curated. Hotel affiliate revenue is thin. Nigerian market requires careful payment-rail integration (Paystack).
