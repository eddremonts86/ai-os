# Norskalkulator – Calculate tax, salary, and savings in seconds for free

## Problem

Norskalkulator combines Norwegian calculators for tax, salary, loans, and savings. The online service lets you calculate everything from table tax and step tax to budgets, housing, and car costs with no login required. All tools use current rates and rules in Norway and are continuously updated. You can use them for free on mobile and PC with no personal data stored. View startup
