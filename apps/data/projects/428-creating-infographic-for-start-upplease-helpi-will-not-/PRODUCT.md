> Auto-enriched product brief.

## Value Proposition

A working, opinionated infographic starter that replaces the founder searching the web for "infographic example" and ending up with a Notion-canvas-style mess.

## Target Users

- Non-designer technical founders in their first 90 days post-launch
- Solo founders preparing for accelerator interviews
- Co-founders asked to handle marketing without prior design experience

## Jobs To Be Done

When I need a one-page visual that explains my startup, I want a starter layout and a checklist of what to put in each slot, so I can produce something credible in an afternoon without hiring a designer.

## Success Metrics

- At least 25 founders publish a finished infographic within 30 days of template release
- Median fill-in time under 90 minutes (self-reported)
- At least 1 in 5 shares the result publicly
