# SPEC.md — XLSX – Scan Excel files for issues and fix them in one click in your browser

## Problem

XLSX inspects Excel workbooks for hidden sheets, broken formulas, volatile functions, external links, and text stored as numbers. It locates issues down to exact cells without uploading your file. Free scans detect problems with precise addresses, while Pro adds one-click fixes like unhiding content, removing links, converting text to numbers, plus previews, CSV exports, and support for files up to 50MB.
