# SPEC.md — NoCaps – Build better habits with AI scoring, swaps, and a social marketplace

## Problem

NoCap is a habit intelligence platform based on one idea: you shouldn't have to figure out every good habit from scratch. You can quickly copy proven habit systems from friends, family, and a community marketplace. One tap gives you a complete HabitStack, ready to use immediately. NoCap's AI scores your habits, suggests smarter swaps, and generates personalized plans. Track in real units like USD, calories, hours, happiness, and more. Every social post ties to a HabitStack, so sharing leads to real results from people who've been where you are. View startup
