# Search for an AI tool to create high-quality and natural-looking animation

> Auto-generated product brief. Review and refine before MVP scoping.

## Value Proposition

An Indian creator or SMB produces a natural-looking 60-second explainer or social short in under an hour, with Indic lip-sync and broadcast-ready quality, at an INR price the market can sustain weekly.

## Target Users

| Stakeholder | Why they care |
|---|---|
| Indian creator | Produces explainer or social content weekly; needs an animation tool tuned for Indic voice and motion. |
| Indian SMB marketer | Wants a video ad for a product launch without hiring an animation studio. |
| Indian educator | Produces educational explainers in Hindi or a regional language. |

## Jobs To Be Done

1. **Functional job** — Produce a 60-second animation in under an hour with Indic lip-sync.
2. **Emotional job** — Stop the 'I cannot afford After Effects + an animator' bottleneck for short-form video.
3. **Social job** — Have a YouTube or Instagram reel that does not read as cheap AI animation.

## Success Metrics

- Time-to-60-second-animation median ≤ 60 minutes.
- Indic lip-sync panel review ≥ 4/5.
- Creator NPS ≥ 50 at month 3.
- Weekly active creator retention ≥ 60% after week 4.

## Pricing & Monetization

Free tier: 3 animations/month, 15-second max, watermarked. Creator Pro (INR 999/month): unlimited 60-second animations, no watermark, 5 Indic languages. Studio Pro (INR 4,999/month): unlimited length, custom brand voice, white-label export, agency seats.

## Competitive Landscape

- Runway / Pika / Sora — strong general motion, no Indic lip-sync, USD pricing, no Indian payment rails.
- After Effects + animator — high quality, expensive, slow.
- CapCut / Canva animation — template-driven, no AI motion, no Indic.

## Risks & Open Questions

- [ ] Indic lip-sync ceiling — Mitigation: per-language prompt tuning + human review for the first 50 outputs per language.
- [ ] Motion quality drift — Mitigation: deterministic scene breakdown before motion synthesis; panel review before render.
- [ ] Stereotype risk in cultural motifs — Mitigation: explicit anti-stereotype prompt set; cultural advisor on retainer.
