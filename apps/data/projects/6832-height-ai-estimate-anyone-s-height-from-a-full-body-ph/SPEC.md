# SPEC.md — Height AI – Estimate anyone's height from a full-body photo in seconds

## Problem

Height AI is an AI height estimator for iPhone that estimates a person's height from a full-body photo. It analyzes body proportions, perspective, and familiar reference objects such as doors or counters, then returns the result in feet and inches or centimeters. Use it to check a dating-profile claim, guess someone's height from a picture, settle a debate, or satisfy your curiosity without a measuring tape or awkward questions. Photos stay private and are processed only to generate the estimate, making measurement fast, simple, and discreet. View startup
