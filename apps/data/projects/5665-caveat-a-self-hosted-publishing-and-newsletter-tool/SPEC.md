# SPEC.md — Caveat, a self-hosted publishing and newsletter tool

## Problem

I’ve been working on Caveat, a self-hosted publishing tool for people who want control over their newsletter’s design, subscriber data, and infrastructure.<p>It includes a rich-text editor, autosave, reusable content blocks, publication designs, typography and colour controls, subscriber management, exports, and Resend email delivery. It includes both the editor and public website.<p>Repo: <a href="https:&#x2F;&#x2F;github.com&#x2F;CaveatJS&#x2F;site" rel="nofollow">https:&#x2F;&#x2F;github.com&#x2F;CaveatJS&#x2F;site</a>
Examples&#x2F;docs: <a href="https:&#x2F;&#x2F;caveat-home.vercel.app&#x2F;" rel="nofollow">https:&#x2F;&#x2F;caveat-home.vercel.app&#x2F;</a><p>I built the first version over a few hours across three sessions with GPT-6 Astra. I chose the features, tested the workflows, and refined the design and implementation.<p>The sessions processed roughly 43.6M tokens, about 97% cached input.<p>Caveat is still early: paid subscriptions, scheduling, and multi-author workflows are not implemented yet.<p>If you publish a newsletter, what part of your workflow would prevent you from using it today?
