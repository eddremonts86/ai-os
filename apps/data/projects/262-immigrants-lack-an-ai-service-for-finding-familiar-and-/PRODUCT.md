# Immigrants lack an AI service for finding familiar and verified places (pharmacies, doctors, banks) in a new country, causing daily stress and time loss

> Auto-generated product brief. Review and refine before MVP scoping.

## Value Proposition

A service that ranks pharmacies, doctors, and banks in Serbia by fit for a specific immigrant's situation — their origin country, languages, medical needs, and banking history — using evidence other immigrants have generated, not a star rating the platform invents. The immigrant finds a place that has served people like them, with an evidence tag that names the origin country and the date.

## Target Users

- Immigrants in Serbia who have just arrived and need to find pharmacies, doctors, and banks they can trust.
- Long-term immigrants in Serbia who need a place that handles a specific need (a prescription, a documentation type, a language).
- Diaspora networks and expat community organisations that would point newcomers to the service.
- Local pharmacies, doctors, and banks in Serbia that want a steady inbound flow from immigrant communities.

## Jobs To Be Done

- When I need a pharmacy that stocks a specific medicine I used back home, I want a list of nearby pharmacies that have served people from my country, so I do not walk into a random one and find out they do not carry it.
- When I need a doctor who speaks my language, I want a list of doctors who have confirmed language coverage, so I do not have to phone around to find out.
- When I need a bank that understands my documentation, I want a list of banks that have served immigrants with similar paperwork, so the visit does not turn into a documentation problem.
- When I am a local pharmacy and I serve immigrant customers regularly, I want my listing to show that fact, so new immigrants find me instead of a random pharmacy.

## Success Metrics

- Number of confirmed "I was helped here" tags per place per month (proxy for evidence accumulation).
- Number of searches per week per city (proxy for usage breadth).
- Confirmation rate: percentage of searches that lead to a "I was helped here" confirmation within 30 days (proxy for search-to-visit conversion).
- Repeat usage: percentage of immigrants who return for a second search within 60 days.

## Competitive Landscape

## Risks & Open Questions

- Validate problem with 5 immigrant interviews before MVP: confirm that the fit-for-immigrant ranking framing matches what they actually need, and that they would trust a confirmation-driven evidence tag over a star rating.
- Evidence honesty: a "served immigrants from [origin country]" tag the platform invents is a marketing surface, not evidence. The MVP must source the tag from a confirmation flow the immigrant triggers.
- Multilingual support is a real cost. The MVP must pick the pilot languages (Russian, Arabic, Chinese, English) and accept that other languages are not in scope at launch.
- Category and geographic coverage is finite. The MVP must be honest about what it covers (three categories, two cities) and not pretend to cover all of Serbia.
- Cold-start: a directory with no evidence tags is just a directory. The MVP must seed 30–50 places per category with operator-verified starter tags and let immigrant confirmations grow from there.
