# SPEC.md — FlewIt – A modern and complete flight logging app

## Problem

FlewIt gives travelers one place to build and explore a complete history of their flights. Users can log detailed flight information, organize flights into trips, visualize routes on interactive maps, and explore statistics across airlines, airports, aircraft, countries, distance, time flown, cabin class, and more. FlewIt adds achievements, badges, travel milestones, and other ways to rediscover your flying history instead of leaving it buried in spreadsheets, emails, or outdated flight loggers. View startup
