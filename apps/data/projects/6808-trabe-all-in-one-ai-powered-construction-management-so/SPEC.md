# SPEC.md — Trabe – All-in-one AI-powered construction management software

## Problem

Trabe integrates project, cost, and financial management for construction companies. It centralizes budgets, planning, purchasing, and real-time tracking, showing progress, actual costs, margins, and deviations. Its AI assistant answers questions about invoices, suppliers, and results, proposes plans, and processes invoices with OCR. Teams clock in and validate timesheets via WhatsApp and from the workers' app. Gain visibility into cash flow, forecasts, and results per project in a single system. View startup
