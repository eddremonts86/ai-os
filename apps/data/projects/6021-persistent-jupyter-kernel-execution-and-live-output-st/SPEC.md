# SPEC.md — Persistent Jupyter kernel execution and live output streaming in VSCode

## Problem

Hi HN, Every time I ran a training loop for a few days via Jupyter Notebook and ran into issues (network disconnected, my laptop suddenly died, etc.), I suffered from losing my ipykernel and cell outputs. I wanted to keep seeing my live outputs right inside the IDE without constantly checking W&amp;B or log files. It is such a hassle. That&#x27;s why I built Tithon. Let me know what you think!
