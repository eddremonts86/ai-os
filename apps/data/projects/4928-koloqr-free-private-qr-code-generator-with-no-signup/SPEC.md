# KoloQR – Free private QR code generator with no signup

## Problem

KoloQR is a free QR code generator that lets you create custom, print-ready codes for URLs, Wi-Fi, business cards, social profiles, payments, and more. You can design codes with branded colors, shapes, frames, and logos, then export them as PNG, SVG, EPS, or PDF. It runs in your browser for privacy and produces reliable, watermark-free static QR codes that you fully own. Built for designers and marketers, KoloQR focuses on high-quality outputs that scan well across digital and print uses. View startup
