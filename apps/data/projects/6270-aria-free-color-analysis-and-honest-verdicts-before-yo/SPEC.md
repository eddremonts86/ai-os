# SPEC.md — Aria – Free color analysis and honest verdicts before you buy

## Problem

Most people have a closet full of clothes they like but wear the same four items. Aria starts with a free 12-season color analysis, then learns your proportions and the pieces you already own. Photograph anything you're thinking of buying and get a straightforward buy or skip recommendation with reasons: what the piece will do on your body and how it fits with your closet. We don't take commissions, so you get honest answers. Free to start, no card needed. We're early and want feedback on what could be better. View startup
