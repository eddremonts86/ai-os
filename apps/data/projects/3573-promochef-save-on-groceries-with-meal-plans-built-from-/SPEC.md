# PromoChef – Save on groceries with meal plans built from your local promos

## Problem

PromoChef builds weekly meal plans from your supermarket's real promotions to help you save money and decide what to cook. Choose from classic, vegetarian, lactose-free, protein, gluten-free, express, weekend, or anti-waste plans and see calories and Nutri-Score. Generate smart shopping lists mapped to your chosen store, and log leftovers to get matching recipes and reduce waste. Use the web or app, with free classic menus at launch. View startup
