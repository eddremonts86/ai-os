# SPEC.md — Relay – an update after three years on anonymous team check-ins

## Problem

Three years ago I posted our product Signals here:
<a href="https:&#x2F;&#x2F;news.ycombinator.com&#x2F;item?id=34405962">https:&#x2F;&#x2F;news.ycombinator.com&#x2F;item?id=34405962</a><p>It’s rebranded to Relay now. Same idea-ish. We’ve cut back a lot but leaned right into the anonymity aspect for staff. We believe that as long as we protect the ability of the team to have a voice without negative consequences, then management gets more accurate data to act on. And also that it’s managements role to listen and respond to the commentary, even if just to acknowledge it.<p>We have definitely taken a hard path on this. The product requires strong management or team leads who aren’t afraid of criticism … funnily enough, that doesn’t make it easily saleable. But the businesses that are using it, and responding weekly to their teams concerns, do see a growth in trust and engagement. That lowers staff churn, increases institutional knowledge, etc. etc.<p>Additionally - we’re an AUS based business where state laws on psychosocial safety are quite strict - we actually meet the reporting, recording and response requirements for that legislation.<p>We’ve made loads of product improvements - AI generated summaries that keep context on the clients over time, easier account sign ups and logins, simpler pricing, better dashboards and an increase in anonymity through how we collect responses.<p>Over the last three years we have learnt that we are not sales people. It’s our single biggest challenge. We’re just not good at it. So that’s something we’re trying to solve.<p>It’s free to sign up and start using it. We’d love any and all feedback - the last time we posted was super valuable and I really appreciated everyones feedback.
