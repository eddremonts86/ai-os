# Why not a utility to save your Mac from a software bug killing your SSD?

## Problem

As you may know, MacBooks are expensive and an SSD killed by excessive writes due to software bug(s) requires a complete motherboard replacement.So why not have a utility that would warn you when an app is writing too much? Maybe you could specify a threshold for the warning.Would you use a utility like this to give you peace of mind, especially with MacBooks?
