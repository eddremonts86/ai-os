# SPEC.md — Is September 8th AGI Day?

## Problem

Today should be celebrated as the day that &quot;AGI&quot; actually means something now. That an AI model gave a solution to the Navier-Stokes problem which was one of the 7 unsolved millennium prize problems.<p>Keep in mind that 1 (or less than 5) expert human mathematician (Grigori Perelman) out of 8 billion humans managed to solve 1 of the seven millennium prize problems which was the Poincaré conjecture.
