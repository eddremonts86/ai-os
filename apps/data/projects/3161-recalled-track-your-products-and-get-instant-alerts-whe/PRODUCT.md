# Recalled – Track your products and get instant alerts when recalls occur

> Auto-generated product brief. Review and refine before MVP scoping.

## Value Proposition

A single place to search and monitor U.S. product-safety recalls across CPSC, NHTSA, FDA and USDA, with a phone alert when a matching recall is filed against a product the user has saved.

## Target Users

U.S. consumers who own appliances, vehicles, food, drugs, or medical devices and want recall alerts pushed to their phone instead of having to check four separate agency sites.

## Jobs To Be Done

When a consumer buys a new appliance, vehicle, food item, drug, or medical device, they save it in Recalled by photo, barcode, or manual entry so that if a recall is filed against it later, they get a phone alert — including an urgent-hazard flag — and a link to the original notice.
