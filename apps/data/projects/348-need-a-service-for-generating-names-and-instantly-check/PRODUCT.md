# Need a service for generating names and instantly checking domain availability

> Product brief authored from the source title and category. The poster's text was not available (source.name: manual); sections below re-state the problem and infer only what the title and category support.

## Value Proposition

A Russian founder types a brief and sees twenty named candidates with .com/.io/.co/.ru/.dev domain availability and X/GitHub/Telegram/VK handle availability - in one screen, in under 10 seconds, instead of 20 minutes of tab-juggling.

## Target Users

- Russian founders at the 'naming the project' step of a new venture.
- Russian indie developers and designers who want a clean .dev or .io without 20 minutes of guessing.
- Marketing freelancers in Russia building microsites and need one name + one URL fast.

## Jobs To Be Done

1. **Functional job** - Pick a project name with a clean URL and clean handles in one session.
2. **Emotional job** - Stop second-guessing after the .com was taken.
3. **Social job** - Be first to a name on the relevant channels, not the third.

## Success Metrics

- **Speed:** median brief-to-ranked-list under 10 seconds.
- **Conversion:** >= 15% of saved shortlists convert to a registered domain within 7 days.
- **Repeat use:** >= 30% of weekly actives return within 30 days with a second brief.

## Competitive Landscape

- **NameMesh / Lean Domain Search** - domain-only; no social handle or brief-driven generation.
- **Namelix (now part of Looka)** - name-only; domain is a paid add-on.
- **Manual tab-juggling** - what founders do today; loses the name in the gaps.
