# SPEC.md — Stitchwink – Cross-stitch pattern making with AI

## Problem

My wife has been cross stitching since she was a child and I wanted to make a tool where she could generate her own custom patterns: <a href="https:&#x2F;&#x2F;stitchwink.com&#x2F;" rel="nofollow">https:&#x2F;&#x2F;stitchwink.com&#x2F;</a><p>You can try chatgpt or other AI tools but the problem is that they don&#x27;t create real patterns. They look good at first glance but it&#x27;s not actually a physically realizable pattern when you look at the details.<p>The way it works is pretty simple, you use text (&amp; optional images) to create and edit patterns. There&#x27;s also a built in editor to manually change any of the stitches.
