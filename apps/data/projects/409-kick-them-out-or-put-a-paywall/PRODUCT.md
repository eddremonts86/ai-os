# Kick them out or put a paywall?

## Value Proposition

A kick-out vs. paywall decision aid for SaaS founders who have a freeloading enterprise customer and want the named consequences of each path in front of them before they send the email. Generic sales content in this space is mostly 'put a paywall in front of them' or 'be patient' posts. This one is grounded in the post: the LOI history, the year of free use, the named consequences of each path.

## Target Users

Primary: a SaaS founder who has a notable enterprise customer running on a free tier long after the initial LOI deal and is weighing kick-out vs. paywall. Secondary: a head of sales or VP customer success at the same kind of company who is being asked to make the call and wants the named consequences in front of them.

## Jobs To Be Done

When I (a SaaS founder with a freeloading enterprise customer) am deciding whether to kick them out or put a paywall in front of them, give me a decision aid with the named consequences of each path so I can make the call with the named trade-offs in front of me, not with a vague 'be patient'.

2. A founder can defend the kick-out vs. paywall decision in the next leadership meeting with the named trade-offs in front of them.
3. The talk-track the founder sends the customer reduces the year's worth of back-and-forth to a single conversation.
