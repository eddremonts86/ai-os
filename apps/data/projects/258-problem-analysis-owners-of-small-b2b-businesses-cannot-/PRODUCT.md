# Problem Analysis: Owners of small B2B businesses cannot build a growth system. Do they need a fractional CMO, or can a SaaS be built?

> Auto-generated product brief. Review and refine before MVP scoping.

## Value Proposition

A small-B2B growth-system surface that combines what a fractional CMO would do — the weekly pipeline review, the monthly coaching call — with what a SaaS can do — the dashboards, the activity log, the monthly review template. The owner runs the system weekly and gets a human review monthly, at a price point that fits a 5–50 person company.

## Target Users

- Owners of small French B2B businesses (5–50 employees).
- Sales leads at the same companies who own a pipeline but not the marketing engine behind it.
- Fractional CMOs and fractional marketing consultants in France who would use the surface as a backbone for their clients.
- Investors and advisors watching small-B2B portfolio companies who want a structured read on the growth system.

## Jobs To Be Done

- When I close out the week, I want a dashboard that tells me pipeline value, new leads by source, and conversion rate, so I can see whether the system is moving.
- When my team has been doing a mix of outbound, content, and referrals, I want a single activity log that ties the activity to the outcome, so I can see what is actually generating pipeline.
- When I finish a month, I want a structured one-page review with prompts, so the next month starts with intention rather than a vague "we'll do more."
- When I am a fractional CMO, I want the owner's dashboard and monthly review already populated, so my monthly call is a real coaching session rather than a data-collection call.

## Success Metrics

- Weekly active owners (proxy for the system being used, not just bought).
- Monthly review completion rate (proxy for the discipline loop).
- Pipeline coverage trend over three months (the owner's own measure; the source does not state a baseline).
- Consultant-panel bookings per month (proxy for the human-review upgrade).

## Risks & Open Questions

- Validate problem with 5 small-B2B owner interviews before MVP: confirm that the missing-system framing matches what owners actually face, and that they would use a hybrid SaaS-plus-consultant surface.
- Activity logging is the binding constraint. The MVP must measure time-to-log per week and stay under five minutes; otherwise the discipline loop dies.
- French-language requirement: the source is in France, so the MVP's primary language is French. A multi-language rollout is out of scope until the French version is validated.
- Consultant panel quality is the human-review differentiator. The MVP must vet partner consultants and publish a quality bar; otherwise the human-review upgrade becomes a generic call.
- Pipeline data privacy: a small-B2B pipeline contains named contacts. The MVP must define a retention policy and a contact-controlled deletion path before launch.
