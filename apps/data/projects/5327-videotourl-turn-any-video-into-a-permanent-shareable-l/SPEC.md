# SPEC.md — VideoToURL – Turn any video into a permanent, shareable link in seconds

## Problem

VideoToURL converts any video file into a permanent, shareable link in seconds. Upload MP4, MOV, WebM, AVI, or MKV files and get a direct URL and embed code without re-encoding or quality loss. Links stream over a global CDN and open in any modern browser. Use it free with no sign-up or watermark, or sign in for higher limits, link history, passwords, and expirations. Paste links into email, Slack, CRMs, docs, and websites for fast, reliable playback anywhere. View startup
