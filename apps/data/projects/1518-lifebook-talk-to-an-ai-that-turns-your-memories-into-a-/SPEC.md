# LifeBook – Talk to an AI that turns your memories into a keepsake book

## Problem

LifeBook helps you preserve life stories through natural conversations with Nora, an AI interviewer that listens, asks gentle questions, and guides you to share your memories. It turns your words into a beautifully written personal story you can read and share. Review and edit your story, then approve it to receive a printed keepsake book that captures the moments that matter. View startup
