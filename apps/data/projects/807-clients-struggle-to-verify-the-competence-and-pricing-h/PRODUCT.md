# Clients struggle to verify the competence and pricing honesty of freelancers when creating websites — there is no AI tool for real-time proposal analysis.

> Auto-generated product brief. Review and refine before MVP scoping.

## Value Proposition

A client evaluating 3+ freelancer proposals for a website build pastes them in and gets a side-by-side comparison with the inflated scopes, vague deliverables, and prior-work mismatches called out — so the honest proposal wins instead of the most-padded one.

## Target Users

| Stakeholder | Why they care |
|---|---|
| SMB + startup clients | Evaluating 3+ freelancer proposals and can't spot the padded ones without a manual spreadsheet. |
| Agency procurement leads + fractional CTOs | Doing the same comparison work for portfolio companies and want a shared tool instead of a bespoke spreadsheet. |

## Jobs To Be Done

1. **Functional job** — Compare 3+ freelancer proposals side-by-side and spot inflated scopes, vague deliverables, and prior-work mismatches.
2. **Emotional job** — Stop the recurring worry that the cheapest proposal is padded and the honest one is being passed over.
3. **Social job** — Share a defensible comparison with a co-founder or board member instead of "I went with my gut".

## Success Metrics

- **Activation:** first proposal comparison completed in under 10 minutes for 80% of users.
- **Red-flag precision:** ≥ 80% of red-flagged line items confirmed by the user as actually problematic.
- **Comparison depth:** ≥ 4 proposals compared per session on average.
- **Repeat rate:** ≥ 30% of clients return for a second comparison within 90 days.

## Pricing & Monetization

Free for 1 comparison (up to 3 proposals). $29/month per client for unlimited comparisons + 5-proposal side-by-side. $99/month for unlimited proposals + public-work-mismatch + red-flag history. No freelancer-side pricing, no commission, no referral.

## Competitive Landscape

- **Spreadsheets (Google Sheets, Excel)** — work for one comparison, don't scale across multiple clients, no percentile bands for deliverable types.
- **AI assistants (ChatGPT, Claude)** — can do one-shot analysis but no persistent comparison view, no percentile bands, no public-work-mismatch check.
- **Freelance platforms with built-in vetting (Toptal, A.Team)** — vet on the way in, but the client doesn't get a tool to evaluate proposals already in hand.
- **Consulting proposal tools (RFP360, Loopio)** — enterprise-priced ($10K+), built for RFP issuance, not proposal evaluation.

## Risks & Open Questions

- [ ] Validate whether deliverable-type percentile bands can be sourced from public freelancer rate surveys or must be seeded from internal data.
- [ ] Confirm the public-work-mismatch check respects freelancer privacy and ToS (no scraping behind logins).
- [ ] Decide whether red-flag history (a freelancer's red-flag pattern across clients) becomes a feature in v2 or stays per-client-only.
