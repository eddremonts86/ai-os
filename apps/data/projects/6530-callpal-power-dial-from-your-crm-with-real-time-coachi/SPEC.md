# SPEC.md — CallPal – Power-dial from your CRM with real-time coaching and analytics

## Problem

CallPal is a bring-your-own-carrier sales dialer that plugs directly into your CRM. Teams can click-to-call or run power-dialer campaigns from the browser, use the company caller ID on every call, and sync recordings and logs back to contact records. Managers monitor live calls with listen, whisper, and barge, and track performance with detailed analytics. Connect a CRM by OAuth or API key and your own SIP trunk. Calling costs stay transparent and under your control as no minutes are resold. It supports outbound calls only with no inbound routing, IVR, or predictive dialing. View startup
