# Sela – Track meds, vitals, and appointments for every loved one

## Problem

Sela helps family caregivers stay organized by putting medications, vitals, pain logs, appointments, and notes in one place. Create patient profiles, auto-generate daily dose schedules, and log doses with a tap. Track trends in blood pressure, blood sugar, and pain, and keep a care journal with visit transcripts. Switch between multiple patients instantly and manage refills with alerts. Sela keeps data private on your device with no account required, with cloud backup, sync, and SMS reminders planned.
