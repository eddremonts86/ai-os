# A psychologist needs an advertising bot that analyzes her workload and automatically adjusts her ads. She is willing to pay for a solution and is open to discussing the details.

> Auto-generated product brief. Review and refine before MVP scoping.

## Value Proposition

A practicing psychologist stops overpaying for ad clicks during full weeks and stops losing leads during empty weeks. The bot pays for itself out of one wasted week of ad spend.

## Target Users

Private-practice professionals in Russia who run ads themselves and have no in-house media buyer.

## Jobs To Be Done

Functional: translate calendar occupancy into ad-spend adjustments without manual intervention. Emotional: stop the dread of checking both the calendar and the ad dashboard every Monday. Social: appear professional and responsive to new inquiries even when fully booked.

## Success Metrics

Reduction in ad spend during full weeks (target: ≥ 30% drop). Recovery of bookings during low-load weeks (target: ≥ 20% recovery). Manual override used less than once per month per account.

## Pricing & Monetization

No willingness-to-pay figure was stated in the source. Pricing model should be tested with the first 10 customers before settling.

## Competitive Landscape

Generic Yandex Direct and VK Ads agency dashboards show statistics but do not act on calendar data. Generic Zapier-style automations require manual recipe maintenance and have no calendar-aware load scoring.

## Risks & Open Questions

Yandex Direct API quotas may rate-limit aggressive rule engines. Calendar connectors vary in latency (Google Calendar near-instant; YCLIENTS webhook can lag). Ad platforms may flag sudden bid changes as bot behaviour.
