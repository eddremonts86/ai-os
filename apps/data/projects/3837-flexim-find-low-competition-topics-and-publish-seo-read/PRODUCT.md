# Flexim – Find low-competition topics and publish SEO-ready content fast

## Value Proposition

Grow organic traffic without a marketing hire. Flexim turns the SEO content process into one pipeline: analyze the site, surface low-competition topics with validated demand, draft with templates and your preferred AI, refine against a checklist, and publish straight to your Next.js site from a headless CMS.

**One-liner:** Find low-competition topics and publish SEO-ready content fast, without hiring marketers.

## Target Users

| Stakeholder | Why they care |
|---|---|
| SaaS founders without marketers | Organic traffic work happens without a new hire. |
| Content operators in small teams | Templates and checklists standardize briefs and quality. |
| Next.js developers | Generated API and LLM docs make the CMS feel native to their stack. |
| SEO-curious product teams | Demand and difficulty validation replaces gut-feel topic choices. |

## Jobs To Be Done

1. **Functional job** — Analyze an existing site to find content gaps worth filling.
2. **Functional job** — Pick topics with low competition and validated demand, with titles and briefs ready.
3. **Functional job** — Draft through preferred AI tools inside templates, then refine with a checklist.
4. **Functional job** — Publish from the same system, headlessly, into a Next.js site.

## Success Metrics

- **Topics to first draft:** median time from site analysis to a drafted brief.
- **Ranked pages:** share of published topics that enter search results within a set window — the whole point of low-competition targeting.
- **CMS adoption:** share of users who publish through the headless CMS rather than exporting.
- **Integration success:** time from API credentials to first fetch in a Next.js app (the LLM-docs promise).

## Risks & Open Questions

- [ ] External LLM dependence: drafting quality is bounded by whatever AI the user brings; the checklist is the only owned quality lever.
- [ ] SEO claims are hard to validate; demand and difficulty scoring must stay defensible or trust erodes.
- [ ] Two products in one (SEO workflow plus headless CMS) risk a shallow feature set on both sides at MVP.
- [ ] No pricing or retention signals in the capture; willingness to pay for small SaaS teams is unknown.
- [ ] Publishing integrations beyond Next.js are unstated; the integration story may be narrow.
