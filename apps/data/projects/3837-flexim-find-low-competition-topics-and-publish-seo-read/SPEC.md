# Flexim – Find low-competition topics and publish SEO-ready content fast

## Problem

Flexim targets SaaS teams that want organic traffic growth without hiring marketers. The BetaList capture describes a workflow tool: it analyzes your site, validates demand and difficulty, and suggests low-competition topics with SEO-ready titles and briefs. Content is drafted through templates for blogs, comparisons and knowledge bases using your preferred AI, then refined in a built-in editor with a checklist. Flexim doubles as a headless CMS and backend: content is managed in a visual builder, published with one click, and exposed through a generated API and LLM docs for Next.js integration. No pricing, team size or launch detail appears in the capture.

## Objective

Build the SEO content workflow the capture describes end to end: topic discovery grounded in a site analysis, validation of demand and difficulty, templated drafting, editorial refinement, and headless-CMS publishing that plugs into a Next.js site without extra integration work.

## Target Users

- SaaS founders and small teams that need organic traffic but have no marketer on staff.
- Content creators producing blog, comparison and knowledge-base pages who want SEO guidance built in.
- Developers who prefer a headless CMS with a generated API and LLM docs over a managed editor.

## MVP Scope

- Site analysis that inventories existing content and surfaces gaps.
- Topic suggestions with demand and difficulty validation, plus SEO-ready titles and briefs.
- Templates for blogs, comparisons and knowledge bases, with drafting through the user's preferred AI.
- Built-in editor with an SEO checklist for refinement.
- Headless CMS: visual builder, one-click publish, generated API and LLM docs for Next.js integration.

## Constraints

- Everything stated comes from a short BetaList listing; no pricing, limits or customer evidence is captured.
- "Your preferred AI" implies the platform orchestrates external LLMs rather than owning a model; the MVP must stay provider-agnostic.
- The dual nature (content tool and headless CMS) is a wide scope; the MVP should land the SEO workflow before the CMS becomes a standalone selling point.
- SaaS focus is stated; serving personal blogs is not, even if the tool could.
