# Can't start a photography and videography business due to lack of clients and systematic approach

> Auto-generated product brief. Review and refine before MVP scoping.

## Value Proposition

Give Kenyan photographers and videographers a turnable system for finding, pitching, and following up with leads so the business actually starts.

## Target Users

| Stakeholder | Why they care |
|---|---|
| Primary user in Kenya | Solo or pair photography/videography operators in Kenya who have the technical skills but no sales process, and the small-business clients who would hire them if they could be reached. |
| Adjacent user | Anyone the primary user would need to coordinate with to use the product. |

## Jobs To Be Done

- When I have downtime between shoots, help me find new prospects in my area.
- When a prospect asks for a quote, give me a one-page proposal I can send in minutes.
- When a prospect goes quiet, remind me to follow up before they forget me.

## Success Metrics

The plan deliberately avoids invented percentages. The only honest metric is whether the user can name their next three leads and a follow-up date for each.

## Pricing & Monetization

The source does not name a price or willingness-to-pay. Pricing is left as an open question to be answered after user interviews, not invented here.

## Competitive Landscape

Photography-booking platforms (Pixieset, HoneyBook) exist, but the source does not name any direct competitor that gives a first-time photography/videography founder a complete launch path.

## Risks & Open Questions

- Lead generation without paid ads depends on Instagram/TikTok work that the user may not have time for; this is a sales risk, not a product risk.
- Kenyan SMB clients often pay by M-Pesa, which is outside the MVP scope; flag this as a follow-up rather than building it now.

- [ ] Confirm willingness to pay with real users
- [ ] Re-check Kenya-specific compliance expectations
