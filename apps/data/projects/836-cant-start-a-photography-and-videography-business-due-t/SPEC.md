# Can't start a photography and videography business due to lack of clients and systematic approach

## Problem

An aspiring photographer and videographer in Kenya reports being unable to launch a business because they have no clients and no repeatable, systematic way to find and convert them. The barrier is not equipment or skill but the absence of a sales pipeline and a documented workflow that takes a new lead from first contact to a booked shoot.

---

## Objective

Help an early-career photographer or videographer in Kenya turn cold interest into booked shoots using a repeatable client-acquisition system.

## Target Users

Solo or pair photography/videography operators in Kenya who have the technical skills but no sales process, and the small-business clients who would hire them if they could be reached.

## MVP Scope

A single-operator dashboard with: a portfolio page builder, a short proposal template tailored to Kenyan event and small-business shoots (weddings, birthdays, product photos), a basic lead tracker, and a follow-up reminder list. The MVP does not include payments, team accounts, or editing tools.

## Design Direction

See `DESIGN.md` for this project's design tokens.

## Constraints

Designed for a single user with limited capital. No paid ads assumed. Must work on low-bandwidth connections common in parts of Kenya. Privacy must respect Kenyan data protection expectations (client contact details are sensitive).
