# Get your free AI search visibility scan

## Value Proposition

Answer the question every site owner now asks: does AI know my site exists? The capture promises a free scan that measures a website's visibility in AI search, and the product's job is to deliver that measurement as a clear, shareable report — a domain in, an honest score and a list of cited queries out. Because the source is a bare URL plus a title, the value proposition is built on the single stated promise and nothing more.

**One-liner:** A free scan that reports how visible a website is when AI assistants answer questions.

## Target Users

| Stakeholder | Why they care |
|---|---|
| Website owners | They want a first read on whether AI assistants cite their site instead of guessing. |
| SEO practitioners | Classic rank trackers do not measure generative answers; a scan extends their toolbox. |
| Indie makers | A free self-serve check fits a solo workflow with no budget for enterprise tooling. |
| Agencies | A free scan doubles as a low-friction lead magnet for visibility services. |
The capture names no users; these roles follow from the title's promise of a scan for websites.

## Jobs To Be Done

1. Find out whether my site appears in AI-generated answers, without prompting each assistant by hand.
2. Get a concrete list of questions where the site is invisible, so fixes can be targeted.
3. Share a readable visibility report with a client, boss or teammate.
4. Re-check after changing the site, to see whether visibility moved.

## Success Metrics

- Scans completed per week: the headline demand signal for a free self-serve tool.
- Median time from domain submission to a rendered report.
- Share of scans that end in a report the user opens or downloads, rather than abandoning.
- Repeat scans of the same domain, indicating the re-check loop is working.
- Queries per scan that produce a definitive cited/not-cited verdict, as a coverage measure.
