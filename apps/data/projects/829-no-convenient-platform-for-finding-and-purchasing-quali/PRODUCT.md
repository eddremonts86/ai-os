# No convenient platform for finding and purchasing quality products from local farms in small wholesale quantities

> Auto-generated product brief. Review and refine before MVP scoping.

## Value Proposition

A small-volume wholesale buyer in Russia finds a local farm, sees what is in season at a small-wholesale price, and places the order from one screen instead of negotiating a full-pallet contract or going farm-to-farm by phone. The framing the post supports is "small-volume wholesale without a sales call per farm", not "cheaper produce".

**One-liner:** One screen for small-volume wholesale from local farms.

## Target Users

| Stakeholder | Why they care |
|---|---|
| Restaurants and small grocers in Russia | Want local-farm quality without negotiating a full wholesale pallet or cold-calling farms |
| Local farms in the same market | Want a steady stream of small wholesale orders without standing up their own e-commerce |

## Jobs To Be Done

- When I plan my week, find which local farms have what is in season at small-wholesale volumes.
- When I place an order, do it from the farm listing without a phone call to confirm the price.
- When a farm earns trust over a few orders, repeat the order without starting from scratch.

## Success Metrics

Source does not state a metric. Outcomes to validate before MVP: orders placed per farm per month, share of buyers returning for a second order, and the share of orders placed below the full-pallet band.

## Pricing & Monetization

Pricing is not stated by the source. Whether the platform takes a transaction fee, a listing fee, or runs on a subscription must be validated with both sides.

## Competitive Landscape

Local-farm sourcing apps (Farmers, Web) exist, but the source does not name any direct competitor that combines local-farm discovery with small-wholesale purchasing.

## Risks & Open Questions

- Country of submission is Russia; payment rails, last-mile logistics, and farm-to-buyer regulation are local and not addressed by the source.
- "Small wholesale" is the post's word; the volume band is not defined and must come from interviews.
- The post lists AgTech, Logistics, and Retail as tags; the MVP should not try to cover all three at once.
- Trust on a farm directory is easy to fake; any review or rating feature should be deliberately minimal until it can be backed by a real signal.
