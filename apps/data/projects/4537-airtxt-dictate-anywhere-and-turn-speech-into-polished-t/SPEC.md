# airtxt – Dictate anywhere and turn speech into polished text fast

## Problem

airtxt lets you speak instead of typing on iPhone and iPad. It transcribes your voice on-device or via cloud, cleans up filler and punctuation, and inserts finished text into any app through its keyboard. You can send a meeting bot to Zoom, Meet, or Teams to capture transcripts and draft follow-up emails, and save quick memos ready to paste. Bring your own Deepgram, Anthropic, or OpenAI keys for direct processing and keep control of privacy, language, and custom vocabulary. View startup
