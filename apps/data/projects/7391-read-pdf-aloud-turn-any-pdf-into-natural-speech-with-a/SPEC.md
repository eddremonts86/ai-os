# SPEC.md — Read PDF Aloud – Turn any PDF into natural speech with AI in your browser

## Problem

Read PDF Aloud lets you listen to any PDF with natural AI voices in 142+ languages. It runs locally in your browser, uses OCR to handle scanned files, and offers precise playback controls for a smooth experience. Upload a document, start listening instantly, and export audio as MP3 for offline use. The service works on any modern device and provides multiple voice options with free daily credits.
