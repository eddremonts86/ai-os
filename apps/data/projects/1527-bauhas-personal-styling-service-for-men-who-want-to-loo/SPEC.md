# Bauhaüs – Personal styling service for men who want to look good but hate to shop

## Problem

Bauhaüs is a personal styling service that helps men find more confidence in their style every day. It uses a "microstyling" process where you share a favorite piece one at a time, and a Bauhaüs professional stylist builds recommendations using clothes you already have or suggests pieces to fill gaps and make your closet work harder for you. Bauhaüs is AI supported but still human powered. A real professional stylist approves every recommendation to ensure it fits your personal style. View startup
