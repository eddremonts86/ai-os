# SPEC.md — Pluribox – Sell digital products from the website you already own

## Problem

Pluribox is a modular selling system for digital products. Add checkout, reviews, forms, and secure digital delivery directly to the website you already own without building a separate store or sending buyers away. Create a product once, then place only the parts you need across your site using a Full Product Box, Button Box, or Reviews Box. Connected boxes stay synchronized while checkout opens on-page. Connect Stripe to receive payments directly. Pluribox also provides tax tools, receipts, and buyer emails, while you remain the merchant of record. View startup
