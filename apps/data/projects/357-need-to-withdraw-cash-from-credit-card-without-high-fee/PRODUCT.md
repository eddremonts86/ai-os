# Need to withdraw cash from credit card without high fees

## Value Proposition

A Portugal-first routing tool that tells a credit card holder the *real* options for getting cash off their card, ranked by total cost, and lets them pick one without having to phone the bank. The user keeps the convenience of the cash they already have available; the platform is paid only when the user picks a partner route, and the fee the user pays is the one they saw before they committed.

## Target Users

| Stakeholder | Why they care |
|---|---|
| Portuguese credit card holder with cash on the card | Cash advance fees are unpredictable; an itemised quote is the missing product. |
| Self-employed worker in Portugal | Income timing is uneven; cash from the card is a working-capital stopgap. |
| Pre-payday cardholder | Used up the debit account; the credit card is the only liquid source. |
| Family member helping a relative | Wants to move money from one card to another without the cash-advance fee. |

## Jobs To Be Done

- "I want to know what the cash advance will actually cost me before I commit."
- "I want to compare the cash advance to the instalment plan to the balance transfer on the same screen."
- "I want to find a Portuguese ATM that does not charge the cash advance fee."
- "I want to move money from my credit card to my bank account in one tap, with the exact fee shown up front."
- "I want to see what I did last time so I can do it again."

## Success Metrics

- A user who reached the booking step saw the itemised total cost and confirmed it.
- The chosen route landed in the user's bank account within the documented timeline for that route.
- Repeat usage within 90 days by the same cardholder, indicating the cost matched their expectations.
- Lower complaint rate than the issuer's standard cash-advance flow for the same amount.

## Competitive Landscape

- **Bank customer-service phone line** — the existing path: call the bank, ask about cash advance, ask about instalment plan, compare manually.
- **Aggregator sites (e.g., Doutor Finanças, Portal do consumidor)** — explain the *concept* of cash advance fees but do not produce a personalised quote for a specific user.
- **Direct bank app** — shows the cash advance fee on the withdrawal flow but does not surface the instalment-plan or balance-transfer alternative on the same screen.

## Risks & Open Questions

- The platform's value rests on the route-explainer being accurate; a Portuguese bank that changes its fee schedule silently breaks the user experience until the next refresh.
- The partner-ATM route only works if there is a partner; until the first partnership is signed, the MVP must not invent one.
- Banco de Portugal's consumer-credit disclosure rules require specific wording; paraphrasing the fee or the interest exposes the platform to a regulatory complaint.
- The user's "cheapest route" sometimes means the longest timeline (e.g., balance transfer takes 2 business days); the route-explainer must show the timeline, not just the cost.
