# Startup founders have nowhere to order quality, in-depth research on specific projects or niches — existing services provide superficial and unreliable reports

> Auto-generated product brief. Review and refine before MVP scoping.

## Value Proposition

A US startup founder commissions a deep research report on a specific niche, partnership, or market entry, and gets back a structured deliverable where every factual claim links to a public record — so they can make the call without wondering whether the report is fabricated.

## Target Users

| Stakeholder | Why they care |
|---|---|
| US seed-to-Series-A founders | Evaluating a niche entry, vertical partnership, or market-expansion decision without an in-house research team. |
| Venture scouts + solo-GP fund managers | Running thesis checks before a partner meeting and need the same source-cited signal. |

## Jobs To Be Done

1. **Functional job** — Commission a deep research report on a specific niche and trust the source citations.
2. **Emotional job** — Stop the recurring worry that a paid research deliverable is fabricated or stale.
3. **Social job** — Hand a real report to a co-founder, investor, or board member as evidence the work was done.

## Success Metrics

- **Activation:** brief intake completed and researcher matched in under 5 business days for 80% of briefs.
- **Source-citation reliability:** 100% of factual claims in delivered reports link to a public record verified by a human reviewer.
- **Founder satisfaction:** ≥ 4.5/5 average founder rating on delivered reports.
- **Repeat rate:** ≥ 30% of founders commission a second report within 6 months.

## Pricing & Monetization

$500 (5 pages), $1,500 (15 pages), $3,000 (30 pages). Per-brief pricing, no subscription. Researcher takes 60%, platform takes 40%. No rush-fee multiplier, no upsell to a "consult" call.

## Competitive Landscape

- **Generic market-research vendors (Gartner, Forrester, IBISWorld)** — expensive ($5K+ per report), generic, not niche-specific, no founder-relevant deliverable shape.
- **Freelance platforms (Upwork, Toptal)** — work for known researchers, no source-citation guarantee, no opposing-view requirement, no review layer.
- **AI assistants (ChatGPT Deep Research, Claude with web search)** — fast, but no source-citation guarantee, no opposing-view requirement, no human review of citations.
- **In-house research analyst** — full-time cost ($80K+ per year) the founder cannot justify on a single decision.

## Risks & Open Questions

- [ ] Validate whether 40% platform take is enough to fund 2-pass human review without losing researchers to direct-founder off-platform work.
- [ ] Confirm the 5/15/30-page tiers match what founders actually commission or whether the menu needs adjusting after the first 50 briefs.
- [ ] Decide whether the platform should expand to in-house researchers or stay a pure marketplace (the in-house option increases margin but caps niche coverage).
