# SPEC.md — Tell HN: On AI, Quality, and Accountability

## Problem

Suppose I do something myself.  I reap all the benefits, but it takes a lot of
time, I may not have the expertise to do a good job, and I get all the blame if
something goes wrong.<p>Suppose I go to the park and find one of several guys who would do it for free.
I get all the of benefits, he might do an excellent job, but, because there&#x27;s no
contract, I still take all the blame.<p>Suppose I actually hire a professional to do it.  I receive fewer of the
benefits. However, he&#x27;s much more likely to do a good job, and the legal system
provides a variety of ways to hold him accountable for the work.<p>Suppose I hire a professional, but then insist that he use legions of guys from
the park to do the actual work, since that&#x27;s going to be some much faster and
cheaper per quantum of work.  I also tell him that if he&#x27;s unwilling to do that,
I&#x27;ll find someone who will.  I also tell him that, since he has so much more
unoccupied time now that the original work is in the hands of all those other dudes,
he gets to supervise still more work done by other swarms of guys from the park.<p>At this point it should be clear that I&#x27;m not interested in quality work, but in
having someone poised to absorb blame.  I want a patsy.<p>Now replace &quot;guys at the park&quot; with LLMs, and replace &quot;free&quot; with &quot;nearly free,
but with no warranty.&quot;
