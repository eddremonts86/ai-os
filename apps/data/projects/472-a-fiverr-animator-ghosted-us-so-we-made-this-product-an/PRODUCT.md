# A Fiverr animator ghosted us, so we made this product animation with 3 prompts and one HTML file

> Auto-enriched product brief.

## Value Proposition

A product-animation generator that turns a brief into a 30-second marketing animation in HTML, exportable as MP4.

## Target Users

- Indie founders who need a product demo animation without hiring a freelancer
- Solo founders who have been ghosted by an animator
- Small SaaS teams producing launch assets on a deadline

## Jobs To Be Done

When I need a product demo animation and the animator has ghosted me, I want a tool that generates a 30-second animation from 3 prompts, so I do not miss the launch.

## Success Metrics

- At least 100 animations generated in pilot
- Self-reported "good enough to ship" rate above 50%
