_Based on source brief:_ Suppose you have a brand you care about — your own business or a nice business you are a fan of. I built a thing which lets you see what shows up in LLM searches and you can send it to my servers for comprehensive analysis, all completely free forever with beautiful dashboards and such!<p>The reason I built it is that I make my purchase decisions with LLMs (I don&#x27;t delegate all my life to them _yet_), since 2023. After you get the data, you can look at the outputs or get geoSurge sentiment analysis for free.<p>My gf is using it to monitor her Japanese baking class studio: <a href="https:&#x2F;&#x2F;okashi-school.com" rel="nofollow">https:&#x2F;&#x2F;okashi-school.com</a>.
