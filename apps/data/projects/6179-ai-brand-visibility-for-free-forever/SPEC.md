# SPEC.md — AI Brand Visibility for Free Forever

## Problem

Suppose you have a brand you care about — your own business or a nice business you are a fan of. I built a thing which lets you see what shows up in LLM searches and you can send it to my servers for comprehensive analysis, all completely free forever with beautiful dashboards and such!<p>The reason I built it is that I make my purchase decisions with LLMs (I don&#x27;t delegate all my life to them _yet_), since 2023. As you can imagine, there was <i>a lot of hallucinations</i>, so I got obssessed for a while with how to make sure that the LLMs respond about brands with facts.<p>I was triggered a lot by wrappers that were charging money for API calls, so I decided to build it properly and hand over the keys to whoever wants to use it. With geoSurge you connect your existing LLM subscriptions to gather LLM sentiment, you use your spare tokens so it doesn&#x27;t cost you anything in usage. After you get the data, you can look at the outputs or get geoSurge sentiment analysis for free.<p>My gf is using it to monitor her Japanese baking class studio: <a href="https:&#x2F;&#x2F;okashi-school.com" rel="nofollow">https:&#x2F;&#x2F;okashi-school.com</a>.
