# AgentBuild – Build and manage your website using ChatGPT

## Problem

AgentBuild lets your AI, ChatGPT or Claude, build and manage a full business web presence. It connects your domain, routes email at that domain, writes and updates the site, and tracks your leads, all from the chat you already use. No dashboard or drag-and-drop. It already has your menu doc and photos, so an update is a chat prompt. Fast sites with SEO defaults built in: structured data, sitemaps, analytics, and pages AI search engines can read. You keep your domain and content and can switch AIs anytime. View startup
