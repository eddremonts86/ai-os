# SPEC.md — What would make you trust an AI's assessment of your understanding?

## Problem

Creating an AI tutor that won&#x27;t let you progress without having learned anything — not just completing a test. You train, are diagnosed for any problems in understanding, and there is now a live voice challenge where you need to verbally explain your response to an AI, and then it drills down with additional questions.<p>The voice feature was created by me under the suspicion that it is way more difficult to pretend to have understood something while talking to someone rather than choosing a proper multiple choice answer. But to be honest, I don&#x27;t know if that is a valuable idea or just another sign of my attachment to the most complicated thing I created.<p>So, the question I want to ask seriously is: What would help you to trust an AI&#x27;s diagnosis of your understanding? And is it even worthwhile, or should I stick to the solution of doing spaced repetition and trusting myself?
