## Value Proposition

_Based on source brief:_ I had a hard time understanding the Japanese address system. Parts of addresses have suffixes like fu, shi, ku, chōme, ban and gō. Which suffixes are used varies between regions in a way that seemed random to me. I couldn&#x27;t find a map of the system with a modern visualization, so I made one. It also shows whether an area has a governor or a mayor. For example Tokyo&#x27;s wards have mayors and Osaka&#x27;s don&#x27;t.<p>You can click around or drop the pin to explore, or write an address in the search bar.
