## Value Proposition

_Based on source brief:_ I built a cool browser scene, a shelter window over a dystopian city in the rain. Pull the breaker and a siren tower spins up. The wind-down is super unsettling and I love it.<p>The siren is not a recording. Two port rings make a 5:6 dual tone, 515 and 618 Hz. The horn turns at 4 RPM and everything (loudness, brightness, doppler and stereo position) follows the horn angle you can see on the tower. Rain and thunder are the only recordings.<p>Build it yourself, source is one HTML file and MIT: <a href="https:&#x2F;&#x2F;github.com&#x2F;beeswaxpat&#x2F;sirenfall" rel="nofollow">https:&#x2F;&#x2F;github.com&#x2F;beeswaxpat&#x2F;sirenfall</a><p>How it works: <a href="https:&#x2F;&#x2F;sirenfall.live&#x2F;how-it-works" rel="nofollow">https:&#x2F;&#x2F;sirenfall.live&#x2F;how-it-works</a><p>Press F for fullscreen, S to save a picture. Landscape on phones. No cookies, no personal data, the only request besides audio is an anonymous page-count pixel.<p>Appreciate any feedback and shoutout to the siren community!
