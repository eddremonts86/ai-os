# SPEC.md — Caredence – Run your daycare with unified check-in, billing, reports, and parent comms

## Problem

Caredence streamlines daycare operations for owners, staff, and parents in one platform. It replaces paper attendance, manual billing, and fragmented messaging with GPS check-in, automated tuition invoicing and payments, daily reports with photos, and real-time parent notifications. Directors track enrollment, revenue, and ratios from a single dashboard and generate licensing-ready reports. Centers onboard in days by importing rosters and inviting families with one click.
