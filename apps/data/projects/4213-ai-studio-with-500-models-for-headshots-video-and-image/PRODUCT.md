# AI studio with 500 models for headshots, video and images

## Value Proposition

500+ top generative models behind one subscription: image, video, audio, headshots, photoshoots, and photo editing, with iPhone and iPad parity and watermark-free HD export.

## Target Users

- Creators producing content across image, video, and audio
- Solo founders running product photo pipelines
- Social media managers who need headshots and ad creatives in one place
- Photographers and retouchers offering AI-powered services

## Jobs To Be Done

- When I need a specific look, I want to pick the right model from one tab instead of subscribing to six
- When I am on the move, I want the same tools on my phone so I can generate from anywhere
- When I export, I want watermark-free HD so the output is presentation-ready

## Success Metrics

- 500K+ creators and 50M+ images (already at launch)
- Median tool launch under 30 seconds from prompt
- 70% of paying users generate across at least three categories

## Pricing & Monetization

One subscription; free path likely limited. Source page does not state a price.

## Competitive Landscape

- Midjourney — images only, single-model
- Runway, Pika — video only
- ElevenLabs — audio only
- Per-model stacks (3–6 paid subscriptions) — what MagicShot replaces

## Risks & Open Questions

- Model pricing volatility — upstream providers can raise prices
- Watermark-free on the free path is a sharp product line to maintain
- Mobile parity at model parity is operationally expensive
- 500+ models is a marketing number; the real question is curation
