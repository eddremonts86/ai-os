# HR problem: subjective hiring and slow adaptation of new employees

## Value Proposition

A structured hiring and onboarding pipeline that turns "the panel felt this way" into a defensible decision and turns "the new hire is taking a while" into a visible ramp. The HR manager gets a scorecard the panel can defend; the hiring manager gets a 30/60/90 plan they publish on day one; the new hire gets a clear path to the first deliverable; the company gets a retrospective that improves the next hire.

## Target Users

| Stakeholder | Why they care |
|---|---|
| HR manager in a Russian mid-sized company | Cannot defend decisions; wants a structured scorecard. |
| Hiring manager | Wants a 30/60/90 plan to publish on day one. |
| New hire | Wants to know what is expected in week 1 and how they will be measured. |
| Founder doing own hiring | Wants a process that survives the company growing past 10 people. |

## Jobs To Be Done

- "I want a structured interview kit per role, not a different interview every time."
- "I want a panel scorecard that produces a written reason for the hire / no-hire decision."
- "I want a 30/60/90 plan that the manager publishes before day one."
- "I want to see, on a dashboard, where each new hire is on the plan."
- "I want to keep the scorecard for the time the company requires and delete it after."

## Success Metrics

- A panel decision that was recorded with a written reason and a scorecard, ready to be reviewed at a later date.
- A new hire whose 30/60/90 plan was published on day one and whose first deliverable was signed off at day 30.
- A retrospective at the end of the 30/60/90 plan compared to the next hire's plan, indicating the process is learning.

## Competitive Landscape

- **Existing ATS systems (Huntflow, Potok, Yva)** — provide resume storage and interview scheduling but do not enforce a structured scorecard or a 30/60/90 plan.
- **Lattice, 15Five** — provide 30/60/90 plan templates but are not Russian-localized and do not address the hiring side.
- **Internal "we have a process"** — the spreadsheet or Notion doc the HR manager keeps; the pain is that it is not enforced and not visible.

## Risks & Open Questions

- A structured scorecard does not eliminate bias; the platform must require a written reason for each decision and surface it on review, not bury it.
- The hiring manager must fill in the 30/60/90 plan before the new hire starts; if the manager does not, the hire starts without a plan and the ramp is back to "we'll figure it out."
- The scorecard contains interview notes about real candidates; data retention must follow the company's written policy and Russian labour law.
- The platform must not produce a "fit score" or rank candidates; the panel's written judgement must remain the decision.
