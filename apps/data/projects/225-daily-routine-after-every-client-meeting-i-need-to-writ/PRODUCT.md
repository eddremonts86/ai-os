# Daily routine: after every client meeting, I need to write a structured report for colleagues. Existing tools are slow, opinionated, or forget the context.

> Auto-generated product brief. Review and refine before MVP scoping.

## Value Proposition

Cut the meeting-to-report time from 30 minutes to under 5. The first 1000 paying users pay for the service from the recovered time in the first week.

## Target Users

UK and EU professionals in consulting, agencies, law firms, and customer-facing teams who write a client report after every meeting. Also internal teams that need a meeting summary in a specific format.

## Jobs To Be Done

Functional: produce a draft report in the format the team actually uses. Emotional: stop the dread of the post-meeting report. Social: be the colleague who always sends a useful report on the same day.

## Success Metrics

Average time to a sent report below 5 minutes. User-rated report quality above 4 out of 5. Less than 10% of reports rejected by the user as wrong-styled (the template wasn't applied).

## Competitive Landscape

Otter, Fireflies, and tl;dv produce generic summaries. Granola is closest but lacks per-client memory. Notion AI and Claude in a chat require the user to do the templating. No mainstream service combines template memory across clients with a per-colleague style and a 60-second review.

## Risks & Open Questions

May hallucinate names or decisions the model inferred. Per-client memory must be respected when the client changes. Risk of being interpreted as a meeting surveillance tool if the user pastes a meeting they were not in.
