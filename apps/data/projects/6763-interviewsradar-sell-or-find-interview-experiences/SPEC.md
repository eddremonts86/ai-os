# SPEC.md — InterviewsRadar – Sell or find interview experiences

## Problem

Hi HN, I’m Lewis, a student building InterviewsRadar as a side project.<p>I built it to help people find someone who recently interviewed for a specific company and role, and have a conversation about the experience.<p>You can post an experience you’re willing to share, or a request for something you’re looking for. Conversations can cover the interview process, preparation, and lessons learned.<p>Browsing is open. To post, sign in with Google and choose a nickname. The platform is free to use; people sharing their experience can offer free or paid conversations. Contact details are exchanged only after both people confirm.<p>I’m initially focusing on Taiwan and the US.<p>If you’ve recently interviewed somewhere, I’d appreciate you posting an experience you’re willing to discuss. 
A short listing is enough—you don’t need to write a full interview review. 
You can also post a request if you can’t find what you need.<p>I care about privacy personally. If the project doesn’t find a community and I decide to shut it down, I’ll delete user accounts and their associated data.<p>You can also reach me privately at [lewislin0706@gmail.com]
