# FeelMeTV – Find TV shows that match your mood with clear, content-aware picks

## Problem

FeelMeTV helps you pick a TV show based on how you want to feel, not just the genre. Take a short quiz to set your moods, adjust comfort, stress, darkness, humor, and intensity, and exclude themes like gore, animal harm, or jump scares. The service ranks about ten shows with match percentages and simple reasons like “Low-stress watch.” You can browse Everything Else or focus on K-dramas or C-dramas, filtering by airing or completed series, all without creating an account.
