# SPEC.md — About map enshittification: Have you noticed the maps have gotten worse?

## Problem

For a long time I was on google maps, and a couple of years ago switched to apple maps because I _felt_ it was better where I lived.<p>But recently apple maps has gotten worse. Then I switched to waze, and it felt fine for a while and now feels like that has gotten worse.<p>A lot of it is feeling, but there are a few discrete examples of how<p>1. I can no longer trust that the fastest route is suggested: sometimes it defualts to a slower route, and other times it tells me the route is fastest, but when I click into a &quot;slower&quot; route (one that is intuitively faster to me because I know the area) the estimated driving distance is literally faster (sometimes by multiple minutes for a drive that might be only 30 - 40 minutes. Why didn&#x27;t it default to this one???<p>2. Worse routes: the one I most consistently notice is there&#x27;s a route near me where you can either make a left turn in one of two ways: a) at a stop light or b) by turning left into traffic on a pretty major throughway. Up until ~3 months ago no map had ever suggested crossing the throughway, and all of a sudden, every map started suggesting it (google&#x2F;apple&#x2F;waze and uber and lyft drivers).<p>Has anyone else noticed this? Is this just garden variety enshittification?
