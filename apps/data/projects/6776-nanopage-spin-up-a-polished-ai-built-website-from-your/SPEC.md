# SPEC.md — Nanopage – Spin up a polished AI-built website from your content in minutes

## Problem

Nanopage lets you launch a functional website in 3–5 minutes by describing what you need and uploading your content. It generates a live page you can refine through chat, then manage with a built-in CMS for blogs, products, events, and more. You can host on a real URL with optional custom domains, built-in analytics, contact forms, and collaboration. Plans are simple and low cost, so you can get a presentable site live tonight without templates or coding. View startup
