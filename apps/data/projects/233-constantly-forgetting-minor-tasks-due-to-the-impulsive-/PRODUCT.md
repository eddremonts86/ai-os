# Constantly forgetting minor tasks due to the impulsive habit of «swiping» reminders away. Needs a tool that breaks this behavioral pattern

> Auto-generated product brief. Review and refine before MVP scoping.

## Value Proposition

Converts the swipe-away habit from a silent failure into a small, visible decision, which is the smallest intervention that reliably changes a behaviour loop. The product does not add a new productivity system — it patches the existing one.

## Target Users

Self-aware habitual dismissers in the USA who already use a system; users who have tried and abandoned Todoist / Apple Reminders precisely because the friction of opening the app is higher than the friction of swiping the notification away.

## Jobs To Be Done

When a reminder notification appears, I want to be forced to choose between three real outcomes (done, soon, later) so that I do not accidentally lose the task. When I look back at my week, I want to see how many of my reminders actually converted into something.

## Success Metrics

Reduction in reminder-to-task conversion rate below 50% (industry default for generic reminders). Weekly retention after 4 weeks. Free-to-paid conversion on CloudKit-shared multi-device tier.

## Competitive Landscape

Apple Reminders and Google Tasks optimise for minimal friction, which is exactly the design that produces the swipe-to-forget pattern. Todoist and TickTick add features rather than addressing the dismiss reflex. Streaks / Habitica focus on motivation, not on the moment-of-decision itself.

## Risks & Open Questions

Behaviour-change apps have notoriously high 30-day churn. Users may resent the added friction. Need to validate with 10 user interviews that the 3-option action sheet is felt as helpful rather than paternalistic.
