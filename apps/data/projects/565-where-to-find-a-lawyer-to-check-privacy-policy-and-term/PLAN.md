# GDPR/EU-compliant Privacy Policy + ToS review for an adults-and-children product

## Tech Stack

No product. Tooling: a shortlist spreadsheet; a template email asking for a fixed-fee quote; a doc-comparison workflow to mark changes between AI-generated and reviewed versions.

## Architecture

Hiring pipeline: (1) candidate list, (2) outreach, (3) quote comparison, (4) engagement, (5) document review.

## Milestones

M1: 5-candidate shortlist. M2: 3 quotes received. M3: engagement signed. M4: reviewed documents in hand.

## Risks

Risk: candidates are slow to reply. Risk: quotes come back above the founder's budget for the review. Risk: reviewed documents require product changes the founder did not anticipate.
