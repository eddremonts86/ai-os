# SPEC.md — Draudykle – Compare insurance offers and buy insurance online

## Problem

Draudykle is a Lithuanian insurance comparison and purchase platform. It lets you quickly compare prices and terms from different insurers and instantly create a contract online. The platform offers car, KASKO, travel, home, health, and business insurance. You can manage your policies conveniently in your account. AI algorithms help select the best offers for your needs, and a licensed broker provides support and transparent information with no hidden fees. View startup
