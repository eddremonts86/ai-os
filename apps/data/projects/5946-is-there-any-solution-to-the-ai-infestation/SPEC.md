# SPEC.md — Is there any solution to the AI infestation?

## Problem

I&#x27;m sure I&#x27;m not the only one who&#x27;s noticed, but nearly every post on Hacker News is getting LLM comments now. For anyone who works regularly with LLMs, it&#x27;s very obvious from the tone alone - and a quick check to previous comments will confirm it. Some have gotten slightly more sophisticated and auto-add typos, or other tricks. I&#x27;m sure there&#x27;s plenty that I don&#x27;t recognize as AI either.<p>I understand the motivation - running a mini bot farm would allow you to make sure your posts get a number of quick upvotes - or manipulate the conversation about your startup, or some other field one has a vested interest in. But seeing this infestation, and the rate at which it is growing is very demoralizing.<p>There probably aren&#x27;t any solutions that don&#x27;t involve significant privacy invasions or a fundamental change in how the site operates, so maybe this is more of a vent post - but I&#x27;m curious if anyone has any suggestions?
