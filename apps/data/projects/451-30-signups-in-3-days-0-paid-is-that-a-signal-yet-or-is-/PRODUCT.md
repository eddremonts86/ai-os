# 30 signups in 3 days, 0 paid. Is that a signal yet or is my sample too small?

> Auto-enriched product brief.

## Value Proposition

A signal-vs-noise calculator that tells a founder whether their early numbers are meaningful, given the sample size they actually have.

## Target Users

- Solo SaaS founders with 0-50 signups and 0 paying customers wondering if their sample is meaningful
- First-time founders 1-4 weeks into launch
- Founders who feel pressure from social-media success stories

## Jobs To Be Done

When I have 30 signups and 0 paying, I want a calculator that tells me whether that is a signal or noise, so I stop comparing myself to founders with 10,000 signups and 200 paying.

## Success Metrics

- At least 200 founder inputs in 90 days
- Self-reported "less anxious about early numbers" rate above 50%
