# SPEC.md — ThoughtLogger – Capture thoughts fast and let AI file, link, and surface answers

## Problem

ThoughtLogger is an AI-powered journal that lets you capture thoughts in one tap and handles the filing for you. You can type, speak, or attach photos, PDFs, and links. It creates a title, picks a type, assigns categories, and builds a context graph. It transcribes audio, translates entries, and makes everything searchable. Later, ask plain-language questions and get cited answers from your own words. It works offline, syncs across Android and web, and allows export anytime. View startup
