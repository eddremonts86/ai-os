# A Russian developer built an app for Nigeria but can't accept payments. App stores are unavailable, PayPal and Stripe are blocked.

> Auto-generated product brief. Review and refine before MVP scoping.

## Value Proposition

Sell to Nigeria from anywhere in the world without a Nigerian shell company. The first 100 developers using the platform pay a flat 5% of every transaction, replacing a setup that would otherwise cost them a year and a local accountant.

## Target Users

Foreign (non-Nigerian) developers and small SaaS founders selling to Nigerian consumers or businesses. Secondarily: African creators or small businesses who would rather work with a developer outside their country than wait for a local equivalent.

## Jobs To Be Done

Functional: accept NGN payments from a Nigerian user without leaving your home country. Emotional: stop the awkward 'sorry, we can't accept your payment' message. Social: sell in Nigeria the way Stripe Atlas says you can, only actually.

## Success Metrics

At least 50 active developers using the platform within 6 months of launch. At least 80% of transactions complete without manual intervention. Refund completion within 24 hours for at least 95% of requests. Customer complaints below 1% of transactions.

## Competitive Landscape

Stripe Atlas is sometimes suggested as a workaround but does not solve the consumer-side payment problem in Nigeria. Paystack's own partner programme is for Nigerian entities only. No mainstream service combines a foreign developer onboarding flow with a Paystack / Flutterwave partner-of-record and a transparent fee structure.

## Risks & Open Questions

Regulatory risk if the partner-of-record is not properly licensed. Sanctions risk if the routing touches a sanctioned entity. Chargeback risk in a market where card-not-present fraud is high. Reputational risk if the partner-of-record disappears with a month's revenue.
