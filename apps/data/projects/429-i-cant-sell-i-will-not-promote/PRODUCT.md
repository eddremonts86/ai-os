# I can’t sell. (I will not promote)

> Auto-enriched product brief.

## Value Proposition

Replaces "ask Reddit how to sell" with an async, recorded critique a real person (or AI) annotates and the founder can replay.

## Target Users

- Solo technical founders 3-18 months into launch
- Bootstrapped SaaS founders with product shipped but no revenue
- Indie devs trying to land B2B

## Jobs To Be Done

When I keep failing to close, I want a recorded breakdown of my actual sales call, so I can see exactly where I lose the buyer instead of being told to "ask better questions."

## Success Metrics

- At least 50 critiques delivered in month 1
- Median 7-day re-upload rate above 30%
- Refund rate below 10%
