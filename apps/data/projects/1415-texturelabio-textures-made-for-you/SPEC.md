# texturelab.io - Textures Made for You

## Problem

Imagine being a 3D artist trying to find the perfect textures for your scene/characters. Instead of going to a regular texture website (like https://www.textures.com/) to get boring static textures that might not be ideal for the vision you have in mind, you go to http://texturelab.io where you can find beautiful textures that you can tweak to your liking. All of our textures are made for a physically-based rendering pipeline, they're unity-ready and our public ones are CC0 licensed.
