# SPEC.md — USDPAY – Accept USDT online with non-custodial, wallet-to-wallet checkout

## Problem

USDPAY is a non-custodial USDT payment gateway for online businesses. It lets customers pay directly to your own wallet on TRON (TRC20), TON, or BEP20 while USDPAY verifies the transaction on-chain and notifies you instantly. You can use hosted payment links, a website checkout, Telegram bot payments, or a REST API with webhooks. Pricing starts at 0.9% per confirmed payment or 0.5% on Pro, with optional AML screening for risk scoring of incoming funds. View startup
