# SPEC.md — Readerbeam – Anything you read. Straight to your eReader

## Problem

I’ve been building Readerbeam, a simpler way to send web pages and files straight to your eReader.<p>It takes a public web page or file and delivers it to your Kindle as a readable document. Web pages are cleaned up and converted to EPUB. PDFs can be sent as-is, while images are converted to PDF and Markdown is converted to EPUB.<p>It currently supports:
- Web pages and PDFs
- EPUB, JPG, PNG, and Markdown uploads
- Drag-and-drop file uploads
- ArXiv papers, delivered as the original PDF
- GitHub READMEs
- Wikipedia, PubMed, Substack, and Medium articles
- Kindle device management
- Delivery history
- One free send per month
- A one-time lifetime Pro upgrade
The goal is deliberately narrow: get useful reading material out of the browser and onto an eReader.<p>I’d especially like feedback on two things:
- Whether the output quality is good enough for regular reading, and;
- Whether the one-time pricing model makes sense.<p>Thanks!
