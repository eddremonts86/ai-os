# Indie developers have nowhere to cheaply and effectively find the first 50-100 active users to test new niche products.

## Problem

The title states the gap: indie developers cannot cheaply find their first 50–100 active users for a new niche product. The post gives no niche examples, no definition of "active" beyond the bare word, and no budget anchor for "cheaply" — only the role (indie developer) and the milestone (50–100 active users).

## Objective

Give an indie developer a way to reach 50–100 active users for a niche product without spending meaningful money.

## Target Users

Indie developers (per the title) launching niche products who need a small, real user base for the first round of testing.

## MVP Scope

A simple web app where an indie developer posts their niche product, what they are looking for in a tester, and what they will give in return. Testers sign up; the developer sees a list of interested users and a way to contact them.
