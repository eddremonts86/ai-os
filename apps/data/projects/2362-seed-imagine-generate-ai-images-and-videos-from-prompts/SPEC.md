# Seed Imagine – Generate AI images and videos from prompts and reference photos

## Problem

Seed Imagine is a browser-based platform for creating AI images and videos from text prompts or reference images. It combines text-to-image, image-to-image, text-to-video, and image-to-video in one workspace, featuring multiple leading models and flexible controls for aspect ratio, resolution, and duration. Use it to create product visuals, character designs, and social content, then refine outputs, upscale images, and remove backgrounds before downloading. Start free with credits or choose paid plans for larger allowances. View startup
