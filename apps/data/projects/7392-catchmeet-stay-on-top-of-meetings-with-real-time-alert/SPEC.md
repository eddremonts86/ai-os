# SPEC.md — CatchMeet – Stay on top of meetings with real-time alerts, context, and answers

## Problem

CatchMeet is a real-time AI meeting assistant for Zoom, Google Meet, and Teams. It helps during conversations by quickly surfacing answers from your docs, tickets, or connected sources, fact-checking claims, and letting you switch focus safely with AI Delegated Listening when your active attention isn't needed. It brings you back in under half a second when required. After the call, you get summaries, transcripts, insights, searchable history, audio playback, and more. View startup
