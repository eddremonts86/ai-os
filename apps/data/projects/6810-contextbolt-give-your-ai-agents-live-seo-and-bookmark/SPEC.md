# SPEC.md — ContextBolt – Give your AI agents live SEO and bookmark data via MCP

## Problem

ContextBolt provides MCP-native servers that feed your AI agents data they can’t reach. It offers ContextBolt SEO for live keyword, SERP, and competitor metrics directly inside Claude and Cursor, and ContextBolt Bookmarks, a cross-network bookmark library your agent can search by meaning. You add a single line of config, ask questions in plain English, and collect answers on a shareable board, avoiding dashboards and lock-in while staying local-first. View startup
