# Branca.ai – Map assets and decisions across critical ops to act faster

## Problem

Branca builds dual-use operational software for defense and civilian markets. Its Heli.OS unifies trucks, ships, sensors, energy nodes, and other assets on a single tactical lattice, delivering authoritative data on reliable, warm hardware. Mira provides lightweight data integration with fast queries and a growing library of adapters, helping teams manage complex civil and defense environments. Branca emphasizes resilience, clarity, and keeping humans in the loop for decisive action. View startup
