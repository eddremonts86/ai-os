# I removed the explanation from my homepage and made users experience the product first

> Auto-enriched product brief.

## Value Proposition

A "show, don't tell" homepage pattern library with editorial review, plus a self-serve template that strips the explanation and replaces it with a live demo.

## Target Users

- Solo founders writing their first homepage and unsure what to put on it
- Marketing leads at small SaaS companies redesigning a stale homepage
- Designers looking for a homepage-pattern reference

## Jobs To Be Done

When I am writing a homepage and not sure what to put on it, I want a catalogue of patterns that lead with the product instead of explaining it, so I can stop guessing what to remove.

## Success Metrics

- At least 50 patterns reviewed in 90 days
- Self-reported conversion-rate lift above 5% on at least 25% of templates shipped
