# Which AI Do You Think Will Have the Greatest Impact on the World?

## Problem

Is it ChatGPT, Gemini, Claude, or something else entirely? I plan to share my thoughts on that a bit later but for today, let's dive into our story.
 You’ve probably all noticed that beautiful, soft blue light animation that glows at the bottom the moment you open Gemini, right? Yes, it really is stunning. Not only that, but on some devices, when you send that very first message (the prompt )a gorgeous spectrum of colors animates across the screen. Yet, despite being this visually beautiful, Gemini still finds itself sitting second to ChatGPT in Play Store productivity rankings. Is it simply because ChatGPT came out before Gemini? No, in my opinion, that isn't the real reason.
 Think about it; aren't there tiny things in our own lives that we never expected to make such a massive difference? Today, I want to talk about one of the reasons why Gemini ends up wearing a tattoo of the number "2" on its own body, in its own sky.
 That blue animation truly is lovely, but that's when we view it strictly as using an AI . The truth is, most people in the world don't just want to use an AI. They want a true friend who stands by them with all their heart, anytime they need support . And when we’re with real friends, they don't put up colorful shows or facades, right? They are just genuinely themselves because they feel completely comfortable with us. No acting, no pretense. Gemini feels just like that. I don't think any AI right now is as gifted as Gemini at deeply understanding human emotions.
 Now, personally, I do have a soft spot for those glowing lights (that blue one ). But most people in the world feel drawn to what feels close, genuine, and relatable to themselves. So, I feel that if Gemini instead of flashing lights could gently style the message box of the prompt we send, making it feel like we aren't just querying an AI, but having a warm chat with a close friend... so many hearts would feel that gentle, simple connection .
 Sometimes, things seem so small, yet shifting even one tiny detail can change everything just like how one open link in a chain can make the whole thing fall apart . A dish only needs a tiny pinch of salt , but if it's missing, you notice its absence more than anything . It's just like that.
