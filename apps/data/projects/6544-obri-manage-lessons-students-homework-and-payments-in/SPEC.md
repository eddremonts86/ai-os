# SPEC.md — OBRI – Manage lessons, students, homework, and payments in one portal

## Problem

OBRI is tutor management software built for independent tutors, online teachers, and small course creators. It centralizes student tracking, lesson scheduling, homework collection, payment tracking, and reporting in a single workspace. You can sync Google Calendar, send reminders, manage rates and debts, and generate reports for parents and yourself. Students get a secure portal to view lessons, submit assignments, and handle payments, helping you look professional and save hours each week. View startup
