# SPEC.md — SparrowCRM – Run your entire sales process with AI agents that move deals forward

## Problem

SparrowCRM is a CRM built for AI-first revenue teams to manage their entire sales process. It automates lead qualification, deal scoring, and pipeline hygiene, turning calls and emails into summaries, action items, and next steps. Sales agents handle follow-ups, research, and data cleanup while Zulie AI answers questions, prepares meetings, and automates tasks. Teams get conversation intelligence, reporting, sequences, and smart routing to prioritize the right deals and close faster with confidence. View startup
