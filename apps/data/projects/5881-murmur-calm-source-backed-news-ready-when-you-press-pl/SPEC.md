# SPEC.md — Murmur – Calm, source-backed news ready when you press play

## Problem

Murmur turns the day's news into short, calm audio briefings you can understand in minutes. It combines reporting from multiple publishers, groups coverage into event-level stories, checks claims across sources, and explains what changed, why it matters, and what it means. Choose your topics and briefing length, listen hands-free throughout the day, read full transcripts, and jump directly to the original reporting whenever you want. View startup
