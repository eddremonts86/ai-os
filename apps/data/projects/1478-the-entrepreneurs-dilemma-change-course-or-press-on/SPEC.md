# The Entrepreneur’s Dilemma: Change Course Or Press On?

## Problem

 How — and when — to question your goals.

 As a startup CEO, my tendency is to push through obstacles to achieve goals no matter what. And, to be sure, I can attribute a great deal of our success to building a culture that values not giving up. That said, sometimes goals get in the way, and the best course of action may be to change or abandon them. But how do you know if that’s a wise decision — or simply taking the easy way out? My husband and I are fortunate to have jobs we can do from almost anywhere, and five years ago we decided we’d had enough of New York winters. Wouldn’t it be awesome to move to Portugal, where it’s warm year-round, the hikes are gorgeous, and our two dogs could chase the odd rabbit?
