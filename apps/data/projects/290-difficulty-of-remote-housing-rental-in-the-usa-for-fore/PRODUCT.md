# Difficulty of remote housing rental in the USA for foreigners without credit his

> Auto-generated product brief. Review and refine before MVP scoping.

## Value Proposition

A foreigner relocating to the US presents a pre-qualified rental profile to a US landlord that includes verified identity, verified income, and a real guarantee — and signs a lease remotely before flying.

## Target Users

| Stakeholder | Why they care |
|---|---|
| Foreign F-1 / H-1B / L-1 relocating to the US | Needs housing secured before the plane lands; cannot pass a normal credit check. |
| US individual landlord or small property manager | Wants to rent to a non-US applicant without absorbing the risk of no-US-credit-history. |
| Foreign trailing spouse / refugee / asylum arrival | Has no US credit, no US bank account, and a lease starts next month. |

## Jobs To Be Done

1. **Functional job** — Sign a US lease remotely before flying, with a deposit the landlord accepts.
2. **Emotional job** — Stop the dread of 'we'll just figure it out when we land' that foreign movers carry.
3. **Social job** — Have a verifiable profile a US landlord can trust at a glance, equal to a US credit report.

## Success Metrics

- Pre-qualified-to-signed-lease conversion rate ≥ 25%.
- Lease-sign-to-fly average gap — measure median days between lease signing and US arrival.
- Landlord NPS ≥ 40 from US landlords who rented to a non-US applicant via the platform.
- Applicant NPS ≥ 50 from non-US applicants who completed a lease through the platform.

## Pricing & Monetization

Applicant fee: $79 per rental profile (covers international credit pull + verification). Landlord fee: 50% of one month's rent on successful lease signing (paid by landlord). Guarantor-substitute escrow service: 5% of annual rent, paid by applicant.

## Competitive Landscape

- Zillow / Apartments.com — listing platforms; no pre-qualification for non-US applicants.
- Insurent / TheGuarantors — US-resident guarantor services; do not cover non-US applicants.
- Rhino / Jetty — deposit-replacement products; US-credit-required for the underlying lease.

## Risks & Open Questions

- [ ] Landlord adoption — many small landlords do not know what to do with a non-US applicant. Mitigation: landlord education content + an applicant profile in a familiar US format.
- [ ] International credit pull coverage gaps — many countries have no consumer credit bureau. Mitigation: income verification + employer letter substitutes; lower confidence score, but still rankable.
- [ ] Fair-housing compliance — Mitigation: legal review of every marketing surface; explicit opt-in to fair-housing disclosure; audit log of any steering language.
