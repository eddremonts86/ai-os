# Pixx – Pick a restaurant your group will love in seconds

## Problem

Pixx helps groups decide where to eat without the back-and-forth. The app gathers each person's dietary needs, cuisine preferences, location, and budget, then uses AI to suggest restaurants that fit everyone. Create a meal plan, invite friends with a link, chat in real time, and vote to lock in a spot. Smart location finds a fair meeting point, and live updates keep the plan on track. Use Pixx on iOS or Android to turn indecisive group chats into quick decisions. View startup
