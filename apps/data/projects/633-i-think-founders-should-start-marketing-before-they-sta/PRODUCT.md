# I think founders should start marketing before they start building.

> Auto-generated product brief. Review and refine before MVP scoping.

## Value Proposition

A founder who validates before building currently does it in Reddit tabs, DMs and memory, and
ends the phase with a feeling rather than a record. This keeps the quotes, the framings people
actually used, and what came back from each post — so the decision to build or drop is made
against something written down.

The source frames this precisely: early marketing "doesn't feel like marketing, it feels more
like research". The product is shaped as research notes, not as a campaign tool.

**One-liner:** Keep the evidence from the phase before you build — what people said about the
problem, in their words, and whether anyone responded.

## Target Users

| Stakeholder | Why they care |
|---|---|
| Pre-build founder | Wants to know whether the problem is real before spending months on the solution, and currently has no record of what was said. |
| Mid-build solo founder | Named in the post: two months of building while wondering whether anyone wants it. Signal during the build is what they are missing. |
| Founder who has been burned | Has finished a product before and watched demand fail to appear, which is the belief the post is arguing from. |

## Jobs To Be Done

1. **Functional** — "keep what people said about this problem, in their words, with a link."
2. **Emotional** — replace two months of wondering with something to point at.
3. **Social** — be able to say "this is what forty people told me" instead of "I think there's a
 market", which is the difference between a claim and evidence.

## Success Metrics

- **Activation:** a first problem with at least five captured quotes. Below that there is
 nothing to compare and the summary view is decoration.
- **Habit:** captures added in a second week. This is the honest test, because the failure mode
 is a tool used once with enthusiasm and then abandoned to a notes file.
- **Outcome:** problems marked as decided — built or dropped. A validation tool that never
 produces a decision has not done its job, however much it recorded.

Reddit and DM threads. General note-taking tools hold the quotes but cannot group competing
framings or connect a post to what came back from it, and audience tools measure reach, which is
