# Helvestor – See your whole portfolio and understand it with AI

## Problem

Helvestor brings every broker, bank, and asset class into one dashboard, then uses AI to explain what you're looking at. See IRR/TTWROR performance, dividend forecasts, allocation by sector, country, and currency, plus an AI assistant that answers questions about your portfolio. It also tracks what top investors and politicians are trading and includes a planner showing when you'll hit your financial goals. Privacy mode lets you share your portfolio without revealing real numbers. View startup
