# Coming soon: validated problems for startups with a high chance of success. Our mission: 0% failed startups.

## Tech Stack

Next.js for the public website and founder dashboard. PostgreSQL for the problem and evidence tables. Meilisearch for full-text search across the corpus. Python + FastAPI for the analyst back-office. Stripe for the submission and refund fees.

## Architecture

Public site (problem browse, methodology page). Founder portal (submit, pay, status). Analyst back-office (queue, score, evidence upload). Each problem has a public page with: source, signals, timestamp, score, and analyst notes.

## Milestones

M0 — method page, three sample problems with full evidence. M1 — founder submission flow with payment. M2 — analyst back-office. M3 — first 100 paid submissions scored. M4 — public launch with the methodology page as the marketing anchor.

## Risks

The '0% failed startups' target is unattainable in any reasonable definition of failure; the platform is on the hook to define failure publicly. Analysts must be unbiased — accepting sponsorship from a submitter would corrupt the score. Founders may treat a passing score as a license to skip their own customer interviews.
