# SPEC.md — Twinly Lab – Create a face-to-face AI twin that earns from paid consultations

## Problem

Twinly Lab lets coaches, influencers, and experts create a face-to-face AI twin trained on their own content. Upload documents, courses, videos, and audio to ground responses so the twin speaks in your voice and stays accurate. Share a single link across platforms, and fans can start paid, real-time video consultations anytime. You set session pricing and receive payouts via Stripe Connect while keeping most of the revenue and controlling your data from an easy dashboard. View startup
