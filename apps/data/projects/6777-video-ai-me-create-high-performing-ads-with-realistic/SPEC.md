# SPEC.md — VIDEO AI ME – Create high-performing ads with realistic AI actors and voices

## Problem

VIDEO AI ME is an AI video generator for brands, marketers, and agencies. It turns a script and product photo into finished ad creative with hyper-realistic AI actors, cloned voices, and native lip-sync in over 70 languages. Choose from more than 300 actors or clone your own from a single photo, then auto-generate captions, trims, and B-roll for publish-ready videos in minutes. Plans include commercial rights and access to leading models, letting you test variants fast and scale winning ads across markets. View startup
