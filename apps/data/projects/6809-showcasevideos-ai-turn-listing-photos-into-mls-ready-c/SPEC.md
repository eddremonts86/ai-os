# SPEC.md — ShowcaseVideos.ai – Turn listing photos into MLS-ready cinematic videos in minutes

## Problem

ShowcaseVideos.ai creates cinematic real estate videos from your listing photos in about ten minutes. Upload up to 30 images or paste a listing URL, and its chatbot director handles virtual staging for empty rooms, applies natural camera motion, selects music, and renders formats for MLS, YouTube, Reels, and feeds.
