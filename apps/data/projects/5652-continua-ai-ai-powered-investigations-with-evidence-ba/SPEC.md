# SPEC.md — Continua AI – AI-powered investigations with evidence-backed analysis

## Problem

Continua AI is an investigation platform for professionals analyzing large, complex document and data sets with high accuracy and traceability. It works across contracts, financials, filings, spreadsheets, transcripts, audio, video, and other files, using full-context reasoning to identify relationships, inconsistencies, risks, and key findings while citing each claim back to its source. Designed for due diligence, legal review, compliance, healthcare, audits, and RFP analysis, Continua combines multiple AI models with a shared data layer to produce structured, evidence-backed reports and emphasizes privacy through zero document retention.
