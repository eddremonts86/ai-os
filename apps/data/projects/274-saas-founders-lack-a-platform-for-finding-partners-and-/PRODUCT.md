# SaaS founders lack a platform for finding partners and organizing joint advertising campaigns with bloggers to reduce costs by 5 times while maintaining the same reach

> Auto-generated product brief. Review and refine before MVP scoping.

## Value Proposition

Replaces the founder-network-and-DM pattern with a structured platform that finds non-competing partners, splits the budget, and attributions the result honestly.

## Target Users

USA SaaS founders (Seed-Series B) with $5k-$50k/month ad budgets; B2B SaaS bloggers and newsletter operators.

## Jobs To Be Done

When I want to run a joint ad campaign, I want a short list of non-competing SaaS partners with overlapping audiences so I do not have to network for weeks. When the campaign runs, I want a clean attribution split so both parties trust the result.

## Success Metrics

Joint campaigns facilitated per month. Customer-acquisition-cost reduction per campaign (vs. solo ad baseline). Repeat-partnership rate. Stripe Connect GMV.
