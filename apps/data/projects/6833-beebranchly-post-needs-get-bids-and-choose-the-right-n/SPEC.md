# SPEC.md — BeeBranchly – Post needs, get bids, and choose the right neighbor to help

## Problem

BeeBranchly is a people marketplace that connects neighbors and online helpers to get things done. Post a need with details and budget or mark it as a FreeBee, then receive bids from people nearby or available online. Compare profiles, reviews, and prices to pick the best match, coordinate the task, and rate each other after completion. Explore categories like errands, moving, tutoring, tech help, and more to hire help or offer your skills to others in your community. View startup
