# Almost Kiss – Play romance stories in chat where your choices shape the plot

## Problem

Almost Kiss delivers interactive romance stories played through chat. You step into the protagonist's role and message the love interest in natural language, without preset choices. It remembers what you share across episodes, so details like your fears or nicknames influence future scenes and dialogue. Episode One of MARKED is free to play in the browser, with additional titles like The Ruin of Miss Everly and Unmated planned for release. View startup
