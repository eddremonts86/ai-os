## Value Proposition

_Based on source brief:_ I am a developer with more than 10 years in the IT industry. I like to code because I get the feeling that it makes me sharper. Unfortunately, I am no longer getting this feeling because of the rise of LLMs.We are all, more or less, using the same LLMs. So I am no longer sure what the difference is between me and other developers.Before, I was proud of the quality of work that I did and the speed at which I was able to deliver it. Now I am just a reviewer of the code that was generated by the LLMs and trying to use my experience to guide it better.I don't have an unlimited budget for LLMs, and I'm guessing most developers don't either. I was thinking that the differentiation factor would be who can do the same job cheaper. My interest was to find the best-value LLMs for the job, in terms of pricing and intelligence.Because of this I built https://aicalculatorpro.com/. A website that doesn't need a sign-up and is free, so that other developers can have the same insight.The site is vibe coded, as you can see. I launched it in July.The page I personally use most is the LLM price changes page: https://aicalculatorpro.com/llm-price-changes/Over time, it will grow and become an audit log. Here I can see which new models are launched. I was also thinking that you can save money by switching providers, so for each model I list the providers. For example, this one: https://aicalculatorpro.com/models/zhipu/glm-5.3-flash/What do you think will be the differentiating factors now that we are all using LLMs?
