# If you could talk to an expert for 30 minutes before starting your business, what would you ask? I will not Promote

> Auto-enriched product brief.

## Value Proposition

A 5-minute expert-answer marketplace structured around the questions founders actually want answered.

## Target Users

- First-time founders who would pay $50-$200 for an expert answer in their industry
- Domain experts who want a low-friction way to monetise 5 minutes of advice
- Accelerators running founder-cohort sessions

## Jobs To Be Done

When I have 30 minutes with an expert, I want the question already vetted and the answer reusable, so my hour does not become a one-off conversation.

## Success Metrics

- At least 50 expert answers posted in first 90 days
- Founder satisfaction rating 4+/5
