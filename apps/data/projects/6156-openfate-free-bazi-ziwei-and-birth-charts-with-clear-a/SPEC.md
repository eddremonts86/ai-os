# SPEC.md — OpenFate – Free Bazi, Ziwei, and birth charts with clear AI readings

## Problem

OpenFate is a free Bazi chart and AI astrology site built around checkable calculations, not AI guesses. Enter your birth date, time, and place to generate a Bazi chart (免費八字排盤／八字命盤) with Four Pillars, Ten Gods, Five Elements, luck cycles, True Solar Time, solar terms, and boundary rules. Then get a clear AI Bazi reading. You can also create Ziwei Dou Shu (紫微斗數命盤), Western and Vedic birth charts, Human Design and Sukuyo charts, compare compatibility, ask follow-ups, and download reports. View startup
