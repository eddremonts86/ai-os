# MeetLive – Free unlimited live transcription and AI meeting notes with no bot needed

## Problem

MeetLive is an AI meeting assistant for Google Meet, Teams, Zoom, and Tencent Meeting with no bot joining your call. It offers Live Notes, rolling AI summaries that refresh every 2 minutes during meetings, so you can walk in late and catch up quickly. LiveTranslate provides real-time speech-to-speech translation in their own voice, a feature no other meeting tool offers. It is truly free with unlimited live transcription, no minute caps, no credit card required. Features include speaker ID, support for 60+ languages, AI summaries, action items, and an Ask AI function across all meetings. The free version is forever available; Pro ($9.99/mo) unlocks LiveTranslate, industry templates, and AI View startup
