# SPEC.md — MuseWork – AI assistant for research, analysis, and ready-to-use deliverables

## Problem

MuseWork is an AI assistant that turns goals, files, and instructions into ready-to-use deliverables. It researches information, analyzes data, and creates reports, presentations, documents, and webpages while managing multi-step tasks. Users can review results, provide corrections during the process, and continue working in the same context instead of starting over. View startup
