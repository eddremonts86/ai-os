# QuickEnrich – QuickEnrich is a B2B data enrichment tool with a free email finder

## Problem

QuickEnrich is a B2B data enrichment tool with a free email finder. You get 300 credits every month, plus a mobile phone and email finder API for real-time lookups. Built as a lightweight alternative to hunter.io or Apollo, QuickEnrich helps founders, sales teams, and RevOps find verified emails and phone numbers fast without the enterprise price tag. Enrich leads, clean CRM contact data, and power outbound campaigns with accurate, CCPA/GDPR-compliant B2B data. Whether you need a quick email finder or a fast enrichment API to sync fresh contact data into your CRM, QuickEnrich fits teams of any size with no credit card required to start. View startup
