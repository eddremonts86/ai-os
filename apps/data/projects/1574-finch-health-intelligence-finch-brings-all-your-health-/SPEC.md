# Finch health intelligence – Finch brings all your health data together and helps you understand it

## Problem

Finch is a patient-side health companion built for everyone, from expecting parents to anyone navigating the health system. Pregnancy alone involves at least 12 appointments, dozens of test results, and a new vocabulary from different providers in various formats. Finch brings this into one clear view, transcribes what was said at your appointments, and translates clinical language into plain English. Finch does not diagnose, triage, or recommend treatment. It helps you understand your own information and know what to ask your provider. A free tier is available; Finch+ costs $99 per year. View startup
