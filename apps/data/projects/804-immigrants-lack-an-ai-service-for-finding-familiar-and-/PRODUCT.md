# Immigrants lack an AI service for finding familiar and verified places (pharmacies, doctors, banks) in a new country, causing daily stress and time loss

> Auto-generated product brief. Review and refine before MVP scoping.

## Value Proposition

An immigrant opens the app, picks their host country and language, and sees a community-verified shortlist of pharmacies, doctors, and banks filtered by what their insurance actually accepts — without the hours-long cross-reference of Maps reviews and Facebook threads.

## Target Users

| Stakeholder | Why they care |
|---|---|
| First-90-days immigrants | Need a reliable, language-filtered shortlist of everyday-service providers without hours of cross-referencing. |
| Longer-tenure immigrants + expats | Want a maintained, community-curated map rather than stale Facebook threads. |
| Host-country community organisations | Want a credible place to point newcomers to instead of repeating the same advice. |

## Jobs To Be Done

1. **Functional job** — Find a verified pharmacy, doctor, or bank that speaks the immigrant's language and accepts their insurance.
2. **Emotional job** — Stop the recurring low-grade anxiety of not knowing whether a place is reliable or a scam.
3. **Social job** — Be pointed to a service by a community organisation instead of piecing it together alone.

## Success Metrics

- **Activation:** 70% of new users complete country + language + insurance profile within 5 minutes.
- **Retention:** ≥ 40% of users return within 30 days.
- **Vouch quality:** ≥ 80% of "verified" places retain at least 3 vouches 90 days after first verification.
- **Coverage:** ≥ 500 verified places across the 3 launch countries within 6 months.

## Pricing & Monetization

Free for the immigrant-facing directory. Sponsored placement (clearly labelled "Sponsored" + ranking weight cap) for vetted service providers who want to be surfaced higher in a category — paid placements cannot appear in the "verified by community" tab. Estimated €19–€49/month per sponsored place.

## Competitive Landscape

- **Google Maps + Yelp** — generic, no community-verification model, no language/insurance filter on top.
- **Facebook immigrant groups** — high signal per answer, no persistence, answers vanish into the feed.
- **Country-specific directories (e.g. Doctolib for France, Jameda for Germany)** — single-country, no cross-country portability for the immigrant audience, no community vouch model.
- **AI assistants (ChatGPT, Claude)** — answer questions but cannot vouch for a place or maintain a directory.

## Risks & Open Questions

- [ ] Validate whether the vouch model is enough to deter paid-review fraud or whether a manual reviewer layer is needed from day one.
- [ ] Confirm the first 3 launch countries should be Germany / USA / UK or follow a different order based on actual community density.
- [ ] Decide whether sponsored placement is the right monetisation or whether B2B contracts with community organisations are a better revenue line.
