# Farm vegetable delivery problem through mobile application

> Product brief authored from the source title and category. The poster's text was not available (source.name: manual); sections below re-state the problem and infer only what the title and category support.

## Value Proposition

A Japanese small grower opens one app on Monday morning, types what was harvested, and ships a box to each subscriber with one tap - no spreadsheet, no printer, no farm-stand queue.

## Target Users

- Japanese small growers (0.5-5 ha) who currently sell only at local markets.
- Neighbourhood subscribers within a Yu-Pack next-day radius of the farm.
- Farm cooperatives that want a shared app across 3-10 growers.

## Jobs To Be Done

1. **Functional job** - Sell weekly boxes without adding a delivery manager to the payroll.
2. **Emotional job** - Stop turning away neighbours who want delivery.
3. **Social job** - Look like a modern farm to a younger subscriber base that expects an app.

## Success Metrics

- **Pilot metric:** >= 30 active subscribers per pilot farm within 90 days.
- **Reliability:** >= 95% of weeks ship on the committed dispatch day.
- **Churn:** <= 8% monthly subscriber churn after month 3.

## Competitive Landscape

- **Tabechoku / Pocket Marche** - platforms that aggregate farmers; commission + per-order friction.
- **Manual LINE + Yu-Mail slips** - what farmers do today; loses the subscription signal.
- **Generic Shopify storefronts** - built for SKUs, not weekly harvest-driven boxes.
