# Convert PNG and JPG to clean SVG in seconds

## Value Proposition

PNG and JPG to clean SVG in seconds. Vectorify's title claim is the pitch: instant raster-to-vector conversion with a preview before any payment, paid per download via one-time credit packs ($3 for 5, $6 for 25, $9 for 50) rather than a subscription. The site's own promises round it out: editable SVGs for Figma, Illustrator and CorelDRAW, transparency preserved, and uploads not used to train public AI models.

**One-liner:** Convert PNG and JPG to clean, editable SVG in seconds — pay per download, not per month.

## Success Metrics

- **Conversion speed:** upload-to-preview in seconds (the title's claim).
- **Preview-to-download conversion:** share of previews that end in a download.
- **Output editability:** SVGs open and edit cleanly in Figma or Illustrator (the site's promise).
- **Transparency preservation:** transparent PNGs keep transparency through the flow.

Stated by the product site, not the post: one-time credit packs — $3 for 5 downloads, $6 for 25, $9 for 50 — processed by Paddle, with previews free and re-downloads of a saved SVG free.
