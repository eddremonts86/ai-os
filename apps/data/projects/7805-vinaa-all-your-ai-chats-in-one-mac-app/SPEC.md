# SPEC.md — Vinaa – All your AI chats in one Mac app

## Problem

Vinaa is a native Mac app for chatting with cloud and local AI models in one organized workspace. Connect OpenAI, Anthropic, and Google with your own API keys, or use Ollama and LM Studio. Organize conversations into projects and nested folders, switch models without starting over, reuse prompts and files, and import your ChatGPT or Claude history. Conversations are stored locally on your Mac, credentials are protected by Keychain, and optional sync uses your private iCloud account. View startup
