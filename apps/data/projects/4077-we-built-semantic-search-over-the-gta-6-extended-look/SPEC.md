# We built semantic search over the GTA 6 Extended Look

## Problem

You can try it here: https://tracker.gg/gta6/trailers/netflix-extended-look/We used SAM3 and Gemini Embedding 2 to capture a limited ontology over the latest GTA 6 preview. We've combined that with Gemini Embedding to allow users to search and find characters, vehicles, weapons and buildings that appeared in the preview.Gemini Flash was also used to produce descriptions of each shot and each scene.
