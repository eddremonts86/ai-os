# I built a free resume builder with $0 budget, what should I do next

> Product brief for the free resume builder and its $0-budget launch playbook.

## Value Proposition

A recent graduate can build a clean resume and export a PDF without paying for a resume builder and without learning LaTeX.

## Target Users

| Stakeholder | Why they care |
|---|---|
| Recent graduates / first-job seekers | Need a resume, do not want to pay. |
| Career switchers | Want a clean resume without LaTeX. |
| University career centres | Want a free tool for their students. |

## Jobs To Be Done

1. **Functional job** — Build a resume with structured fields and a live preview.
2. **Functional job** — Export a PDF that looks the same on every computer.
3. **Emotional job** — Stop procrastinating the resume because the tool is the blocker.

## Success Metrics

- **Activation:** first PDF export within 7 days of visit.
- **Retention:** at least 2 resume edits per active user per month.
- **Conversion:** none — the product is free.

## Pricing & Monetization

Free forever. No paid tier in v1.

## Competitive Landscape

- **Canva / Google Docs resume templates** — generic; not a builder.
- **Resume.io / Enhancv** — paid builders with paywalls.
- **LaTeX Overleaf templates** — powerful but a learning curve.

## Risks & Open Questions

- [ ] The product is free, so the only measure of success is traffic and word-of-mouth.
- [ ] The $0-budget playbook is the real deliverable, not the resume builder itself.
