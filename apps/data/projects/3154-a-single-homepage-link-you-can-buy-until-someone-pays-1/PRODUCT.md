# A single homepage link you can buy until someone pays $1 more

## Value Proposition

Buy the homepage link; hold it until someone outbids you by $1.

## Target Users

Curious HN readers and link-buyers who want to play a one-link ad auction for fun or promotion.

## Jobs To Be Done

Curious HN readers and link-buyers who want to play a one-link ad auction for fun or promotion.

## Success Metrics

Average hold time of the homepage link per cycle and total unique holders.
Number of bids per day after the first 30 days.

## Competitive Landscape

_Source does not name any competing product._ The post links mostexpensivelink.com only; no comparable single-link $1-increment auction site is named.

## Risks & Open Questions

Caching/CDN may keep showing the old holder's link briefly after a new bid; need cache-busting on transfer.
Payment-provider failure mid-auction can lock the link on the wrong holder.
