# SPEC.md — Mu, two-minute visualized lessons on modern AI

## Problem

Hi HN, I am Sreedath Panat. A year ago, I had started a habit of making small explainer videos of 2-3 minutes to help me understand, in a nutshell, some of the difficult-to-understand research papers. I&#x27;m not saying that this gave me a deep understanding, but it definitely helped me set foot into some of the research papers (eg: I-JEPA, flamingo paper etc). Then I thought, why can&#x27;t the same technique be used for getting a minimal understanding of different concepts within AI? There is a reason why I try to convert this thought into a simple product. The 2-minute micro lessons are fully built using extensive workflow I have set up in Claude Code with a good voice agent from ElevenLabs. I have extensively tested this myself. If I find the micro videos interesting or not, I found it extremely helpful, and after that, I decided to release the product. There are a couple of free starter courses that you can check out, and if interested, feel free to subscribe. Would love to hear your feedback also.
