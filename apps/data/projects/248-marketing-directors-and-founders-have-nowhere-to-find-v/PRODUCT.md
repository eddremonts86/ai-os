# Marketing directors and founders have nowhere to find verified contractors (targeting specialists, SMM managers) — the existing search process is a lottery that wastes time and budget

> Auto-generated product brief. Review and refine before MVP scoping.

## Value Proposition

A curated directory of marketing contractors — targeting specialists and SMM managers first — where each profile carries verification signals the buyer can trust before reaching out: identity, prior engagements, references the buyer can actually call, and outcome claims backed by evidence. The buyer shortlists from a smaller, vetted pool and stops running the lottery.

## Target Users

- Marketing directors at Russian SMBs and startups hiring paid-traffic or SMM contractors.
- Founders of early-stage Russian companies running their own marketing hiring.
- Agency outsource leads who sub-contract to specialists for overflow work.
- Senior contractors (targeting, SMM) who want a profile that proves their track record once and reuses it across engagements.

## Jobs To Be Done

- When I need to hire a targeting specialist this week, I want a short list of three verified candidates with references I can call, so I can shortlist without running a public test assignment.
- When a contractor claims a "3x ROAS in fintech" case, I want the case to be linked to a client reference who confirms it, so I can decide whether the number is real.
- When I am a senior contractor and I have done this work for years, I want a profile that signals my track record once, so I do not have to re-prove it on every new pitch.
- When I am a buyer burned by a bad hire, I want to see verification status before I send a message, so I do not repeat the mistake.

## Success Metrics

- Number of verified contractors in the directory per specialization (targeting, SMM) at month three.
- Number of inquiries per week that reach a verified profile.
- Reference-call conversion: share of inquiries where the buyer contacts a reference before engaging (proxy for the verification signal being used).
- Repeat contractor engagement: same contractor re-engaged by the same buyer within 12 months (proxy for trust holding over time).

## Risks & Open Questions

- Validate problem with 5 buyer interviews before MVP: confirm that the "lottery" framing matches what Russian marketing directors and founders actually experience, and that they would rely on a verified directory.
- Confirm willingness to pay on the contractor side: the source does not name a price; whether contractors will pay to be listed is an open question.
- Reference-check rigor: the verification depends on a human operator actually calling references. The cost of that call must stay below the listing fee; otherwise the unit economics break.
- Profile-fabrication risk: contractors will still try to inflate numbers. Verification must be re-runnable (not a one-time badge), and references must be re-contacted periodically.
- Avoid drifting into a generic freelance marketplace. The MVP is targeted at two specializations for a reason — broadening dilutes the verification signal.
