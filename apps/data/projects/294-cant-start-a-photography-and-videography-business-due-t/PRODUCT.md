# Can't start a photography and videography business due to lack of clients and sy

> Auto-generated product brief. Review and refine before MVP scoping.

## Value Proposition

A Kenyan photographer runs the entire business from a phone: portfolio, packages, M-Pesa booking, delivery, and balance collection, with a discover surface that brings the next client.

## Target Users

| Stakeholder | Why they care |
|---|---|
| Kenyan solo photographer/videographer | Has the camera; needs the system to convert inquiries into paid jobs. |
| Kenyan small studio (1–4 creatives) | Wants to coordinate bookings and split payouts without spreadsheets. |
| Kenyan event planner / corporate marketing | Wants a vetted roster to hire for shoots. |

## Jobs To Be Done

1. **Functional job** — Quote, book, deliver, and collect payment for a shoot without five separate apps.
2. **Emotional job** — Stop losing bookings because the inquiry sat in WhatsApp for 24 hours without a deposit link.
3. **Social job** — Have a portfolio link that makes the next client reach out, instead of a buried Instagram profile.

## Success Metrics

- Time from inquiry to deposit ≤ 24 hours median.
- Deposit-to-completion conversion rate ≥ 80%.
- Average bookings per photographer per month — ≥ 5 within 90 days of onboarding.
- Customer-rated portfolio NPS ≥ 50.

## Pricing & Monetization

Free tier: 1 active portfolio, watermark-protected previews, M-Pesa booking. Pro tier (KES 1,500/month): unlimited active packages, delivery galleries, WhatsApp automation. Studio tier (KES 4,500/month): multi-creative accounts, payout splits, event-planner roster listing.

## Competitive Landscape

- Instagram + WhatsApp — free but no booking, no payment collection, no delivery gallery.
- Pic-Time / Pixieset — strong gallery + delivery; no M-Pesa, no Kenyan discover surface.
- Squarespace + Calendly + M-Pesa — works but is 3 tools and 3 bills, no integrated delivery.

## Risks & Open Questions

- [ ] M-Pesa Daraja API integration complexity — Mitigation: start with C2B STK push; add B2C payout in v2.
- [ ] Portfolio scraping / image theft — Mitigation: signed Cloudinary URLs, watermark on previews, signed delivery URLs with short expiry.
- [ ] Discover surface cold start — Mitigation: city- and category-tagged SEO; partner with Kenyan wedding/event vendor directories.
