# OpenBudget – Connect your bank accounts to AI and get answers about your money

## Problem

OpenBudget connects US bank and card accounts to ChatGPT, Claude, and other MCP clients so you can ask questions about transactions, balances, debts, subscriptions, and budgets. It categorizes transactions, lets you add notes, split charges, and save rules through your AI assistant, and syncs data to Google Sheets or Excel. Plaid handles account connections, and OpenBudget never stores bank credentials. View startup
