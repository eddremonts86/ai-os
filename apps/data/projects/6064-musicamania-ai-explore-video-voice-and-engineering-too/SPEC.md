# SPEC.md — Musicamania AI – Explore video, voice, and engineering tools built for live precision

## Problem

Musicamania AI develops signal-focused products across video, voice, and engineering. It offers macOS and iOS apps for live video capture, color, replay, and overlays, plus a voice assistant that answers from your own files. For engineers, it provides THP coherence engines for aerodynamics, EV powertrains, structural health, and combustion analysis. The site unifies these tools under a clean catalog to help you choose and deploy the right solution. View startup
