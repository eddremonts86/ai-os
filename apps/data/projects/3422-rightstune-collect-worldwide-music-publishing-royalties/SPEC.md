# Rightstune – Collect worldwide music publishing royalties without giving up ownership

## Problem

Rightstune helps songwriters, artists, and producers collect their publishing royalties worldwide while keeping 100% ownership. The service registers songs across 200+ territories, tracks down missing royalties, and pays out monthly after the initial quarter. With transparent, low-percentage collections on mechanical, performance, and sync earnings and no upfront fee, it streamlines publishing administration for independent creators worldwide.
