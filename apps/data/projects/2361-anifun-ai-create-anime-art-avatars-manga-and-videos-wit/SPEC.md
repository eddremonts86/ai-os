# Anifun AI – Create anime art, avatars, manga, and videos with AI in seconds

## Problem

Anifun AI provides free, browser-based tools to turn photos, prompts, and clips into anime and manga visuals. You can convert selfies to anime avatars, design manga pages, translate and colorize comics, and generate anime-style videos with styles like Studio Ghibli and manga. It delivers fast results, watermark-free downloads, and privacy-friendly processing. It also includes image enhancers like upscaling, background removal, and extensions to refine creations without sign-up. View startup
