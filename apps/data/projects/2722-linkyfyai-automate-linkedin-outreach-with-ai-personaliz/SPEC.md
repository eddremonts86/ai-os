# Linkyfy.ai – Automate LinkedIn outreach with AI-personalized messages

## Problem

Linkyfy is an AI-powered LinkedIn and email outreach automation tool for founders, CXOs, and sales professionals. It finds the right prospects, writes AI-personalized messages, automates LinkedIn connection requests, discovers business emails, and runs coordinated multichannel campaigns across LinkedIn and email. Linkyfy is a secure cloud-based platform running on dedicated servers. It never stores your LinkedIn password, helping keep your account safe as you scale your outreach. View startup
