# AntSeed – Route any AI model through a self-hosted private P2P marketplace

## Problem

AntSeed is a peer-to-peer network for AI inference that removes the middleman between users and model providers. You run a self-hosted Virtual Private Router on your computer, point your existing AI tools to localhost, and route requests to 600+ models from 150+ providers by price, speed, reputation, or privacy. Payments settle on-chain directly to providers, and TEE-verified options keep prompts private. Anyone can provide capacity and earn without approvals or lock-in. View startup
