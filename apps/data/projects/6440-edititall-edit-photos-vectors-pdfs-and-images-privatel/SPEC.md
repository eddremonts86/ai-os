# SPEC.md — EditItAll – Edit photos, vectors, PDFs, and images privately in your browser

## Problem

EditItAll is a free suite of browser-based editors for photos, vectors, PDFs, and images. It includes a Photoshop-style photo editor with layers, blend modes, selections, and filters; an Illustrator-style vector editor with pen tool, Pathfinder booleans, and SVG import/export; and a PDF editor for organizing and annotating documents. It also converts and compresses over 20 formats. All processing happens locally on your device with no uploads, accounts, or watermarks. View startup
