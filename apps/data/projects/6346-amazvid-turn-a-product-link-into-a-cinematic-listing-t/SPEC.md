# SPEC.md — AmazVid – Turn a product link into a cinematic, listing-true video

## Problem

AmazVid is an AI video tool for e-commerce sellers that turns product links and stills into cinematic commercial clips with one click. Designed for Amazon, Etsy, Shopify, and Temu owners, it skips shooting, editing, and post-production so anyone can create listing visuals without technical skills. The output is up to 2K with 5–10 second clips—product shots, ad creatives, and promo footage that help increase conversion. View startup
