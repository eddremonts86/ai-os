# A freelancer often loses in proposal competitions due to the inability to quickly create personalized proposals — weeks of work wasted.

> Auto-generated product brief. Review and refine before MVP scoping.

## Value Proposition

Recover the proposal pipeline from the competitor who types faster. The first 1000 paying freelancers pay for the service from the recovered wins in the first month.

## Target Users

Australian and UK freelancers in design, dev, writing, and consulting who run a proposal pipeline and lose to faster competitors. Also small agencies with the same pain.

## Jobs To Be Done

Functional: produce a personalised draft in 10 minutes, not hours. Emotional: stop the dread of the proposal inbox. Social: be the freelancer who always responds on the same day with a quality proposal.

## Success Metrics

Average time to a sent proposal below 15 minutes. Win rate improvement above 20% (user self-reported). Less than 5% of proposals rejected as wrong-tone.

## Competitive Landscape

Trello and Notion are organisational, not generative. Refrens and Bonsai are invoicing, not proposal. No mainstream service combines a brief-to-draft proposal with the freelancer's voice and a per-client memory.

## Risks & Open Questions

Voice imitation may drift toward generic. Per-client memory must respect confientiality. The freelancer may over-rely on the service and lose the genuine edge. Rate-line auto-suggestions must respect the freelancer's pricing.
