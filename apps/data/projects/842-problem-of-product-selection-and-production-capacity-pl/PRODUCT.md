# Problem of product selection and production capacity planning

> Auto-generated product brief. Review and refine before MVP scoping.

## Value Proposition

Replace gut feel and Excel with a planning answer an owner can defend in two sentences: what to make, and how much.

## Target Users

| Stakeholder | Why they care |
|---|---|
| Primary user in India | Indian small-to-mid manufacturers and cloud-kitchen operators with a fixed production capacity and a portfolio of candidate SKUs. |
| Adjacent user | Anyone the primary user would need to coordinate with to use the product. |

## Jobs To Be Done

- When I plan next week, tell me which SKUs to make and in what quantity.
- When demand shifts, let me re-run the plan with the new numbers.
- When I review a plan, show me the bottleneck resource.

## Success Metrics

Whether a user can produce and defend a next-week production plan in under thirty minutes.
