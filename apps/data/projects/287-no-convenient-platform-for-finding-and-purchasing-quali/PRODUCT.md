# No convenient platform for finding and purchasing quality products from local fa

> Auto-generated product brief. Review and refine before MVP scoping.

## Value Proposition

A Russian household orders the week's farm-direct honey, dairy, sourdough, and seasonal produce in one checkout, with delivery from a real farmer whose face and farm they have seen.

## Target Users

| Stakeholder | Why they care |
|---|---|
| Urban Russian consumer | Wants farmer-market quality with supermarket convenience. |
| Small Russian farm (IP or small LLC) | Has product, has no time for online sales; wants a steady weekly order book. |
| Russian foodie / 'сельские продукты' buyer | Already drives to markets monthly; wants the same producers accessible weekly. |

## Jobs To Be Done

1. **Functional job** — Reorder the household's farm-direct staples without thinking about which farmer has stock.
2. **Emotional job** — Trust that the farmer is who they say they are, and the food is what they say it is.
3. **Social job** — Gift a curated farm box to a friend in another city without packing and shipping it yourself.

## Success Metrics

- Weekly active buyers — ≥ 40% of buyers place a second order within 30 days.
- Farmer retention — ≥ 70% of onboarded farmers list new products 60 days after onboarding.
- Order NPS ≥ 50 (measured at delivery confirmation).
- Average order value ≥ RUB 3,500.

## Pricing & Monetization

Marketplace commission: 12% of order subtotal, paid by the farmer on settled payouts. Featured-listing slot for farmers (RUB 4,900/month) for top-of-category placement.

## Competitive Landscape

- Lavka / Yandex Lavka — fast delivery, but commodity retail, not farm-direct.
- Instagram and Telegram farmer channels — high friction, no payments or shipping baked in.
- Existing marketplaces (Ozon, Wildberries) — allow farm listings but bury them; no farm-direct filter.

## Risks & Open Questions

- [ ] Cold-chain breakage in shipping — dairy and honey can sit in transit. Mitigation: SDEK/Boxberry shipping SLA monitoring; farmer-set shipping radius.
- [ ] Fake farm listings — someone re-sells commodity honey as 'farm'. Mitigation: passport + farm-photo verification on onboarding, mystery shopper QA.
- [ ] YooKassa payout holds — 7-day hold can strain a small farmer's cashflow. Mitigation: clear policy in onboarding; Pro tier after 50 completed orders can opt into faster payouts.
