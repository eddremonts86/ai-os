# SPEC.md — Best sentence corpora with native audio for language learning?

## Problem

I’m looking for good structured sentence and native audio corpora to acquire languages with flashcard and speaking repetition. In short, the Glossika service&#x2F;app has taken a turn for the worse and I’m looking for something else.<p>I’ve used and recommended Glossika for the mass-sentence method of language acquisition.<p>Unfortunately Glossika seems to be enshittifying like everything else. The new app forces recording; doesn’t have a way to only listen and repeat (e.g., for practicing while driving in the car); doesn’t have a simple list of the sentences you’ve learned; and seems to be leaning into AI generated content. And there are hints that the old version will be going away. It’s a big bummer.<p>Does anyone have recommendations for the best place(s) to obtain:<p>- mass sentences (base language &#x2F; target language pairs)<p>- curated for accuracy (not LLM-generated)<p>- with native audio (not TTS &#x2F; computer generated)<p>- curated in a logical and efficient structure (like Glossika’s old presentation of roughly 7000 sentences, separated into A1–C1 levels, that efficiently present and reinforce key grammar and vocabulary for each language)?<p>- Bonus points for included IPA transcriptions.<p>- I’m open to paid services but happy to put together my own system with Mochi or Anki.<p>I’m aware of Tatoeba but hoping for something better structured (i.e., French A1, A2, etc.).<p>On the paid side, I’m aware of Clozemaster and Flexling.<p>Thanks for any help you are able to offer!
