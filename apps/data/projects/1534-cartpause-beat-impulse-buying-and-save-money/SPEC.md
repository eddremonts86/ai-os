# CartPause – Beat impulse buying and save money

## Problem

CartPause puts a pause between "I want it" and "I bought it." Share any item from any store, set a timer, and see if you still want it once the urge fades. Most impulse buys don't survive the wait. Tap Share → CartPause. Name, price, and image are captured automatically. Choose your pause length: 72, 48, or 24 hours. When it ends, you decide: buy it or keep the money. It works with any shopping app or site. The free plan offers unlimited pauses, notes, alerts when a pause ends, and reflection prompts. The pro plan adds a running savings total, weekly and monthly insights, a Someday wishlist, and a Home Screen widget. It was built by an indie developer who struggled with impulse buying at View startup
