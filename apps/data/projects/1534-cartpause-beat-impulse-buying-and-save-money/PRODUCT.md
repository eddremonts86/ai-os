_Based on source brief:_ CartPause puts a pause between "I want it" and "I bought it." Share any item from any store, set a timer, and see if you still want it once the urge fades. Most impulse buys don't survive the wait. Tap Share → CartPause. Name, price, and image are captured automatically. Choose your pause length: 72, 48, or 24 hours.
