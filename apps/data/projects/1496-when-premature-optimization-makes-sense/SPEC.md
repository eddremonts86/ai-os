# WHEN PREMATURE OPTIMIZATION MAKES SENSE

## Problem

 We've all been told stay away from prematurely optimizing our code. Whether you're building for the enterprise or trying to get your first MVP out, chances are you shouldn't spend too much time tweaking your code for marginal efficiency gains. As Donald Knuth describes in his 1974 book, Computer Programming as an Art, "worrying about, the speed of noncritical parts of their programs, and these attempts at efficiently actually have a strong negative impact....". But how can we determine what will have a strong negative impact further on down the line? In this write-up, I will detail a small decision I made within my latest SaaS project Novelty, a social media marketing platform for Etsy sellers. The decision to fight premature optimization.
