# Export procedure solution for Pakistan to Europe trade

> Auto-generated product brief. Review and refine before MVP scoping.

## Value Proposition

A Pakistani exporter can paste a product description and an EU destination and get a single ordered checklist of the full export procedure, with the right documents attached, instead of piecing it together from TDAP, EU customs pages, and forwarder emails.

## Target Users

| Stakeholder | Why they care |
|---|---|
| First-time Pakistani exporter | Needs a guided path; current process is multi-agency and error-prone. |
| Small-to-mid Pakistani trader | Adding EU to existing Asia / Middle East business; needs a faster intake. |
| Trade consultant / forwarder | Wants a faster intake process with new clients. |

## Jobs To Be Done

1. **Functional job** — Get a complete procedure for "this product, this EU country" without a 2-week back-and-forth.
2. **Emotional job** — Reduce the dread of "did I miss a form?" before the shipment leaves Karachi.
3. **Social job** — Be able to share the procedure with a freight forwarder in a single link.

## Success Metrics

- **Time to first checklist:** median ≤ 60 seconds from product + country to the full procedure.
- **Checklist completion:** ≥ 60% of generated checklists have all steps marked complete within the shipping window.
- **Forwarder hand-off:** ≥ 30% of cases are shared with a forwarder via the in-app link.
- **Repeat usage:** ≥ 25% of exporters return for a second shipment within 60 days.

## Pricing & Monetization

Free tier: 2 active cases, document templates. Paid tier (USD/month per exporter): unlimited cases + forwarder hand-off + audit log.

## Competitive Landscape

- **TDAP (Trade Development Authority of Pakistan) PDFs** — authoritative but static, not product-specific.
- **EU Access2Markets / Taxation and Customs portals** — accurate but EU-centric, no Pakistan-export framing.
- **Freight forwarder intake calls** — slow and inconsistent across forwarders.

## Risks & Open Questions

- [ ] Confirm HS-code suggestion accuracy with a TDAP-licensed customs broker before locking the v1 list.
- [ ] Decide whether to bundle forwarder recommendations or stay neutral; the latter is safer legally.
- [ ] Define the data-refresh cadence for GSP+ lists and EU country VAT rates so the checklist does not go stale silently.
