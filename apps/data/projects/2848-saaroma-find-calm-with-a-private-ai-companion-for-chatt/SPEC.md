# Saaroma – Find calm with a private AI companion for chatting, journaling, and breath

## Problem

Saaroma is a private AI companion for everyday mental wellbeing. It listens without judgment, remembers what matters to you, and helps you untangle thoughts at your own pace. Chat to process your day, journal with on-device storage, and use short breathing and grounding exercises to reset. Each Sunday, it compiles a personal reflection and score from your week so you can spot patterns and focus on one next step. Your long-term memory is end-to-end encrypted and your data stays yours.
