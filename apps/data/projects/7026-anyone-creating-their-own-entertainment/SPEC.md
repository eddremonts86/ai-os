# SPEC.md — Anyone creating their own entertainment?

## Problem

This week I kept hearing animated sounds from my wife&#x27;s phone while she played a new puzzle game. She very quickly got upset with the app after it started demanding she watch more ads to continue playing.<p>Last night, Claude recreated the app - a single HTML page with a daily challenge, all-time stats, as many generated puzzles as she wants without ads. Oh and it&#x27;s cat themed this time (of course).<p>At dinner we started talking about whether people will start creating their own personal entertainment, beyond just simple puzzle games, like TV episodes, maybe entire TV series, maybe movies...<p>We were (somewhat in hesitation) imagining a world where you are on the commuter train home and an agent is working on a new episode to watch that night. Maybe starring Prad Bit.<p>Anyone actually creating their own personal entertainment at the moment?<p>EDIT: Economically this was quite stupid... Token cost was ~$25. With a subscription this barely ate up a single 5 hour window of usage, but, on an API cost basis this was sort of ridiculous to replace a free mobile game...
