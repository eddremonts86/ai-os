## Problem

The captured ProductHunt blurb for Mossy reads: "A desk plant that wilts. You write what the break is." The post positions the product around a felt absence — the moment in a workday when a break is needed and the user is not going to step away, so they instead type one line ("coffee refill", "look at the window", "stretch the back") and the wilting plant recovers. The metaphor is the entire job: the plant is a *physical proxy* for a recovery ritual that is otherwise invisible, and writing the break is the *acknowledgement* that turns a distracted half-hour into a logged one. The capture does not name any *frequency* rule (every break, every two hours, every Pomodoro), any *plant mechanic* (gradual wilting timer, monochrome desaturation, leaf droop animation), any *reward loop* (the plant greens back up, the plant grows a leaf, the day's breaks form a streak), or any *retention promise* (a calendar of plant months, a journal of break types). It states only the metaphor (wilting plant) and the act (writing what the break is).
