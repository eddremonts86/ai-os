# SPEC.md — Zagvo – Turn ideas into AI-ready build plans your coding agents can execute

## Problem

Zagvo helps builders turn a single idea into a complete, evolving product plan. It defines users and outcomes, creates phased roadmaps, generates builder-ready prompts, and maintains living documentation as the build progresses. Use it alongside Lovable, Cursor, Claude Code, Replit, Windsurf, and other AI tools while Zagvo tracks decisions, updates requirements, and surfaces the next highest-priority task to keep your product aligned from idea to launch.
