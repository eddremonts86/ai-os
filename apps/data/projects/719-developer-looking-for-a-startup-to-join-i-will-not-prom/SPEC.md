# Developer looking for a startup to join, I will not promote

## Problem

A Reddit ask-the-community post from a developer with 4+ years of experience in React, Next.js, Node.js, and React Native who has been trying to join early-stage startups with very little luck. They have searched Wellfound, LinkedIn, YC jobs, and a few other job boards; cold-emailed and Discord-DM'd founders, but most are either not hiring yet or never reply. They are asking for any communities, websites, Discord servers, Slack groups, or other places where founders are more active and open to talking with developers.

## Objective

Capture a developer-to-startup introduction map (job boards, communities, founder channels) the poster can use, without inventing hiring-volume claims, success stories, or named hiring partners the source did not state.

## Target Users

- Primary: the poster, a developer with React / Next.js / Node.js / React Native experience looking to join an early-stage startup.
- Secondary: other developers trying to break into early-stage startups through channels other than big-tech recruiting.

## MVP Scope

- A community-sourced short list of places where early-stage founders are reachable.
- A short list of what works in cold outreach (or does not), based on replies.

## Design Direction

See `DESIGN.md` for this project's design tokens.

## Constraints

- The poster's stack is React / Next.js / Node.js / React Native (4+ years).
- Channels already tried: Wellfound, LinkedIn, YC jobs, email, Discord — none yielded a role.
- No specific startup, geography, or compensation expectation is named.
- No willingness-to-pay signal — this is not a product brief.
