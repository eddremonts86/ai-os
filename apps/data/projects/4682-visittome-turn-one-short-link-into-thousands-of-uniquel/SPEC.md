# SPEC.md — visitto.me – Turn one short link into thousands of uniquely attributed clicks

## Problem

visitto.me delivers click attribution at scale so you see which link, source, and campaign drives results. Create a single destination on your own domain, then mint unique short codes or QR codes per visitor, placement, or source. Track who clicked, when, where, device, and roll insights up to campaigns. Use styled QR, smart routing, real-time analytics, CSV export, and a public API. Built with privacy by design, it keeps PII out of links and supports GDPR-ready controls. View startup
