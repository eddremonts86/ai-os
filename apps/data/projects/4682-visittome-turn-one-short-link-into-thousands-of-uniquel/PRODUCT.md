## Value Proposition

_Based on source brief:_ visitto.me delivers click attribution at scale so you see which link, source, and campaign drives results. Create a single destination on your own domain, then mint unique short codes or QR codes per visitor, placement, or source. Track who clicked, when, where, device, and roll insights up to campaigns.
