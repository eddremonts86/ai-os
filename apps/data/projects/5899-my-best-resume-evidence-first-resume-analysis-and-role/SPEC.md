# SPEC.md — My Best Resume – Evidence-first resume analysis and role-specific rewriting

## Problem

My Best Resume helps job seekers understand and tailor their existing experience. It extracts evidence from a resume, highlights strong achievements and missing context, and compares that evidence with a target role. Users review a short summary and lead-experience preview before completing the full resume, reducing drift and making the direction easier to correct. The finished document can be edited and exported as PDF, DOCX, or plain text. The workflow does not invent credentials, dates, employers, or numerical results, and it does not claim to predict ATS scores or hiring decisions. View startup
