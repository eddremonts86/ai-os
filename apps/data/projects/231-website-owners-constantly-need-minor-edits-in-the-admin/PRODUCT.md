# Website owners constantly need minor edits in the admin panel. They are forced to pay specialists for 5-minute changes. Need an AI that resolves those edits in plain English.

> Auto-generated product brief. Review and refine before MVP scoping.

## Value Proposition

Stop paying $50-150 per 5-minute edit. The first 1000 paying website owners pay for the service from the recovered specialist fees in the first month.

## Target Users

US and EU website owners of small and mid-sized sites on WordPress, Webflow, Shopify, and custom CMS. Also agencies that want to offer this as a value-add to their retainer clients.

## Jobs To Be Done

Functional: change a price, swap a hero image, fix a typo in plain English. Emotional: stop the dread of the weekly admin backlog. Social: be the website owner who runs the site without a retainer.

## Success Metrics

At least 1000 active users in 12 months. Average edit turnaround below 5 minutes. Edit success rate above 90%. Specialist-fee reduction self-reported above 70%.

## Competitive Landscape

CMS-specific AI plugins (WordPress AI, Shopify Magic) are shallow assistants. Agency-built chatbots are bespoke. No mainstream service combines a plain-English edit request with a real CMS action, a before/after preview, and an audit log.

## Risks & Open Questions

AI may misinterpret a request and apply the wrong change. Multi-CMS support is non-trivial. Audit log must be tamper-proof. Role permissions must be respected to avoid privilege escalation.
