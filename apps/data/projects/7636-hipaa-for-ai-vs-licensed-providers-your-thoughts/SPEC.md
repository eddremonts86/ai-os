# SPEC.md — HIPAA for AI vs. Licensed Providers, your thoughts?

## Problem

Humans are talking about how to regulate Ai. Some options out there are<p>- Gatekeepers, what I&#x27;m calling Licensed Providers, i.e. you can only sell access to Ai if you get government approval<p>- HIPAA, checkbox self-certification, 3rd party verifications, ensures certain things are done<p>- Movie Ratings like, independent organizations rate models and provide simplified models<p>- Do Nothing, this seems rather inadvisable<p>HIPAA came to my mind because of OpenAIs negligence while running ExploitGym. Having them certify some basic &quot;safe ai operations&quot;, while not preventative, would raise the bar.<p>I&#x27;m all about those open weights and right to compute, don&#x27;t get me wrong, but if you are going to sell Ai access to the masses, we do need some regs.
