# SPEC.md — QuickPod – Rent GPUs and CPUs with Jupyter ready environments, up to 80% less

## Problem

QuickPod is a marketplace for on-demand GPU and CPU compute. Pick a machine, get a Jupyter environment with TensorFlow or PyTorch already set up, and pay only for the hours you use, typically up to 80% less than big cloud providers. It is suited for fine-tuning, Stable Diffusion, LLM inference, rendering, and any batch job that needs a GPU for hours rather than months. GPU owners can list their hardware through the hosting program and earn from idle cards. View startup
