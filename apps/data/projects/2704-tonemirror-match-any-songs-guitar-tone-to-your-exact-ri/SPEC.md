# ToneMirror – Match any song's guitar tone to your exact rig

## Problem

ToneMirror matches any song's guitar tone to the gear you already own. Add your guitar, amp, and pedals once, then get knob-by-knob amp settings, pickup position, and pedal configurations tailored to your rig, plus technique tips and a clear match score. Browse thousands of songs and save favorites or setlists for quick recall. It adapts to pickup output, amp headroom, and your pedals, so you spend less time chasing tones and more time playing.
