# SPEC.md — FunParty – Collect guest photos via QR and stream them live on your TV

## Problem

FunParty turns any TV into a live photo wall for your event. Guests scan a QR code, choose a photo, and send it from their phones. Hosts control approvals, themes, motion, and pacing from the Host Hub to create a designed big-screen show. After the event, you can download all approved photos as a ZIP. Use it for birthdays, weddings, showers, family gatherings, and more with a TV or projector and a reliable internet connection. View startup
