# SPEC.md — WHVCapAlert – Cap alerts for the Australian 462 Working Holiday Visa

## Problem

WHVCapAlert monitors Working Holiday Visa cap statuses and emails you when your chosen country's status changes. It checks the official page every minute, around the clock, so you can apply promptly when paused caps reopen. You can choose one country for a one-time 31-day subscription with no auto-renewal. Stay ahead of sudden reopenings and avoid constant manual checks while planning your WHV application confidently. View startup
