# Difficulty finding relevant respondents for b2b research

> Product brief authored from the source title and category. The poster's text was not available (source.name: manual); sections below re-state the problem and infer only what the title and category support.

## Value Proposition

A Russian B2B research team specifies a buyer persona and gets a fit-scored panel of opt-in respondents booked onto their calendar within a week, instead of spending a week on Telegram and cold-email chasing for 30 interview slots.

## Target Users

- Russian B2B research agencies running 5-20 client studies per quarter.
- Russian in-house product and growth teams running 2-3 B2B discovery waves per quarter.
- Russian consultancies validating a market entry thesis with primary research.

## Jobs To Be Done

1. **Functional job** - Fill 30 interview slots for a target persona in one week.
2. **Emotional job** - Stop dreading the 'recruitment' week of every study.
3. **Social job** - Hand the client a participant list with a fit index, not a screenshot of a Telegram thread.

## Success Metrics

- **Time:** median time from persona spec to first booked interview under 5 business days.
- **Quality:** >= 70% of booked interviews reach completion with a fit index >= 60.
- **Repeat:** >= 50% of pilot research teams run a second study within 60 days.

## Competitive Landscape

- **Respondent.io / User Interviews / Schlesinger (international)** - capable but RU-resident panel depth is weak.
- **Manual Telegram + Telegram recruitment channel** - what RU teams use today; slow and unfittable.
- **Russian survey panels (Tiburon, OMI)** - decent quality; persona targeting is manual.
