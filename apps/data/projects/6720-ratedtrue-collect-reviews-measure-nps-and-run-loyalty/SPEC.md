# SPEC.md — RatedTrue – Collect reviews, measure NPS, and run loyalty rewards in one platform

## Problem

RatedTrue is an independent platform for e-commerce brands to collect verified reviews, measure customer loyalty with NPS, and drive repeat purchases with points, referrals, and rewards. It provides hosted review forms, a fast on-site widget with SEO-rich JSON-LD, verified-buyer badges, and merchant replies. Built-in AI moderation enforces FTC-safe handling of incentives and screens risky health and income claims. Pricing is volume-based, and merchants retain ownership of their data. View startup
