# Welche Herausforderungen hast Du? i will not promote — Design direction

Plain German newspaper layout. The poster wrote in German; the design should not feel translated.

- Surface: single-column form, public feed as a long list.
- Color: ink + one accent.
- Typography: serif for headings (Fraktur-friendly fallback to system serif), sans for body.
- No marketing illustrations.
