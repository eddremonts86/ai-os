# Welche Herausforderungen hast Du? i will not promote

> Auto-enriched product brief.

## Value Proposition

A workplace pain-point intake that ranks and dedupes so the same friction does not get reported 12 different ways.

## Target Users

- German-speaking employees and team leads who repeatedly hit the same workflow friction
- Internal-tools developers looking for demand signals
- Team leads doing process audits

## Jobs To Be Done

When I am asked to "build three wishes," I want a structured intake so the wishes are comparable across teams, so developers pick the friction that is actually most common.

## Success Metrics

- At least 200 pain points in the first 60 days
- At least 10% of submissions lead to a developer follow-up
