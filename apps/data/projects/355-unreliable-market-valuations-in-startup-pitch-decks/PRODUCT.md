# Unreliable market valuations in startup pitch decks

> Product brief authored from the source title and category. The poster's text was not available (source.name: manual); sections below re-state the problem and infer only what the title and category support.

## Value Proposition

A Russian founder types a market question, gets TAM/SAM/SOM with a primary source + a 'last-checked' date for every number, and exports a citation pack the investor can read in two minutes - instead of inheriting 2018 numbers from a deck template.

## Target Users

- Russian founders preparing a Series-A or pre-seed deck who need defensible market numbers.
- Russian-speaking VCs running diligence on a deck and wanting a third-party check on the numbers.
- Russian student pitch-club participants building a serious deck before a competition.

## Jobs To Be Done

1. **Functional job** - Put defensible numbers in a deck without inheriting unverified figures.
2. **Emotional job** - Stop worrying about an investor asking 'where did this number come from?'.
3. **Social job** - Hand a co-founder or accelerator mentor a citation pack they can defend.

## Success Metrics

- **Activation:** first citation pack ready within 30 minutes of signup.
- **Coverage:** >= 80% of common TAM queries for the Russian market return at least one primary-source figure.
- **Defensibility:** >= 90% of pilot founders report zero investor pushback on citations.

## Competitive Landscape

- **Statista / Euromonitor** - paid; the citation-grading is the new layer.
- **Manual web search + spreadsheet** - what founders do today; inherits and decays.
- **Generic AI assistants** - produce numbers without source verification; this product does not.
