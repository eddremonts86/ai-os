# SPEC.md — Skywarden – Smart astrophotography planning companion

## Problem

I don&#x27;t get too many clear nights over at my place and when they happen, I usually spend quite some time looking for the best deep sky object to capture.<p>Skywarden is my solution to this problem, a smart planning companion with catalogs of over 7000 objects, evaluating and rating them based on my location, weather and equipment, to recommend the best targets for the night.<p>Check <a href="https:&#x2F;&#x2F;adfr.io&#x2F;projects&#x2F;skywarden" rel="nofollow">https:&#x2F;&#x2F;adfr.io&#x2F;projects&#x2F;skywarden</a> for all the features. Mobile app is coming soon too.
