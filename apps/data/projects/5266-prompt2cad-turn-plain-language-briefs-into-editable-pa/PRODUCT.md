## Value Proposition

_Based on source brief:_ Prompt2CAD lets you describe objects in natural language and turns them into real parametric CAD models you can adjust and export. It guides you through planning, asks for missing details, builds geometry with constraints, and supports quick refinements via controls or chat. You can export STEP, DXF, STL, GLB, and more, and generate a geometry-based parts list for estimating and fabrication. Use it for custom furniture, fixtures, mechanical parts, and product designs without prior CAD experience. View startup
