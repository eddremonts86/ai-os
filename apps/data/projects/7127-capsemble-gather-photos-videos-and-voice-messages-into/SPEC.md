# SPEC.md — Capsemble – Gather photos, videos, and voice messages into a private celebration space

## Problem

Capsemble brings guests into the storytelling of your celebration by collecting photos, videos, and voice messages into a single, private place. Hosts control privacy and storage, invite contributors without requiring an app, and moderate content shown on live walls during events. Capsemble helps shape contributions into albums and recap films, tailored for private celebrations, organized events with live displays, and destination weddings with itineraries and guest passes.
