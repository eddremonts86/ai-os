# EDN – macOS workspaces that remember where you left your windows

## Problem

I love FlashSpace, but wanted each virtual workspace to remember and restore its own window layout. I built EDN to solve that. Add the apps you want to a workspace, assign a hotkey, and switch between workspaces with your windows where you left them. It’s a very early build, but I’d love some feedback.
