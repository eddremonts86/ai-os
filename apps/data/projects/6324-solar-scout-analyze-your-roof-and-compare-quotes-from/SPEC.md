# SPEC.md — Solar Scout – Analyze your roof and compare quotes from vetted NZ solar installers

## Problem

Solar Scout helps New Zealand homeowners plan and buy residential solar with confidence. It analyzes your roof using aerial data and government datasets to estimate panel capacity, savings, and payback. It then matches you with up to three vetted, EWRB-registered installers for side-by-side quotes. The site also offers tools to compare batteries, panels, inverters, and buy-back rates, plus guidance on green loans and regional solar factors for clear, cost-effective decisions. View startup
