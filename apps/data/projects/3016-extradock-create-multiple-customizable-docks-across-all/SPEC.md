# ExtraDock – Create multiple customizable docks across all your Mac screens

## Problem

ExtraDock lets you create multiple lightweight docks on macOS and place them anywhere across your displays. You can customize each dock's colors, size, icons, and behavior. Choose horizontal or vertical layouts that snap to edges or float freely. Use widgets like Live Dock to mirror the native Dock and Dock Awareness to show apps by Space. Assign docks to specific monitors and set them to auto-hide or collapse to a button. You can replace the native Dock entirely or run them side by side for a faster workflow. View startup
