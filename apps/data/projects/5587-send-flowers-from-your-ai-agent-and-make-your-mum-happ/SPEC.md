# SPEC.md — Send flowers from your AI agent and make your mum happy

## Problem

Hi,<p>I have created an MCP for AI agents to send flowers for your mum’s birthday, for a great customer who just renewed his contract or what ever you can think of!<p>I created this after working on a family AI assistant (hermo.ai) which desperately needed to trigger something tangible - like a flower delivery.<p>You can send flowers without having to create an account to make it easy to get started.<p>Currently you can send one type of bouquet in the USA ($100), UK(£100), Germany(€100), Switzerland and Italy.<p>Link to the new service is here: <a href="https:&#x2F;&#x2F;preznt.net" rel="nofollow">https:&#x2F;&#x2F;preznt.net</a><p>The goal of this service is to have AI agents send flowers. It’s pretty simple.<p>I have lots of ideas how to extend and order all sorts of things directly from an MCP, like chocolates, gift boxes, etc.<p>Let me know what you think! I&#x27;d really like some feedback.<p>Fabian
