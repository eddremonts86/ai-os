# Pick China Directory – Explore Chinese internet platforms with clear English guides and links

## Problem

Pick China Directory maps the Chinese internet for global readers by curating leading platforms across social, video, e-commerce, AI, gaming, and more. It explains each service in accessible English, highlights Western equivalents, and links directly to official sites so you can navigate the ecosystem confidently. Use category pages to discover top apps and understand how giants like Tencent, Alibaba, and ByteDance shape China's distinct online landscape. View startup
