# Talk Like Claude Day

## Problem

In the US we have a joke holiday, "Talk like a Pirate Day". I propose "Talk like Claude day". I wrote this, it is not LLM generated.Yes, this is actually people talking like a computer, not a computer talking like people. Sit with that for a moment.There are two outcomes for production use, and both are very bad.*Emphasis Inflation*. Humans will be required to use lots of *bold stuff* in written messages, and in spoken conversation there will be more inflection. The potential negative outcomes for carpal tunnel syndrome, voice-to-text feature gaps, and laryngitis must all be buttoned down before production use. This is a *very* *large* *unknown* across multiple physical dimensions. It's worth thinking about.*Physical Violence*. A person who begins to read a post with an expectation of gaining insight or knowledge is known to experience feelings of betrayal when they realize it is LLM-generated. This is fact, not conjecture. A sudden rise in apparently LLM-generated content from otherwise trusted sources could lead to a spike in feelings of betrayal past a tipping point where otherwise reasonable people become violent.*Reputational Damage*. Those who participate in "Talk like Claude Day", in an environment where the holiday is not known, or not known to be a joke, may experience reputational damage if they invoke feelings of betrayal in their audience. This is a real adoption risk, really a dead stop.One thing worth noting here, I said two very bad outcomes but I listed three. That's on me. I've saved a memory to be more careful with off-by-one errors.I considered adding an em-dash, but decided to rise above the obvious. It's up to the readers to decide if I should put one in.One thing I flagged while writing this post: we never defined what is meant by a "day". Is this a 24 hour cycle or a sun-up to sun-down? If we do 24 hours, are we going by GMT worldwide, or a rolling 24 hour period for local 00:00 - 23:59?Would you like me to write up the complete description of this holiday along with the open questions on precise day definitions and the risks to health, including potential violent behavior?
