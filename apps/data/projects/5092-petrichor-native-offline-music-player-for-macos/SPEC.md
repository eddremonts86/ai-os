# SPEC.md — Petrichor – Native offline music player for macOS

## Problem

Over a year ago I shared about Petrichor on HN[1] while it was still in alpha, it started out as a hobby for me and has now grown into a mature product while staying free and open source[2]. I&#x27;ve just released v1.7 that added a whole bunch of features that I originally envisioned for this app. It remains free and open source, doesn&#x27;t do any usage tracking or analytics, and still strives to be one of the best music players available on the platform.<p>Features:<p>- Everything you&#x27;d expect from an offline music player!<p>- Supports more than 40 audio file formats.<p>- Map your music folders and browse your library in an organized view.<p>- Stream Internet Radio and organize saved stations into collections.<p>- Synced lyrics of a playing track when available, including ability to download missing lyrics.<p>- Create, import or export playlists.<p>- Smart playlists with conditional, negative, and regular-expression rules.<p>- Manage the play queue interactively using drag and drop.<p>- Browse music using folder view when needed.<p>- Pin anything (almost!) to the sidebar for quick access to your favorite music.<p>- Navigate easily: right-click a track to go to its album, artist, year, etc.<p>- Native macOS integration with menubar and dock playback controls, plus dark mode support.<p>- Miniplayer and immersive mode.<p>- Gapless playback, Crossfade, ReplayGain volume normalization, Equalizer, Spatial Audio, and AirPlay 2 support.<p>- Last.fm scrobbling support.<p>- Shortcuts and Automation API support along with Siri.<p>- Works well with large libraries containing thousands of songs.<p>[1]: <a href="https:&#x2F;&#x2F;news.ycombinator.com&#x2F;item?id=44515266">https:&#x2F;&#x2F;news.ycombinator.com&#x2F;item?id=44515266</a><p>[2]: <a href="https:&#x2F;&#x2F;github.com&#x2F;kushalpandya&#x2F;Petrichor" rel="nofollow">https:&#x2F;&#x2F;github.com&#x2F;kushalpandya&#x2F;Petrichor</a>
