# Luten – Sound that learns what calms and focuses you

> Brief derived from the source post. No facts added beyond what the post asserts.

## Value Proposition

Tell Luten how you feel. Get a soundscape. Luten learns. Nothing leaves your phone.

## Target Users

| Stakeholder | Why they care |
|---|---|
| Insomnia / ADHD adult | A personalised soundscape beats a generic playlist. |
| Parent | No ads, no talking, no course upsell — kid-safe. |
| Focus worker | A loop that does not break concentration is the value. |

## Jobs To Be Done

1. **Functional job** — find a soundscape that matches how they feel right now.
2. **Emotional job** — feel the app is learning them, not serving them a generic list.
3. **Social job** — trust the app with sensitive feelings because nothing leaves the device.

## Success Metrics

- **Activation:** % of downloads that complete the feelings-input flow within 7 days.
- **Retention:** weekly active users; subscription retention past the trial.
- **Revenue:** $59.99/yr or $119.99 lifetime; conversion from the 16-free-sounds tier.

## Competitive Landscape

- Calm / Headspace: subscription-heavy, talking-led, cloud-dependent.
- Endel: algorithmic soundscapes; on-device is not a stated stance.
- Apple Health sleep sounds: free, generic, not personalised.

## Risks & Open Questions

- Apple NaturalLanguage on-device is the privacy claim; the model must be auditable.
- Learning loop must not leak signals into telemetry; the user-trust contract is the product.
- Sixteen sounds is a thin MVP for the launch tier; content expansion is the obvious next move.
- Lifetime tier creates revenue front-loading risk; the post does not address it.
