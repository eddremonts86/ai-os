# Audited every post I'd made for my SaaS. The problem was never the writing.

## Value Proposition

A scoring system for small SaaS founders who are doing their own distribution and want to know which surface actually returns the time. Generic distribution content in this space is mostly 'post every day' or 'comment on every thread' advice. This one is grounded in the post: posts +143 across two attempts, comments +5 across nearly twice as many attempts — the named asymmetry.

## Target Users

Primary: a small SaaS founder (1-3 people) who is doing their own distribution across Reddit, LinkedIn, X, Hacker News, and Indie Hackers and wants to know which surface is actually returning the time. Secondary: a first marketing hire at the same kind of company who is being asked to replicate the founder's distribution and needs the measurement framework.

## Jobs To Be Done

When I (a small SaaS founder doing my own distribution) am wondering whether my posting and commenting are returning the time, give me a scoring system and the named channel-shape insight so I can stop treating posts and comments as the same channel and put my time on the surface that actually returns it.

1. The poster (or a comparable founder) can name, after one read, the score difference between posts and comments and the named pattern behind it.
2. A founder can defend the post-vs-comment time split in the next planning meeting with a specific number.
3. The founder's distribution-research time after reading the output is shorter than the time they would have spent cold.
