# Orval – AI receptionist that answers calls, books appointments, and captures leads

## Problem

Orval is an AI receptionist that answers calls, books appointments and captures leads around the clock. The BetaList capture describes natural, human-like conversations that check live calendar availability, send SMS confirmations, and route complex inquiries to the team with context. Named integrations are Outlook, Freshdesk, Zendesk, Calendly and Jobber — a mix that suggests small service businesses. Pricing starts at £19.99/month with no setup fees or contracts, which the listing positions against the cost of a human answering service.

## Objective

Replace the missed call with a qualified outcome: an AI receptionist that picks up every call, holds a natural conversation, books real appointments against live calendar availability, confirms by SMS, and hands complex inquiries to the team with the conversation context attached.

## Target Users

- Small service businesses (the Jobber, Calendly and Freshdesk mix points at trades, salons, clinics) that miss calls during work.
- Teams using Outlook calendars who want calls turned into booked slots.
- Businesses currently paying a human answering service — the comparison the pricing explicitly makes.

## MVP Scope

- 24/7 call answering with natural, human-like conversation.
- Live calendar availability checks before booking.
- Appointment booking with SMS confirmation.
- Routing of complex inquiries to the team with context.
- Integrations: Outlook, Freshdesk, Zendesk, Calendly, Jobber.
- Pricing from £19.99/month, no setup fees, no contracts.

## Constraints

- The capture is a BetaList listing; voice quality and human-like behavior are vendor claims.
- Five named integrations bound the MVP; anything beyond them is new work.
- Telephony is the infrastructure risk: call quality, latency and pickup reliability decide the product.
- The £19.99/month entry price is stated; everything about what it includes is not.
