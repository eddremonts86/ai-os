# Orval – AI receptionist that answers calls, books appointments, and captures leads

## Value Proposition

Never miss the call that books the job. Orval answers calls 24/7 with natural conversation, checks live calendar availability, books the appointment and confirms by SMS, and routes the complicated inquiries to your team with full context — for less than a human answering service, from £19.99/month with no setup fees or contracts.

**One-liner:** An AI receptionist that answers calls, books appointments and captures leads, around the clock.

## Target Users

| Stakeholder | Why they care |
|---|---|
| Small service businesses | Missed calls become booked jobs, even outside opening hours. |
| Teams on Outlook and Calendly | Bookings land in the live calendar, not a disconnected system. |
| Support teams on Freshdesk and Zendesk | Complex calls arrive as tickets with conversation context. |
| Jobber users | The receptionist fits their existing field-service workflow. |

## Jobs To Be Done

1. **Functional job** — Answer every inbound call with a natural, human-like conversation.
2. **Functional job** — Check live calendar availability and book the appointment on the spot.
3. **Functional job** — Send SMS confirmations so both sides have the booking.
4. **Functional job** — Route complex inquiries to the team with context attached.

## Success Metrics

- **Answer rate:** share of inbound calls answered, including off-hours — the core replacement for a missed call.
- **Booking conversion:** share of answered calls that become calendar bookings.
- **SMS confirmation delivery:** share of bookings confirmed by SMS within minutes.
- **Handoff quality:** complex inquiries routed with context, rated useful by the receiving team.

## Risks & Open Questions

- [ ] Voice AI in real calls: latency, accents and noisy environments can break the human-like promise quickly.
- [ ] Booking errors are costly in the field-service world; a double-booked slot costs trust, not just money.
- [ ] The £19.99 entry price must cover telephony and model costs per call, or the unit economics invert.
- [ ] Five integrations are named; keeping them all healthy is ongoing maintenance, and the capture names no others.
- [ ] Regulation around call recording and consent varies by region and is not mentioned.
