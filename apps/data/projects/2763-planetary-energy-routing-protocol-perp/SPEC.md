# Planetary Energy Routing Protocol (PERP)

## Problem

Designed to balance global energy supply and demand in real time without requiring a centralized corporate or government monopoly. By treating global climate stabilization as a system-wide optimization problem, PERP aims to connect emerging Space-Based Solar Power (SBSP) arrays and local terrestrial microgrids into a single, self-balancing energy pool.
