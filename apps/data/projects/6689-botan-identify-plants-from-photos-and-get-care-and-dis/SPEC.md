# SPEC.md — Botan – Identify plants from photos and get care and disease guidance

## Problem

Botan is a plant identification and care app that recognizes plants from photos and guides you through treatment and upkeep. It detects diseases and pests, checks toxicity, and builds step-by-step care plans with reminders so you water, fertilize, and prune on time. Browse a database of 30,000+ species and chat with a botanist when you need advice. Use it to identify houseplants, trees, succulents, and more, then keep them healthy with clear, science-based tips.
