# SPEC.md — GroundWorks – Project management built for the field, not the office

## Problem

GroundWorks brings jobs, crews, equipment, test holes, permits, and locate tickets into one place. It's built for excavation, utilities, and the trades, and you can configure it to match how your industry actually works. You can configure job types, pipelines, and field data to match how your crews work, then track daily logs, approvals, and documents right on each job. Enforce workflow gates so work only advances when permits and locates are cleared, and monitor assets with rates and assignments so costs reflect what's deployed in the field. View startup
