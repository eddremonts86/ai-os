# SPEC.md — Byosynch – Continuous two-way file sync over SSH for Mac and Linux

## Problem

Disclaimer: I&#x27;m the developer of byosynch.<p>Long time Dropbox customer. Really like its simplicity and convenience, but a couple things always bothered me: their lackluster command line support, and the fact that they have my files.<p>Byosynch tries to fix these gaps (first-class CLI support, backing store of your choice, privacy-friendly) while being easy to use.<p>We currently support macOS and Linux, with an iOS client coming soon.<p>This is a paid service, designed to finance continued development.<p>Happy to answer any questions and hear your feedback!
