# Is less, more?

> Auto-enriched product brief.

## Value Proposition

A weekly time-cap coaching tool for indie founders, with reflection prompts and an opt-in public log.

## Target Users

- Indie founders with a working app who feel they are spending too much time on it
- Solo developers burning out on a side project
- Bootcamp / accelerator participants who want a sustainable pace

## Jobs To Be Done

When I am spending too much time on my app and not seeing traction, I want a weekly time cap with reflection prompts, so I stop stress-looping and start focusing on what actually moves the needle.

## Success Metrics

- At least 100 founders set a cap in pilot
- Self-reported "I am less anxious about my app" rate above 50%
