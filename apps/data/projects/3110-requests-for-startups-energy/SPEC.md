# Requests for Startups: Energy

## Problem

The poster links to YC's Requests for Startups page and approves of it, but argues that "energy" is conspicuously missing from the list. They argue cheap and reliable energy access is a bottleneck on the development of many sectors in a region, especially in the AI era. They note that projects to solve the energy problem already exist (fusion, space-based solar, advanced nuclear) but that the problem is far from solved. They ask why YC does not call energy out as an RFS category.

## Objective

There is no product idea in the post. The poster is making an editorial argument.

## Target Users
