# PairBook – correlation and holdings overlap for +4,700 US stocks/ETFs

## Problem

The Show HN post links to https://www.pairbook.io/ but the scraper captured only the link. From the title alone, PairBook is a tool that shows correlation and holdings overlap for US stocks and ETFs (over 4,700 instruments named in the title).

## Objective

Help investors see which stocks or ETFs move together and which hold the same underlying names, without doing the math in Excel.

## Target Users

Retail and professional investors building diversified portfolios who care about correlation and hidden overlap between positions.

## MVP Scope

A web app that, for any pair of US-listed stocks or ETFs, returns a correlation number and a holdings-overlap table.
