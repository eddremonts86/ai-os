# Solo founder building FetchSandbox, no network. How are you actually getting VC intros?

> Auto-enriched product brief.

## Value Proposition

A solo-founder-friendly VC directory with transparent warm-intro paths and explicit "no network" guidance for every VC.

## Target Users

- Solo founders at pre-seed with no prior VC network
- First-time fundraisers who do not know how warm intros work
- Founders in niche geographies who feel excluded from SF/NYC networks

## Jobs To Be Done

When I am a solo founder at pre-seed with no VC network, I want a directory that tells me how to reach each VC without a warm intro, so I stop guessing whether cold outreach will work.

## Success Metrics

- At least 50 founders use the directory in pilot
- Self-reported "I sent my first cold email to a VC" rate above 30%
