# need help with outreach at a "local" AI law startup (i will not promote)

> Auto-generated product brief. Review and refine before MVP scoping.

## Value Proposition

A professional SaaS in one language cannot buy its way to the people who would be power users.
Ad platforms sell job titles and keywords; the poster needs practitioners who work with documents
all day, in one country, and there is no field for that. This builds the list from where
professional identity is already public, qualifies it on observable work traits, and puts a cost
per qualified person reached on the spend.

**One-liner:** Turn "throw money at it" into a list of practitioners who actually do the work,
with a defensible basis for contacting them.

## Target Users

| Stakeholder | Why they care |
|---|---|
| Founder of a small-language professional SaaS | Organic is one keyword and one dead news article; there is no dial to turn and no list to buy. |
| Growth lead with a narrow ICP | Needs practitioners qualified by how they work, not by job title, which is the only field ad platforms expose. |
| Anyone selling into a regulated profession | Needs the legal basis for contact recorded alongside the contact, or the list is a liability. |

## Jobs To Be Done

1. **Functional** — "give me a list of lawyers in my country who work with documents all day, and
 a defensible way to contact them."
2. **Emotional** — replace putting off a Reddit post with a step that can be taken today.
3. **Social** — reach practitioners as a company rather than as the young founder story that
 worked once and no longer fits.

## Success Metrics

- **Coverage:** share of a profession's public register resolved into unique practitioners. Below
 a high number the list is a sample, and the poster's market is too small for sampling.
- **Qualification precision:** share of contacted practitioners who turn out to be
 document-heavy, checked by hand on a sample. This is the claim the product makes and the only
 one worth measuring early.
- **Cost per qualified practitioner reached** — the poster's dial, expressed per person rather
 than per click.

The source states no baseline for any of these, so no targets are asserted.
