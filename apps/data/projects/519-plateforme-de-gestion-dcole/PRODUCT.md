# Plateforme de gestion d’école

> Auto-generated product brief. Review and refine before MVP scoping.

## Value Proposition

A French K-12 school director can run enrollment, attendance, grade reporting, and parent communication from a single French-language platform with RGPD-compliant data residency, while parents check attendance and grades from a phone without installing an app.

## Target Users

| Stakeholder | Why they care |
|---|---|
| School director | Tired of stitching together Excel + WhatsApp + a paper grade book. |
| Teacher | Wants attendance + grade entry that takes <30 seconds per student per day. |
| Parent | Wants to know "did my child arrive?" and "what was the last grade?" without calling the school. |

## Jobs To Be Done

1. **Functional job** — Replace the patchwork of Excel + WhatsApp + paper with a single system.
2. **Emotional job** — Stop worrying about RGPD exposure from a US-hosted spreadsheet tool.
3. **Social job** — Be able to tell parents "we have a portal" instead of "check your email".

## Success Metrics

- **Activation:** 90% of teachers enter attendance daily within 30 days of go-live.
- **Parent adoption:** ≥50% of parents log into the portal at least once per week.
- **Compliance:** zero RGPD findings in the first annual audit.

## Pricing & Monetization

€4/student/month, billed annually. Minimum €800/year per school. Free for schools under 50 students during the pilot year.

## Competitive Landscape

- **PRONOTE** — the French market leader; powerful but a 20-year-old UI and steep learning curve for parents.
- **Google Workspace + Sheets** — free, but no enrollment workflow and no RGPD guarantees.
- **Generic international SIS (PowerSchool, ManageBac)** — too expensive, English-first, no French school-year conventions.

## Risks & Open Questions

- [ ] Confirm RGPD data residency requirements with the director of the pilot school.
- [ ] Validate whether the parent portal needs a mobile app eventually.
- [ ] Decide whether to integrate with the French national student identifier (RNIE).
