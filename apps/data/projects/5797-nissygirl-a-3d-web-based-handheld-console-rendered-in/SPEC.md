# SPEC.md — NissyGirl – A 3D web based handheld console rendered in HTML/CSS

## Problem

Hello hn :) I am Tanis I&#x27;m showing off a passion project of mine, NissyGirl.<p>This began with the question &quot;what if I rendered a Game Boy with only CSS&quot; - I loved the result and couldn&#x27;t stop myself from continually adding to it.<p>My goal is to provide the tactile experience of playing with a retro console and its cartridges from the browser.<p>As of today NissyGirl hosts:<p>- fully interactive 3D handheld rendered with only HTML&#x2F;CSS<p>- XState machines orchestrating the many layers of behavior<p>- tactile cartridge flow (with more 3D HTML&#x2F;CSS)<p>- custom game engine featuring fixed-timestep simulation and state machine driven behavior (with PixiJS for canvas rendering our screen<p>- touch-first interactive control scheme (with keyboard &#x2F; mouse support as well)<p>- two unique cartridges with their own UI and gameplay<p>- a prompt system<p>- a sound system and sounds mastered and produced by me (some even recorded) and loads of pixel art<p>I&#x27;ve put a lot of work into making the interactions feel physical and intuitive. I&#x27;d love feedback on those, as well as any weird unexpected browser behavior you run in to.<p>Thanks all!
