# SPEC.md — Who or What is it? – A Picture Quiz

## Problem

Hi, I made a simple picture quiz. You see a picture of a thing or a person and have to give the correct answer. I know, nothing fancy.<p>My initial idea was to just have scientists quiz with people like Lee Smolin, Max Tegmark (some “personal heroes”), etc. because I am huge fan of scientific interviews&#x2F;content on youtube (my favorite channel is “Closer to Truth” with Robert Lawrence Kuhn) and I wanted to see if I know them all or get to know new ones.<p>Now it contains many more categories but the scientists one is still the biggest.<p>I am a developer for over 20 years. This is one of my first purely “vibe coded” apps. I mainly used Claude Fable and Opus (some GPT Sol e.g. for the app store screenshots).<p>It also turned into a halfway commercial product because it sells the full version containing all pictures and categories. So far it got 1 purchase... which was from a friend testing :p<p>It&#x27;s hosted entirely on cloudflare (uses workers and durable objects). React 19, TypeScript 5.9, Vite 8, Capacitor 8, with native Swift&#x2F;StoreKit. Android version is coming next weeks.<p>Happy about feedback and maybe some nice reviews, should you enjoy it :)<p><a href="https:&#x2F;&#x2F;whoorwhatisit.com&#x2F;" rel="nofollow">https:&#x2F;&#x2F;whoorwhatisit.com&#x2F;</a>
