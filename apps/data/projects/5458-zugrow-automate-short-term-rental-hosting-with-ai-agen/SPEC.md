# SPEC.md — Zugrow – Automate short-term rental hosting with AI agents across all channels

## Problem

Zugrow is an autonomous hosting platform for short-term rentals that centralizes channels like Airbnb, Booking.com, Vrbo, Expedia, and Google while AI agents handle guest messaging, booking vetting, pricing, and daily operations. Hosts connect listings once and keep calendars, inboxes, and tasks in sync. Plans include a unified inbox, team access, and 0% commission, with direct-booking sites and verification features planned. Start free for one property or scale with flat per-property pricing. View startup
