# 14 years building data platforms. Zero sales experience. How do I get my first SME clients? (I will not promote)

## Problem

A Reddit founder-advice post from a data engineer with 14 years of experience who recently started a small company helping SMEs build modern data platforms so they can actually use their data (rather than letting it sit in spreadsheets, outdated systems, or half-broken pipelines). Strong on the technical side (designing and building scalable data platforms); weak on sales, GTM, finding clients, and talking about money. They are struggling to get the first few clients and have considered doing the first project(s) at a very low rate or free in exchange for a testimonial and word of mouth, but are unsure if that is a good idea. They are asking other technical founders / small agencies what worked to get the first clients.

## Objective

Document the technical-founder-with-zero-sales-experience situation and the open question (do discounted first projects work, or are they a bad idea), without inventing pricing, conversion rates, or testimonial outcomes.

## Target Users

- Primary: the poster, a 14-year data engineer who has just started an SME-focused data-platform company.
- Secondary: other technical founders / small-agency owners in the same "delivery-strong, sales-weak" position.

## MVP Scope

- The founder situation (technical, no sales, first clients).
- The open question on discounted / free first projects for testimonials.
- Community replies that confirm or contest the discounted-first-project approach.

See `DESIGN.md` for this project's design tokens.

## Constraints

- No specific SME vertical, geography, or company size is named.
- The poster explicitly says "I will not promote" — do not promote the agency.
- No pricing is stated; "very low rate or free" is the only signal.
- The poster's experience is in data platforms specifically; do not generalise to all technical founders.
