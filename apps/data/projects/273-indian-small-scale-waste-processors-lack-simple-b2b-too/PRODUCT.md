# Indian small-scale waste processors lack simple B2B tools for finding suppliers and controlling copper quality

> Auto-generated product brief. Review and refine before MVP scoping.

## Value Proposition

Replaces the personal-network and manual-XRF pattern with a B2B tool that makes copper quality data portable and verifiable across the supply chain.

## Target Users

Indian small-scale waste processors extracting copper; downstream copper buyers; scrap suppliers.

## Jobs To Be Done

When I receive a load of copper-bearing scrap, I want to log the XRF reading with photo and GPS so my downstream buyer trusts the purity. When I am looking for new scrap suppliers, I want a directory ranked by past-quality consistency so I do not get burned.

## Success Metrics

Active processors on platform. Batches logged per month. Marketplace GMV. Buyer-reported purity accuracy.

## Competitive Landscape

IndiaMART is a generic B2B directory with no quality-data integration. ScrapRunner and similar apps exist in Western markets but are not India-tuned. No source-named direct competitor combining XRF logging with marketplace.

## Risks & Open Questions

XRF-device integration is the gating item — most processors use handheld XRF devices with proprietary export formats. WhatsApp Business API approval for India is slow. Quality-data tampering risk — must be defensible.
