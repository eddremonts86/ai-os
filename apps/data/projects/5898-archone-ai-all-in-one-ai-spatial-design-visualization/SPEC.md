# SPEC.md — ArchOne AI – All-in-one AI spatial design visualization platform

## Problem

ArchOne AI is an all-in-one AI spatial design visualization platform for early-stage workflows across architecture, interiors, exteriors, and landscapes. The platform combines focused AI design tools with ArchOne AI Studio, a visual workspace where architects and designers can develop connected design directions on an infinite canvas. Users can start with site photos, room images, building exteriors, sketches, renders, reference images, or a written brief. View startup
