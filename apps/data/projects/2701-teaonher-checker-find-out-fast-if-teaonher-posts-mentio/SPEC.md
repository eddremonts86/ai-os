# Teaonher Checker – Find out fast if TeaOnHer posts mention you with proof in 24 hours

## Problem

Teaonher Checker is a private, analyst-run scan that searches the TeaOnHer app for posts tied to your name, photos, city, and age. You submit identifiers securely, and an in-house analyst uses long-standing accounts to investigate and delivers a verified report within one business day. Reports include screenshots and transcripts when found or a dated Not Found confirmation, plus an optional introduction to a takedown partner. The service charges a flat, Stripe-secured fee and keeps your identity off TeaOnHer and out of internal dashboards. View startup
