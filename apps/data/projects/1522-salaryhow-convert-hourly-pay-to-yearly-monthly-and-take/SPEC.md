# SalaryHow – Convert hourly pay to yearly, monthly, and take-home income fast

## Problem

SalaryHow converts hourly wages into annual, monthly, biweekly, weekly, and daily pay, updating live as you change hours, weeks, and tax estimates. Use it to evaluate job offers, raises, or contracts and see take-home pay without spreadsheets or accounts. The site also explains the formulas and highlights common rates so you can quickly understand your time's worth. View startup
