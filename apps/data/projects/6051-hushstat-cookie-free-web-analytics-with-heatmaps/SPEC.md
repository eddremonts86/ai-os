# SPEC.md — Hushstat – Cookie-free web analytics with heatmaps

## Problem

Hi HN, I built Hushstat to combine traffic analytics with a closer look at what visitors do on a website.<p>It includes traffic sources, funnels, heatmaps, and signals such as rage clicks. The tracker does not use tracking cookies. The stack is Go, Postgres, and React.<p>There is a 20-day free trial with no card required. I would appreciate feedback on the dashboard and installation experience. What would you need before using it on your own website?
