# Thank you to this community. I posted here about needing one stranger to buy my app before my mom let me buy a domain.

## Value Proposition

**One-liner:** A student-side AI-cheating counter-evidence tool: records every keystroke, runs 25+ motor / rhythm / composition checks, and produces a replay link a teacher can inspect — premium adds a PDF exhibit and a personal email to the teacher.

## Target Users

- Primary: students who need to prove an essay or assignment was written by a human.
- Secondary: teachers and professors who receive a Receipts link and need a defensible artifact.

## Jobs To Be Done

1. Functional — generate evidence that an essay was typed by a human, not pasted from a chatbot.
2. Functional — survive a teacher's verification step without a long back-and-forth.
3. Emotional — give the accused student a way to clear the accusation instead of being stuck.
4. Social — give the teacher a record / replay they can audit, not just a "trust me".

## Success Metrics

- Sales to strangers (the poster's earlier milestone was the first stranger-sale; the post confirms two since).
- Marketing-channel signal from community replies (YouTube vs. other).
- Concrete retention or conversion metrics are not in the source.

## Pricing & Monetization

Premium tier only: "premium tiers get more proof like a pdf exhibit and a personal email to the teacher". No price is stated.

## Competitive Landscape

Not stated in the source. The poster does not name any AI-detection tool or competing essay-provenance service.

## Risks & Open Questions

- [ ] Validate that 25+ motor/rhythm/composition checks are defensible against determined bypassing; the poster asserts this but does not publish the checks
- [ ] Decide on a sustainable marketing channel — the poster's only stated attempt is YouTube and they call marketing "a pain"
- [ ] Note explicitly that the founder is 14; legal / payment / data-handling concerns are higher-stakes than for an adult solo founder
- [ ] Confirm the replay / PDF artifact is acceptable evidence in the teacher-facing context, not just acceptable to the student
- [ ] Privacy risk: a keystroke record is sensitive; retention, deletion, and storage must be designed in from day one
