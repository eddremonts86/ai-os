# SPEC.md — FlightFinder – Aviation safety data for any flight, aircraft, or airline

## Problem

FlightFinder shows the safety record behind any flight you can look up. Type a flight number, aircraft type, airline, or airport to get its accident and incident history, with 282,990 occurrences from 128 official sources since 1902. Data is gathered from the NTSB, ATSB, MAK, CADORS, EASA, and FAA, which are normally separate archives. It also includes 240,558 NASA ASRS reports, 7,219 airworthiness directives, and counts for wildlife strikes, runway incursions, and on-time performance. Safety scores explain their calculations, not star ratings, and every record links to the official investigator's report. The service is free to use, with a Pro version offering saved watches, alerts, and a a View startup
