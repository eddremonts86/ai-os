# "3k monthly visitors and $80 MRR. Would you focus on traffic or conversion?"

## Problem

I added paid plans to my side project in May, and I'm trying to decide where to spend my time next. It's a free Pomodoro timer called Pomofox. Most of the product is free, and I charge $5/month for extra customization. Right now: 3k–4k visitors/month 16 paying users $80 MRR Almost all paying users are from the US I'm also seeing that people actually use the customization quite a lot, which was one of my concerns when I made it paid. So I'm at a point where I don't really want to keep building more features just because I can. I'm thinking about spending the next few months almost entirely on distribution and trying to get from 3k to 10k monthly visitors. But I'm wondering if that's the wrong way to look at it. If these were your numbers, would you focus on getting more traffic or try to squeeze more revenue out of the traffic that's already there? I'd be especially interested to hear from anyone who has been around this stage before. submitted by /u/ineedthealgorithm [link] [comments]

---

## Objective

Help a solo founder with 3,000 monthly visitors and $80 MRR decide between pushing more traffic or improving conversion, by producing a one-page diagnostic that estimates the dollar value of each lever at their actual numbers and surfaces the cheapest experiment to run first.

## Target Users

- Primary: solo SaaS founder with 1k-10k monthly visitors and <$500 MRR, unsure which lever to pull.
- Secondary: a marketing consultant running the same diagnostic for multiple clients.

## MVP Scope

- Input form: monthly visitors, MRR, current conversion rate (optional, otherwise estimated), traffic source mix.
- Output: a single-page diagnostic with two scenarios ("more traffic" vs. "better conversion"), expected MRR after 90 days under each, and a recommended first experiment.
- No accounts; shareable URL via query string.
- No live analytics integration in v1 — the founder enters the numbers.

## Design Direction

See `DESIGN.md` for this project's design tokens.

## Constraints

- The diagnostic must be honest about uncertainty; ranges, not false precision.
- No upsell to a course or agency.
- Single-page tool, not a platform.
