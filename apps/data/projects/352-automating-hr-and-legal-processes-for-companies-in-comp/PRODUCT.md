# Automating HR and legal processes for companies, in compliance with the Russian Labor Code

> Product brief authored from the source title and category. The poster's text was not available (source.name: manual); sections below re-state the problem and infer only what the title and category support.

## Value Proposition

A Russian SME opens the dashboard, picks the action (hire, vacation, sick-leave, terminate), and gets a Labor-Code-correct artefact signed with a CryptoPro e-signature and stored in a 75-year archive - without the SME hiring an HR lawyer.

## Target Users

- Russian SMEs with 10-200 employees and no in-house HR or legal lead.
- Russian tax-accounting firms serving 5-30 SMEs who want a defensible HR pipeline for clients.
- Russian HR freelancers who want a tool that produces Labor-Code-correct artefacts without re-checking the law on every job.

## Jobs To Be Done

1. **Functional job** - Run the 80% HR/legal paperwork on Labor-Code-correct artefacts.
2. **Emotional job** - Stop worrying that a sick-leave flow is one-page off the legal text.
3. **Social job** - Hand the auditor a compliant archive with every artefact code-versioned and signed.

## Success Metrics

- **Activation:** first contract generated and signed within 30 minutes of signup.
- **Coverage:** >= 80% of the customer's hire/leave/terminate paperwork uses the tool in pilot.
- **Audit:** zero Labour Inspectorate findings related to artefacts produced by the tool across pilot year.

## Competitive Landscape

- **1C:HRM / SBIS / Kontur** - capable but heavy; priced and configured for enterprise.
- **Google Docs + freelance HR consultant** - what the SME uses today; risky and slow.
- **Kontur.Elba / MoyBusiness** - micro-business offerings with thin HR scope.
