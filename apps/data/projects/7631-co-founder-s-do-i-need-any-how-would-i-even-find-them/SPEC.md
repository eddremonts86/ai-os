# SPEC.md — Co-Founder(s). Do I need any? How would I even find them?

## Problem

I&#x27;m a technologist and have experience in academia, private, and now public sector work. I had an idea stewing in the back of my mind for about a year and it became &#x27;fully-formed&#x27; enough to be a proof-of-concept such that I filed a provisional patent a few months ago. At this point, I don&#x27;t want to quit my day job (federal agency, GS11, but have an in to be GS15 within 3 years). But I really want my idea to succeed (or at the very least, see the light of day). I have some savings piled up, but I&#x27;m hesitant to spend it on &#x27;grown-up&#x27; patent papers yet. I also already have 3 other &#x27;side gigs&#x27; (not to mention 4 kids and a blushing bride) that take up more than most of my time. How would I even find someone that I could trust enough to help me take my idea and hit a home run with it?<p>I don&#x27;t want to talk about the details of my invention. I did talk to a patent attorney that&#x27;s done a couple dozen startups through their firm though. He said I could probably get $20k-$50k in seed money for it. It&#x27;s not a &quot;better mousetrap&quot;, it&#x27;s more a &quot;new thing&quot; that&#x27;s never existed before (but also never really &#x27;needed to&#x27; before).<p>Do I NEED a co-founder, or can I &#x27;slow-walk&#x27; launching a business based solely on a new gizmo? I&#x27;m not very good at social media&#x2F;marketing, which is where I think my gizmo would thrive the most (but that&#x27;s purely a guess). I feel like if I could find the perfect person, it really could explode, but I have literally not even the faintest clue where&#x2F;how to start that. Am I an employer all-of-a-sudden even though I have no revenue to pay salaries?<p>Somebody heard that I had invented this thing and they asked me, &quot;So, I hear you&#x27;re an inventor?&quot; And I kind of stammered and said &quot;well.... it was an accident. I didn&#x27;t mean to, I promise.&quot; This was mostly to be funny&#x2F;silly, not that I don&#x27;t believe in the idea. But I know this sort of &#x27;startup&#x2F;founder(s)&#x2F;seed-money&#x27; thing is HN&#x27;s bread-and-butter, so I at least thought I&#x27;d ask.
