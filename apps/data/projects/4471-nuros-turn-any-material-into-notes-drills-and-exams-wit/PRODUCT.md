## Value Proposition

_Based on source brief:_ Nuros is an AI study app designed for exam preparation rather than note storage. Students can upload a PDF, slide deck, image, or audio file, paste a YouTube link or web page, or name a topic. Nuros then writes structured notes organized around concepts instead of page order. From the same material, it generates quizzes with AI grading and explanations, flashcards with spaced review, and full-length timed mock exams. Its adaptive review cycle scores mastery by concept, finds weak spots, and reschedules study with fresh explanations until mastery is achieved. Available on web, iOS, and Android, with a free tier.
