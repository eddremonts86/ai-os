# SPEC.md — A social network with one post a day and no replies

## Problem

I had barely used social media before.
I once joined a social network to promote another app,
but it didn’t feel right for me so I decided to make my own.<p>I was building it so I would have one I could use,
but somewhere along the way I began to enjoy making it, and that became the purpose.<p>What didn’t work for me was people posting short messages over and over,
attacking each other in the comments, and using posts mainly to send people to external links.<p>On landscape, you can only post once a day.
There are no replies or DMs, and you can’t include URLs in posts.
