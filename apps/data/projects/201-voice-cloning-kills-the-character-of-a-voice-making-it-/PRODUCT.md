# Voice cloning kills the character of a voice, making it too perfect and lifeless. Need a way to preserve natural imperfections without sacrificing quality.

> Auto-generated product brief. Review and refine before MVP scoping.

## Value Proposition

Keep the voice your audience already chose, but stop sounding like a robot. The first ten narrators and game studios save the cost of re-recording a chapter or a line of dialogue because the clone was too clean to publish.

## Target Users

Indie audiobook narrators, podcast producers, indie game audio designers, and YouTube creators who currently use cloned voices but get complaints about flatness from their audience.

## Jobs To Be Done

Functional: get a synthetic voice that still breathes and hesitates like a real one. Emotional: stop apologizing to listeners for the 'AI voice'. Social: keep authorship credit on a piece that still sounds like the narrator you hired.

## Success Metrics

Creator NPS above 50 within the first 100 paid users. At least 30% of uploads result in a re-export after slider adjustment, indicating the controls are actually doing useful work. Less than 5% of processed files get a 'still sounds synthetic' complaint on the resulting audio.

## Competitive Landscape

ElevenLabs and OpenVoice provide post-processing presets but not per-imperfection sliders. Open-source DAWs (Audacity, Reaper) require manual breath re-insertion and audio engineering skill. No mainstream tool combines clone ingestion with selective imperfection preservation.

## Risks & Open Questions

Per-imperfection detection models can misclassify noise as breath. Side-by-side A/B preview doubles GPU cost. Voice actors and SAG-AFTRA may object to a tool that makes clones more convincing without consent mechanisms.
