# SPEC.md — Rythmox – Track training with one score that unites fitness and recovery

## Problem

Rythmox is an AI-powered fitness app that combines training, nutrition, supplements, and recovery into a single clear score. You log your training with one tap and instantly see how stable your system is. The app tracks your progress over weeks, recognizes patterns, distinguishes slips from declines, and gives personalized suggestions to help you stay on track. This way, you integrate progress into your daily life without juggling four separate apps. View startup
