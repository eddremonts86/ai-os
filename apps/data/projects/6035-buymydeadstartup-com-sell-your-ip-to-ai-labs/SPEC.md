# SPEC.md — Buymydeadstartup.com – Sell your IP to AI labs

## Problem

I have had my fair share of failed startups, but none so recent that I could have sold our IP (code, Slack messages -- you name it) to Anthropic, OpenAI or any other AI lab or data buyer.<p>I know some companies are actively approached by some brokers, but most are not, because a startup often dies as a zombie (= not in a big bang, in most cases).<p>BuyMyDeadStartup is a bulletin board, not a marketplace. A founder posts a text description of what the company left behind, verified against a working email address and read by a person before it goes up. Buyers write to the founder directly.<p>Whatever they agree is between them.<p>I really tried to keep this as simple as possible.
