# Transmutify – Clean messy onboarding spreadsheets with API-driven mapping and review

## Problem

Transmutify streamlines customer onboarding by turning messy files into clean, import-ready records. It collects uploads through your product while its API handles schema mapping, validation, and a hosted human review before delivering approved JSON or CSV back to your workflow. It supports CSV, TSV, XLSX, TXT, and images, safeguards PII during AI mapping, and verifies webhooks. You can use your own AI keys and storage, dry-run integrations, and track every run from review to completion. View startup
