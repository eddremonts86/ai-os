## Value Proposition

_Based on source brief:_ Every board deck carries a single confident number, but no one can say how much that number deserves to be believed. FinHelm is the instrument layer that answers this. Connect a ledger and the UES™, the Uncertainty Exposure Score, returns a 0 to 100 reading across five bands comparing your budget versus actual history. Lower is better. From there, you plan in ranges rather than points with features like Variance Analyzer, Probabilistic Cash Runway, Scenario Builder, Planning Grid, and Driver Sensitivity. FinHelm holds no copy of your ledger; every read is fetched live, used, and discarded. The score is free forever. View startup
