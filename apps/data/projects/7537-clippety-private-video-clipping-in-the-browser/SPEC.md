# SPEC.md — Clippety – Private video clipping in the browser

## Problem

Hi I&#x27;m Per, the author of Clippety!<p>I wanted a way to turn videos into clips formatted for social media. But the solutions I found forced me to upload the source video which I did not want to do for privacy reasons.<p>So I made Clippety, which reframes, creates captions and adds a search against the transcript so you can easily find and create clips 100% private in your browser.<p>Works best in Chrome on desktop right now, but it&#x27;s still a work in progress.<p>Unlimited usage&#x2F;exports with a watermark. No account needed to try it out.<p>Feedback much appreciated!
