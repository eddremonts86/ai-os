# SPEC.md — Rankdesk – Automate SEO research, writing, and publishing to grow traffic

## Problem

Rankdesk automates the full SEO content lifecycle for your site. It crawls your pages, studies competitors and keywords, then drafts long-form blog articles and landing pages with structured headings, links, tables, videos, and brand-matched images. You can connect platforms like WordPress, Webflow, Shopify, Ghost, and more to auto-publish on your domain. Rankdesk refreshes outdated posts, adds internal links to new content, and supports local, national, or global SEO strategies to drive compounding organic growth. View startup
