# SPEC.md — SubmitRank – Find and track the highest-value sites to submit your product

## Problem

SubmitRank helps founders and marketers choose where to submit their products for backlinks and visibility. It ranks directories, launch platforms, and communities using traffic, DR, pricing, submission fit, and freshness, then refreshes the list monthly. You can inspect tiers, see why sites moved, and save targets to a dashboard that tracks status, costs, listing URLs, and notes. Roadmap features include link verification and index checks to confirm placements remain live and discoverable. View startup
