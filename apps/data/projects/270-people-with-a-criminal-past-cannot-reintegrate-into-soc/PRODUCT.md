# People with a criminal past cannot reintegrate into society because their real skills are «invisible»

> Auto-generated product brief. Review and refine before MVP scoping.

## Value Proposition

Surfaces real, evidence-based skills as portable credentials that employers can verify without the candidate having to disclose the criminal record at application time.

## Target Users

Norwegian residents with a criminal past seeking employment; Norwegian employers open to this population; reintegration NGOs.

## Jobs To Be Done

When I am looking for work, I want a credential that shows I can do the job without having to lead with my criminal record. When I am hiring, I want a verified skill signal that does not require the candidate to disclose at application stage.

## Success Metrics

Issued credentials per month. Employer verification rate per credential. Job-placement rate at 6 months post-credential. NGO adoption rate.

## Competitive Landscape

LinkedIn profiles do not solve the criminal-record disclosure problem. Open Badge issuers exist (Mozilla, Credly) but are not tailored to reintegration. Norwegian reintegration programmes (e.g. Røde Kors, NKS) do this work in person but not at scale. No source-named direct software competitor.

## Risks & Open Questions

Employer adoption is the gating item — without employer side, the credentials are worthless. Skill-assessment quality must be defensible to employers. Privacy-of-record disclosure must be airtight. Need 10 NGO and 10 employer interviews before claims about job-placement are credible.
