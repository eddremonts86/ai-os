# SPEC.md — ZULO – Beat any craving in 30 seconds with AI that knows when it's coming

## Problem

ZULO predicts your craving windows before they arrive using AI trained on your behavioral patterns. It guides you through a 30-second intervention loop that redirects your attention before the urge peaks. There's no calorie counting, food logging, or meal plans—just a short moment that breaks the craving cycle. The free tier includes streaks and habit loops, while Pro adds predictive AI alerts that learn your personal craving schedule and get smarter over time. Available on iOS, Android, and web.
