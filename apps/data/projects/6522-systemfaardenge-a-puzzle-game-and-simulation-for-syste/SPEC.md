# SPEC.md — SystemFaarDenge – a puzzle game and simulation for system designing

## Problem

Here you can simulate your system designs and solve system design puzzles incrementally based on the given scenario.<p>Backstory:
I am a Data Engineer by profession. While I was preparing for a System Designing interview round for some companies, I realised most of the materials available on the internet was just someone solving a specific system design problem either in form of a very long text or a long video, but almost none of them thought me how to think through it. And if I had another way of designing it, there was no reliable way to validate if it would work and how would it compare to the optimal system.
So I thought of building a simulator. And while building I realised I could turn it into a game.
