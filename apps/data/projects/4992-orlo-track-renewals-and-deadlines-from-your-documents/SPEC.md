# SPEC.md — Orlo – Track renewals and deadlines from your documents with smart reminders

## Problem

Orlo helps you stop missing renewals and deadlines by turning documents into actionable reminders. Forward bills and receipts to a personal email or upload photos and PDFs, and its AI extracts key details like dates, amounts, and references across 18 document types. You review and confirm, then receive timely email and push notifications. Workspaces keep personal, family, and business separate, and Pro adds a built-in calendar so you can plan everything in one place securely. View startup
