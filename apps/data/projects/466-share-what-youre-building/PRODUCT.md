# Share what you're building

> Auto-enriched product brief.

## Value Proposition

A community feed where makers post 1-2 line product pitches and upvote each other, replacing spammy "build in public" threads with a fair-visibility surface.

## Target Users

- Makers (founders, indie hackers) looking for low-friction visibility
- Early-stage founders who cannot afford paid launch channels
- Bootcamp / accelerator participants who want a shared launchpad

## Jobs To Be Done

When I have just shipped something and want low-friction visibility, I want a community feed where I post 1-2 lines and a link, so I do not have to write a launch post or pay for distribution.

## Success Metrics

- At least 100 pitches in pilot
- Median weekly upvote engagement above 20%
