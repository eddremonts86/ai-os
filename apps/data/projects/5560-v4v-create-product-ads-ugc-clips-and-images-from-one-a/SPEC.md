# SPEC.md — v4v – Create product ads, UGC clips, and images from one AI workspace

## Problem

v4v is an AI video and creative studio for ecommerce and marketers. It lets you generate product videos, UGC-style clips, TikTok hooks, tutorials, and image creatives from templates or finely controlled runs. Use Studio for quick outputs, ProVid to turn product URLs into structured inputs, Lab for model-level control, and Workflows to reuse and batch successful pipelines. Pay per use with credits and keep full rights to the content you create across ads and social channels. View startup
