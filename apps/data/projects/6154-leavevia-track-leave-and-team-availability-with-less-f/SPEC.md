# SPEC.md — LeaveVia – Track leave and team availability with less friction

## Problem

LeaveVia is a lightweight leave and team availability platform designed for small businesses and growing teams. It brings leave policies, balances, requests, approvals, public holidays, and staff availability into one clear workspace, helping managers understand who is away and how proposed leave may affect team coverage before making a decision. Designed for teams that have outgrown spreadsheets, email, and shared calendars but do not need a large or complicated HR system, LeaveVia makes everyday leave management simpler, clearer, and more reliable.
