# MentatNOC – Monitor, fix, and prove security camera fleet health and compliance

## Problem

MentatNOC is a network operations center for physical security camera fleets. It monitors device health 24/7, sends tamper and downtime alerts, and tracks availability. Teams can push and roll back firmware, rotate passwords and credentials, manage certificates, and enforce approvals with staged rollouts and automatic rollback. Every action leaves audit-ready evidence with compliance reporting, exports, and point-in-time posture attestation for the frameworks auditors expect. Built for integrators, it focuses on Axis, Bosch, and Hanwha cameras. View startup
