# Search for a controlled and stable channel for attracting clients to a banquet hall, as an alternative to unpredictable social media advertising

> Auto-generated product brief. Review and refine before MVP scoping.

## Value Proposition

A controllable, repeatable client-acquisition pipeline for banquet-hall operators in Algeria: a verified venue profile that ranks in search, a small network of partner channels (wedding planners, wedding fairs, city directories) that point to it, and a monthly read on which channel actually produced inquiries and bookings. The operator replaces the volatility of social-media advertising with a process whose inputs and outputs are both visible.

## Target Users

- Banquet-hall owners and venue operators in Algeria booking weddings, engagements, corporate events.
- Venue marketing managers who own the calendar three to nine months out.
- Wedding planners and event agencies that act as repeat referral partners.
- Engaged couples and corporate event planners searching for halls through a structured directory.

## Jobs To Be Done

- When I am planning a wedding six to twelve months out, I want a single place to compare halls by capacity, location, and price band, so I can shortlist three and book site visits.
- When my hall is half-empty next month, I want a list of partner channels I can activate, so I can fill the calendar without paying for ads whose reach I cannot predict.
- When a wedding planner refers a couple, I want to record the source so I can see which planners produce confirmed bookings, so I can invest in the right relationships.
- When I am choosing a city for my next hall, I want to see demand signals from the same directory, so I can compare locations on something other than gut feel.

## Success Metrics

- Number of qualified inquiries per month reaching the venue's contact form.
- Share of inquiries that include a verifiable source tag (referrer, planner name, directory page).
- Conversion rate from inquiry to confirmed booking (operator-defined; the source does not set a baseline).
- Calendar occupancy three to nine months ahead (operator's own measure; the source does not state a target).
