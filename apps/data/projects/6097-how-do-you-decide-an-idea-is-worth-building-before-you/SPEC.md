# SPEC.md — How do you decide an idea is worth building before you start coding?

## Problem

I’ve been thinking about the gap between having an idea and actually deciding it deserves weeks or months of work.<p>Building has become much cheaper. A developer can prototype something surprisingly fast now.<p>But the decision before building still seems messy.<p>When you’re evaluating an idea, what evidence do you actually trust?<p>Customer interviews?
Existing workarounds?
Competitor reviews?
Search demand?
People already paying for alternatives?
Your own experience with the problem?<p>And maybe more importantly:<p>What evidence would make you decide not to build it?<p>I often see founders collect a lot of information, but the final decision still comes down to a mix of browser tabs, notes, spreadsheets and gut feeling.<p>Curious how HN founders and developers actually make this decision in practice.<p>Have you found a process that works consistently?
