# SPEC.md — Gora Academy – Master Serbian, Croatian, Bosnian, and Montenegrin with clear grammar

## Problem

Gora Academy teaches Serbian, Croatian, Bosnian, and Montenegrin with a focus on how the languages work. It guides you from first phrases to real conversations using plain-English grammar explanations, native-quality audio, spaced review, and sentence building. A built-in AI tutor lets you practice speaking safely and get instant feedback. Learn in the app or browser, keep progress synced across devices, and follow a structured course based on solid linguistic references. Turkish and Russian coming soon. View startup
