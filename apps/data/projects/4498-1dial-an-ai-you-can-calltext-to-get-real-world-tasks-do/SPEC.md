# 1Dial, an AI you can call/Text to get real-world tasks done

## Problem

I built 1Dial because LLMs have become great at telling you what to do, but I still find myself making calls, waiting on hold, finding appointments, getting quotes, etc.With 1Dial, you call or text one number and tell it what you need. It can search, call businesses, follow up and coordinate the task.We've processed about 100+ tasks in our early beta so far. 81% were completed better than what a human would have done.You can try it at 1-844-440-3463 (Call or Text).Would particularly love feedback on where this breaks and whether you'd trust something like this, when and for what you want to use this.
