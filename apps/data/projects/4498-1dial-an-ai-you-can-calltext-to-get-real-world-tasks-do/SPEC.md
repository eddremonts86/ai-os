# SPEC.md — 1Dial, an AI you can call/Text to get real-world tasks done

## Problem

I built 1Dial because LLMs have become great at telling you what to do, but I still find myself making calls, waiting on hold, finding appointments, getting quotes, etc.<p>With 1Dial, you call or text one number and tell it what you need. It can search, call businesses, follow up and coordinate the task.<p>We&#x27;ve processed about 100+ tasks in our early beta so far. 81% were completed better than what a human would have done.<p>You can try it at 1-844-440-3463 (Call or Text).<p>Would particularly love feedback on where this breaks and whether you&#x27;d trust something like this, when and for what you want to use this.
