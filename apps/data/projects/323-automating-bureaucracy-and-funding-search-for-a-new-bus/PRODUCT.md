# Automating bureaucracy and funding search for a new business

> Auto-generated product brief. Review and refine before MVP scoping.

## Value Proposition

A new Canadian founder goes from 'I want to start a company' to 'incorporated, BN, GST/HST, business licence, first grant submitted' in under 21 days, at one flat price below the consultant-led path.

## Target Users

| Stakeholder | Why they care |
|---|---|
| Canadian first-time founder | Has the idea; does not have the CA relationship; needs the paperwork handled. |
| Canadian 2-cofounder team | Needs shareholding + founder agreement + the registrations in one flow. |
| Canadian accountant / bookkeeper | Wants a white-label setup flow for new-business clients. |

## Jobs To Be Done

1. **Functional job** — Set up the business and submit the first grant in days, not weeks.
2. **Emotional job** — Stop the dread of 'I should have started last year' that new founders carry.
3. **Social job** — Tell a co-founder or investor 'the company is registered, the grant is in' on day one.

## Success Metrics

- Time-to-BN median ≤ 10 days.
- Time-to-first-grant-submission median ≤ 14 days.
- Customer-reported savings vs consultant-led setup ≥ CAD $1,500 median.
- Founder NPS ≥ 50 at month 3.

## Pricing & Monetization

Flat CAD $799 for the setup bundle (incorporation + BN + GST/HST + business licence + first grant submission). Premium CAD $1,999 adds: founder agreement, payroll account, WSIB, and a CA partner on Slack for 90 days.

## Competitive Landscape

- Local CA / bookkeeping firms — strong but bespoke, slow, no online tracking.
- Canada.ca / CRA portals — official, but each portal is its own flow.
- LegalZoom Canada / Ownr — incorporation-only, no funding or licence guidance.

## Risks & Open Questions

- [ ] CRA / provincial API instability — Mitigation: status-tracked filings with manual e-filing fallback.
- [ ] Province-by-province variance — Mitigation: per-province templates maintained by a Canadian CA on retainer.
- [ ] Grant database freshness — Mitigation: weekly refresh; sibling product (322) for deeper grant coverage.
