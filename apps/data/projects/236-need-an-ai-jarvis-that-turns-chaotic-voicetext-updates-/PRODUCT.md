# Need an AI «Jarvis» that turns chaotic voice/text updates into automatically structured tasks, projects, and dashboards for managing all of life and work

> Auto-generated product brief. Review and refine before MVP scoping.

## Value Proposition

Removes the structuring step that prevents people from ever using what they capture. The user captures in whatever surface they already use, and the structuring happens out of band.

## Target Users

USA-based knowledge workers and founders with high capture volume and low follow-through; people who already pay for Notion AI, Mem, or similar capture tools and want something that actually closes the loop.

## Jobs To Be Done

When I have a thought in the car, I want to send a 20-second voice note and see it land as a task or a project card by the time I'm at my desk. When I look at my morning digest, I want to confirm or correct the structuring so the model learns my categories.

## Success Metrics

Capture-to-confirmed-action conversion rate above 60% within 14 days. Daily active users with at least one capture per day after 30 days. Reduction in average time from capture to action.

## Competitive Landscape

Notion AI structures notes that already live in Notion. Mem captures and lightly tags but does not project into tasks. Rewind / Limitless record screen and audio but do not structure into projects. None of the existing tools cross work-and-personal life cleanly, per the source.

## Risks & Open Questions

LLM cost per capture is non-trivial at scale. Users may resent silent structuring and want a manual override on every capture. Privacy of voice data is a hard sell to enterprise buyers. Need to validate willingness to pay — source does not state a price.
