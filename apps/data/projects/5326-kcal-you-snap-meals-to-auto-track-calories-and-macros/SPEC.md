# SPEC.md — kcal.you – Snap meals to auto-track calories and macros in seconds

## Problem

kcal.you lets you track what you eat by snapping a quick photo. It uses AI to recognize your meal and instantly estimate calories and macros, so you skip manual entry and searching. Set daily goals and see clear visualizations of your progress to stay on track. With fast, photo-first logging and simple insights, kcal.you helps you build consistent habits and understand your nutrition without disrupting your day. View startup
