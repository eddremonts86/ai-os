# MajlisDesk – Unify WhatsApp and email in one shared team inbox

## Problem

MajlisDesk brings WhatsApp and email into a single shared inbox so your team can respond quickly from one place. Connect your WhatsApp number once, and chats sync alongside emails with full history. You can assign owners, add labels, leave internal notes, and convert messages into trackable tickets. Use canned replies and manage everything from a web panel, including inside WhatsApp Web. Built for GCC teams, it supports unlimited users on flat pricing so nothing goes unanswered. View startup
