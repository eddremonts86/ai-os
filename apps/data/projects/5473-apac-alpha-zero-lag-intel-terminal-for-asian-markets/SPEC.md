# SPEC.md — APAC Alpha – Zero-lag Intel terminal for Asian markets

## Problem

I built APAC Alpha (https:&#x2F;&#x2F;apacalpha.com) to solve a major data blind spot for individual and international investors looking at Asian markets.<p>The Problem:<p>Enterprise terminals like Bloomberg or LSEG are prohibitively expensive and largely gated against individual retail investors.<p>Local platforms (like QUICK in Japan) and standard broker reports often require a local brokerage account just to view research—creating a massive barrier for foreign and international investors.<p>Most modern financial tooling focuses almost exclusively on the US and Europe, leaving Asian markets fragmented and difficult to access.<p>The Target &amp; Solution:<p>Focused on individual, international, and independent investors who trade based on analyst reports and catalysts across Asian markets (starting with Korea and Japan, expanding to Taiwan, China, India, and Southeast Asia).<p>APAC Alpha is a zero-bloat terminal that pre-computes data via background workers to eliminate client-side lag, bypassing manual research rabbit holes.<p>I&#x27;d love to hear your thoughts on the architecture, data pipeline, or workflow!
