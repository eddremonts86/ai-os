# SPEC.md — Sorank – Grow organic traffic from Google and AI on autopilot

## Problem

Sorank is an autopilot SEO and GEO platform that grows your traffic from Google and AI. It researches keywords, writes and auto-publishes SEO-optimized articles with sources and images, and acquires contextual backlinks from a vetted network. Sorank audits and fixes technical issues, tracks brand citations across ChatGPT, Perplexity, Gemini and more, and reports results in a clear dashboard.
