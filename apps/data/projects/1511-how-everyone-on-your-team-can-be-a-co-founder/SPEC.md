# How Everyone on Your Team Can Be a Co-Founder

## Problem

 TL;DR Many of us start a venture with business partners or friends. Sharing equity is the simplest way to start a business having little money. The question of fair equity split is a pain in... you know, especially when you have 10 partners on your team. In this article I share how each month we convert partners’ hours and little bit of cash into equity in a fair way. As a bonus, you get calculation templates to try the idea on your own. Co-founding a startup without the proper regulation of equity shares is a bitter experience. I’ve had it — never again! It resulted in continuous boardroom rows, loss of trust and even ruining friendships. That’s something you want to avoid when starting a business, right? When I was starting Y-Productive , this experience inspired an early creative decision to establish an “everybody can be a co-founder” equality principle.
