# Phished Microsoft account, recovery useless

## Problem

The poster's child fell for a phishing scam and lost their Microsoft / Minecraft account. Despite being part of a Microsoft Family (with the poster's partner as organiser), the automated recovery process has been denied. They provided previous passwords, an address of use, and the last four digits of a card that had been used on the account. The attacker has since changed the password and recovery email, cutting off the family entirely. The poster is asking for a Microsoft contact or any other recourse.

## Objective

There is no product idea in the post. The poster wants account recovery, not a tool to build.

## Target Users

A parent of a child on a Microsoft Family plan whose family organiser role does not actually grant authority to recover a stolen child account.

## MVP Scope

No MVP. The blocker is on Microsoft's side.

## Constraints

The recovery process and the family-organiser permission model are owned by Microsoft. The poster does not control them.
