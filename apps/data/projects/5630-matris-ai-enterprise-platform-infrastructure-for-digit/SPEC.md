# SPEC.md — Matris AI – Enterprise platform infrastructure for digital agencies

## Problem

MatrisAI gives digital agencies the infrastructure to launch enterprise platforms for their clients in minutes under their own brand. It starts with ecommerce features like payments, orders, inventory, and shipping, then expands into restaurants, bookings, marketplaces, and other verticals. Agencies pay based on usage and can set their own commission on card transactions, turning client platforms into an additional recurring revenue stream. View startup
