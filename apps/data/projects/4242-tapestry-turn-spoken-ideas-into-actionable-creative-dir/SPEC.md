# Tapestry – Turn spoken ideas into actionable creative directions

## Problem

Tapestry is a creative thinking companion for artists, writers, designers, and makers. Record yourself talking through a project and it transcribes the session, surfaces useful connections as you speak, and turns the strongest ideas into practical next steps. Suggestions stay grounded in your own words and adapt to your interests, tools, goals, and constraints. You can revisit every recording, transcript, and saved idea when you're ready to create.
