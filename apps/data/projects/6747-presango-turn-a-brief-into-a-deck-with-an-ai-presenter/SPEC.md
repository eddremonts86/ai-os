# SPEC.md — Presango – Turn a brief into a deck with an AI presenter delivering it live

## Problem

Presango turns a short brief or existing PPTX/PDF into a polished, on-brand presentation and pairs it with Hannah, an AI presenter who delivers it live. You can revise slides in plain language, generate a knowledge base for accurate Q&A, and present in the browser or have her join Teams, Meet, or Zoom as a named participant. Pricing meters only live minutes, while deck creation and revisions remain free. Start on a free plan with monthly live time included. View startup
