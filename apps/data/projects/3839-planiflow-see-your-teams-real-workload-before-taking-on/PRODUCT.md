# Planiflow – See your team's real workload before taking on new projects

## Value Proposition

See your team's real workload before saying yes. Planiflow puts tasks, two-way-synced calendar meetings and each person's daily capacity on one weekly view: green means room, red means stop assigning, and overload shows on the exact day it happens — so a 130% Tuesday is visible even when the week averages fine.

**One-liner:** One weekly view of tasks, meetings and capacity, so overload shows up before the commitment, not after.

## Target Users

| Stakeholder | Why they care |
|---|---|
| Team leads assigning briefs | See who has room before promising a date. |
| Asana-and-calendar teams | Meetings and tasks finally counted together. |
| Google Calendar and Outlook users | Both calendars sync two ways, so coordination time stops being invisible. |
| Agencies guarding deadlines | Day-level overload surfaces before a week goes sideways. |

## Jobs To Be Done

1. **Functional job** — Merge Asana tasks and calendar meetings into one per-person weekly view.
2. **Functional job** — Compare total load against each person's daily capacity with green and red bars.
3. **Functional job** — Catch day-level overload, like a 130% Tuesday, even when the weekly average looks fine.
4. **Functional job** — Count meeting hours automatically through two-way Google Calendar and Outlook sync.

## Success Metrics

- **Pre-commitment usage:** share of new briefs checked against the capacity view before acceptance — the core behavior.
- **Day-level overload catches:** number of flagged days that were invisible in weekly averages.
- **Sync reliability:** calendar events present in the view within a set window after booking.
- **Team coverage:** share of the team whose tasks and calendars are fully connected.

## Risks & Open Questions

- [ ] Estimate quality: capacity bars are only as honest as task estimates and capacity settings.
- [ ] Two-way calendar sync is the riskiest engineering surface; a bad write-back erodes trust instantly.
- [ ] Tool coverage is narrow (Asana plus two calendars); teams on other stacks fall out of scope.
- [ ] Red and green bars may be read as judgment on people rather than load; framing matters.
