# Editorial PR and guest post placements, without the guesswork

> Auto-generated product brief. Review and refine before MVP scoping.

## Value Proposition

A SEO or digital-PR buyer can browse a single vetted catalogue of 1,300+ guest-post and news publishers, filter by comparable ahrefs/Moz/Majestic metrics (DR, DA, TF, traffic, RD), niche and price, add the exact placements they want into a single quote, approve the draft, and only get invoiced after the link is verified live on the publisher's domain.

## Target Users

| Stakeholder | Why they care |
|---|---|
| In-house SEO and link-building specialists | Replace scattered email outreach and spreadsheet tracking with a comparable-metrics inventory. |
| Agency account managers | Need one quote-of-record per client and proof of live-link delivery for invoicing. |
| Solo founders / growth marketers | Want pre-vetted placements without learning publisher outreach. |
| Publishers on the supply side | Want a steady flow of briefs without negotiating every placement by hand. |
| Digital PR teams | Buy news-tier placements at higher price points and need clear sponsored/discreet flagging. |

## Jobs To Be Done

1. **Functional job** — Buy a vetted guest-post or digital-PR placement in one quote, see comparable third-party metrics, and only pay after the link is live.
2. **Emotional job** — Stop second-guessing whether the DR/DA/TF numbers on a marketplace listing are real and recent.
3. **Social job** — Hand a client a clear, screenshot-able evidence trail (catalogue row, brief, approval, live-link confirmation, invoice).

## Success Metrics

- **Activation:** buyer submits a quote with at least one publisher within 7 days of signup.
- **Live-link rate:** ≥ 90% of paid placements are verified live within the publisher's stated ETA window.
- **Dispute rate:** < 5% of paid placements trigger a refund or credit request.
- **Repeat purchase:** ≥ 40% of buyers place a second quote within 60 days of the first live link.
- **Catalogue freshness:** every visible DR/DA/TF/traffic number has a refresh timestamp ≤ 14 days old.

## Pricing & Monetization

Per-placement markup over the publisher's wholesale price (visible on the catalogue row) — the buyer sees both numbers. No monthly subscription for buyers in v1; agencies on ≥ $20k/quarter volume get net-30 invoicing. Publishers pay no listing fee; the marketplace takes its cut from the buyer-side markup.

## Competitive Landscape

- **Direct outreach + spreadsheets** — bespoke per buyer, no comparable metrics, no live-link guarantee.
- **Existing guest-post marketplaces** (e.g. FATJOE, LinksManagement) — wide inventory but typically opaque metrics and upfront payment before delivery.
- **PR distribution services** (e.g. PR Newswire, EIN Presswire) — press-release focus, not editorial placement; placement fees are higher and editorial control is absent.
- **SEO agency retainers** — full-funnel but ≥ $1k/month minimum; overkill for one-off placements.

## Risks & Open Questions

- [ ] Confirm refresh cadence with ahrefs/Moz/Majestic to keep DR/DA/TF fresh without exceeding API quotas.
- [ ] Define the dispute and refund SLA before MVP — buyers expect "live link or credit" and that has to be enforceable.
- [ ] Decide whether to add LLM/AEO placements (sites that explicitly accept AI-overview citations) as a first-class filter or as a tag.
- [ ] Validate willingness-to-pay at the top of the catalogue (DR90 listicle slots priced $7,999) — these need a sales motion, not self-serve.
