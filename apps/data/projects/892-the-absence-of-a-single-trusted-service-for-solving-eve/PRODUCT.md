# The absence of a single trusted service for solving everyday tasks

> Auto-generated product brief. Review and refine before MVP scoping.

## Value Proposition

A concierge service, not a marketplace: the customer describes a life task, a human concierge matches it to a verified contractor, and the platform — as the legal entity — guarantees the outcome. Escrow holds the customer's money until they accept the result, dispute mediation is human, and every contractor has been identity-verified and background-checked before they see a task. One point of contact for car service, delivery, legal, travel, and childcare, with the platform standing behind the work.

## Target Users

| Stakeholder | Why they care |
|---|---|
| Busy urban professional (Moscow / SPb) | Outsources life tasks without gambling on a private contractor from an open aggregator. |
| Relocating professional / family | New city, no existing contractor network; one point of contact for life logistics. |
| Small-business owner | Overflow operational tasks (courier dispatch, document collection, light legal research) without hiring a full-time ops person. |
| Verified contractor (indirect) | Gets matched tasks from a platform that vouches for them and handles the customer's paperwork. |

## Jobs To Be Done

1. **Functional job** — Describe a life task once, get a matched, verified contractor who does the task, and pay only when the result is accepted.
2. **Emotional job** — Stop the anxiety of "who do I trust to do this for me?" by replacing it with "the platform stands behind this person, and I can reject and get my money back".
3. **Social job** — Be able to tell a partner or spouse "I sorted the tire change, the document pickup, and the school run" without making three separate calls to three separate contractors.

## Success Metrics

- **Task acceptance rate:** ≥ 90% of completed tasks are accepted by the customer on first delivery (no dispute).
- **Time to match:** 30 min median for urgent tasks, 4 h median for normal tasks, 24 h median for planned tasks, sustained over a 12-week pilot.
- **Verified-contractor coverage:** ≥ 95% of matched tasks go to a contractor who has passed identity verification + background check; 0 matches to unverified contractors.
- **Escrow discipline:** 100% of customer payments pass through escrow; 0 direct customer → contractor payments.
- **Retention:** ≥ 70% of pilot customers return for a second task within 30 days and a third within 60 days.

## Pricing & Monetization

The author did not name a price. A per-task service fee layered on top of the contractor's invoice, plus an optional monthly concierge subscription:

- **Per-task fee** — 15% of the contractor's invoice, minimum ₽300, maximum ₽3,000. Covers the concierge's matching time, escrow handling, and dispute mediation.
- **Concierge subscription** — ₽1,990/month for up to 10 tasks with reduced per-task fees (₽150 minimum / ₽1,500 maximum). Targets busy professionals who outsource several tasks a month.
- **Annual plan** — ₽19,900/year (≈ 2 months free) for the same 10-task subscription; targets households that have adopted the concierge workflow.
- **Contractor side** — the platform takes 15% from the contractor's invoice too; contractors keep the rest. No listing fees, no lead fees.

## Competitive Landscape

- **Avito Services (Uslugi)** — the largest Russian classifieds board; an open marketplace of private contractors, no verification, no escrow, no legal-entity relationship. Exactly the surface the author rejects.
- **Profi.ru** — a curated-services marketplace with reviews; still a marketplace (customer pays the contractor directly), with weaker verification than the author wants and inconsistent legal-entity treatment.
- **YouDo** — task-based marketplace similar to TaskRabbit; same marketplace risks as Avito / Profi.
- **Personal assistant services (Assistant.ru, personal concierge agencies)** — the closest functional equivalent, but priced at ₽10k+/month retainer, far above the per-task model the author wants.
- **In-house personal assistant (hired directly)** — what high-earning professionals do today; full trust but expensive and not scalable.
- **General-purpose task apps (Todoist, TickTick) + ad-hoc outsourcing** — what busy people patch together today; no contractor network and no legal backing.

## Risks & Open Questions

- [ ] Concierge hiring is the scaling choke point. The matching + dispute-mediation role cannot be fully automated in v1; the founding team must either hire concierges or build a thin ops layer. Mitigation: a playbook that a non-founder concierge can follow, so each new hire compounds capacity instead of just adding hands.
- [ ] Contractor verification at scale. Passport + INN + background check costs ₽1,500–₽3,000 per contractor and takes 3–5 days. Mitigation: batch verification with a Russian background-check partner; a "verified" badge that customers see at the contractor-card level so the cost is justified by trust.
- [ ] Escrow float. Holding customer money in escrow is a regulatory obligation in Russia and ties up working capital. Mitigation: a Russian partner bank with a custodial escrow account; the float is matched 1:1 against pending tasks.
- [ ] Dispute outcomes. A wrong decision by a concierge damages trust. Mitigation: a documented dispute playbook with a 24 h SLA, an appeal path, and a quarterly audit of dispute outcomes by the founders.
- [ ] Pricing calibration. The author gave no number. Mitigation: anchor on comparable Russian services (personal concierge agencies ₽10k+/month, Profi.ru take rate ~10–15%) and on ROI math (hours saved × hourly cost).
- [ ] Geography expansion beyond Moscow / SPb. The concierge model depends on a dense verified-contractor pool, which is hard to bootstrap in smaller cities. Mitigation: phase-2 expansion tied to concierge hiring; do not promise coverage outside the launch cities.
- [ ] Trust-building with new customers. The author explicitly distrusts aggregators; the platform's first 50 customers will be the hardest. Mitigation: a "first task free or refunded" guarantee during the pilot, and a public dashboard of acceptance rate and dispute outcomes.
