## Problem

This is an Encyclopedia of sorts, that prioritizes discovery of different concepts how they relate to other concepts. It provides ability to dig deeper on any concept with progressive levels of complexity.
The idea is to gather/scout the "dots" so that you can connect them when inspiration strikes.
