# Need an AI app: upload a photo → get a weekly verdict «progress / no progress» and advice on when to retake the photo.

> Auto-generated product brief. Review and refine before MVP scoping.

## Value Proposition

Get the honest verdict on whether your week mattered without scrolling between two photos at midnight. The app saves the mental load and tells you when the photo wasn't a fair comparison so you can retake it.

## Target Users

Adults in Greece and Southern Europe who track weight, posture, body recomposition, or skin progress with weekly photos and are tired of the manual comparison step. Secondary: physios and personal trainers who want a tool to send to clients.

## Jobs To Be Done

Functional: know if your week produced a real change. Emotional: stop second-guessing the photo booth. Social: feel proud of consistent progress without making it a public competition.

## Success Metrics

At least 60% of weekly users reach 12 weeks of unbroken streaks. Verdict accuracy (compared to a manual reviewer) above 80%. Less than 1% of verdicts trigger a 'this is wrong' report.

## Competitive Landscape

ProgressPics and similar photo-tracking apps do not have an AI verdict. Fitness apps with photo timelines (e.g. MyFitnessPal companions) do not normalise for lighting. No mainstream app combines lighting-robust comparison with an adaptive reminder flow.

## Risks & Open Questions

Computer-vision false positives on lighting changes. Risk of triggering eating-disorder patterns in vulnerable users. Photo storage cost can grow fast for heavy users. Verdict phrasing must be reviewed by a mental-health professional before launch.
