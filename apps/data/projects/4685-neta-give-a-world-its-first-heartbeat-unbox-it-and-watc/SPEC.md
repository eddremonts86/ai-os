# SPEC.md — Neta – Give a world its first heartbeat, unbox it, and watch it grow new apps

## Problem

Neta Studio is an AI-agent world workbench. Describe a world in a sentence like "a wizard school, but pirates" and it creates its first heartbeat. It unfolds into multiple surfaces: an app grid, interactive maps, a living wiki, roleplay scenes, POV videos, playable portals—all from one world. It's not one-shot. Your world is living and shared: every action updates canon, characters, relationships, places, and events, and every surface reflects these changes. An agent sits underneath, allowing the world to grow. Keep marking the map, and it may create a brand-new card. Built by the team behind Meta's billion-scale video systems, 13M+ people spend 110 minutes a day in a world like this. View startup
