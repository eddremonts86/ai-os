## Value Proposition

_Based on source brief:_ ExactPour helps bars and restaurants track every pour and reduce shrinkage. Staff count bottles using a phone by scanning barcodes and tapping fill levels, with no scales required. Nightly POS sync keeps poured versus sold data current so over-pours, theft, comps, and dead stock surface quickly. ExactPour AI reads counts, sales, and invoices to explain losses clearly, flag coaching moments, and size next orders by forecast.
