# ExactPour – Count with your phone and catch over-pours, theft, and dead stock

## Problem

ExactPour helps bars and restaurants track every pour and reduce shrinkage. Staff count bottles using a phone by scanning barcodes and tapping fill levels, with no scales required. Nightly POS sync keeps poured versus sold data current so over-pours, theft, comps, and dead stock surface quickly. ExactPour AI reads counts, sales, and invoices to explain losses clearly, flag coaching moments, and size next orders by forecast. Managers get daily briefs, evidence for fair training, and leaner operations in one login. View startup
