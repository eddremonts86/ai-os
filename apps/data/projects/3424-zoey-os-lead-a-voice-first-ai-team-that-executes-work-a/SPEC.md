# Zoey OS – Lead a voice-first AI team that executes work across your tools

## Problem

Zoey OS is a voice-first AI workspace where you collaborate with an orchestrator and customizable companions that manage research, creative work, operations, sales, and more. You speak or type a task, and Zoey routes it to the right companion, which can dispatch parallel bots and take real actions in your connected tools. Your workspace stays organized in a visual 3D environment, and memory builds over time, so context carries forward helping you delegate faster and get work done. View startup
