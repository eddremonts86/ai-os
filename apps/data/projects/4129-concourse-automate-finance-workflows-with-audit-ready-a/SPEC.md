# Concourse – Automate finance workflows with audit-ready AI agents

## Problem

Concourse is the AI execution layer for enterprise finance. It builds and runs agents that adapt to your data, logic, and workflows to handle variance analysis, close, forecasting, accounts receivable, and weekly reviews. Outputs are audit-ready with full traceability and governance, delivered via email, Slack, Teams, Excel, and PowerPoint. Concourse connects to your finance stack, meets SOC 1 and 2 standards, and pairs you with ex-CFOs to deploy AI that saves hours and drives results. View startup
