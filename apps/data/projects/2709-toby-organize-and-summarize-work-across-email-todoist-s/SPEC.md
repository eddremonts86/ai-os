# Toby – Organize and summarize work across Email, Todoist, Slack, Jira, and Calendr

## Problem

Toby is a native macOS assistant that organizes and summarizes your work across Email, Todoist, Slack, Jira, and Apple Calendar. Chat in a familiar SwiftUI interface to triage messages, review tasks, and plan your day while it pulls context from your integrations. Set up personas, skills, memories, and schedules to drive recurring prompts, audio transcription, and Mac control. Use @mentions and chat surfaces to steer conversations and keep tickets, emails, and events in sync from one place. View startup
