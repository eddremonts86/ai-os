# Text Logo – create text logos and wordmarks in the browser

## Value Proposition

A wordmark editor that lives in the browser: editable fonts, per-character colors, icons, shapes and templates, with export to SVG, PNG, WebP or ICO. The value is self-sufficiency — a production-ready text logo in minutes, with no design software, no signup and no file left trapped in a proprietary format. The capture claims no revenue or audience; the pitch is the capability itself.

**One-liner:** Distinctive text logos and wordmarks composed and exported in the browser — SVG, PNG, WebP or ICO, with per-character color control.

1. **Functional job** — Compose a text logo by editing fonts, per-character colors, icons and shapes.
