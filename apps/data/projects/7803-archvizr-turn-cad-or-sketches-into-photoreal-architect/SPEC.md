# SPEC.md — ArchVizr – Turn CAD or sketches into photoreal architectural renders in seconds

## Problem

ArchVizr delivers AI-powered architectural rendering from your existing drawings. Upload a SketchUp, Revit, Rhino, CAD export, or even a hand sketch, and get photoreal images in as little as 20 seconds while preserving geometry, camera, and composition. Generate consistent sets across exteriors, interiors, landscapes, and multiple lighting conditions, and apply style references to match materials and mood. Use it per project or via subscription, with your first render free. View startup
