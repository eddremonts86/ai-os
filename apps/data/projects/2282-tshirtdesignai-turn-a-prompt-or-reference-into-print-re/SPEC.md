# TshirtDesignAI – Turn a prompt or reference into print-ready t-shirt designs fast

## Problem

TshirtDesignAI is an AI t-shirt design generator for designers, brands, and print-on-demand sellers. Describe your idea or upload a reference to get polished, print-ready artwork previewed on multiple garment types and colors in about a minute. You can export transparent 300 DPI PNGs sized for DTG and screen printing, batch designs on higher plans, and keep your prompts private. Start free with one design after Google sign-in, then choose pay-as-you-go or subscriptions. View startup
