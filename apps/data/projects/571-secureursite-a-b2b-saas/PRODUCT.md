**One-liner:** Black-box SaaS security testing by a pentester — for B2B SaaS teams that want a credible, human-performed test instead of an automated scanner.

B2B SaaS founders and security leads who need an external black-box pentest to satisfy customer security questionnaires or pre-sales requirements.
