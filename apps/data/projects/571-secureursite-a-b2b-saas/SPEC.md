## Objective

Validate the idea of a B2B SaaS that does black-box security testing of other SaaS applications — using the founder's penetration-testing background to differentiate from incumbent scanners.

## Target Users

B2B SaaS founders and security leads who need external validation of their application's security posture. The founder is a cybersecurity student with penetration-testing experience; the buyer is a non-security B2B SaaS team that needs a credible test.

## MVP Scope

A manual black-box pentest deliverable for one paying customer, plus the methodology that makes it repeatable. The MVP is service-first (consultancy-shaped) with a SaaS wrapper later.

## Design Direction

See `DESIGN.md` for this project's design tokens.

## Constraints

Founder is a student, not an established pentester — credibility and references are the binding constraint. B2B SaaS security buyers expect a named methodology and ideally a report they can hand to a customer.
