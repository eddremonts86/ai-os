# Institutional Credit, ABS & LBO Underwriter open to early-stage startups I will not promote

## Problem

The poster is looking to transition their credit background into the early-stage startup ecosystem. They have a Master's in finance, several years as a credit risk associate at a global bulge-bracket bank, an earlier Big 4 risk advisory background, and hands-on experience underwriting and structuring complex corporate facilities — broadly syndicated loans, high-yield issuances, asset-backed finance (ABF/ABS), and sponsor-backed LBO financing. They regularly validate integrated financial models through extreme downside scenarios. They are open to early-stage startups and want to support what founders are building. The post is an offer, not a question. No country, no specific startup target was stated.

## Objective

Give a startup founder who needs a credit-minded advisor or fractional finance lead a match surface for someone with the named credit-risk background — clear scope, clear compensation, clear boundaries. The job is not to hire the poster — it is to make the founder's first outreach land with the named context.

## Target Users

Primary: a startup founder (pre-seed to Series A) who is building a credit-, lending-, or asset-backed-finance-adjacent product and needs a credit-minded advisor. Secondary: a venture-capital firm or accelerator program manager who is curating a network of fractional finance leads and is sourcing this kind of profile.

## MVP Scope

In scope for v1:

- A scope template: the named engagement shapes a credit-minded advisor can take on at the pre-seed to Series A stage — fractional finance lead, advisory board, deal review, model validation, second-pair-of-eyes.
- A compensation template: the named ranges and the named equity-vs-cash trade-off.
- A 'first outreach' template: the named context the founder should put in the first email so the named background reads as relevant, not generic.
- A one-page export the founder can drop into a Notion doc or a contract note.
