# Need a Co-founder

> Auto-generated product brief. Review and refine before MVP scoping.

## Value Proposition

A solo founder can run through a guided intake, get a clear co-founder profile, a shortlist of channels where that profile already hangs out, a copy-paste post, and a first-conversation script — in under an hour, instead of spending months writing and rewriting the same vague "looking for a co-founder" message.

## Target Users

| Stakeholder | Why they care |
|---|---|
| Solo technical founder | Has hit solo-execution limits; wants a co-founder not an employee. |
| Non-technical founder | Has shipped an MVP with contractors; wants a technical co-founder. |
| Founder considering equity split | Needs to think through role, stage, equity range before talking to anyone. |

## Jobs To Be Done

1. **Functional job** — Structure a co-founder search instead of winging it.
2. **Emotional job** — Stop feeling like the search will never end.
3. **Social job** — Be able to post a clear, serious "looking for a co-founder" message without sounding desperate.

## Success Metrics

- **Intake completion:** ≥70% of founders who start the intake finish it.
- **Post quality:** ≥50% of generated posts get a reply within 14 days.
- **First-conversation outcome:** ≥30% of first conversations produce a second meeting.

## Pricing & Monetization

Free, supported by a single affiliate link to a recommended equity-split calculator (e.g. Slicing Pie, EquitySplits).

## Competitive Landscape

- **IndieHackers / Reddit threads** — what the founder is doing today, with no structure.
- **Co-founder matching platforms (YC co-founder matching, Lunchclub)** — useful but biased toward Bay Area, technical co-founders.
- **Books / advisors** — slow, expensive, not tailored to the founder's actual role need.

## Risks & Open Questions

- [ ] Validate the channel shortlist against 10 actual successful co-founder searches before launch.
- [ ] Decide whether to add an equity-split calculator in v2.
