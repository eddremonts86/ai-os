# Modern job hunting — Design direction

Like a personal CRM for the job hunt, not a "career site".

- Surface: tracker first, signal feed second.
- Color: ink + one accent for stale applications.
- Typography: sans for tracker fields, monospace for status codes.
- No "career advice" hero copy.
