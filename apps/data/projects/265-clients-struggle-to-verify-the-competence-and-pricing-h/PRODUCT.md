# Clients struggle to verify the competence and pricing honesty of freelancers when creating websites — there is no AI tool for real-time proposal analysis

> Auto-generated product brief. Review and refine before MVP scoping.

## Value Proposition

Replaces the client's hours of gut-feel proposal review with a structured read that names the risks in plain language.

## Target Users

USA clients hiring freelancers for web / SaaS work; small agencies; procurement teams.

## Jobs To Be Done

When I receive three freelance proposals, I want each analysed on the same four dimensions so I can compare apples to apples. When I am about to sign with someone, I want to know if their price is fair for the scope they propose.

## Success Metrics

Free-to-paid conversion rate. Analyses per active user per month. Customer-reported accuracy (calibration) at 30 days post-decision.

## Competitive Landscape

Upwork and Toptal vet freelancers but do not analyse proposals client-side. Generic AI proposal-review tools exist but are not domain-tuned to website work. No source-named direct competitor.

## Risks & Open Questions

Market-rate dataset freshness is the long pole; pricing-honesty scoring degrades quickly as market rates shift. LLM analysis can hallucinate competence signals — needs verification hooks. Need 10 client interviews before claiming calibration.
