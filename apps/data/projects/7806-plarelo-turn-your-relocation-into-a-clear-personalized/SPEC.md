# SPEC.md — Plarelo – Turn your relocation into a clear, personalized action plan

## Problem

Plarelo helps individuals and relocation advisors plan moves with clarity. It creates an AI-tailored plan based on destination, visa type, family, and work details, then organizes everything into decisions, tasks, and paperwork. Users track deadlines on a visual timeline, prioritize immigration, housing, and schools, and export PDFs to share. Advisors use structured intake, manage multiple clients in one place, and review AI-drafted plans to guide progress with expert context. Relocation professionals can also create reusable project templates to make creating relocation plans for their clients easier. View startup
