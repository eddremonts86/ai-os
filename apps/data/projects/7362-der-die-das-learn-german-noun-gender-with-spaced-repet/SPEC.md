# SPEC.md — DER DIE DAS – learn German noun gender with spaced repetition and games

## Problem

I built this app for my language buddy who is learning German (while he teaches me Chinese). He struggled so much with German articles&#x2F;determiners. So I made this, it also teaches article changes and noun declensions.
There is spaced repetition, audio loops, multi-player games, some cultural lessons and more. It would be nice to get some feedback from German learners!
