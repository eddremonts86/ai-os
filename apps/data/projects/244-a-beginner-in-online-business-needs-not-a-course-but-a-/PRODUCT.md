# A beginner in online business needs not a course, but a personalized AI guide that will create and lead them through an individual step-by-step plan

> Auto-generated product brief. Review and refine before MVP scoping.

## Value Proposition

Replaces the generic 40-video course with a personalised plan that is specific to the beginner's actual idea and capacity, and walks them through it.

## Target Users

USA-based online-business beginners who have completed at least one course and bounced; coaches and communities who want a guided-plan product for their members.

## Jobs To Be Done

When I finish a course and still do not know what to do, I want a plan built for my idea so I have something specific to work on this week. When I check in next week, I want the plan to have updated based on what I actually did.

## Success Metrics

Plan completion rate at 30 / 60 / 90 days. Subscription retention at 90 days. NPS among users who complete the 90-day plan.

## Competitive Landscape

Generic courses (Coursera, Udemy, Skillshare) are content libraries, not personalised. AI business coaches exist (e.g. MentorAI) but mostly answer questions, not generate plans. No source-named competitor that combines plan generation with guided check-ins.
