# SPEC.md — HSL-Noise – A noise generator mapped to the HSL color space

## Problem

I felt that the classical &quot;colors of noise&quot; (Pink, Brownian, White...) were very limiting when describing a very specific noise profile. I decided to map all colors to noise distributions, from Red to Blue. I like having green noise in the background when working.
