# Concourse – Automate finance workflows with audit-ready AI agents

## Value Proposition

Automate finance workflows without losing the audit trail. Concourse runs AI agents that adapt to your data, logic and workflows across variance analysis, close, forecasting, accounts receivable and weekly reviews, delivering audit-ready outputs with full traceability and governance into email, Slack, Teams, Excel and PowerPoint — deployed with SOC 1 and 2 posture and guided by ex-CFOs.

**One-liner:** The AI execution layer for enterprise finance, with audit-ready agents.

## Target Users

| Stakeholder | Why they care |
|---|---|
| Finance and FP&A teams | Weekly reviews, close and variance analysis run by agents instead of hours of manual work. |
| Controllers and auditors | Traceability and governance on every output makes AI acceptable in a control environment. |
| CFOs | Deployment support from ex-CFOs who know finance workflows. |
| Enterprise IT and security | SOC 1 and 2 posture fits existing vendor-assessment processes. |

## Jobs To Be Done

1. **Functional job** — Run variance analysis, close, forecasting, accounts receivable and weekly reviews on the company's own data and logic.
2. **Functional job** — Produce outputs that show the source data and steps behind every number.
3. **Functional job** — Deliver results into email, Slack, Teams, Excel and PowerPoint, the formats finance already uses.
4. **Emotional job** — Let finance teams adopt AI without feeling they are surrendering control to a black box.

## Success Metrics

- **Workflow coverage:** finance workflows actually running on the platform, of the five named.
- **Traceability rate:** share of outputs with complete source-to-result trails.
- **Hours saved per workflow:** manual time displaced per close cycle or review.
- **Adoption depth:** share of pilot teams that move from one workflow to several.

The post does not name competitors. The category is AI copilots and workflow automation for the finance office (close management, FP&A software, general enterprise agents); Concourse's stated differentiators are audit-ready traceability, SOC 1 and 2 posture and ex-CFO-led deployment.

## Risks & Open Questions

- [ ] The SOC 1 and 2 claims are vendor statements; customers will demand audit reports, and delivery must match.
- [ ] Finance logic is judgment-heavy; agent errors in close or forecasting carry real financial consequences.
- [ ] "Connects to your finance stack" is unqualified; each customer's ERPs and tools may need bespoke work.
- [ ] Excel and PowerPoint outputs are the trust bridge; formatting and fidelity must be near-perfect.
- [ ] The ex-CFO pairing implies a services margin; unclear how it scales alongside the product.
