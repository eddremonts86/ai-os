# Concourse – Automate finance workflows with audit-ready AI agents

## Tech Stack

- **AI agent orchestration for finance:** agents that chain data retrieval, computation and review steps.
- **ERP and finance-stack connectors:** per-customer integrations into accounting and planning systems.
- **Audit trail and traceability store:** every output records its inputs, transformations and approvals.
- **Excel and PowerPoint report generation:** finance-grade formatting and fidelity.
- **Slack, Teams and email delivery adapters:** results land where finance works.
- **SOC 1 and SOC 2 compliant deployment:** controls, logging and access management built into the platform.

## Architecture

- **Connector layer:** authenticated, read-mostly access to each customer's finance systems.
- **Agent runtime:** workflows (variance, close, forecast, accounts receivable, weekly review) as orchestrated agent graphs.
- **Traceability store:** immutable record of inputs, steps, outputs and sign-offs per run.
- **Output layer:** renders results to Excel, PowerPoint and chat or email channels.
- **Governance:** roles, approvals and audit exports aligned to SOC 1 and 2 control sets.

## Milestones

1. **M0 — One auditable workflow.** Variance analysis or weekly review end to end with a full traceability trail.
2. **M1 — Format coverage.** Excel and PowerPoint outputs plus Slack, Teams and email delivery for the first workflow.
3. **M2 — Workflow breadth.** Close, forecasting and accounts receivable added on the same governance rails.
4. **M3 — Compliance and services.** The SOC 1 and 2 evidence pack and the ex-CFO deployment playbook.

## Risks

- **Accuracy risk is financial, not cosmetic:** a wrong variance number is a real problem.
- **Connector sprawl:** every new finance stack expands the maintenance surface.
- **Traceability is only as strong as its ingestion:** gaps upstream break the audit promise.
- **Enterprise sales cycles are long:** the capture gives no signal on demand maturity.
