# Concourse – Automate finance workflows with audit-ready AI agents

## Problem

Concourse positions itself as the AI execution layer for enterprise finance. The BetaList capture describes agents that adapt to a company's data, logic and workflows to run variance analysis, close, forecasting, accounts receivable and weekly reviews. Two claims anchor the pitch: outputs are audit-ready with full traceability and governance, and delivery happens through the channels finance already uses — email, Slack, Teams, Excel and PowerPoint. The platform connects to the existing finance stack, meets SOC 1 and 2 standards, and pairs customers with ex-CFOs to help deploy.

## Objective

Make AI agents usable inside finance's control environment: build agents that execute real finance workflows (variance analysis, close, forecasting, accounts receivable, weekly reviews) against the company's own systems, with every output traceable and governable, delivered in the formats and channels finance teams already consume.

## Target Users

- Finance teams at mid-size and enterprise companies running close, forecasting and variance analysis.
- Controllers and FP&A leads who need audit-ready output, not black-box answers.
- CFOs who want AI deployed without rebuilding governance from scratch (the ex-CFO pairing speaks to them).

## MVP Scope

- One workflow vertical first (for example variance analysis or weekly reviews) run by an agent on real finance data.
- Connectors to the finance stack named by the customer's environment (the capture says "connects to your finance stack" without naming vendors).
- Full traceability: every output tied to source data and the logic applied.
- Delivery through email, Slack, Teams, Excel and PowerPoint.
- A SOC 1 and 2 aligned deployment posture.

## Constraints

- The capture is a BetaList listing; the SOC 1 and 2 claim is stated by the vendor, not independently verified.
- Audit-readiness is the differentiator and the hardest promise; traceability must be designed in, not added.
- Enterprise finance stacks vary; connector scope is per-customer work until patterns emerge.
- The ex-CFO pairing implies a services component; the MVP must define the boundary between product and services.
