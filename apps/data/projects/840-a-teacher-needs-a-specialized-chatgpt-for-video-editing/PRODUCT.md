# A teacher needs a specialized «ChatGPT for video editing»

> Auto-generated product brief. Review and refine before MVP scoping.

## Value Proposition

Let a teacher say what they want edited and get an edited file back, without opening Premiere or Final Cut.

## Target Users

| Stakeholder | Why they care |
|---|---|
| Primary user in USA | K-12 and community-college teachers in the US who record lectures or class clips and need to publish trimmed, captioned versions without learning a desktop NLE. |
| Adjacent user | Anyone the primary user would need to coordinate with to use the product. |

## Jobs To Be Done

- When I record a 50-minute class, give me a 10-minute highlight automatically.
- When I have a transcript, give me a captioned version of the highlight.
- When I want a specific cut, let me say it in a sentence and get the file back.

## Success Metrics

Whether a teacher with no video-editing background can produce a captioned 5-minute clip in under ten minutes.

## Pricing & Monetization

The source does not name a price or willingness-to-pay. Pricing is left as an open question to be answered after user interviews, not invented here.

## Competitive Landscape

Video-editing assistants (CapCut, Descript) exist, but the source does not name any direct competitor that is purpose-built as a teacher-specialized ChatGPT for video editing.

## Risks & Open Questions

- Browser-side ffmpeg.wasm struggles with hour-long 4K files; a teacher-class clip is usually fine but it is a real ceiling.
- Whisper transcripts of student voices raise privacy questions; make local-only the default.
