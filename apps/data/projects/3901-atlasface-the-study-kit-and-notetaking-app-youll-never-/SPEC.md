# Atlas:Face – the study kit and notetaking app you'll never leave

## Problem

The capture for this plan is a URL-only Show HN submission pointing at https://atlasface.resistancelabs.tech/. The product claim carried by the title is Atlas:Face, a study kit and notetaking app positioned with a bold retention promise — the app you'll never leave. The capture states nothing further: no feature list, no platform, no pricing and no evidence behind the retention claim.

## Objective

Build the MVP the title describes: one workspace where notes and study live together — notes become flashcards, flashcards drive a review schedule, and the schedule brings the user back. The MVP must earn the retention claim mechanically through a review loop, not assume it.

## Target Users

- Students managing courses, notes and exam prep in too many separate apps.
- Lifelong learners taking notes that never turn into retained knowledge.
- Professionals studying for certifications alongside daily work.
- Note-takers who have tried one tool per job and want one workspace.

## MVP Scope

- A note editor with basic markdown.
- Note-to-flashcard conversion.
- A spaced-repetition review queue.
- Search and organization of notes.

## Constraints

- The source is a bare URL plus title; the feature set is our design.
- The retention claim is marketing until a review loop exists; the MVP must not overpromise it.
- Data portability matters in this category: users must be able to export what they put in.
- No platform, pricing or user evidence exists in the capture; none may be invented.
