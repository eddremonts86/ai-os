# Own Miami – Compare Miami condo buildings with live data and clear insights

## Problem

Own Miami organizes Miami’s condo market building by building so you can buy with confidence. It tracks every tower, listing, and sale across key neighborhoods, then computes rankings, price indices, and estimates from live MLS data. You can explore building health, value, liquidity, and Airbnb policies, spot deals below estimate, and review pre-construction timelines. Use neighborhood sites, buyer guides, and market reports to zero in on the right building before you make an offer. View startup
