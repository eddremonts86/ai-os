# AI images and videos from text, free, no signup

## Value Proposition

Generate an image — and, per the post's title, a video — from text without creating an account or paying anything, and keep the result without a watermark. The pitch is zero friction: no signup wall, no credit card, no trial countdown before the first generation. The service's own description stresses free, registration-free image generation with watermark-free saves; the HN title extends that to video.

**One-liner:** AI images and videos from text, free and without signup.
