# Thread Otter – AI marketing agent for founder-led GTM on Reddit, X, and LinkedIn

## Problem

Somewhere on Reddit right now, someone is asking for exactly what you build. You'll never see it because you have a company to run. Thread Otter is an AI marketing agent for founder-led GTM. It watches Reddit, X, LinkedIn, and Bluesky, scores every mention for buying intent, and drafts replies in your voice based on your product docs. Messages go out from your accounts with safety guardrails like warm-up, pacing, rules checks, and removal verification. You can start with approval-first, then hand channels to autopilot as drafts earn it. Conversations are tracked to signups. See it free: drop your URL at threadotter.com/buyer-report to see the threads where your buyers are asking. View startup
