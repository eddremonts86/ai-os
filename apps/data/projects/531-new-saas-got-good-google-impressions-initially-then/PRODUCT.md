# "New SaaS got good Google impressions initially, then"

> Auto-generated product brief. Review and refine before MVP scoping.

## Value Proposition

A solo founder who sees a Google impressions drop in their first 2 weeks can upload a Search Console screenshot and get a verdict in under a minute — settling curve or real signal — plus a 3-check plan before drawing any conclusion, instead of guessing from a week and a half of data.

## Target Users

| Stakeholder | Why they care |
|---|---|
| Solo founder / indie hacker | Just submitted a new site; needs to know whether the drop is normal. |
| SEO consultant | Runs the same diagnostic for new clients; wants a fast first read. |
| Small in-house marketer | Watching the same report quarterly; needs a sanity check. |

## Jobs To Be Done

1. **Functional job** — Decide whether the impression drop is worth acting on.
2. **Emotional job** — Stop worrying about a week and a half of GSC data.
3. **Social job** — Be able to defend the "wait" vs. "act" call with a verdict the founder didn't write themselves.

## Success Metrics

- **Diagnostic accuracy:** ≥60% of users report the verdict matched the outcome 4 weeks later.
- **Time-to-verdict:** median under 60 seconds from upload.
- **Trust:** ≥3 founder posts cite the tool within 90 days.

## Pricing & Monetization

Free. Supported by an affiliate link to a recommended SEO tool (Ahrefs, Semrush) for founders who need deeper diagnostics after the verdict.

## Competitive Landscape

- **GSC alone** — shows the data, no verdict.
- **SEO blogs on "new site impressions"** — long-form, generic, not personalized.
- **SEO consultants** — expensive, slow, overkill for a week-old site.

## Risks & Open Questions

- [ ] Validate the verdict logic against 30 real new-site GSC curves before launch.
- [ ] Confirm screenshot OCR is reliable enough for small UIs.
