# What's the job market like for Sales Engineers

## Problem

I'm a Staff-level PM at a tech company right now, and have found myself in a really rough corner. I transitioned from a Sales Engineer role to a Product Manager role almost over 6 years ago, mostly to spend more time with family and not have to travel as much. It's been a fun ride.I increasingly find myself wanting to go back to being customer-facing. I'm not super interested in this whole FDE thingy. It seems like it's going to fizzle out once VC subsidization of consulting services expenses becomes less en vogue.Is Sales Engineering still a fun job? What's the market like right now for more Senior/Staff-level SE?
