# SPEC.md — Remove people from photos by brushing over them

## Problem

I built a product for removing people over the weekend. I made this tool because I like photography. Every time I go through my photos, I find a few photos that are very good but also include other tourists, and in those cases I need to remove the tourists.<p>The website has just been set up, and the main functionality is already working. In some cases, for example when people overlap with each other, the removal may not be complete. I am still looking into this.<p>You are welcome to try it, and I really look forward to your feedback.
