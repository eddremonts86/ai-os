# TurboHog – Auto-schedule your day to manage meetings and finish tasks

## Problem

TurboHog plans your day for you. Connect Google Calendar, add tasks in natural language, and it automatically schedules work around meetings, priorities, and deadlines across multiple calendars. It manages invites, recurring tasks, and shifting workloads so you always know what to do next. Use bulk import to easily create tasks and follow a schedule that adapts as your day changes.
