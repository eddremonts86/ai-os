_Based on source brief:_ I created an interactive map of genetic similarity between populations, based on the Moriopoulos Collection 2025 (<a href="https:&#x2F;&#x2F;genarchivist.net&#x2F;showthread.php?tid=1449" rel="nofollow">https:&#x2F;&#x2F;genarchivist.net&#x2F;showthread.php?tid=1449</a>).<p>Each data point is a 25-dimensional representation (Global25), and I use Euclidean distance.<p>There is already <a href="https:&#x2F;&#x2F;genetic-distance.com&#x2F;" rel="nofollow">https:&#x2F;&#x2F;genetic-distance.com&#x2F;</a>, where you can look at the closest populations as a table.<p>The vis should come with many caveats.
