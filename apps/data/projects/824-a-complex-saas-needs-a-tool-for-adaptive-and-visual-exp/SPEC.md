# A complex SaaS needs a tool for adaptive and visual explanation of the product to different audiences

## Problem

A complex SaaS team needs to explain its product to several different audiences — engineers, business buyers, end users, and partners — without writing the same deck four times. The poster, filed in the marketing category from India, frames this as a tooling gap: the explanation is one product, but the audience is many, and there is no single tool that adapts and visualises it for each audience.

The source describes the pain only at the level of the title; the post itself contains no metrics, no named users beyond the implied multi-audience, and no budget. Anything beyond that framing would be invented.

## Objective

Give a SaaS marketing or product team one workspace where they define the product once and produce audience-tailored, visual explanations from the same source.

## Target Users

Marketing and product teams at complex SaaS companies who must explain the same product to engineers, business buyers, end users, and partner channels. The poster frames the buyers as the team that needs the tool, not the audiences they address.

## MVP Scope

- A single product description authored once by the team (facts, claims, screenshots).
- Audience templates — engineer, business buyer, end user, partner — that select which facts surface and how they are framed.
- Generated visual explanation per audience (slides, annotated screenshots, or short walkthroughs).
- Export to the formats a marketing team already uses (PDF deck, share link, embed code).
