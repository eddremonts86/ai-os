# Paid Lens – Turn cross-platform ad data into ranked, evidence-backed actions

> Auto-generated product brief. Review and refine before MVP scoping.

## Value Proposition

A read-only decision layer on top of a team's ad platforms and CRM: it connects, validates measurement quality, and produces a ranked list of "do this next" actions — each with expected outcome, confidence, and the evidence behind it — plus blended analytics and a plain-English AI analyst for the underlying data. The team's ad accounts stay locked down; the team gets a ranked list to review, approve, and track.

## Target Users

- Performance marketing teams running paid media across multiple ad platforms that need to choose where the next dollar goes.
- Marketing leaders who want their team to act on ranked, evidence-backed suggestions rather than chasing dashboards.
- Teams unwilling to grant third-party tools write access to their ad accounts.

## Jobs To Be Done

- When I sit down to plan this week's spend, I want a ranked list of the highest-impact moves with expected outcome and confidence, so I do not have to triangulate across platform dashboards.
- When an action is ranked #1, I want to see the evidence behind it, so I can decide whether to approve without taking it on faith.
- When I have a question about the data ("how is Meta performing against Google this month?"), I want to ask in plain English and get an answer grounded in the same data the rankings use.
- When I connect a new data source, I want to know up front how trustworthy its measurement is, so the ranking carries a confidence I can defend.

## Risks & Open Questions

- Read-only constraint vs. automation temptation. Teams will want actions to be applied automatically; the product's own pitch is that write access is not given. If a future feature crosses that line, the value proposition changes silently.
- Measurement-quality judgments need to be defensible. If a "low data trust" flag is wrong, the team's confidence in the rankings erodes; the risk is that the validation step is treated as a black box.
- AI analyst accuracy is the credibility front. The plain-English analyst shares the brand with the ranked actions; an analyst that hallucinates erodes the trust the ranking needs.
- Supported platforms / CRMs are unstated. The post does not name which ad platforms or CRMs Paid Lens connects to. Without that list, a prospective buyer cannot tell if their stack is covered.
