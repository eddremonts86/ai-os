# SPEC.md — Gorizzume – Land UK jobs with visa sponsorship using ATS-proof CVs and prep

## Problem

Gorizzume helps international students and recent graduates in the UK target roles that sponsor visas and get past ATS filters. It includes a UK-format CV builder, tailored cover letters, an ATS scanner, interview coaching with STAR scoring, a job tracker, and a job match engine that flags visa-sponsoring employers. The platform also offers a live sponsor register and graduate test prep for Watson Glaser, SJT, and SHL formats, guiding users from application to offer letter. View startup
