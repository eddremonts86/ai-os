# No quick, cheap fix for leaky faucets: no clear DIY guide or affordable plumber

> Auto-generated product brief. Review and refine before MVP scoping.

## Value Proposition

An Indian household goes from 'tap is dripping' to 'fix booked or part ordered' in under two minutes, with a price that matches the size of the repair, not the size of the call-out.

## Target Users

| Stakeholder | Why they care |
|---|---|
| Indian homeowner/tenant with a dripping tap | Wants a fast, cheap, low-risk fix; will trade privacy for a working faucet tonight. |
| Local Indian plumber | Wants a steady stream of small, paid jobs in their pin-code without running their own marketing or taking cash-only walk-ins. |

## Jobs To Be Done

1. **Functional job** — Identify which ₹20 part actually fixes this specific tap without buying the wrong one.
2. **Emotional job** — Stop the daily annoyance of a dripping tap without feeling ripped off by a plumber.
3. **Social job** — Have a record of 'what part was changed when' that a landlord, spouse, or future tenant can read.

## Success Metrics

- Time-to-fix median ≤ 1 hour for DIY path, ≤ 4 hours for plumber path in pilot cities.
- First-attempt correct-part rate ≥ 75% (user does not reorder a part after one wrong pick).
- Plumber job completion rate ≥ 90% with rating ≥ 4/5.
- Repeat use: ≥ 30% of households log a second tap repair within 90 days.

## Pricing & Monetization

Free DIY path with an affiliate fee on parts purchased via linked retailers. Plumber path: flat ₹99 booking fee + transparent part+labor quote shown upfront before the visit is confirmed.

## Competitive Landscape

- YouTube DIY plumbing videos — long, Western tap models, no parts catalogue for India, no booking path.
- UrbanClap / HouseJoy / NoBroker plumber services — generalist, dispatch a plumber for a fee without diagnosis upfront, so the user still does not know if the problem needs one.
- Local hardware store referral — word of mouth, slow, no rating.

## Risks & Open Questions

- [ ] Plumber fraud — a plumber could quote a higher repair than needed. Mitigation: capped price per repair type, paid via the app, released on completion.
- [ ] Parts catalogue freshness — prices and stock on Amazon/Flipkart change daily. Mitigation: link out, do not host the SKU; show 'verify on retailer site' badge.
- [ ] Tap model coverage — India has hundreds of tap SKUs; v1 covers the top 30 single-lever and two-handle models that account for most household installs.
