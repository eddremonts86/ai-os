# Problem of market access for francophone African freelancers

> Auto-generated product brief. Review and refine before MVP scoping.

## Value Proposition

A francophone African freelancer can publish a credible profile, apply to international briefs in their language, and receive escrow payouts to mobile money or a local bank — without needing a US/EU payment account to participate.

## Target Users

| Stakeholder | Why they care |
|---|---|
| Francophone African freelancer | Skills match international demand but is locked out by language-default platforms and payment friction. |
| International SMB / agency | Needs French-language deliverables and a faster path than generic freelancer marketplaces. |
| Diaspora buyer | Hires for personal projects in French; wants escrow and a clear dispute path. |

## Jobs To Be Done

1. **Functional job** — Find a paying international client for French-language work without spending weeks applying on the wrong platforms.
2. **Emotional job** — Be treated as a peer professional, not as a "developing market" candidate.
3. **Social job** — Have a profile link to share that proves real, paid, reviewed work.

## Success Metrics

- **Activation:** freelancer profile + first application within 30 minutes of signup.
- **Conversion:** ≥ 25% of vetted freelancers land a paid brief within 60 days of going live.
- **Payout reliability:** ≥ 95% of payouts land in the freelancer's chosen rail (mobile money / local bank) within 48 hours of approval.
- **Dispute rate:** ≤ 5% of escrowed jobs end in a dispute.

## Pricing & Monetization

Freelancer side: 10% commission on paid invoices. Buyer side: optional "managed" tier at 5% added on top of freelancer fee for full dispute handling + translation review.

## Competitive Landscape

- **Upwork / Fiverr** — global reach but English-default, payment rails favour US/EU, review systems are slow for new accounts from francophone Africa.
- **Local gig platforms (e.g. Jobberman Africa)** — strong on local gigs, limited international demand for French work.
- **Direct outreach via LinkedIn / WhatsApp** — works for senior freelancers but burns hours per brief and has no escrow.

## Risks & Open Questions

- [ ] Confirm Stripe Connect availability for Cameroon-based accounts in the pilot period; if blocked, default to a local payout partner.
- [ ] Define the "paid trial" vetting bar before launch — who reviews, who pays for the trial task.
- [ ] Decide whether to translate proposals automatically or require the freelancer to author both languages.
