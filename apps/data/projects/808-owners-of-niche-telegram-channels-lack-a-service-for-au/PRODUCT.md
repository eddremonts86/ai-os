# Owners of niche Telegram channels lack a service for automatically finding and organizing cross-promotion with relevant channels without spending hours on manual selection and negotiations

> Auto-generated product brief. Review and refine before MVP scoping.

## Value Proposition

A Telegram channel owner finds complementary channels, sees audience-overlap and engagement-quality signals, and proposes a post-swap or paid cross-promo in one click — without hours of manual outreach and DM negotiation.

## Target Users

| Stakeholder | Why they care |
|---|---|
| Niche Telegram channel owners (1K–100K subs) | Run cross-promo as a growth lever and want to skip the manual outreach grind. |
| Telegram-channel agencies + growth consultants | Run cross-promo for multiple channel owners and need a shared marketplace. |

## Jobs To Be Done

1. **Functional job** — Find complementary channels for cross-promo and propose a swap or paid deal in one click.
2. **Emotional job** — Stop the recurring wasted hours on outreach that never closes.
3. **Social job** — Be known as a channel owner who runs cross-promo at scale, not as one who pitches every channel manually.

## Success Metrics

- **Activation:** channel profile imported in under 5 minutes for 80% of new signups.
- **Match precision:** ≥ 30% of match suggestions lead to a sent proposal.
- **Close rate:** ≥ 20% of sent proposals result in a closed cross-promo.
- **Cross-promo count:** ≥ 2 cross-promos closed per active channel per month.

## Pricing & Monetization

Free for 5 match searches per month + unlimited post-swap proposals (no fee on the swap). $19/month for unlimited match searches + paid cross-promo settlement. Platform takes 8% of paid cross-promo value held in escrow. Telegram Stars + USDT settlement only; no fiat custody.

## Competitive Landscape

- **Manual Telegram search + outreach** — works at 1–2 cross-promos per quarter, doesn't scale.
- **Telemetr.me + TGStat** — channel analytics, no cross-promo matchmaking or settlement.
- **Instagram / YouTube cross-promo tools (Collabstr, AspireIQ)** — same mechanic, Telegram unsupported.
- **Telegram-channel directories (tlgrm.ru, telemetr.me catalog)** — discovery only, no proposal or settlement layer.

## Risks & Open Questions

- [ ] Validate whether public t.me/preview metadata is rich enough to compute reliable engagement-quality signals, or whether post-level sampling is required.
- [ ] Confirm the 8% escrow fee covers Telegram Stars + USDT settlement costs without losing money on small deals.
- [ ] Decide whether the platform should expand to other messenger platforms (Discord, Slack communities) in v2 or stay Telegram-only through 2026.
