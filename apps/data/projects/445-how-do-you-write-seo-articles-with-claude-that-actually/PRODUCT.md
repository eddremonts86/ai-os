# How do you write SEO articles with Claude that actually rank?

> Auto-enriched product brief.

## Value Proposition

A structured SEO-article pipeline that pairs Claude with keyword research and SERP analysis, producing drafts that pass a citation + entity-coverage rubric.

## Target Users

- SEO agencies producing content at scale
- In-house content marketers at B2B SaaS companies
- Solo founders running content as a distribution channel

## Jobs To Be Done

When I am producing SEO content with Claude, I want a pipeline that catches unsupported claims and missing entities before publish, so the articles rank instead of being politely ignored.

## Success Metrics

- At least 30 articles produced in pilot
- At least 50% reach page 2 of Google within 90 days of publish
