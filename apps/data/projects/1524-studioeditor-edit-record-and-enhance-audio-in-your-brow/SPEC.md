# StudioEditor – Edit, record, and enhance audio in your browser for free

## Problem

StudioEditor is a browser-based audio editor that lets you import, record, cut, arrange, and mix speech, music, and effects with an intuitive timeline. It offers free editing, project saving, and exporting with a free account, while paid processing credits handle tasks like audio enhancement, automatic pause tightening, transcription with subtitle exports, and episode summaries. AutoDuck and Auto Enhance streamline mixing and polish so you can produce clean, professional audio quickly. View startup
