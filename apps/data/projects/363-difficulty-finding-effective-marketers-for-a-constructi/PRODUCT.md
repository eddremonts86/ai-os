# Difficulty finding effective marketers for a construction business

> Product brief authored from the source title and category. The poster's text was not available (source.name: manual); sections below re-state the problem and infer only what the title and category support.

## Value Proposition

A Russian construction-business owner posts a budget and a region, picks a vetted marketer who has produced construction campaigns, sees the leads and the signed-contract value in one dashboard, and pays the marketer a percentage of the contracts signed - not the clicks.

## Target Users

- Russian small construction-business owners (renovation, modular, finish work) with RUB 50-300k/month marketing budget.
- Russian marketers with a track record in construction campaigns who want lead-gen-for-percentage deals instead of flat retainers.
- Russian construction holding-company marketing leads evaluating a vetted external marketer per region.

## Jobs To Be Done

1. **Functional job** - Get a marketer who understands construction buying cycles without hiring one in-house.
2. **Emotional job** - Stop worrying that the marketing spend is producing traffic, not contracts.
3. **Social job** - Hand the business partner a marketing report that names the contracts, not the impressions.

## Success Metrics

- **Activation:** first vetted marketer picked within 7 days of signup.
- **Cost-per-signed-contract:** within the marketer's agreed cap on >= 80% of campaigns.
- **Retention:** >= 60% of builders renew with the same marketer after the first 90 days.

## Competitive Landscape

- **Yandex Direct / VK Ads direct** - what owners try first; marketer expertise is the gap.
- **Generalist freelance marketplaces** - marketers are not vetted for construction campaigns.
- **Construction industry word-of-mouth** - what owners use today; regional coverage is uneven.
