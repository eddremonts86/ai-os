# GeoCopy – Publish AI-citable articles that drive traffic from chatbots

## Problem

GeoCopy helps you publish articles that ChatGPT, Claude, and Perplexity can easily extract, cite, and link. The platform structures content for AI citations and SEO, then pushes it directly to WordPress, Webflow, Shopify, or Wix with one click. You can research keyword opportunities, generate drafts quickly, and scale output with plans supporting hundreds of articles per month. Teams track impact with contribution dashboards and optional AI citation reports for measurable growth. View startup
