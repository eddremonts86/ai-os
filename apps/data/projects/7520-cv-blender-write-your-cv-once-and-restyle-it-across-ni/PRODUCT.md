## Value Proposition

_Based on source brief:_ CV Blender lets you write your resume once and switch among nine designer templates with a single click. It generates a live, print-ready PDF as you type and offers region-ready layouts for the EU, U.S., Canada, Australia, and India that follow local conventions and remain ATS-friendly. A built-in AI polishes any field with one click, and an AI chat assistant edits your whole CV on request, from tightening bullet points to tailoring it to a job ad you paste in. You can customize colors with palettes or gradients, import existing CV JSON, and download three PDFs or Word files a month for free, with one-time passes and AI credit packs for higher limits.
