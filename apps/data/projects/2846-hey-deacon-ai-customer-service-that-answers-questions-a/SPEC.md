# Hey Deacon – AI customer service that answers questions and surfaces user insights

## Problem

Hey Deacon is an AI customer service agent you can add to your app. It is aimed at startups and answers customer questions using natural language, while gathering pain points and feedback to reveal patterns and priorities. It bases responses on your documentation, scores their validity, and logs every conversation. You install a single script, add docs, URLs, and videos, then go live in minutes, improving activation and guiding your roadmap with real customer intent. View startup
