# SPEC.md — Ori's List – Hire SEO agencies vetted by live Google Search Console data

## Problem

The question every business asks before hiring an SEO agency is: can they actually get results? But there's never been a real way to check since agencies show case studies and screenshots they picked themselves. So I built Ori's List: it connects to an agency's own Google Search Console and transparently shows whether they can rank their own website. If they can't rank themselves, why would you trust them to rank you? View startup
