# Hot take: PMF can overcompensate for marketing, but onboarding journeys are pre-requisite for them

## Tech Stack

Not applicable — the post contains no product. The author's own SaaS context is event-tech with 150k+ users and 4+ years in market, but no technology choices are discussed.

## Architecture

None. There is no product. The structure of the post itself is a four-question funnel diagnostic that the author recommends every founder apply to their own product's signup → first-value path.

## Milestones

The post has no milestones. If a "do the work" plan were extracted from the author's advice, the steps would be:

1. Map the steps from signup to the moment the user experiences value.
2. Measure how many users reach each step.
3. Identify the step with the largest drop.
4. Make that step easier before drawing any PMF conclusion.

## Risks

- **Self-report bias.** The author's experience is one niche (event-tech) at one scale (150k+ users); generalising to all SaaS is the source of the "hot take" framing.
- **Funnel diagnosis without instrumentation.** Many small SaaS teams do not have the analytics to actually answer "how far do users get?", so the advice stalls at the measurement step.
- **Advice decay.** The author defers references to a follow-up post that may or may not arrive; readers who act on this thread alone do so without the supporting material.
