# SPEC.md — RadioMap24 – Explore and play radio and podcasts worldwide on an interactive map

## Problem

RadioMap24 is a browser-based atlas for discovering and listening to radio stations and podcasts worldwide. It lets you explore an interactive map or a text list to find nearby or global streams, filter by genre and language, and save favorites locally. Features like Daily Journey, Radio Passport, Vibe Lens filters, and Drift routes help you create listening paths and track your exploration. Streams play directly from station hosts and only load when you choose to listen. View startup
