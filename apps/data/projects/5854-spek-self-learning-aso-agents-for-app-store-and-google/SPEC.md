# SPEC.md — Spek – Self-learning ASO agents for App Store and Google Play growth

## Problem

Spek helps app teams grow their app by turning real-time store data into keyword opportunities, competitor insights, and localized store updates to improve visibility on the App Store and Google Play. Your agents learn from results and refine their actions so your ASO keeps improving as the market changes. You can review recommendations, publish approved updates, and track search visibility in one workspace. Spek lets you spend less time on manual research and more time growing your app. It is built for app founders, mobile growth teams, and ASO agencies. View startup
