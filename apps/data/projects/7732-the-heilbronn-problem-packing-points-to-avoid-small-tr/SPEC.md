# SPEC.md — The Heilbronn Problem – packing points to avoid small triangles

## Problem

Hi HN,<p>I made this site to track the latest records for the Heilbronn problem. This is a pretty famous problem in the world of optimization, and I wanted to encourage people to contribute to it with a nice-looking site that visually shows what solutions look like.<p>If you&#x27;re an HN reader, you can probably contribute to this site! A large number of recent contributions have been made by people with a non-math background, and actually the person holding the most records on the site currently is a computational biologist. I&#x27;d love to hear what you think!
