# Indie developers have nowhere to cheaply and effectively find the first 50-100 active users to test new niche products

> Auto-generated product brief. Review and refine before MVP scoping.

## Value Proposition

Replaces the paid-ad / Product-Hunt / cold-DM pattern with a curated, niche-matched, human-approved outreach flow that delivers the first 50-100 active users at a fraction of the cost.

## Target Users

Russian indie developers launching niche products; solo founders with a v1 who need real users.

## Jobs To Be Done

When I launch a niche product, I want a short list of niche communities where my target user is active, with draft posts I can approve and send so I do not spend a week cold-DMing. When I get users, I want to know which community produced them so I can double down on what worked.

## Success Metrics

Active indie developers onboarded. Launches reaching the 50-100 active-user milestone within 30 days. Paid-tier conversion. Customer-reported quality of acquired users at 90 days.

## Competitive Landscape

Product Hunt is a launch destination but produces bursty traffic, not niche engagement. BetaList and Launching Next are similar. Reddit and Telegram direct outreach work but are unscalable. No source-named direct competitor that combines curated matching with human-approved outreach.
