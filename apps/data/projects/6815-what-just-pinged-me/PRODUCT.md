_Based on source brief:_ My wife kept getting some random-seeming notification sounds on her macbook that we couldn&#x27;t identify - a little quarter second &quot;bloop&quot; that would play and then go away without any trace we could find.<p>So I made (prompted) this app that will listen to the audio on the system and identify the sound.
