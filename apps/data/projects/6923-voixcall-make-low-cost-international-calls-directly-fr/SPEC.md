# SPEC.md — VoixCall – Make low-cost international calls directly from your browser

## Problem

VoixCall lets you place crystal-clear international calls right from your browser with transparent, pay-as-you-go pricing. Skip apps, SIMs, and roaming; just sign up with email, add credits, and dial landlines or mobiles in 160+ countries. Rates start at $0.02 per minute and you pay only for connected time. VoixCall also offers iOS and Android apps, caller ID options, contact saving, and quick top-ups, making it a practical choice for travelers, expats, and global teams. View startup
