# SPEC.md — DigiWarranty – Scan receipts, track warranties, and get timely reminders

## Problem

DigiWarranty helps you keep receipts, warranties, and rewards in one secure place. Capture paper or digital receipts in seconds, store them with product details, and see coverage timelines at a glance. The app sends reminders before warranties expire and lets you track loyalty points and personalized offers tied to real purchases. Available on iOS, Android, and web, DigiWarranty prioritizes privacy and gives customers confidence after every purchase. View startup
