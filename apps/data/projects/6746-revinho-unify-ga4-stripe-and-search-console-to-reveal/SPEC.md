# SPEC.md — Revinho – Unify GA4, Stripe, and Search Console to reveal what changed and why

## Problem

Revinho helps founder-led and lean SaaS teams understand what's driving growth and revenue. It connects Google Analytics 4, Stripe, and Search Console, normalizes historical data, and highlights the important changes with clear evidence and suggested next steps. Revinho uncovers opportunities across traffic, conversion, search, and revenue, and labels attribution quality as verified, modeled, or associated. It also provides read-only context to agents via MCP and REST. View startup
