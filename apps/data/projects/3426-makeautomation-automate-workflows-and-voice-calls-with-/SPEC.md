# MakeAutomation – Automate workflows and voice calls with fast, ops-ready AI systems

## Problem

MakeAutomation designs and builds AI-driven voice agents, internal dashboards, and workflow automation for B2B SaaS, SMBs, and mid-market teams. It connects calls, forms, and emails to CRMs and operational tools, enabling sub-600ms voice interactions, routing, logging, and smooth human handoff. The service maps real workflows, delivers a scoped phase one, and provides white-glove AI ops support so teams reduce manual steps and run a connected, usable system. View startup
