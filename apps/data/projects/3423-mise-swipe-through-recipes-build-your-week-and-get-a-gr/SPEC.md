# Mise – Swipe through recipes, build your week, and get a grocery list

## Problem

Mise is a meal planning app for iPhone and Android that helps you decide what's for dinner in one Sunday session. Swipe through over 1,500 recipes, confirm a weekly plan, and generate a grocery list organized by store section. Set dietary preferences to see only meals that fit, then cook with step-by-step instructions and timely reminders. View startup
