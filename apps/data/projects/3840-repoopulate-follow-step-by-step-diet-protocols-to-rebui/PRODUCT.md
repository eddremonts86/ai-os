# Repoopulate – Follow step-by-step diet protocols to rebuild your gut after disruption

## Value Proposition

Know what to eat when your gut needs a rebuild. Repoopulate turns post-disruption recovery — after a colonoscopy, antibiotics or food poisoning — into short, day-by-day protocols that say what to choose, what to avoid and why, pairing meal suggestions with clear biochemical reasoning so the first critical days stop being a guessing game.

**One-liner:** Step-by-step diet protocols that rebuild your gut after disruption, with the reasoning for every food choice.

## Target Users

| Stakeholder | Why they care |
|---|---|
| Post-colonoscopy patients | Day-by-day food guidance for the days after the procedure. |
| Antibiotic-course finishers | A deliberate path to rebuild the microbiome. |
| Food-poisoning recoverers | Gentle, low-irritation choices when digestion is fragile. |
| Health-conscious self-managers | Understanding the why behind each food, not just the what. |

## Jobs To Be Done

1. **Functional job** — Pick a recovery track matching the disruption (colonoscopy, antibiotics, food poisoning).
2. **Functional job** — Follow day-by-day phases telling what to eat and what to skip, with reasons.
3. **Functional job** — Choose meals from practical suggestions tied to the biochemical rationale.
4. **Emotional job** — Feel confident making gentle food choices during fragile first days.

## Success Metrics

- **Protocol starts:** share of visitors who begin a track after landing.
- **Day-by-day completion:** retention through the protocol's phases — the short-protocol promise.
- **Reasoning engagement:** reads of the why alongside meal choices.
- **Return or referral:** users who come back for a second track or share the protocol after recovery.
