# Community bots could become a real SaaS category

> Auto-generated product brief. Review and refine before MVP scoping.

## Value Proposition

_Based on source brief:_ When people talk about community bots, I still think most picture moderation, support tickets, welcome messages, that kind of thing. But there are a lot of workflows inside paid communities that people are still handling across separate tools. Memberships, payments, access, analytics, automations, even transactions depending on the community. Feels like there’s room for bots that handle one valuable workflow really well and charge a monthly subscription for it. Basically micro-SaaS that lives inside the community instead of sending everyone to another dashboard. I’d be curious if anyone here has built something like this or would consider paying for one. submitted by /u/Sea-Alternative-3505 [link] [comments]

**One-liner:** A subscription-priced bot that owns one community workflow end-to-end inside the community itself, instead of routing operators to a separate dashboard.

## Target Users

| Stakeholder | Why they care |
|---|---|
| Operators of paid online communities (Discord, Slack, Circle, Mighty Networks) | Currently stitch together moderation + memberships + payments + analytics across separate tools. |
| Community builders | Would pay a monthly subscription for a bot that owns one workflow rather than context-switching to a dashboard. |
| The founder / future founders | Validating whether the market thesis — "one workflow really well, subscription-priced, in-community" — converts into a real SaaS category. |

## Jobs To Be Done

1. **Functional job** — Run a community workflow (memberships / payments / access / analytics / automations / transactions) without leaving the community surface.
2. **Emotional job** — Stop paying for multiple SaaS dashboards that no operator actually opens.
3. **Social job** — Make the bot feel like a member of the community (it lives there), not a vendor tool.

## Success Metrics

The post does not publish specific numbers; it is itself the validation probe. Implied metrics for any product in this category:

- Monthly subscription conversion per community install.
- Workflow completion rate inside the community (how often the bot's workflow is used vs the operator falling back to a manual / dashboard flow).
- Net revenue per workflow per community per month.

## Pricing & Monetization

The post explicitly proposes a monthly subscription, not usage-based pricing. The exact tier is TBD; the natural shape is per-community (one subscription unlocks the bot for a whole Discord / Slack / Circle community) with workflow-based packaging.

## Risks & Open Questions

- [ ] The post is a market thesis, not a product — the founder has not picked which workflow to ship first. Picking the wrong one wastes the wedge.
- [ ] The "no dashboard" stance is a deliberate constraint; some operators will expect a dashboard regardless, which creates tension.
