# A gambling addict hasn't been able to quit online casinos for years. All known methods have failed. Need a long-term accountability system that adapts to relapse.

> Auto-generated product brief. Review and refine before MVP scoping.

## Value Proposition

Quit the relapse loop without having to fight it alone. The first 1000 yearly subscribers represent the cohort who tried everything else and finally want a system that adapts to them, not the other way around.

## Target Users

US adults in active recovery from online casino gambling who have already tried at least one app or group and relapsed. Secondary: their designated family or sponsor contacts who receive escalation messages.

## Jobs To Be Done

Functional: stop the slow drift back to the casino. Emotional: stop the shame of having tried and failed again. Social: be the person in the family who finally has a working plan, not a new app nobody else knows about.

## Success Metrics

Median time-to-first-relapse after signup is at least 4x longer than the user's prior pattern. At least 60% of yearly subscribers are still active at 6 months. Less than 10% of escalated contacts disengage from the thread.

## Competitive Landscape

Generic habit apps (Habitica, Streaks) do not handle the late-night geofence or escalation. Blocking software (BetBlocker, Gamban) is useful but the user can remove it. Sponsor-based 12-step programs are human but not adaptive. No US product combines Plaid-based transaction monitoring with SMS escalation and late-night geofence in one thread.

## Risks & Open Questions

Risk of liability if the system fails to escalate and a user has a serious incident. Risk of being seen as a substitute for clinical treatment. Plaid data access is regulated and expensive. SMS-based flows must respect TCPA opt-in carefully.
