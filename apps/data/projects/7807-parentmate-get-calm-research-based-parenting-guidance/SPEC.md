# SPEC.md — ParentMate – Get calm, research-based parenting guidance for real-life moments

## Problem

ParentMate offers on-demand parenting guidance for everyday challenges like tantrums, bedtime battles, and teen communication. It lets you describe real situations and provides thoughtful, research-based steps grounded in child psychology and developed with licensed youth counselors. You can track what helps over time with saved plans and follow-ups, browse age-relevant activities, and maintain profiles for each child. Your conversations stay private, giving you a judgment-free space to get unstuck. View startup
