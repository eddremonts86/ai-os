# Looking to contribute to an early-stage startup: GTM/PMM, research, or customer acquisition (open to unpaid work)

## Problem
 Hi everyone, I'm a 3rd-year BTech CSE student currently trying to build practical experience in product marketing and GTM. I want to be upfront about where I stand: I don't have professional GTM experience yet, and I'm not going to pretend that a few personal projects make me an experienced GTM professional. What I do have is a foundation in product work and a strong interest in learning through actual execution. I've previously done internships/volunteer work around product management, including: Market and competitive research Research around LLM-based products/apps Working with specialists on feature development Collaborating with engineering teams on feature implementation/integration Over the past few months, I've been moving toward PMM/GTM and have been using real-world startups as case studies. I've worked on things like: ICP and market research Positioning & messaging Competitive analysis Customer lifecycle / time-to-value analysis Identifying potential growth bottlenecks Thinking through acquisition and GTM channels Developing marketing/GTM strategies from publicly available data The limitation I've repeatedly run into is that personal projects can only take you so far. You can analyze a company's positioning from the outside, but you don't have access to its actual customer conversations, sales pipeline, conversion data, retention data, experiments, etc. And that's exactly the experience I'm trying to gain now. So here's what I'm looking for: I'm not specifically looking for a "GTM internship", because GTM covers a huge range of work. I'd be interested in helping an active early-stage startup with whatever area actually needs attention: customer research, competitive research, positioning, messaging, acquisition experiments, channel research, conversion, or other marketing/growth work. I'm also open to doing this unpaid initially. The main thing I'm looking for is the opportunity to actually work on a real product, with real customers and real constraints, rather than continuing to work only through hypothetical case studies. There are no big expectations from my side. Give me a real problem, let me put in the work, and we can see whether I'm actually useful. If you're building something and could use an extra pair of hands on the product/GTM/marketing side, feel free to DM me. I'd be happy to share the projects I've worked on and see if there's a fit. submitted by /u/Stunning-Extension80 [link] [comments]

## Objective

Capture a Reddit post where a 3rd-year BTech CSE student offers to do unpaid GTM/PMM work for an early-stage startup in exchange for hands-on access to real customer conversations, sales pipelines and conversion data. The post is a job-seek, not a product spec. The plan documents the underlying structural gap — students who can analyse positioning from the outside but cannot access the in-house data that GTM decisions actually run on — without inventing a product that would close it.

## Target Users

- Primary: a 3rd-year BTech CSE student with PM internships/volunteer work behind them, who has built PMM case studies on public SaaS products and now wants real in-house exposure.
- Secondary: other students in the same position who would trade unpaid work for access to startup data, plus early-stage founders who might consider such an arrangement.

## MVP Scope

The source post is a job-seek / collaboration request. No MVP is proposed. If anything were extracted, it would be a 'student-in-residence' matching tool, but the post never asks for one. Scope here is limited to documenting the gap and the offer.
