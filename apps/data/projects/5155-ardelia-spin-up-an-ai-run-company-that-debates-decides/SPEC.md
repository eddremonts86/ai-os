# SPEC.md — Ardelia – Spin up an AI-run company that debates, decides, and remembers

## Problem

Ardelia lets you create a persistent AI company staffed by executives, managers, and teams that operate continuously. It runs daily standups, holds real-time debates in channels, and escalates key decisions to you. Every decision you make becomes institutional memory that guides future actions. With Ardelia, you watch an AI workforce coordinate, refine strategy, and execute without micromanagement while you intervene only when necessary. View startup
