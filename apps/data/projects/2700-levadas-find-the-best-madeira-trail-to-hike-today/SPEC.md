# Levadas – Find the best Madeira trail to hike today

## Problem

Levadas scores every Madeira trail live so you always pick the right hike. It reads weather along each route at trail altitude, combines 33 data sources into a 0–100 score, and updates every 20 minutes. See official IFCN closures, crowd and booking heatmaps, and photo-worthy Moments with the best time window. Compare trails side by side, get start and end times for daylight, and tailor results to your fitness. Plan your days, save favorites, and unlock once for your whole trip. View startup
