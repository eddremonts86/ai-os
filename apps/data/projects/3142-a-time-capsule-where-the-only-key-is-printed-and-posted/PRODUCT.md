# A time capsule where the only key is printed and posted to you

## Value Proposition

A time capsule whose only key arrives in the post, so opening it needs a physical object rather than a password someone can lose to a breach.

## Target Users

People who want a secret held for later and want the act of opening it to require a printed key delivered to a physical address.

## Jobs To Be Done

- Lock a message or file until a physical key reaches me or someone I chose
- Hold something with no digital recovery path, deliberately
- Give a key as an object, by post, rather than as a link

## Success Metrics

- Capsules created and keys posted
- Share of posted keys that are eventually redeemed
- Time between posting and redemption, which is the product's real clock

## Competitive Landscape

Encrypted vault services (CryptoSteel, Glacier) exist, but the source does not name any direct competitor that prints and posts a physical key.

## Risks & Open Questions

- The source says nothing about what happens to the ciphertext if a key is never redeemed
- Postal reach and cost per capsule are unstated and bound where the service can operate
- No recovery is a feature and a support problem at the same time
