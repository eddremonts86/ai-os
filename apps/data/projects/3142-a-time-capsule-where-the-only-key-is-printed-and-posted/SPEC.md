# A time capsule where the only key is printed and posted to you

## Problem

The poster published a service at madebyahuman.global whose only key is printed and physically posted to the user. The HN post body contains no further detail beyond the link.

## Objective

Hold a secret for a person that can only be opened with a physical key mailed to them.

## Target Users

People who want a 'time capsule' that requires a physical artefact (a printed key delivered by post) to open.

## MVP Scope

Web service that accepts a secret, prints its key on paper, posts it, and reveals the secret once the holder redeems the key.

## Design Direction

See `DESIGN.md` for this project's design tokens.

## Constraints

Postal delivery is slow and geographically limited; lost keys are unrecoverable by design.
