# SPEC.md — MyEcoCred – Unify home solar, battery, heat pump, and EV data in one passport

## Problem

MyEcoCred provides a single dashboard to track solar, battery, heat pump, and EV performance for your home. It logs readings, ties them to your property, builds analytics, creates your renewables dashboard, and turns them into a Home Energy Passport. The platform calculates real payback against your tariffs, tracks costs and carbon avoided, stores certifications, and highlights green premium. View startup
