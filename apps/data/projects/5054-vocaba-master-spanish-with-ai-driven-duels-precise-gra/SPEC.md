# SPEC.md — Vocaba – Master Spanish with AI-driven duels, precise grading, and custom sentences

## Problem

Vocaba is a Spanish learning platform that uses an adaptive AI rival, precise linguistic analysis, and measurable ranks to push you to your limit. It grades full-sentence translations on meaning and grammar, accepts valid alternatives, and flags exact errors with context. A proprietary NLP engine parses every sentence into granular structures and CEFR modules. You can build or generate targeted practice in the Studio, then duel the Nemesis to close real gaps and climb from Newcomer to Grandmaster. View startup
