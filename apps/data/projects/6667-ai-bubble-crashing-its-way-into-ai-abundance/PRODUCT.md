I think the closest analogy may actually be the 2022 crypto crash, rather than the dot-com bubble or 2008.<p>The basic idea is a deflationary spiral that starts as a financing and liquidity problem near the top of the stack and propagates downward through the physical compute market. If GPU-backed infrastructure becomes distressed, forced selling could push GPU prices and rental rates down sharply, which could then create more distress elsewhere in the stack.<p>The more interesting question to me is what happens after that. What do the second- and third-order effects look like if GPUs temporarily become absurdly cheap?<p>Has anyone considered this possibility or know of good writing on the topic? I&#x27;d especially love to hear from people who have thought about GPU financing, data center economics, or the secondary GPU market.
