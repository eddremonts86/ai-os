# Stickyboard – Kanban on real sticky notes, no account needed

## Value Proposition

A Kanban board that feels like moving real sticky notes, with no signup, no install, and a keyboard-first workflow. Sign up only if you want your boards to follow you across devices.

## Target Users

- People who want to try a Kanban without committing to an account
- Students and hobbyists running personal project boards
- Keyboard-first users
- Casual teams with a side Kanban for scratch work

## Jobs To Be Done

- When I have a small project, I want to drag sticky notes between columns so I can see progress at a glance
- When I want to add a task, I want one keystroke so I do not break my flow
- When I switch boards, I want a number key so I can move between projects without a mouse

## Success Metrics

- Time to first board under 10 seconds
- 30% of returning visitors sign up for sync within the first quarter
- Median session length over 4 minutes (driven by drag-and-drop loop)

## Pricing & Monetization

Free for browser-local use; signup is free; source page lists no paid tier. Likely a Pro tier for collaboration, history, or advanced integrations.

## Competitive Landscape

- Trello — full Kanban, account required, learning curve
- Notion Kanban — buried inside Notion's docs surface
- Apple Notes / Reminders — checklist, no board metaphor
- Pen and paper — the original sticky note

## Risks & Open Questions

- Free + browser-local has unclear monetisation
- Sketching inside a sticky note is hard on touch devices
- Cross-device sync without an account is impossible by definition
- Drag-and-drop on mobile is awkward without a long-press hint
