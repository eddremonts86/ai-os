# The problem of discrimination, wage delays, and fear of firing/deportation among immigrant construction workers in the USA

## Value Proposition

A private logbook and a vetted legal-aid directory that lets immigrant construction workers in the USA keep records and find help without exposing them to retaliation.

## Target Users

Immigrant construction workers in the USA, often Spanish-speaking, who face discrimination, delayed wages or fear of firing and deportation if they speak up.

## Jobs To Be Done

When I am owed wages or treated unfairly on a job, I want a private place to log what happened and a way to find a lawyer or legal aid that knows immigration-sensitive cases, so I can decide what to do without putting my status at risk.

## Success Metrics

Whether immigrant construction workers use the logbook + legal-aid access together; the source gives no signal to ground a claim.
