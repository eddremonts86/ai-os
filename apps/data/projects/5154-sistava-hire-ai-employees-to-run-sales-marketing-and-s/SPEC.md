# Sistava – Hire AI employees to run sales, marketing, and support 24/7

## Problem

Sistava lets founders hire AI employees to handle sales, marketing, support, finance, and legal work 24/7. With no setup, workflow builders, or coding needed, these AI employees join your Slack channels, attend meetings, and send emails from dedicated mailboxes. You can talk to them through natural conversation and let them run core operations directly inside your existing tools. View startup
