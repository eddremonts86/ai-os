# SPEC.md — CleanTrade – Accept USDT with 0% fees and no KYC, funds go to your wallet

## Problem

CleanTrade is a USDT (TRC20) payment gateway for Telegram bots and micro-SaaS. The free plan costs $0 and allows up to 5 orders per month. There are no per-transaction fees; instead, a flat $29.99/month fee replaces the typical 0.4–1% per payment. It is non-custodial, so funds go straight to your own wallet with no merchant KYC or smart contracts. Create an order via API, get a unique amount and address, and when the buyer pays that exact amount, CleanTrade matches it in about 30 seconds and triggers your webhook. View startup
