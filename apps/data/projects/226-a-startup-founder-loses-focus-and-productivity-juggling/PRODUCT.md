# A startup founder loses focus and productivity juggling 5-7 tools for a single project. Existing 'all-in-one' tools are bloated, expensive, or stack-specific.

> Auto-generated product brief. Review and refine before MVP scoping.

## Value Proposition

Stop the 5-tool handoff tax. The first 1000 paying founders pay for the service from the recovered focus in the first month.

## Target Users

Indian startup founders and small-team leaders in the 2-10 person range who currently juggle 5-7 tools and are losing the project to the handoffs.

## Jobs To Be Done

Functional: have the spec, the decisions, the meetings, and the money in one place. Emotional: stop the dread of opening five tabs to find the project state. Social: be the founder who always has the answer on the project page.

## Success Metrics

At least 1000 active founders in 12 months. Tool count per active user below 3. Weekly active usage above 60% of monthly. Renewal rate above 75% after 6 months.

## Competitive Landscape

Notion is bloated. ClickUp is bloated. Linear is too narrow for the meetings and money. No mainstream service combines the project, the decisions, the meetings, and the project finance in one place with per-project pricing.

## Risks & Open Questions

Opinionated tools can lose users when the opinion is wrong. Import from Notion or Linear will miss context. Per-project pricing may not match the way teams grow. The finance piece requires a real payment-processor integration.
