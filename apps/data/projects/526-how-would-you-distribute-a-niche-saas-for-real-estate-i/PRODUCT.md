# How would you distribute a niche SaaS for real-estate investors?

> Auto-generated product brief. Review and refine before MVP scoping.

## Value Proposition

A niche-SaaS founder targeting real-estate investors can pick the top 5 distribution channels by expected CAC within an hour, run a 90-day experiment sequence, and track the result in a single worksheet, instead of guessing where the audience actually is.

## Target Users

| Stakeholder | Why they care |
|---|---|
| Niche-SaaS founder (real-estate investor focus) | Has a working product; doesn't know which channels actually reach the audience. |
| Proptech marketing lead | Entering the investor segment; needs a defensible CAC estimate. |
| Real-estate investor community admin | Wants to understand how tools are reaching their members. |

## Jobs To Be Done

1. **Functional job** — Pick distribution channels without trial-and-error for 6 months.
2. **Emotional job** — Stop feeling like every channel is a black box.
3. **Social job** — Be able to defend the GTM choice to an investor or co-founder with numbers, not vibes.

## Success Metrics

- **Playbook completion:** ≥70% of founders who start the playbook finish reading it.
- **Worksheet usage:** ≥30% download the CSV worksheet.
- **90-day outcome:** ≥3 founders report running the experiment sequence with measurable CAC by day 90.

## Pricing & Monetization

Free, supported by a single affiliate link to a recommended email outreach tool (Instantly, Smartlead) for founders who run cold outreach.

## Competitive Landscape

- **Generic SaaS GTM books (Lenny's, April Dunford)** — solid frameworks, no real-estate specificity.
- **Proptech agency blogs** — usually vendor pitches disguised as advice.
- **IndieHackers threads** — anecdotal, frequently contradictory.

## Risks & Open Questions

- [ ] Validate the channel rankings against 10 actual real-estate investor SaaS launches before publishing.
- [ ] Confirm whether cold email to investors is realistic or whether the audience is unreachable that way.
- [ ] Decide whether to add a paid "GTM audit" tier in v2.
