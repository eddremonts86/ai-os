# Problem with choosing a business niche in the local market

> Auto-generated product brief. Review and refine before MVP scoping.

## Value Proposition

For $50, a business plan for your actual city rather than a generic answer: current local market data, a profitability comparison of the models you are weighing (manufacturing versus white label), and step-by-step instructions built around the experience you already have.

## Target Users

| Stakeholder | Why they care |
|---|---|
| Experienced local operator choosing a niche | 12 years in F&B sales, many opportunities in Kolkata, no way to assess which niche has potential. |
| First-time founder in an Indian city | Global startup content — Y Combinator material, business podcasts — offers nothing for their local market. |
| Someone with a family that has stopped believing them | The author's family does not support him after past network marketing failures; a defensible, sourced plan is also an argument. |

## Jobs To Be Done

1. **Functional job** — Decide which niche and which business model to commit to in a specific city, with numbers behind the choice.
2. **Emotional job** — Stop cycling through ideas without ever being able to assess them.
3. **Social job** — Have a plan credible enough to show a family that has watched previous ventures fail.

## Success Metrics

- **Groundedness:** share of claims in a delivered report backed by a citable local source, versus the generic output the author got from ChatGPT.
- **Decision conversion:** users who choose a niche and act on the step-by-step instructions after receiving the report.
- **Report cost:** data acquisition plus generation cost per report against the $50 price.
- **Subscription conversion:** share of one-off buyers who subscribe, which the author says depends on the service showing real results.

## Pricing & Monetization

$50 one-time for a personalised business plan with step-by-step instructions for the user's region — the author's stated figure. He would consider a subscription if the service shows real results, so recurring revenue is conditional on demonstrated outcomes rather than assumed.

## Competitive Landscape

What the author tried, in his own account:

- **ChatGPT** — used for market analysis, gives generic and often irrelevant answers; does not account for local specifics or work with current market data.
- **Business podcasts and Y Combinator content** — watched, but they lack solutions for the local Indian market.
- **Grok and Perplexity** — suggested by a commenter on the grounds that ChatGPT is limited to pre-trained data plus web search, and that a research-specific tool may do better. Untested by the author.

## Risks & Open Questions

- [ ] Data availability is the whole bet: city-level, current profitability data for Indian local markets may not exist in an accessible form, and the source names no sources.
- [ ] A commenter's suggestion — that Grok or Perplexity might already be enough — has not been tested. If a $20/month research tool answers the question, a $50 report has no room.
- [ ] Unit economics at $50 per report are unproven once real local data acquisition, and any human review, are included.
- [ ] The author is looking for a co-founder rather than a vendor, which changes this from a customer signal into a build-it-together proposal.
