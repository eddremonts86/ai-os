# A complex SaaS needs a tool for adaptive and visual explanation of the product to different audiences

> Auto-generated product brief. Review and refine before MVP scoping.

## Value Proposition

Replaces the scattered decks-and-docs pattern with a single product-description source that automatically derives audience-adapted explainers, updated once and propagated everywhere.

## Target Users

Indian SaaS companies with complex products that sell to multiple buyer personas; developer-tools and infra SaaS specifically; sales and marketing teams.

## Jobs To Be Done

When I update the product, I want all audience-adapted explainers to update from one source so I do not have to chase five decks. When I pitch to a CTO, I want an explainer that uses CTO vocabulary, not generic marketing language.

## Success Metrics

Active SaaS companies on platform. Audience-adapted explainers generated per company per quarter. Sales-team adoption rate. Reduction in time-to-update sales collateral.

## Competitive Landscape

Notion and Confluence are knowledge bases, not audience-adaptation engines. Tome and Gamma are AI slide generators, not explainer tools. No source-named direct competitor combining product-description-as-source with multi-audience derivation.

## Risks & Open Questions

Audience-adaptation quality is bounded by GPT-4o's audience modelling — needs careful prompt design and review. Diagram generation is the long pole — Mermaid / Excalidraw output quality varies. Need 10 SaaS marketing-team interviews before claims about time-savings are credible.
