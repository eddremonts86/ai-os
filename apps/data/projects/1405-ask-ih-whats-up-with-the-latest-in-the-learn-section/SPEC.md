# Ask IH: What's up with "the latest" in the Learn section?

## Problem

The new **Learn** category appears to have three components—podcasts, products and "the latest". Podcasts and interviews are super clear to me. They're audio and text interviews with people who have built profitable businesses.

However, "the latest" isn't as clear and it dominates the learn page. It's got content from the podcasts and the interviews but there are also a lot of blog posts mixed in with them. Some of them are educational, some are announcing various ventures and others are trashy top 7 lists or even spam.

Are these posts in "the latest" the same thing as what's in the **Community** tab? Are they vetted in any way? So far I'm not a huge fan of them, but I'm curious what everyone else thinks. Do any of you go to the **Learn** tab looking for those blog posts?
