# SPEC.md — MyMarkdown – Make AI Markdown easier to read

## Problem

I made a small free tool for humans (not AI) to read Markdown faster.<p>If you build AI tools, you probably read a lot of Markdown. And you may have noticed that as it gets bigger, it gets harder for human eyes to digest.<p>Here, just paste your Markdown, and it makes it cleaner and easier to read. It also makes JSON much nicer, which I needed a lot.<p>It’s free. Maybe useful for other people building AI too.
