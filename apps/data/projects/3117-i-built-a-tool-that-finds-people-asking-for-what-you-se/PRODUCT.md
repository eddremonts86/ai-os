# I built a tool that finds people asking for what you sell

> Auto-generated product brief. Review and refine before MVP scoping.

## Value Proposition

Replace low-response-rate cold LinkedIn outreach with a daily list of posts where the customer has already publicly asked for what you sell — and decide how to reply yourself.

## Target Users

Daily outreach operators and small sellers: cybersecurity consultants, indie SaaS founders, Etsy and e-commerce owners, Fiverr sellers, Skool community operators. The poster's pentesting firm is the included niche B2B example.

## Jobs To Be Done

Functional: turn a single seller URL into a ranked list of public posts on Reddit, X, LinkedIn and Facebook that already ask for what is sold. Emotional: stop feeling that cold outreach is a volume game. Social: enter the buying thread as a helpful participant, not as a spammer.

## Success Metrics

No user-facing metric is stated. The poster reports two throughput numbers from their build: 350+ kept leads per 4,000+ posts read for a consumer product, and ~15 for a niche SaaS — that ratio is the operating baseline they benchmark against.
