# Quiriz – Ask questions of your spreadsheets and get accurate answers fast

## Problem

Quiriz lets you ask questions of your data and get trusted answers and reports. Upload Excel or CSV files, connect Google Drive or OneDrive, or import from Slack, then schedule refreshes to keep results up to date. Use Quick Answers for on-the-fly insights or Generate Report to combine multiple sources. Install the Slack app to collaborate in channels and share results. Control access by project or dataset. Quiriz stores structured data securely in the U.S. and deletes source files after import. View startup
