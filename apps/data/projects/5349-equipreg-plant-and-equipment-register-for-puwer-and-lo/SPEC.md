# SPEC.md — EquipReg – Plant and equipment register for PUWER and LOLER compliance

## Problem

EquipReg helps UK businesses stay on top of PUWER and LOLER compliance. Track your plant and equipment register, schedule thorough examinations, and get ahead of inspection deadlines, all in one place. Built for health and safety and facilities managers, and plant operators currently managing compliance in spreadsheets, EquipReg replaces manual work with a simple, audit-ready system. View startup
