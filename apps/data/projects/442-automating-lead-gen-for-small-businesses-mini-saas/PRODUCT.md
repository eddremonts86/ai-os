# Automating Lead Gen for Small Businesses (Mini-SaaS)

> Auto-enriched product brief.

## Value Proposition

A managed lead-gen service that does the part small businesses hate (research, scraping, validation, sequencing) and hands them replies in their normal inbox.

## Target Users

- Small businesses (agencies, local services, B2B SaaS) without an in-house growth team
- Solo founders doing client services who want to productise a manual workflow
- Agencies running lead-gen for 2-5 clients who currently do it by hand

## Jobs To Be Done

When I am a small business owner who needs more leads, I want a service that does the research, validation, and sequencing for me, so I get replies in my normal inbox instead of another SaaS to log into.

## Success Metrics

- At least 5 small businesses onboarded in pilot
- Median reply rate above 4% on cold outreach (industry benchmark; not enforced)

## Competitive Landscape

Apollo, Instantly, Smartlead exist in the broader world; not named in source. Plan is a managed-service wedge, not a tool.

## Risks & Open Questions

- Source of leads (Instagram in the post) carries TOS risk; mitigation = operator-curated sources only
- Reply quality depends on ICP accuracy; weekly reviews
