# Automating Lead Gen for Small Businesses (Mini-SaaS) — Design direction

Like a Friday-afternoon lead report, not a marketing automation suite.

- Surface: weekly digest email + a simple dashboard.
- Color: ink + one accent for "replied."
- Typography: sans for emails, monospace for IDs.
- No "funnel" charts in MVP.
