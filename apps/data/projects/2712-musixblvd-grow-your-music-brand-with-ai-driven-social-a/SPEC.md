# MusixBlvd – Grow your music brand with AI-driven social and streaming insights

## Problem

MusixBlvd helps creators grow their audience by turning social and streaming data into clear next steps. Starting with SoundCloud as our first active API integration, MusixBlvd is building connections with Instagram, Facebook, YouTube, and TikTok to analyze creator performance, identify trends, highlight top content, and deliver AI-powered recommendations for content strategy, captions, hooks, and scheduling. Use the dashboard to see what to do next, plan content that resonates, and focus on channels that move your numbers. View startup
