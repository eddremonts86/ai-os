# Lunar Log – Capture dreams before they fade and find patterns over time

## Problem

Lunar Log is a private dream journal designed for the few minutes when dreams are still fresh. You can speak or type as soon as you wake, and Lunar Log captures your thoughts as they come. It quietly organizes recurring people, places, symbols, emotions, and themes. Over time, your journal becomes a searchable map of your dream life, revealing patterns you might otherwise miss. Your journal stays private, works offline, and supports full Markdown export whenever you want. Journaling is free forever, with optional AI for deeper, context-aware dream exploration. View startup
