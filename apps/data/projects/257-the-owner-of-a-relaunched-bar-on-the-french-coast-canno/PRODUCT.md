# The owner of a relaunched bar on the French coast cannot attract an evening audience due to the legacy of its past format (nightclub) and its isolated location

> Auto-generated product brief. Review and refine before MVP scoping.

## Value Proposition

A venue acquisition surface for one relaunched bar on the French coast: a single venue page that honestly describes the new format and shows what's on this week, a cheap weekly short-form video loop that builds the new format's reputation over months, and a one-page referral handout for local collaborators (holiday-rental managers, surf schools) who send visitors. The owner trades the old nightclub reputation for a multi-month content investment that gives the audience a reason to make the trip.

## Target Users

- The bar owner and venue team running operations and seeking a repeatable way to bring new visitors.
- The audience the new format is for: people within driving distance who would come if they knew the format existed.
- Local collaborators (other venues, holiday-rental managers, surf schools, hiking guides) who would refer visitors if the referral were easy.
- Returning seasonal visitors to the French coast who would benefit from a curated list of venues the platform has vetted.

## Jobs To Be Done

- When I relaunch my bar with a new format, I want a venue page that honestly describes what the venue is now, so a visitor who Googles us does not bounce on the nightclub-era photos.
- When I want to build a new audience over a season, I want a 60-second weekly video loop I can sustain on a phone, so the new format's reputation accumulates without a film crew.
- When a holiday-rental manager asks "where should my guests go for a drink at sunset," I want a one-page handout they can keep on the counter, so the referral happens without me pitching.
- When I am a first-time visitor planning a trip to the venue, I want to see what's on this week, so I can plan the trip around a specific event, not a vague "come any time."

## Success Metrics

- Number of first-time visitors per week (proxy for the relaunch breaking through to new audiences).
- Repeat-visit rate: percentage of first-time visitors who come back within 60 days.
- Referral visits per month per active collaborator (proxy for the referral handout's usefulness).
- Content cadence: number of weekly videos published over a 12-week season (sanity check on the sustainability of the loop).

## Risks & Open Questions

- Validate problem with 5 owner interviews before MVP: confirm that the relaunch framing matches what the owner actually faces, and that the content loop is sustainable for one season.
- Reputational legacy takes months to overwrite. The MVP must set expectations: a one-season commitment to the weekly video loop, not a launch-week spike.
- Content production must stay cheap. A 60-second phone-shot video per week is the ceiling; anything more elaborate will not be sustained.
- Local-collaborator outreach depends on personal relationships the owner has, not a platform feature. The MVP must accept that the handout is a tool, not a substitute for the conversation.
- Seasonality: the French coast is busy in summer, quiet in winter. The MVP must plan around shoulder seasons and not overstate year-round demand.
