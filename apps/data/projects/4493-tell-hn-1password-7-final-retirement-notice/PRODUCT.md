_Based on source brief:_ I can't find any web accessible news release or the like on 1Password.com, articles, or even blogs discussing this yet, but our teams have stuck with 1P7 and I just received an email indicating that the final EOL for that is coming up. They used to just randomly turn up sometimes in various Mac forums directly to answer questions and talk ideas.
