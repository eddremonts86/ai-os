# Ask IH: New Dad trying to Build MVP

## Problem

Hi all. I work for an enterprise software company where I build ROI models to help prospective customers understand the value of our products. Since we began this type of activity, it has become and integral part of our sales strategy as it gives purchasing managers something to wrap their heads around and a target to shoot for when fully implemented.

I believe there is an opportunity to build a platform that allows all B2B SaaS businesses to provide ROI and value estimates to prospective customers.

I was (slowly) working on my MVP when my wonderful son was born last week. Now, I am concerned that I won't make any progress for the next 6-9 months. I am looking beyond the traditional landing page validation.

I am looking for any advice from IH on how to manage MVP while helping raise my kid or a front-end focused person who would be willing to work together on this project. Thanks!
