# No quick, cheap fix for leaky faucets: no clear DIY guide or affordable plumber

## Problem

A household plumbing leak — the kind that turns into a wasted-tap and a higher water bill — does not have a quick, cheap path to fix in the poster's city. Either the homeowner tries to do it themselves without a clear guide, or they call a plumber whose visit costs more than the leak is worth. The post, filed from India under "Other", frames the pain as both: no good DIY instructions on the one hand, and no affordable professional on the other. Source contains no named brand, no price, and no specific leak type.

## Objective

Give a household in the poster's market one place to decide between a guided DIY fix and a vetted, affordable plumber for a leaky faucet, without having to research both separately.

## Target Users

Households in the poster's India market who experience a leaky faucet and need either a clear self-fix path or a reasonably priced plumber. Secondary user: independent plumbers in the same market who want a steady stream of small jobs without the overhead of a marketplace brand.

## MVP Scope

- A short triage flow that identifies the leak type from a photo and a few answers (washer, cartridge, O-ring, supply line).
- A DIY guide per leak type, written for someone with no plumbing experience.
- A directory of plumbers willing to take small jobs in the poster's city, with the price band for a single faucet fix.
- A way to book or request a plumber visit from the same screen.
