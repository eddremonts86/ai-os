# Barely getting any standard tier buyers for my SaaS

> Product brief for the digital-passport-for-events SaaS scoped in the source post.

## Value Proposition

An event organiser can issue per-attendee digital passports with QR code + identity verification, self-serve for small events or managed for large ones.

## Target Users

| Stakeholder | Why they care |
|---|---|
| Small event organisers (1-5 events/year) | Want a self-serve digital passport. |
| Large event organisers (10+ events/year) | Want a managed service with custom branding and on-site support. |
| Festivals, conferences, sports events | Need ticketing + identity verification. |

## Jobs To Be Done

1. **Functional job** — Create an event with branding.
2. **Functional job** — Issue per-attendee digital passports with QR + identity verification.
3. **Functional job** — Manage on-site check-in.

## Success Metrics

- **Activation:** first event created within 14 days of signup.
- **Standard tier:** ≥ 4% free-to-paid conversion within 90 days.
- **Enterprise tier:** at least 60% of enterprise deals convert from the first call.

## Pricing & Monetization

Standard tier: $49/event (1-100 attendees). Enterprise tier: $2,500+/event (100-10,000 attendees, managed).

## Competitive Landscape

- **Eventbrite / Tito** — ticketing-focused; identity verification is an add-on.
- **Bizzabo / Cvent** — enterprise event management; expensive for small organisers.
- **Custom code** — what large events do today.

## Risks & Open Questions

- [ ] The strategic question (self-serve vs managed) is real; the founder must run the rubric below before pivoting.
- [ ] The two tiers share the same product surface; the enterprise tier is a wrapper, not a separate product.
- [ ] Decision rubric: if the founder can convert ≥ 5 standard-tier buyers in the next 60 days at the current price, stay self-serve. If not, the unit economics suggest a managed pivot.
