# Sitmap – a World in Conflict inspired map maker for Cold War scenarios

## Problem

I loved the game World in Conflict (2007)One of those aspects I enjoyed was the maps shown during the loading screens. I managed to get something quite useable and probably useless to most people shipped.If you enjoy maps, military, strategy etc, hopefully it is of interest to you.
