# SPEC.md — BugSnap-AI – Autonomously explores your app to find, reproduce, and report real bugs

## Problem

BugSnap-AI autonomously tests running applications to find real, reproducible defects. Point it at a staging URL and credentials, and it maps roles and flows, exercises real user workflows, detects behavioral anomalies, and re-runs issues to confirm them before reporting. It produces developer-ready reports with steps, screenshots, and traces, without source access or instrumentation. Validated findings become regression checks and integrate with Jira, Linear, GitHub, Slack, and CI/CD. View startup
