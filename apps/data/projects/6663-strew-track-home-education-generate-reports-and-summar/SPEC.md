# SPEC.md — Strew – Track home education, generate reports, and summarize learning

## Problem

Strew is a home education app that helps parents track learning, document activities, and create clear reports. Capture your child’s progress in a rolling feed, measure time spent across subjects, and use AI to summarize learning outcomes. Generate printable PDF reports for families and educational authorities in minutes. Strew keeps memories private with on-device data storage and supports a wide range of home education approaches. It's available on iOS and Android. View startup
