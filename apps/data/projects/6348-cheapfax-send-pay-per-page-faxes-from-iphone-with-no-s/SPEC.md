# SPEC.md — Cheapfax – Send pay-per-page faxes from iPhone with no subscriptions

## Problem

Cheapfax lets you send outbound faxes from your iPhone without subscriptions or minimums. Buy credits through Apple, pay 50¢ per page, and only pay for pages that deliver successfully. Scan with your camera or import PDFs and photos, preview the exact price, and confirm payment via In-App Purchase. The service supports US and Canada numbers, refunds failed transmissions automatically, and deletes documents after 30 days while keeping transfers encrypted in transit. View startup
