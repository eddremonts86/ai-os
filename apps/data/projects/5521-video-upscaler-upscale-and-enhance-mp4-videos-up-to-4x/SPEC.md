# SPEC.md — Video Upscaler – Upscale and enhance MP4 videos up to 4x for sharper, clearer results

## Problem

Video Upscaler lets you enlarge and enhance MP4 videos up to 4x while improving clarity and detail. Upload a clip up to 500 MB and 30 minutes, choose between a fast in-browser preview for animation or a high-quality cloud model for stronger results, then download a clear MP4 file. The cloud option securely processes your video online and supports 2x or 4x upscaling, while browser previews stay on your device for quick testing before using credits. View startup
