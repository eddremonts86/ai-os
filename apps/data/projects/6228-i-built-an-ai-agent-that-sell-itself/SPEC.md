# SPEC.md — I built an AI Agent that sell itself

## Problem

Quick bit of context: SendRoq is a LinkedIn automation tool.<p>Before the AI era (pre-2021), I ran a marketing agency. Back then, client acquisition on LinkedIn was all manual. Once AI agents started becoming a thing, I built one for my agency&#x27;s internal use, never intended to sell it. It worked really well for us.<p>A few colleagues and partners kept pushing me to turn it into a SaaS. I said no, we were already profitable running the agency, why complicate things.<p>Then a few weeks later, I was on an onboarding call with a new client, and he asked, &quot;How did you guys know I was looking for marketing help?&quot;<p>I told him straight up: we have an AI agent that analyzes LinkedIn profiles and picks up on signals. If someone comments, engages, or posts anything hinting they&#x27;re looking for marketing, it flags it and reaches out.<p>He got interested immediately and wanted to buy the agent for his own sales team, was ready to pay well for it. I told him it wasn&#x27;t public, not for sale. He pushed back: &quot;So make it a SaaS.&quot;<p>That conversation is basically what got the ball rolling. A few months later we actually started building it out.<p>The hardest part, unsurprisingly, was getting our first users. Our GTM was pretty meta: we used our own tool to book meetings with potential customers and sell them directly.
