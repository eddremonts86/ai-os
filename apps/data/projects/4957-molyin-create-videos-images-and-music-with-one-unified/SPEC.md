# SPEC.md — Molyin – Create videos, images, and music with one unified AI studio

## Problem

Molyin is an AI creation platform that combines the best generative models into one studio. You can create cinematic videos up to 4K with native audio from text, images, or reference files, restyle existing footage with a single instruction, generate production-ready images, and write original songs with your own lyrics. It includes Seedance 2.5, Veo 3.1, Kling 3.0, Nano Banana Pro, GPT Image 2, FLUX.2, Suno 5.5, and Google Lyria 3 under one account. Free credits are given on sign-up, and failed generations automatically refund credits. View startup
