# SPEC.md — Fluidify – A wiki of the industrial world being built (post-2026)

## Problem

I built a system with the objective of mapping all the major announced infrastructure and industrial projects around the world. The vast majority of listed projects are expected to become operational after 2026.<p>As you would expect, I used some agents to fill in the map, but for several reasons, some information may not be up to date or as accurate as it could be. The agents provide the initial “breadth,” while the idea is for the community to improve the accuracy over time with what they know: a new source URL, a correction, or a time-stamped picture from the ground.<p>I see several use cases here:<p>- finding a job where industrial activity is getting stronger
- finding new logistics opportunities for a freight forwarder, as new industrial plants generate new recurring freight demand - helping select a future site in an emerging industrial cluster
- detecting future real estate opportunities where the market hasn’t fully priced in<p>I also see Fluidify as a way to explore beyond the places and projects you would normally think to search for. Most discovery starts with a location, company, or market you already know. On Fluidify, you can simply move across the map and discover industrial activity somewhere you were not looking: a new factory, an emerging cluster, or a region attracting several large investments at once.<p>That can lead to opportunities you would never have thought to search for in the first place.<p>As I invested my own money to build this, contractor data is not available for every project. Mapping and verifying contractors globally would simply be too expensive for me to do alone at this stage.<p>You can message me (email in &quot;About&quot; on the website at the bottom), I’d be glad to show you the data behind the &quot;hidden&quot; tabs.
