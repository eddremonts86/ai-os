# SPEC.md — Freemo – Discover and share free events and pop-ups in your city

## Problem

Freemo is a community platform to discover and share free local events, pop-ups, and brand activations. It lets you avoid digging through random social feeds or tracking dozens of influencers to find affordable plans. It centralizes Toronto markets, festivals, and giveaways into one clear space. It is 100% free to use. Discover hidden city gems, support your local scene, and never miss out. Join the Toronto beta, share your feedback, and help build the ultimate community for local discovery.
