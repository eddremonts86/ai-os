# Unitpost – Send transactional and marketing emails from editor, code, or AI

## Problem

Unitpost unifies transactional and marketing emails on one platform with a single API, a shared contact list, and consistent deliverability. Build emails in a visual editor or with React components, use a template gallery, and send from code, the dashboard, or AI agents. Track deliveries, opens, clicks, bounces, and complaints with per-message events, webhooks, and analytics. Manage consent, segments, and compliance automatically, authenticate domains, and protect reputation. Brand Kit and on-brand AI help you draft, template, and ship faster. View startup
