# SPEC.md — Sharden: A mineable coin for paying miners

## Problem

I built Sharden as an autonomous mineable PoW cryptocurrency on Ethereum blockchain which also has an interesting potential use case for miners themselves as a payment method.<p>Miners can mine SHARDEN themselves, trade it on crypto markets where it can acquire a market price, and then use SHARDEN as an additional payment method when mining other cryptocurrencies.<p>For example, a miner could receive the cryptocurrency they are mining plus SHARDEN as additional compensation for their mining work.<p>This gives miners a coin they can independently mine and then use as a payment method for their mining work elsewhere.<p>SHARDEN has no premine, treasury, or centralized reward issuer.
