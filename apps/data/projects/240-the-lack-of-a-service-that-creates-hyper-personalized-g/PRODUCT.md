# The lack of a service that creates hyper-personalized, gamified English courses (in the Duolingo format) for narrow professional niches (e.g., for a barista in a vegan coffee shop or a startup founder pitching to VCs)

> Auto-generated product brief. Review and refine before MVP scoping.

## Value Proposition

Replaces a one-size-fits-all curriculum with a course generated for the learner's actual job, in the format that already works for habit formation (Duolingo-style gamification).

## Target Users

Adult English learners in the USA with a professional niche; ESL program administrators; HR teams at US employers that sponsor English upskilling.

## Jobs To Be Done

When I start a new job that requires English, I want a 30-day course built for my specific role so that I can talk to customers / colleagues / investors without freezing on vocabulary. When I come back the next day, I want the app to remember what I struggled with and re-surface it.

## Success Metrics

30-day course completion rate. Spaced-repetition recall accuracy improvement after 30 days. Subscription retention at 90 days. NPS among users who complete the role-play scenario.
