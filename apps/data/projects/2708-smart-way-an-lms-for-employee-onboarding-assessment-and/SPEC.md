# Smart Way – An LMS for employee onboarding, assessment, and continuous learning

## Problem

LMS Smart Way helps organizations onboard employees, assess knowledge and skills, and deliver structured corporate learning on one platform. HR and L&D teams can create interactive courses, assign learning paths, run tests and practical tasks, and track progress through detailed reports and analytics. Automation supports consistent onboarding and development, while AI-powered tools and integrations reduce routine administration. The platform is designed for organizations that want a clear, scalable approach to employee learning and development. View startup
