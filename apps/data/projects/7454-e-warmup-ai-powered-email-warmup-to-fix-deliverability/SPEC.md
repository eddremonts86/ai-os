# SPEC.md — E-Warmup – AI-powered email warmup to fix deliverability and reputation

## Problem

E-Warmup improves email deliverability by building and repairing sender reputation across major providers. It automates inbox warming with human-like interactions from a large network of real mailboxes, monitors SPF, DKIM, and DMARC, and scans over 100 blacklists with real-time alerts. Teams use E-Warmup to boost inbox placement, stabilize reputation, and track performance with analytics, language targeting, and provider selection for Gmail, Outlook, SMTP, and leading ESPs.
