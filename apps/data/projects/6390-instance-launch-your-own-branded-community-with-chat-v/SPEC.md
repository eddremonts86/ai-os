# SPEC.md — Instance – Launch your own branded community with chat, voice, and storefront

## Problem

Instance gives businesses and creators a branded community app on their own domain. Visitors can explore before creating an account, purchase access through Stripe in one tap, and receive the correct role immediately. Operators manage member accounts, payments, data, and communication through chat, voice, storefronts, push notifications, email, moderation, and automation. An assistant and Bot API help manage the community and commercial operations, making Instance the owned infrastructure behind a member-based business. View startup
