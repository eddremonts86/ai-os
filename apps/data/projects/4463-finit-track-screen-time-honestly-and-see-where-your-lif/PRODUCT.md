## Value Proposition

_Based on source brief:_ finit is a screen time tracker for iPhone and Apple Watch that keeps a complete, private history of your usage. It records every app each day and turns the data into weeks, months, years, and a lifetime projection. Four hours a day equals ten years of your life. finit never blocks or nags, it simply shows you an honest number you can trust. See your whole life as a grid of weeks, compare with friends, add sleep and exercise data from Apple Health, and place widgets on your Home and Lock Screens. Everything stays on your device and syncs through your own iCloud account. View startup
