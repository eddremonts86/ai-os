# My Intranet HRIS – Manage leaves, time, and expenses with a simple modular HRIS

## Problem

My Intranet is an HRIS that helps companies manage leave requests, time tracking, expense reports, and personnel files in one modular platform. Teams submit requests easily, follow clear approval workflows, and export data to payroll. Managers get clear planning, work time tracking, and comprehensive reports. Delivered as SaaS, it requires no training, reduces paperwork, and improves HR efficiency while keeping data secure and accessible anywhere. View startup
