# SPEC.md — Titlo – Design broadcast-ready lower thirds online without heavy software

## Problem

Titlo lets you create professional lower thirds in your browser for live and post-production workflows. It provides a timeline-based editor with layers, keyframes, and real-time preview, and exports PNG and MOV ProRes 4444 files up to 4K at broadcast frame rates. Use it with ATEM, OBS, vMix, and DaVinci Resolve. Choose from Free, Pro, or Team plans to match your needs, with features like custom fonts and shapes, render storage, rolling end credits, and shared workspaces for studios. View startup
