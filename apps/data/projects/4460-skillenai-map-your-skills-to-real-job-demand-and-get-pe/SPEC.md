# Skillenai – Map your skills to real job demand and get personalized hiring paths

## Problem

Skillenai provides job market intelligence based on real posting data. It lets you upload a resume, analyze skill gaps for target roles, and see skills employers actually request. An AI agent runs a multi-signal job search matching skills, level, salary, and location. You can close gaps through a learning marketplace with mentors and custom paths, and track market trends over time. Email alerts keep you updated with fresh, relevant opportunities. View startup
