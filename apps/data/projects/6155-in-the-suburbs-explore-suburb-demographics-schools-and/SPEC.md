# SPEC.md — In The Suburbs – Explore suburb demographics, schools, and amenities across Australia

## Problem

In The Suburbs helps you research Australian suburbs with clear, structured data on demographics, schools, amenities, councils, and electorates. You can browse over 15,600 suburbs, filter more than 8,700 schools by state, type, and gender, and explore 2021 Census statistics for 541 Local Government Areas. The site also maps 540+ electorates and offers a mortgage calculator to estimate repayments. Use it to compare areas and make informed decisions about where to live or buy property. View startup
