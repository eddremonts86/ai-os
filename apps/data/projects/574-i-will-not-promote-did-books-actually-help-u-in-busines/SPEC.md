# Books vs doing — what actually changed how founders run things

## Problem

does reading books really helped u with doing business? im asking because i keep seeing people say books changed everything for them, and then others say its all theory and u only learn by doing. so which one is it in real life? what im curious about specifically, \\- was there one book that actually changed how u run things, or just small bits here and there \\- did u apply it right away or did it just sit in your head \\- do u re reading business books or more general stuff like psychology, biographies, whatever and if books didnt do much for u, what did? mentors, just failing a lot? honest answers please, even if the answer is no they didnt help at all, and also how often do u read books? thanks!! I Will not promote submitted by /u/Recent-Ad7093 [link] [comments]

---

## Objective

Surface honest, non-promotional answers to whether business books changed how founders run their business, what they applied right away, and what was theoretical vs operational.

## Target Users

Aspiring and current founders asking whether reading is a productive use of founder time. The author is the questioner; readers on r/startups are the audience.

## MVP Scope

Not a product MVP. The deliverable is a synthesis of community answers into a one-page 'was the book worth it' brief — with attribution and the specific book when named.

## Design Direction

See `DESIGN.md` for this project's design tokens.

## Constraints

Source is a question post, not a problem statement. Any plan written from it is constrained by what respondents actually answer; the author explicitly invites honest 'no' answers.
