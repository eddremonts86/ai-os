# A local tool that logs every time you swear at your AI coding assistant

## Value Proposition

Run the tool in the background and accumulate a frustration log over time.

## Target Users

Developers who want a private, local-only record of how often their AI coding assistant is making them swear.

## Jobs To Be Done

Developers who want a private, local-only record of how often their AI coding assistant is making them swear.

## Success Metrics

Frustration entries logged per week and streaks (days with at least one entry).
Self-reported usefulness from users who keep the tool running long-term.

## Competitive Landscape

_Source does not name any competing product._ The post links the repo only; no other AI-coding swear-counter is named.

## Risks & Open Questions

Audio-based detection raises obvious privacy concerns in shared spaces; keystroke-based keeps it local and obvious.
Detection rules for swearing vary by language and culture; users will want to edit them.
