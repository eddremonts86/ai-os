# Trolevo – Scale any recipe, track EU-14 allergens, and see cost per plate

## Value Proposition

A recipe tool that scales, costs, and labels from one source of truth — so the chef changes one ingredient and the quantities, the food cost, and the EU-14 allergen list update everywhere the sub-recipe is used.

**One-liner:** One recipe graph produces the scaled quantities, the per-plate cost, and the EU-14 allergen label.

## Target Users

| Stakeholder | Why they care |
|---|---|
| Restaurant chefs and kitchen managers | They maintain a real recipe binder; portion changes happen daily and have to land across sub-recipes, cost lines, and the label they put in front of a health inspector. |
| Catering and commissary kitchens | They cook the same dish at 4 and at 400 covers on different days; the math has to hold against a real invoice. |
| Recipe developers and food stylists | They hand a spec to a client and want the cost per serving and the allergen list already attached. |

## Jobs To Be Done

1. **Functional job** — Scale a recipe and its sub-recipes to a new cover count, and produce a cost-per-plate and an EU-14 allergen label in one place, without retyping or hand-calculating.
2. **Emotional job** — Stop the 6pm feeling of "did I update the cost and the allergens after that swap?" and trust the binder the kitchen runs on.
3. **Social job** — Walk a health inspector through a printed label that came out of the same tool the kitchen uses, and have the math hold up.

## Success Metrics

- **Activation:** % of waitlist signups who create their first recipe with at least one sub-recipe within 14 days of invite.
- **Retention:** Weekly active kitchens / monthly active kitchens — a kitchen that runs on the binder checks it daily, so the floor is high; a kitchen that drifts off has churned.
- **Revenue:** MRR in CHF; average kitchen size (number of active recipes per paying kitchen) as a leading indicator of expansion revenue.

## Pricing & Monetization

Landing page points at `/pricing/` with "a free plan at launch" and paid tiers for "kitchens with lots of recipes and staff". The post does not state CHF figures or per-seat / per-kitchen pricing. Founder has stated the tool is built for "one kitchen to a whole team" — that implies a per-kitchen tier with a staff-seat add-on is the obvious shape, but the post does not commit to numbers.

## Competitive Landscape

The founder's framing is direct: "I was never happy with any recipe tool I'd used, and a chef and restaurateur in my family kept hitting the same wall: change one ingredient in a sub-recipe, and the quantities and the allergen label should update everywhere it's used. Few tools handle that." That is the differentiation — explicit sub-recipe references and an allergen rollup, not flat ingredient lists. The BetaList / landing copy does not name competitors.

## Risks & Open Questions

- The Swiss / EU labeling context is real but narrow. The MVP targets a Swiss-German / French / Italian kitchen audience. The EU-14 list is EU FIC, not UK / US — a UK or US launch needs a different allergen model and is out of scope for the MVP.
- One founder, self-funded, no investors. The risk is execution velocity: every feature competes with founder hours.
- The free public scaler has no persistence and no auth. Converting that anonymous traffic into waitlist signups is the GTM question the post does not answer.
