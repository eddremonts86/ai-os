# Stashkit – Design branded QR codes with editable destinations and tracking

## Problem

Stashkit lets you design, download, and manage branded QR codes with editable destinations and scan analytics. You can create codes for links, PDFs, Wi-Fi, contacts, and more, then customize colors, patterns, frames, labels, and logos to match your brand. Website and PDF codes route through a Stashkit link so you can change where they lead without reprinting. Your dashboard shows scan counts and activity. Start with a free plan and scale as your campaigns grow. View startup
