# SPEC.md — Reviving my $15,000 browser arcade with Astra

## Problem

Back in 2021, I put roughly $15,000 into a project called Anbu. The original idea was a decentralized cloud gaming platform, but I scaled it down to what I could afford with no cofounders... a browser arcade where people could play games with friends.<p>I designed the platform myself in Figma, hand-drew the original art and logos, and commissioned development, pixel art, music, sound effects and some 3D models. That budget got us one game &quot;Egg on the Run&quot;, along with game lobbies, friend invitations, chats, and a publishing platform.<p>The plan was to attract players with the first game, have them bring friends, and eventually bring in other developers to publish their games.<p>BUTTT it never got that far lol and the first game wasn’t compelling enough to attract much interest, and the cherry on top was the EC2 hosting cost around a fortune. AWS credits covered it initially, but once they ran out I couldn’t justify the expense so I shut it down never thought to see i&#x27;d see again.<p>But then while working on my other startup Sky, I picked it up again as a weekend side project. I still had the original assets, and I wanted to see how much I could do with Astra.<p>Now I am OBSESSED... EggVerse is now back online with an expanded arcade of 18 multiplayer games and more on the way!<p>What’s been satisfying is returning to ideas I previously had to cut because I couldn’t afford to develop them. The original artwork and commissioned assets gave me a substantial starting point.<p>Whether people enjoy it enough to return is still an open question.<p>You can play at eggverse.co<p>P.S. I’d suggest starting with Neon Nest (think mariokart) or grab a couple buddies&#x2F;co-founders&#x2F;employees and play Bag Egg (think imposter).<p>I’d be interested to hear what you tried and whether you wanted to play another round. Looking forward to some feedback
