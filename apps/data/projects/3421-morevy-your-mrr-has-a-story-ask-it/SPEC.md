# Morevy – Your MRR has a story, ask it

## Problem

Morevy helps finance and revenue operators explain monthly MRR and ARR changes. It turns billing, bookings, or CRM data into customer-level MRR and ARR movement analysis, editable commentary, and exportable finance outputs. AI drafts commentary and exports the analysis as a brief or a formula-driven financial model. Private beta participants start with one closed month run alongside current workflows for comparison. No credit card is required for signup. View startup
