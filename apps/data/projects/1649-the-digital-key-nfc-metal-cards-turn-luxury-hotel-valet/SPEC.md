# THE DIGITAL KEY – NFC metal cards turn luxury hotel valet parking into a revenue stream

## Problem

The Digital Key replaces lost paper tickets and SMS valet systems with reusable, matte-black NFC metal cards for luxury hotels. Guests tap the card with any smartphone to request their car in 3 to 5 minutes, with no app downloads, user registrations, or personal details required. While parked, the card acts as a digital concierge, letting guests book spa treatments, reserve dining, and purchase upgrades. Hotels and valet operators share commissions on amenity bookings, turning valet parking into a revenue channel. View startup
