## Value Proposition

_Based on source brief:_ The Digital Key replaces lost paper tickets and SMS valet systems with reusable, matte-black NFC metal cards for luxury hotels. Guests tap the card with any smartphone to request their car in 3 to 5 minutes, with no app downloads, user registrations, or personal details required. While parked, the card acts as a digital concierge, letting guests book spa treatments, reserve dining, and purchase upgrades.
