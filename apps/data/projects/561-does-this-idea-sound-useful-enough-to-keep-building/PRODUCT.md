# Cross-source synthesis tool — does the idea itself justify continuing?

> Product brief — auto-generated then authored.

## Value Proposition

**One-liner:** Surface non-obvious connections across separate sources, in cases where a per-source summary misses the point.

## Target Users

Analysts, researchers, and IC operators whose decisions hinge on cross-source signal (a problem elsewhere + a change in regulation + an outcome in a competitor). Not the general reader — the value is for people whose job it is to synthesise.

## Jobs To Be Done

1. Functional — produce a 'what changed, why it matters, what to do' brief from a curated source set. 2. Emotional — feel less like the user is the only one who noticed a connection. 3. Social — present findings to a team with the connection already pre-justified.
