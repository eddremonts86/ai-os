# Bury.lol – a $2 pixel-art graveyard for things that died

## Problem

The Show HN post links to https://bury.lol/ but the scraper captured only the link. From the title alone, Bury.lol is a $2 service that creates a small pixel-art memorial page for things that have died — projects, pets, ideas, relationships.

## Objective

Give people a cheap, low-friction way to mark the death of something they cared about, in a more lasting and personal form than a tweet.

## Target Users

Internet users who want a small, semi-permanent tribute to a failed project, a deceased pet, an ended relationship, or any other "thing that died".

## MVP Scope

A web form that takes a name, an optional message and an optional image, charges $2, and produces a permanent-looking pixel-art memorial page.

The source provides no detail on payment provider, hosting permanence (the title implies permanence), or moderation; everything beyond the title is TODO.
