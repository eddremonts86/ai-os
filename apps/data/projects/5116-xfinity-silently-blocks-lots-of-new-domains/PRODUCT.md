## Value Proposition

_Based on source brief:_ We built a site for a launch and it was unreachable for anyone on an Xfinity router. We only found out because we have an Xfinity router at home. There&#x27;s no notice to the site owner.<p>Xfinity&#x27;s Advanced Security draws on third-party domain reputation lists and blocks at the gateway. We found out a blacklist called SURBL had flagged us. Not sure exactly what tripped it. Since then I&#x27;ve hit the same block on 10+ unrelated sites and told the site owner. Now sharing with everyone here.<p>How to check whether it&#x27;s happening to you:<p>https:&#x2F;&#x2F;spa.xfinity.com&#x2F;check_url_status
If blocked, look for the upstream list: https:&#x2F;&#x2F;mxtoolbox.com&#x2F;blacklists.aspx
Report at https:&#x2F;&#x2F;spa.xfinity.com&#x2F;report and call them nonstop (reviews take ~3 business days) and get off the upstream list too.<p>Trying to help get this out there so it happens to less people who are launching products.
