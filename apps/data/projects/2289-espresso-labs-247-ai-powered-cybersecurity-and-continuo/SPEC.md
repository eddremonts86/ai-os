# Espresso Labs – 24/7 AI-powered cybersecurity and continuous compliance

## Problem

Espresso Labs is your next cybersecurity and compliance hire. It offers an AI-powered platform that runs your IT, cybersecurity, and compliance, supported by live experts. It monitors devices and cloud apps 24/7, enforces controls, responds to incidents, and collects audit evidence in real time. The platform supports SOC 2, CMMC, HIPAA, NY DFS, GLBA, and HITRUST, consolidating tools into a single managed service that lowers costs while keeping your organization continuously compliant.
