# Looking for teammates for pitch comps (I will not promote)

> Auto-enriched product brief.

## Value Proposition

Replaces "post on Reddit looking for a teammate and hope" with a structured, deadline-aware co-founder search.

## Target Users

- Student founders entering pitch competitions without a co-founder
- First-time founders relying on competition deadlines to ship a team

## Jobs To Be Done

When I have a pitch-competition deadline and no co-founder, I want to find someone with the missing skill in the next 7 days, so I can submit a credible team.

## Success Metrics

- At least 100 verified profiles in the first cohort
- At least 20% form a team within 14 days of joining

- Co-founder matching is typically free
