# How many startups is too many startups to be involved with at a time? I will not promote

## Problem

The poster is an entrepreneur and founder. Ideas keep flowing and with the help of AI it's never been easier to formulate an idea to get to where you need to start doing the leg work for customer discovery and validation. They have one main B2B SaaS vertical startup, at MVP and validating shortly. It is getting some of their time and a lot of their free capital. It is their biggest passion. But they have had some ideas spin up before and after founding of that company they would like to pursue. For background, they already run a lifestyle company in the industry of their primary startup. Some of the others are... The post is a question, not a complaint. No country, no specific number of startups was stated.

## Objective

Give a founder who runs a primary B2B SaaS startup and is being pulled by secondary ideas a named-portfolio framework — the named cap, the named signal that the primary is under-served, the named signal that the secondary is ready, the named exit path for the secondary. The job is not to tell the founder to drop the secondaries — it is to give them a named-portfolio framework so the decision is a deliberate one.

## Target Users

Primary: a founder who runs a primary B2B SaaS startup and is being pulled by secondary ideas and wants the named-portfolio framework. Secondary: a chief of staff or operations lead at the same kind of company who is being asked to model the portfolio and wants the named cap in front of them.

## MVP Scope

In scope for v1:

- A 'named-portfolio' framework: the named cap on concurrent startups (one primary, one secondary, etc.), the named signal that the primary is under-served (revenue, retention, named bottleneck), the named signal that the secondary is ready (validation, named channel, named team).
- A 'before you start the next one' checklist: the named preconditions for the primary (named retention, named revenue, named runway), the named preconditions for the secondary (named validation, named cash, named calendar).
- A 'first-12-months' sketch: the named monthly time on each startup, the named exit path for the secondary (named sunset, named spinout, named acquisition).
- A one-page export the founder can drop into a Notion doc or a personal journal.
