> Auto-generated product brief. Reviewed and enriched from source.

## Value Proposition

Stop losing context after every call — log the call in one click, attach the email automatically, see what's missing.

## Target Users

Early-stage founders running their own sales motion on top of Google Calendar + Gmail. Possibly extendable to any founder / sales rep in the same posture.

## Jobs To Be Done

- When a call ends, log a one-line summary without opening the CRM.
- When a contact appears in an email thread, attach the thread to the contact without searching.
- When I forget to log something, see a clear "you missed this" surface.
- When I need to prep for tomorrow's calls, see the relevant history in one place.

## Success Metrics

- Time from call end to CRM-updated (target: under 30 seconds).
- % of calls that have a logged note (target: near 100%).
- % of email threads auto-attached to the right contact.

## Pricing & Monetization

Not stated. A productivity tool of this size could be freemium or $10–30/mo.

## Competitive Landscape

Adjacent: Superhuman (inbox), Folk (CRM-lite), Clay (enrichment + outbound). The poster's pain is closest to Folk's wedge, but Folk is a CRM, while the poster wants a CRM-adjacent layer.

## Risks & Open Questions

- [ ] Google API scope + ToS limit what the tool can read/write.
- [ ] Auto-attaching emails correctly is hard; misclassification erodes trust.
- [ ] The poster does not say they will pay for this — WTP is unstated.
