# A photographer moving to the US needs clients. Platforms require reviews, agencies are expensive. Wants a website + ads that bring bookings. Willing to pay $100–300/month.

> Auto-generated product brief. Review and refine before MVP scoping.

## Value Proposition

A recently-relocated photographer can launch a credible US web presence and run a tight-radius ad campaign against it in under a day, with the first paid booking expected within 30 days and a built-in review funnel that unlocks booking-platform marketplaces once enough US reviews accumulate.

## Target Users

| Stakeholder | Why they care |
|---|---|
| Recently-relocated photographer | No US reviews; cannot get on Thumbtack, The Knot, Bark without them; agency retainers are unaffordable. |
| Newly-launched US photographer (1st year) | Same review deficit; would also benefit from a low-touch ads manager. |
| US wedding/event clients | Want to book someone local with verifiable work, but the photographer they find needs to actually exist on a US zip code. |

## Jobs To Be Done

1. **Functional job** — Get booked by a US-based client without spending weeks on marketing setup.
2. **Emotional job** — Feel like a legitimate US-based professional instead of a tourist with a camera.
3. **Social job** — Be presentable on platforms that require a baseline of US reviews.

## Success Metrics

- **Activation:** photographer completes portfolio + first campaign within 24h of signup.
- **Acquisition:** cost per acquired booking < $40 within the first 60 days.
- **Reviews:** average 3 new Google reviews per booked client within 14 days of the shoot.
- **Retention:** photographer logs in to check bookings/deposits at least 4× per month.

## Pricing & Monetization

$49/month base (covers site, review funnel, one ad campaign) + 4% platform fee on Stripe deposits. Annual plan at $39/month locked.

## Competitive Landscape

- **Squarespace + IG ads** — generic, no booking flow, no review funnel.
- **Thumbtack / The Knot** — marketplaces, but lock out photographers without US review history.
- **Local marketing agencies** — full-funnel, but $1k–$3k/month retainers, far above the stated budget.

## Risks & Open Questions

- [ ] Verify the 30-day booking claim with the first 5 photographers in a US pilot.
- [ ] Confirm Google Business Profile API review-write eligibility post-launch.
- [ ] Decide whether to bundle Meta + Google ads or pick one to keep cost predictable.
