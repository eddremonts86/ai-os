# Procrastination is Killing Your Business

## Problem
 Hello fellow IndieHackers! I've been around in a community for a while, mostly in read-only learning from questions and answers. There is but one topic I want to discuss with you and keep the enemy loud. I'm talking about the procrastination that may keep you slightly off goal daily - and result in a huge dead end in a long-term perspective. I'm sure some of you share the pain of starting the day with the clear intention to do X and Y, but finish up doing Z and maybe a bit of Y. Ask yourself: "How many times I was distracted by an unimportant mess, operational activities that eat at least 1/4 of my workday?" "Do I know where exactly goes my time intended for work?"
