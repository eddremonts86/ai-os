## Jobs To Be Done

1. **Functional job** — Run a four-question audit on a signup funnel, identify the step where attention bleeds, and write a one-page brief naming the first onboarding step to fix.
2. **Emotional job** — Replace the anxiety of "is it the marketing or the product?" with the relief of a specific step to change on Monday morning.
3. **Social job** — Be the founder who diagnoses funnel drops from first principles rather than the one who blames marketing for everything.
