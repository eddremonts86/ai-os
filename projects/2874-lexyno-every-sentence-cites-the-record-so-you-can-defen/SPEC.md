# Lexyno – Every sentence cites the record so you can defend the whole letter

## Problem

a href='https://betalist.com/startups/lexyno?utm_campaign=startup-179736 utm_medium=atom utm_source=newsfeed' img src='https://resize.imagekit.co/s1IspHRxL2CuUVH3x3qcb69zoEbF-zwJvZD9hW6B1fE/rs:fill:1000:750/plain/s3://betalist-production/n1wfb1q6bnvdvwzefe2f5fdd6q0l' width='500' /a br / p Personal injury attorneys spend days building a demand letter from 500 to 2,000 pages of medical records. Lexyno turns that case file into a finished 18-section letter where every sentence cites the page it came from. Fact-Lock tags each extracted fact, assembles the letter only from tagged facts, and flags anything it cannot trace so nothing reaches an adjuster the attorney cannot defend. You also get a medical chronology, a case strategy, and Attorney POV plus Insurer POV reports. Built for about 63,000 US firms with 1-5 attorneys, with public pricing and a one-time 350-credit Evaluation Access. /p p a href='https://betalist.com/startups/lexyno?utm_campaign=startup-179736 utm_medium=atom utm_source=newsfeed' View startup /a /p
