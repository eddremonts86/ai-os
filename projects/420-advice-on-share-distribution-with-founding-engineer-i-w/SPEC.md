# Advice on share distribution with founding engineer. I will not promote.

## Problem

The poster is working through a founder / founding-engineer deal for a pre-revenue startup and wants blunt feedback before they sign anything. The business: lifestyle-tech platform (web + mobile) selling curated guides based on the user's habits, history, and other signals. The poster is the founder and has found the founding engineer they'd like to work with. They currently do not have any website / mobile apps. However, the poster has done the following: Figma specs, V1 features, purchased the domain, 30 guides ready to be launched, put an initial investment of $5k and will assume the cost of everything until they have something. The poster is asking for advice on share distribution. The post is a question, not a complaint. No country, no specific equity range was stated.

## Objective

Give a first-time founder who is about to sign a founder / founding-engineer deal a named-share-distribution framework with the named thresholds for the founding engineer's equity, the named vesting, the named cliff, the named buyback, and the named scenarios where the founder-engineer leaves early. The job is not to draw up the cap table — it is to give the founder a named framework so the conversation with the engineer lands with the named context, not a generic 'we'll figure it out'.

## Target Users

Primary: a first-time founder who is about to sign a founder / founding-engineer deal and wants the named share-distribution framework in front of them. Secondary: a first-time founding engineer who is the other side of the deal and wants the named thresholds in front of them.

## MVP Scope

In scope for v1:

- A 'founding-engineer share' framework: the named equity range (5-15% based on the named $5k investment by the founder, the named 30 guides, the named Figma specs), the named vesting (4 years, 1-year cliff), the named buyback, the named scenarios.
- A 'before you sign' checklist: the named preconditions (vesting acceleration, IP assignment, non-compete, named-exit path).
- A 'first-engineer conversation' template: the named context that makes the founder's $5k investment and 30 guides read as concrete, not hand-wavy.
- A one-page export the founder can drop into a Notion doc or a contract note.
