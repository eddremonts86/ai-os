## Problem

Hello,

I love to travel but it is too boring and a feeling of "still missing something" as I've to visit hundreds of websites/apps to get valid information (and not repetitive) on how my itinerary could look like.
Thus, I thought of creating a simple webpage, where people can enter the places where they want to visit along with some details and within 24 hours, I'll send an email where you can have all the details for those places and for free :)
