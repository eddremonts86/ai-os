## Objective

A mobile / desktop app for small and medium businesses that records transactions (including cash), surfaces analytics, and uses an "AI mode" to let the owner chat with their business data. The poster also lists supplier management, product management, and pending-payment tracking as common SMB needs that the app covers.

## Target Users

Owners of small and medium businesses who currently keep transactions in notebooks or scattered spreadsheets and want analytics + an AI assistant on top. The poster's text is conversational and the feature list reads as a survey of common SMB asks, not as one founder's specific problem.

## MVP Scope

- Transaction entry (digital + cash) with date, amount, category, optional receipt.
- Supplier + product management with stock / pending payment fields.
- Analytics: revenue, expenses, top products, top suppliers.
- AI mode: chat with the transactions ("how much did I sell last week?" / "which supplier is overdue?").
- Pending-payment tracking.

## Constraints

- The poster's writing is non-native English with light garbling; specific feature intent is approximate. Authoring should mirror what was said, not over-interpret.
- Cash transactions imply an offline-tolerant input flow.
- SMB owners are price-sensitive; pricing has to clear the "I'll just use a notebook" bar.
