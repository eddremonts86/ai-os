## Value Proposition

A home inventory app that fills in an item's category, brand, and model from a photo, removing the manual data-entry burden of traditional inventory apps.

## Target Users

- Homeowners documenting belongings for insurance
- Renters moving frequently who want a portable inventory
- Estate planners cataloguing items for heirs

## Jobs To Be Done

When I am cataloguing my belongings for insurance, I want a tool that fills in the details from a photo, so I do not spend an hour per item typing brand and model.
