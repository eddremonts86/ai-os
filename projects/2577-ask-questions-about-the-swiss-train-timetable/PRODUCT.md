_Based on source brief:_ I use the SBB website and app every day to find my Swiss train journeys, but even with the touch tiles I find it cumbersome to use, especially for extra filters like avoiding bus or finding trains with space for bicycles.So I built https://fragplan.ch/ to describe a train journey in natural language, and translate it into an SBB timetable request.
