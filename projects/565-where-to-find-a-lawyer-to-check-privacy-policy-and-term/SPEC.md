# GDPR/EU-compliant Privacy Policy + ToS review for an adults-and-children product

## Problem

I'm building a product that can be used by adults and children, so it should comply with gdpr and some other EU/Spain policies. I had AI generated Privacy Policy and Terms of Service for me. What is the best way to find a qualified person to read through it and confirm that it all looks good? submitted by /u/Dear-Record9954 [link] [comments]

---

## Objective

Find a qualified EU/Spain lawyer to review AI-generated Privacy Policy and Terms of Service for a product used by both adults and children, confirming GDPR and adjacent EU/Spain compliance.

## Target Users

Founders shipping consumer products in the EU, especially products whose user base includes minors — a category with extra GDPR-K obligations. The author is one such founder; the question is asked publicly so other founders will read it.

## MVP Scope

Not a product MVP. The deliverable is a hiring decision: a specific lawyer or firm named, with a brief engagement letter for the review. The scope of the review is AI-generated ToS + Privacy Policy for an EU product with minors.

## Design Direction

See `DESIGN.md` for this project's design tokens.

## Constraints

The product has minors in scope, so the review must cover GDPR-K (children's data) in addition to standard GDPR. Spain-jurisdiction friendly is implied by 'EU/Spain policies'. AI-generated policy text is the starting point, so the reviewer is reviewing machine output, not authoring from scratch.
