# GDPR/EU-compliant Privacy Policy + ToS review for an adults-and-children product

> Product brief — auto-generated then authored.

## Value Proposition

**One-liner:** Help founders find a qualified lawyer to review AI-generated EU/Spain privacy and ToS — a small, concrete step that unblocks shipping.

## Target Users

Solo or small-team EU founders about to ship a consumer product, especially one with minors in the user base. Secondary: privacy lawyers who want to be findable for this kind of work.

## Jobs To Be Done

1. Functional — hire a lawyer who can review and certify the documents. 2. Emotional — feel confident the documents are defensible at launch. 3. Social — signal to users (and their parents) that the product meets EU standards.

## Success Metrics

Activation: a named lawyer within 2 weeks. Retention: not applicable — one-shot engagement. Revenue: not applicable to the founder; the lawyer's fee is the cost.
