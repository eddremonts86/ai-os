# Buying a house for the first time? Check out these important areas before making the decision

## Problem
 The dream house does not come easy. Also, if you are buying a home for the first time, there can be a number of essentials you might miss on. The usual practice might include checking the property, looking for a good neighbourhood and the finances you have. Apart from the checklist of a perfect home in your mind, there can be chances that you deviate from what you are looking for if you find a sweet deal. It is important that you run a comprehensive check of the following parameters before making a decision.

 Check the subsidy you can get under PMAY scheme - The Government of India has introduced Pradhan Mantri Awas Yojna. Under this scheme, they aim to provide affordable housing to the urban poor with a target of building 20 million affordable houses by 31 March 2022.
