# A private breeder needs a platform to find trusted sellers who work for a commission. One helper at 10% commission already boosted sales. Need more people like that.

> Auto-generated product brief. Review and refine before MVP scoping.

## Value Proposition

A small breeder can hire vetted, commission-only sellers without paying for listings, interviews, or flat platform fees — and pays only when a sale closes.

## Target Users

| Stakeholder | Why they care |
|---|---|
| Small private breeders | Have one seller who works; need to scale the pattern without taking on payroll. |
| Commission sellers (ex-breeders, vet techs) | Want flexible, performance-based income with credible listings. |
| Pet-shop resellers | Want a vetted supply source with predictable commission terms. |

## Jobs To Be Done

1. **Functional job** — Find and onboard commission-only sellers without leaving the breeder's region.
2. **Emotional job** — Stop worrying about fly-by-night sellers damaging the breeder's reputation.
3. **Social job** — Be seen as a serious operation with a sales team, even at small scale.

## Success Metrics

- **Activation:** breeder's first listing live and a seller applied within 14 days.
- **Conversion:** ≥ 1 closed deal per active listing per quarter.
- **Vetting quality:** < 5% seller churn for cause in the first 90 days.
- **Regional coverage:** 5 active regions by month 6.

## Pricing & Monetization

Free to list, free to apply. Platform fee is 15% of the seller's commission on a closed deal, billed via Stripe Connect. No subscription, no listing fee, no interview fee.

## Competitive Landscape

- **General freelancer platforms (Upwork, Fiverr)** — flooded with non-specialists; commission-only contracts are second-class.
- **Local classifieds (Myadvert, ss.ge)** — low-quality leads, no vetting, no commission machinery.
- **Direct Telegram groups** — work for the first hire; do not scale beyond personal networks.

## Risks & Open Questions

- [ ] Validate whether 15% of commission is high enough to fund vetting without deterring sellers.
- [ ] Confirm Telegram bot integration does not require a separate paid plan.
- [ ] Decide whether to require seller background checks (cost vs. signal).
