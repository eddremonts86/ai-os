# Founders' daily stack — what tools actually earn their place

## Problem

I'm looking to know more about how founders and directors handle the day-to-day administration and management of their startups. Specifically, I’m trying to map out which apps and software tools are actually worth using for things like project management, finance, communication, and HR. Would you be open to sharing a quick list of the core tools your team relies on? I feel like some of them are too complex for me to spend too much time trying to learn. Thanks a lot for your time and insights! submitted by /u/No_Building_2801 [link] [comments]

---

## Objective

Map out which tools founders and directors actually use for project management, finance, communication, and HR — and which are too complex to be worth learning.

## Target Users

Solo and small-team founders assembling a daily stack. The author is one such founder; readers on r/SaaS are the audience.

## MVP Scope

Not a product MVP. The deliverable is a curated list — the founder's daily stack — based on community-sourced recommendations, with explicit 'too complex' annotations.
