## Design direction

The product is a payment bridge, not a bank. The visual register is ledger-style: dense tables, calm colors, no "🚀 launch in 30 seconds" copy. Founders using this surface are being careful; the UI must match that posture.

Three principles drive the screen:

- **Numbers come first.** A founder's first visit shows the held-funds balance and the recent transaction list. Marketing copy does not appear above the balance.
- **Merchant-of-record disclosure is unmissable.** Every public payment page names the service as the merchant on the statement, with a one-line plain-language explanation in French and Arabic. The disclosure is not buried in a footer.
- **Reminders are respectful.** The 60 / 90 / 120-day incorporation reminders are framed as help, not pressure. No urgency banners, no "your funds will be lost" copy.

The MVP surface is functional: a founder dashboard, a public payment page, an admin back-office. There is no public marketing site beyond a single landing page that explains the model.
