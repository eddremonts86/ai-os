# Every day this site posts one joke and one photo. One of each is AI. You guess.

> Auto-enriched product brief.

## Value Proposition

A daily human-vs-AI guessing game where one joke and one photo are posted every day, one of each is AI, and you guess, with a permanent answer log.

## Target Users

- Casual visitors looking for a quick daily game
- AI-enthusiasts testing their detection skills
- Anyone who wants a low-stakes daily ritual

## Jobs To Be Done

When I want a quick daily ritual, I want a guessing game where one joke and one photo are AI and I have to guess which, so I can test my detection skills in 30 seconds.

## Success Metrics

- At least 500 daily visitors in pilot
- Self-reported "I come back daily" rate above 20%
