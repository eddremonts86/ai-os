## Objective

Turn the recurring "Feedback Friday" Reddit thread (indie founders post their landing pages, the community gives critique) into a structured, asynchronous feedback engine: a weekly open thread, a small set of written-critique templates so the feedback is actually actionable, and a public scoreboard of who gave and who got the most useful feedback.

## Target Users

- Primary: an indie founder with a landing page that needs 3-5 fresh pairs of eyes.
- Secondary: a Reddit/community regular who likes giving feedback but doesn't have a structured way to do it.

## MVP Scope

- A weekly landing-page feedback thread on a public site (no Reddit dependency).
- 3 critique templates: "first impression", "what's missing", "what I'd change".
- A scoreboard: founders who got feedback, critics who gave feedback, both visible.
- A "thanks" button so the founder can mark which critique was useful.

See `DESIGN.md` for this project's design tokens.

## Constraints

- No upvotes/downvotes on individual critiques — only "this was useful" thanks.
- Threads close after 7 days; old critiques remain searchable.
- No account required to read; email required to post (anti-spam).
