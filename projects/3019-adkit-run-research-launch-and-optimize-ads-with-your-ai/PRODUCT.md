# AdKit – Run research, launch, and optimize ads with your AI agent

> Auto-generated product brief. Review and refine before MVP scoping.

## Value Proposition

A single chat-and-dashboard workspace that lets a founder, agency operator, or growth team research competitor creative, generate static ads, launch campaigns across Meta, Google, TikTok, Reddit, LinkedIn, and X, and diagnose performance from one surface — with an explicit human-approve-before-ship gate so AI-drafted changes cannot reach a live ad account without a human signature. The 500,000+ ad library and the clone-creative workflow turn competitive research into a starting draft rather than a screenshot in a slide deck.

## Target Users

| Stakeholder | Why they care |
|---|---|
| Solo founders and small growth teams | Tired of juggling six ad-platform dashboards and want one chat surface to orchestrate the work. |
| Performance marketing agencies | Need a single pane to track competitor creative across all six networks and clone winning ads. |
| In-house B2B growth teams | Concentrated on LinkedIn and Google and want the same chat-orchestrated workflow for a two-network reality. |
| AI agent builders and power users | Already live in a chat-first tool and want ad operations to follow the same shape rather than a separate dashboard. |
| Compliance and brand reviewers | Need an explicit approve-before-ship checkpoint so agent-drafted changes cannot reach a live account without a human signature. |

## Jobs To Be Done

1. **Functional job** — Research a competitor's ad creative on any of the six networks, generate a static ad draft in our brand voice, and queue it for approval, all from one surface.
2. **Emotional job** — Replace the anxiety of "did the agent publish something I didn't see?" with a workflow where every draft sits in an approval queue until a human says go.
3. **Social job** — Be the operator who runs six ad accounts from one tab while peers still juggle six logins, and credit the agent for the speedup.

## Success Metrics

- **Activation:** Percentage of new accounts that connect at least one ad-platform OAuth and run their first competitor-research query within 14 days of signup.
- **Time savings:** Median time from "I want to clone this competitor ad" to "approved draft in queue", measured against the operator's prior workflow.
- **Throughput:** Number of agent-drafted changes that pass human approval per workspace per week — a proxy for whether the chat surface is actually used.
- **Coverage:** Share of operators connecting all six supported networks versus a subset, since the six-network story is the wedge.
- **Compliance:** Zero agent-initiated changes reaching a live ad account without a logged human approval, which is the product's safety contract.

## Risks & Open Questions

- Six network integrations on day one is a heavy engineering surface; a v1 that ships only Meta and Google may be more honest than a six-network promise that half-fails.
- The agent-approval model depends on the agent drafts being good enough that an operator approves them; if drafts are bad, the approval queue becomes a graveyard.
- Each ad platform changes its API and policy on its own cadence; a six-network connector has six times the maintenance surface of a single-network tool.
- The BetaList brief gives no benchmark for "approved per week" or time-saved; the success metrics are inferred, not stated.
