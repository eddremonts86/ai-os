# Working on a project that makes being thoughtful, easy!

## Problem

Hi Indie Hackers,

I just started my second project, The Greeting Card Club, with some friends. We created The Greeting Card Club because we kept forgetting to buy cards for our moms, and lost on easy chances to show that we cared. But beyond forgetting, if even remembered and went to a store, we found that we spent 10+ min just trying to find the "right card."

We wanted to make it easy to be thoughtful, and built The Club to mail you cards, before an occasion, that you can then personalize and gift. It eliminates forgetting to get a card, and going to a store!

I would love to get your thoughts on what I'm working on. I'm also really curious to learn better growth hacking techniques that I can leverage to get more customers. What are some growth hacking resources that you've used?

Looking forward to reading your thoughts!
