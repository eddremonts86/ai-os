# The absence of a single trusted service for solving everyday tasks

> Product brief authored from the source title and category. The poster's text was not available (source.name: manual); sections below re-state the problem and infer only what the title and category support.

## Value Proposition

A Russian household posts one everyday task, sees a list of ID-checked taskers in their area, accepts one, knows the money is held until the task is marked done, and the tasker carries a per-task liability policy - all in one app.

## Target Users

- Russian urban households with one-off errands and time-pressure tasks.
- Russian small businesses needing a part-time, vetted runner or assistant.
- Russian seniors and their adult children who want a known, ID-checked person for recurring small tasks.

## Jobs To Be Done

1. **Functional job** - Get a small, time-boxed task done today by a known, verified person.
2. **Emotional job** - Stop worrying about handing keys to a stranger.
3. **Social job** - Be the family member who 'organised help', not the one who delegated with a vague message.

## Success Metrics

- **Activation:** task posted within 5 minutes of signup.
- **First match:** median time from post to first-accept under 30 minutes in pilot cities.
- **Completion rate:** >= 90% of matched tasks complete without a dispute.

## Competitive Landscape

- **YouDo / Profi.ru** - the closest existing services; youdo is broad with weaker vetting, profi is for longer specialist gigs.
- **Neighbourhood WhatsApp / Telegram chats** - what people use today; no ID check, no escrow, no insurance.
- **Generic Yandex services / local flat-rate couriers** - too narrow a slice of everyday tasks.
