# Call My Laptop – Give your laptop a phone number

## Problem

Hey HN! I built https://callmylaptop.com, an app that lets you access the coding agents running on your laptop by phone.Call My Laptop has three key features: 1) it gives your laptop its own phone number, 2) it lets you start a new or resume an existing Codex, Claude Code, or OpenCode session by phone, and 3) it calls you when a task finishes or needs your input.I hope Call My Laptop makes coding agents easier and more fun to use away from your desk. I've personally been using it a bunch when I'm riding my bike to spin up sessions on random ideas :).Happy to chat about the voice, telephony, or dev stack behind the product. Let me know if you have questions, comments, or feature requests!
