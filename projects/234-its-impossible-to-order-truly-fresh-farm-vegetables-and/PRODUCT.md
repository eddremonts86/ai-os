# It's impossible to order truly fresh farm vegetables and bread through delivery aggregators — product quality is low, and you have to go to the market yourself

> Auto-generated product brief. Review and refine before MVP scoping.

## Value Proposition

Aggregators optimise for selection and speed; this product optimises for a single attribute — freshness — by binding every listing to a verifiable harvest or bake date and a vendor that lives within a short delivery radius.

## Target Users

Russian urban households willing to pay a small premium for same-day-harvest produce and same-day-baked bread, and the smallholder farms / artisan bakeries in their region who currently lack a low-friction sales channel.

## Jobs To Be Done

When I open the app, I want to see what was picked or baked today within 50 km of me so that I can order something I know is genuinely fresh. When I receive an order, I want to confirm that the freshness claim was real so the next time I trust the rating.

## Success Metrics

Repeat-order rate within 14 days. Vendor-side monthly active sellers. Disputed-freshness claim rate below 5%.
