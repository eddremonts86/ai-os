# Free way to actually see videos of the errors your SaaS users are running into

> Auto-generated product brief. Review and refine before MVP scoping.

## Value Proposition

A solo founder can install PostHog's free tier, run the guided setup, and within 10 minutes watch the actual sessions of users who signed up but never interacted, instead of asking an LLM to wire it up ad-hoc or paying for Hotjar.

## Target Users

| Stakeholder | Why they care |
|---|---|
| Solo SaaS founder (<100 MAU) | Wants session replay without a $300/month bill. |
| Small team (2-5) | Needs the workflow without per-seat pricing. |
| Aspiring indie hacker | Wants to learn the founder's session-replay habit early. |

## Jobs To Be Done

1. **Functional job** — Watch the sessions of stuck users without paying for Hotjar.
2. **Emotional job** — Stop emailing users for feedback that never comes back.
3. **Social job** — Be able to say "I watched it happen" in a standup or to a co-founder.

## Success Metrics

- **Setup completion:** ≥70% of visitors who start the guide finish it without leaving.
- **Time-to-first-replay:** median visitor reaches their first replay in <15 minutes.
- **Trust:** ≥3 founder posts cite the guide within 90 days.

## Pricing & Monetization

Free. Supported by a single affiliate link to PostHog's paid tier (their affiliate program exists).

## Competitive Landscape

- **Hotjar / FullStory / LogRocket** — paid, with per-seat or per-session pricing that hurts at small scale.
- **PostHog free tier alone** — works, but the workflow is not obvious to a non-expert.
- **DIY LLM prompt** — what the source explicitly does today; works once, not repeatable.

## Risks & Open Questions

- [ ] Validate that the PostHog query template actually returns useful users (vs. returning everyone who hit a slow network).
- [ ] Decide whether to add a second template (e.g. "users who reached checkout but didn't pay").
- [ ] Confirm whether the guide needs an account to track which step founders drop off at.
