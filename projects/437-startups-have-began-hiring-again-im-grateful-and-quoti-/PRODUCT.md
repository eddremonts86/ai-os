# Startups have began hiring again - I'm grateful and "I will not promote"

> Auto-enriched product brief.

## Value Proposition

A hiring-readiness layer for early-stage startups that have never hired outside their co-founding team, focused on the rubric and signal-quality side of the loop, not the job-board side.

## Target Users

- Early-stage startup founders making their first 3-5 hires after a small funding round
- Generalist growth operators advising small startups
- First-time hiring managers who have never run an interview loop

## Jobs To Be Done

When my startup starts hiring for the first time, I want rubrics and signal-quality guidance written for a first-time interviewer, so I do not accidentally filter out strong non-traditional candidates.

## Success Metrics

- At least 100 first-time interviewers use the rubric templates in 90 days
- Self-reported hire-quality rating improves for at least 30% of users
