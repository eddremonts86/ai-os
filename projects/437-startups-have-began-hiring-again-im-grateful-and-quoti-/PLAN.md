# Startups have began hiring again - I'm grateful and "I will not promote"

## Tech Stack

Chosen for this problem:

- Next.js
- TypeScript
- PostgreSQL
- Resend
- Stripe
- Vercel

## Architecture

Next.js; Postgres for rubrics + templates + (public) compensation snapshots; Resend for digest; Stripe for Pro tier; Vercel.

## Milestones

- Rubric template library for "first-time interviewer"
- Public dashboard of "hiring now" early-stage startups (data sourced, not scraped from job boards)
- Skills-based match score MVP
- Self-report survey to capture hire-quality improvement

## Risks

- Compensation data sourcing is the main constraint
- No aggregation without a credible source
