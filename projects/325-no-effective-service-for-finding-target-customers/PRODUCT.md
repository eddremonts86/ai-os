# No effective service for finding target customers

> Auto-generated product brief. Review and refine before MVP scoping.

## Value Proposition

A Russian SMB with a RUB 50k–500k monthly marketing budget runs an effective channel mix across Yandex Direct, VK Ads, Telegram Ads, Avito, and Dzen — without hiring a marketing agency.

## Target Users

| Stakeholder | Why they care |
|---|---|
| Russian SMB founder | Has the budget and the customer in mind; needs the channel mix and the assets. |
| Russian SMB marketing lead | Wants a faster iteration loop than the agency quarterly cadence. |
| Russian marketing freelancer | Wants a channel-mix recommender for their SMB clients. |

## Jobs To Be Done

1. **Functional job** — Reach the target customer without burning the budget on the wrong channel.
2. **Emotional job** — Stop the 'I am spending but I cannot tell if it works' feeling.
3. **Social job** — Have a clear channel-mix rationale to show a CFO or co-founder, not 'we tried a few things'.

## Success Metrics

- Cost-per-acquisition delta vs prior baseline — target −20% relative.
- Time-to-first-campaign ≤ 1 day from onboarding.
- Channel-mix acceptance rate ≥ 70% (operator runs the recommended mix).
- SMB NPS ≥ 50 at month 3.

## Pricing & Monetization

Free tier: channel-mix recommendation only. SMB Pro (RUB 2,490/month): campaign-asset generation + budget allocator + per-channel performance dashboard. Agency tier (RUB 12,490/month): white-label reports, multi-client workspaces.

## Competitive Landscape

- Yandex Direct / VK Ads self-serve — works, but no cross-channel recommender.
- Russian marketing agencies — bespoke, expensive, slow.
- Spreadsheets + gut feel — the current baseline; works at <100k RUB/month, breaks above.

## Risks & Open Questions

- [ ] Ad-policy violations — Mitigation: per-platform policy enforcement; required-disclaimer library; review queue for high-risk categories.
- [ ] Budget blowout — Mitigation: per-channel spend caps; daily-burn alerts; explicit user review before upload.
- [ ] Performance dashboard accuracy — Mitigation: pull from platform APIs where available; user-uploaded CSV fallback.
