# I keep missing my favorite farmers-market vendors—would this solve it?

> Auto-enriched product brief.

## Value Proposition

A farmers-market vendor tracker that alerts you when your favourite vendors are at a market this week, with vendor self-service schedule updates.

## Target Users

- Regular farmers-market shoppers with favourite vendors
- Vendors who want to notify regulars about their schedule
- Local-food communities tracking vendor schedules

## Jobs To Be Done

When I have favourite farmers-market vendors, I want a tool that alerts me when they are at a market this week, so I stop missing them.

## Success Metrics

- At least 100 shoppers in pilot
- Median alert-to-visit conversion above 30%
