# I keep missing my favorite farmers-market vendors—would this solve it?

## Tech Stack

Chosen for this problem:

- Next.js
- TypeScript
- PostgreSQL
- Resend
- Twilio (SMS)
- Vercel

## Architecture

Next.js; Postgres for vendors + schedules + alerts; Resend for email alerts; Twilio for SMS; Vercel.

## Milestones

- Vendor directory
- Schedule management (vendor self-service)
- Alert engine
- Email + SMS delivery

## Risks

- Schedule accuracy
- SMS cost
