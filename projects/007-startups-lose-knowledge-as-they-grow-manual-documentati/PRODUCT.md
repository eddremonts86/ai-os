# Startups lose knowledge as they grow. Manual documentation doesn't work. Need a smart tool for automatic knowledge capture and retrieval. Willing to pay from $25/month.

> Auto-generated product brief. Review and refine before MVP scoping.

## Value Proposition

A 20–100-person startup gets the right answer to any question that has already been answered inside the company, with citations back to the original Slack thread or meeting transcript — without anyone writing a wiki page.

## Target Users

| Stakeholder | Why they care |
|---|---|
| Founders / CTOs | New hires take 4–6 weeks to ramp; same questions answered 10× across teams. |
| People-ops leads | Onboarding is the bottleneck to headcount plans. |
| Engineering managers | Lose senior engineer time answering the same architectural question. |

## Jobs To Be Done

1. **Functional job** — Answer a question using the company's own past answers.
2. **Emotional job** — Stop feeling like every new hire reinvents the wheel.
3. **Social job** — Look like a company with memory in front of investors and candidates.

## Success Metrics

- **Activation:** first answer returned in < 10s within 7 days of install.
- **Reuse:** ≥ 5 questions answered per active user per week after month 2.
- **Onboarding impact:** time-to-first-PR cut by ≥ 30% for engineering hires.
- **Retention:** ≥ 75% of customers still active at month 6.

## Pricing & Monetization

Free for the first 25 seats. $25/month for 25–100 seats. Custom pricing above 100 seats. Annual plans at 20% off.

## Competitive Landscape

- **Notion + a wiki culture** — requires humans to write; rots within weeks.
- **Slack search** — keyword-only, no answer synthesis, no citation.
- **Glean / Slack AI** — enterprise-priced ($100+/seat), not accessible at $25.

## Risks & Open Questions

- [ ] Validate citation quality on real Slack data without surfacing confidential channels by mistake.
- [ ] Confirm latency budget for answer synthesis on LATAM-region latency budgets.
- [ ] Decide whether to include Notion and Google Docs as sources from day one or v2.
