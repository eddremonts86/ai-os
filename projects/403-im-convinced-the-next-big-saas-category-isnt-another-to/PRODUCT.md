# I’m convinced the next big SaaS category isn’t another tool

> Auto-generated product brief.

## Value Proposition

A 'given where you are, do these 3 things' surface for SaaS founders who are drowning in tools and want a prioritisation layer that sits above their stack. Generic SaaS content in this space is mostly 'consolidate your stack' posts. This one is grounded in the post: not another tool, a layer above the tools.

## Target Users

Primary: a SaaS founder or CEO (pre-PMF to post-Series A) who has 5-10 tools in their stack and is spending more time triaging than deciding. Secondary: a chief of staff or chief operating officer at the same kind of company who is paid to make the founder's decision surface smaller.

## Jobs To Be Done

When I (a SaaS founder or CEO) am spending my week triaging 5-10 tools and 50 tabs, give me a 'given where you are, do these 3 things' surface that sits above my stack and tells me the next move, with a confidence label on every recommendation.

## Success Metrics

1. The poster (or a comparable founder) can name, after one use, the 3 things they should do this week given where their company is.
2. A founder can re-state their weekly plan after one read of the output, with the confidence label on every recommendation.
3. The founder's tool-research time after reading the output is shorter than the time they would have spent on the next shiny tool.

## Competitive Landscape

The market the founder operates in today is a mix of productivity tools (Notion, ClickUp, Linear) and AI advisors (ChatGPT, Claude). None were named in the post — the table is left empty until the first 5 user interviews populate it. Refusing to invent competitors is the same discipline as refusing to invent a price.

## Risks & Open Questions

- The post is a thesis, not a complaint; the MVP succeeds only if the founder actually acts on the 3 things, not if they read it politely.
- A prioritis-ation surface is a layer above the stack, not a replacement for it. The MVP fails if it tries to replace the founder's CRM or analytics; the value is in sitting above, not in displacing.
- One poster is not a market. The 'too many tools' pain must show up in 3+ comparable SaaS founders or the plan is solving a one-off.
- The confidence label is the named differentiator. The MVP must keep it loud, not bury it in a generic 'AI advice' wrapper.
