# What would make you pay for a screen recording SaaS?

> Auto-generated product brief. Review and refine before MVP scoping.

## Value Proposition

A power screen recorder who hits Loom's free tier limit can switch to a browser-only recorder with auto-transcript, AI summary, viewer analytics, and a public feature scoreboard so the roadmap is shaped by users who actually pay.

## Target Users

| Stakeholder | Why they care |
|---|---|
| SaaS founder / PM / engineer | Records 5-30 demos/month; approaching the Loom limit. |
| Customer success manager | Walkthroughs for prospects; needs viewer analytics. |
| Educator | Async lessons with chapter navigation. |

## Jobs To Be Done

1. **Functional job** — Record more demos without paying per-seat SaaS pricing.
2. **Emotional job** — Feel that the tool's roadmap is shaped by paying users.
3. **Social job** — Share demos with analytics that show the prospect actually watched.

## Success Metrics

- **Activation:** ≥70% of signups record their first demo within 24 hours.
- **Conversion:** ≥10% of free users upgrade to Pro within 30 days.
- **Scoreboard engagement:** ≥30% of paid users vote on at least one feature per quarter.

## Pricing & Monetization

Free: 10 recordings/month, 10 min max, watermark. Pro $12/month: unlimited recordings, 60 min max, no watermark, AI summary, viewer analytics. Team $29/user/month: shared workspace, 5 hr max, SSO.

## Competitive Landscape

- **Loom** — the baseline; free tier limit is the explicit trigger.
- **Vidyard** — enterprise pricing, B2B sales focus.
- **Tella, Screen Studio** — Mac-only, more polished but smaller audience.

## Risks & Open Questions

- [ ] Validate the AI summary feature is reliable enough to ship without embarrassing hallucinations.
- [ ] Decide whether the feature scoreboard should be public or paid-tier-only.
- [ ] Confirm browser-only recording handles long sessions (>30 min) without tab-throttling dropping frames.
