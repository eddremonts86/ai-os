# I think AI has made building SaaS easier while quietly making one part of SaaS much more dangerous

> Auto-enriched product brief.

## Value Proposition

An AI-assisted "first impression" review focused on the one part AI has made harder: trust signals on a landing page.

## Target Users

- Solo SaaS founders who feel their landing page is technically competent but not landing
- Marketing leads at small SaaS companies optimising a single landing page
- Agencies running A/B tests for clients

## Jobs To Be Done

When my landing page is technically competent but not landing, I want a trust-signal review that names what is missing and shows how others fixed it, so I stop guessing what to add.

## Success Metrics

- At least 100 landing pages reviewed in 90 days
- Self-reported conversion-rate lift above 5% on at least 25% of reviewed pages
