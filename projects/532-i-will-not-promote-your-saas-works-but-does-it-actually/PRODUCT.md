# (I will not promote) Your SaaS works. But does it actually look good enough to make people trust it?

> Auto-generated product brief. Review and refine before MVP scoping.

## Value Proposition

An indie SaaS founder wondering whether to pay for a fixed-scope SaaS UI polish engagement can join a validation cohort, take a free discovery call, and confirm (or not) the price point + engagement shape — while watching a public validation status count update.

## Target Users

| Stakeholder | Why they care |
|---|---|
| Indie SaaS founder | Wants UI polish without a retainer; needs to confirm WTP before buying. |
| The source poster | Wants validation before building a product around the offer. |
| Other freelancers | Watching how the validation plays out before considering the same pivot. |

## Jobs To Be Done

1. **Functional job** — Decide whether to buy a SaaS UI polish engagement at the proposed price.
2. **Emotional job** — Stop wondering if the offer is real or just another freelancer cold-DM.
3. **Social job** — Be able to point a co-founder to the public status page.

## Success Metrics

- **Cohort fill:** 10 paid-cohort signups within 30 days.
- **WTP confirmation:** ≥7 of 10 confirm WTP at the proposed price points.
- **Public verdict:** positive or negative verdict published within 60 days.

## Pricing & Monetization

The cohort is free. If validation is positive, the eventual paid offering is $1,200/$3,500 packages as proposed by the source poster.

## Competitive Landscape

- **Generic freelancer DMs** — what the source poster is doing today; lacks validation.
- **Productized agencies** — proven model; what the source poster is considering pivoting to.
- **In-house hire** — the alternative buyers usually pick.

## Risks & Open Questions

- [ ] Confirm whether the cohort is filled from the source poster's existing network or cold outreach.
- [ ] Decide whether to publish the price points in the public landing page or only after the cohort.
