# (I will not promote) Your SaaS works. But does it actually look good enough to make people trust it?

## Problem

AI has made shipping functional products insanely fast. But I keep seeing the same problem: the product works, yet the interface feels generic, inconsistent, or unfinished. That's where I can help. I design + build polished SaaS interfaces from landing pages to the actual product UI. What I can help with: - Landing pages that communicate the product clearly - SaaS dashboards & product UI - UX/UI cleanup and visual polish - Responsive, production-ready implementation - Better copy + clearer conversion flow Basically: you bring the product, I make the experience feel like a real product. I'm looking to work with a few early-stage SaaS/AI founders right now. If your product currently feels more like "it works" than "holy shit, this looks good" then send me the link. ## Objective

Validate, before building, that the source poster's service offering (SaaS UI polish) is actually something indie SaaS founders will pay for at the price points and engagement shapes the source describes — using a public signup page + a 10-call paid validation cohort as the gate.

## Target Users

- Primary: the source poster (a SaaS UI polish freelancer) who needs validation before committing to a paid product.
- Secondary: other freelancers considering the same pivot.

## MVP Scope

- A simple landing page stating the offer (fixed-scope SaaS UI polish packages) and a "join the validation cohort" form.
- 10 paid discovery calls (free to the cohort) to confirm willingness-to-pay.
- A public "validation status" page (count + verdict) updated weekly.
- No SaaS built until the cohort confirms WTP.

See `DESIGN.md` for this project's design tokens.

## Constraints

- Build gate: 10 paid-cohort confirmations of WTP at the proposed price points.
- Public verdict (positive/negative) regardless of outcome — the validation is the deliverable.
- No SaaS in v1; only landing + cohort + status page.
