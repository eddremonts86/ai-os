# "3k monthly visitors and $80 MRR. Would you focus on traffic or conversion?"

> Auto-generated product brief. Review and refine before MVP scoping.

## Value Proposition

A solo founder can paste their real numbers (visitors, MRR, conversion) and get a one-page read on whether to push more traffic or fix conversion first, with a recommended first experiment sized to their actual funnel.

## Target Users

| Stakeholder | Why they care |
|---|---|
| Solo SaaS founder (1k-10k visitors, <$500 MRR) | Stuck between two lever pulls; wants data, not advice. |
| Marketing consultant | Needs a defensible diagnostic to share with multiple clients. |
| Bootstrapped SaaS at the plateau | Knows something is wrong but cannot tell whether it's traffic or conversion. |

## Jobs To Be Done

1. **Functional job** — Pick the right next move without spending another month on the wrong one.
2. **Emotional job** — Stop second-guessing every growth decision.
3. **Social job** — Be able to defend the choice to a co-founder with numbers, not vibes.

- **Trust:** ≥3 founder posts link back to the tool within 90 days.
