# I built my portfolio site with an AI website builder in an afternoon, here's what broke and what held up

> Auto-enriched product brief.

## Value Proposition

A portfolio site builder that produces a focused, fast single-page portfolio from a one-page brief in an afternoon, with a "what worked / what didn't" review of the AI-builder process.

## Target Users

- Engineers / designers / researchers who have been putting off a portfolio
- Job seekers who want a focused, fast portfolio site
- Freelancers who need a quick "here is my work" page

## Jobs To Be Done

When I have been putting off a portfolio site, I want a builder that takes a one-page brief and produces a focused site in an afternoon, so I stop procrastinating.

## Success Metrics

- At least 200 portfolios built in 90 days
- Median build time below 3 hours
