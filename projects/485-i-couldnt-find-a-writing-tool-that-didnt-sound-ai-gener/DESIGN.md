# I couldn't find a writing tool that didn't sound AI-generated for GTM work, so I built my own and dogfooded it for 2 months — Design direction

Like a CMS with a "sounds human" panel, not a chat UI.

- Surface: brief → draft → editor with highlighting.
- Color: ink + accent for AI-tells.
- Typography: sans for body, monospace for highlight IDs.
- No chat-style conversation.
