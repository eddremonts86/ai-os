## Objective

Reduce the time a solo founder spends on "looking for a co-founder" from months to a structured 4-6 week funnel: clarity on what role they're actually hiring, a profile that filters for it, a shortlist of channels where that profile already hangs out, and a script for the first conversation.

## Target Users

- Primary: a solo founder (technical, usually) 0-18 months into the business who is hitting the limits of solo execution and wants a co-founder specifically (not a hire, not an agency).
- Secondary: a non-technical founder who has built an MVP with contractors and wants a technical co-founder.

## MVP Scope

- A guided intake that nails down: which role (technical, GTM, ops), what stage, what equity range, what hours/week, what "must-haves" vs. "nice-to-haves".
- A profile generator that turns those answers into a 200-word co-founder-wanted post the founder can paste into IndieHackers, Reddit, LinkedIn, or a community.
- A channels shortlist based on the role (e.g. technical → GitHub, HN, dev-focused Discords; GTM → LinkedIn, Twitter).
- A first-conversation script: 12 questions to ask in the first 60 minutes that surface misalignment early.

See `DESIGN.md` for this project's design tokens.

## Constraints

- No marketplace, no profiles, no matching algorithm in v1 — the founder still does the outreach.
- All output is copy-paste, not gated behind an account.
- Honest about the reality: most co-founder searches take 3-6 months, not 4-6 weeks. The tool helps structure the search, not compress it.
