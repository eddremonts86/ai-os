# Need a tool that automatically finds people in social media using deeper criteria. Willing to pay $50/month.

> Auto-generated product brief. Review and refine before MVP scoping.

## Value Proposition

A growth marketer replaces 4 hours of manual keyword search with a 5-minute query that returns a ranked, deduplicated prospect list.

## Target Users

Solo sales reps and growth marketers in the USA who already do cold outbound.

## Jobs To Be Done

Functional: produce a prospect list from a behavioural query in minutes. Emotional: stop feeling that every existing option returns the same junk. Social: look like the team that responds to real intent, not spam.

## Success Metrics

Activation: user runs a first query within 24h of signup. Retention: ≥ 60% of users active at month 2. Time-to-first-list under 10 minutes.

## Pricing & Monetization

Based on the poster's stated willingness to pay: USD 50–50 per month.

## Competitive Landscape

Apollo and ZoomInfo focus on contact data, not behavioural signals. TweetHunter and PhantomBuster are single-platform and brittle against API changes. Manual Twitter advanced search is free but does not combine signals.

## Risks & Open Questions

Twitter/X API pricing has changed several times in the last 24 months; the business must be resilient to one more price hike. LinkedIn explicitly forbids scraping and aggressively enforces ToS against third-party automation.
