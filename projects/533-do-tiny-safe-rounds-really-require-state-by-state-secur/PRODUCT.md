# Do tiny SAFE rounds really require state-by-state securities compliance? I will not promote

> Auto-generated product brief. Review and refine before MVP scoping.

## Value Proposition

A founder closing a tiny SAFE round can answer 5 questions about their raise (states, size, accreditation mix) and get a state-by-state filing requirement list with sources and the last-verified date for each row — without paying a securities lawyer for an answer they could have read themselves.

## Target Users

| Stakeholder | Why they care |
|---|---|
| US founder (first SAFE, <$500k) | Doesn't know whether they actually need to file state-by-state. |
| Non-US founder (US-based angels) | Same question; even less context on US Blue Sky laws. |
| Lawyer's paralegal | Wants a quick reference for the most common small-round scenarios. |

## Jobs To Be Done

1. **Functional job** — Know which states require a filing before closing the round.
2. **Emotional job** — Stop worrying about accidentally missing a state filing.
3. **Social job** — Be able to tell investors "we've checked all the relevant states".

## Success Metrics

- **Coverage:** all 50 states + DC with at least one sourced row.
- **Trust:** ≥3 founder posts cite the explainer within 90 days, with no "got it wrong" pushback in comments.
- **Disclaimer adherence:** 100% of pages show the legal-advice disclaimer.

## Pricing & Monetization

Free. Supported by an affiliate link to a recommended SAFE template provider (e.g. Clerky, Stripe Atlas) for founders who don't have templates yet.

## Competitive Landscape

- **State securities portals** — authoritative, dense, founder-hostile.
- **Y Combinator SAFE docs** — useful, do not cover state filings.
- **Securities lawyers** — accurate, expensive ($1k+ for an opinion on a small round).

## Risks & Open Questions

- [ ] Validate the state-by-state table against a securities lawyer before publishing.
- [ ] Confirm the disclaimer copy is strong enough to deter founders from treating this as legal advice.
- [ ] Decide whether to add a paid "deferred filing review" tier (founder uploads their investor list, gets a per-state filing checklist).
