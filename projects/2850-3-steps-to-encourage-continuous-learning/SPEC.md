# 3 Steps to Encourage Continuous Learning

## Problem
 Continuous improvement is among the foundations of Lean . Yet, it is the principle most teams are struggling with because it’s easy to get started with at first, but quick to reach a plateau. Many leaders fail to understand that continuous improvement is more than process optimization. It is a constant drive to learn and apply. Therefore it is fair to say that continuous improvement = continuous learning. Achieving continuous learning on a large scale (e.g. company level) is not an easy task if you don’t know where to start and how to guide your team in the process. In reality, you can set your company on the way to continuous learning by following a simple 3-step model that we’ll cover in this article. 1.
