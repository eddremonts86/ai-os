# Best MoR provider out there?

> Auto-generated product brief. Review and refine before MVP scoping.

## Value Proposition

A SaaS founder can answer "which MoR is right for me?" in under 10 minutes by filtering a comparison matrix on their actual volume, geography, and subscription share, instead of opening five pricing tabs and reading sales copy.

## Target Users

| Stakeholder | Why they care |
|---|---|
| SaaS founder (first MoR pick) | Doesn't know whether the fee premium over Stripe Direct pays for itself. |
| SaaS founder (switching MoRs) | Has outgrown one provider (refund friction, missing geographies) and needs a side-by-side. |
| Indie hacker (low volume) | Wants to know whether MoR is worth the fee at <$1k MRR. |

## Jobs To Be Done

1. **Functional job** — Pick an MoR without spending a week on it.
2. **Emotional job** — Stop worrying about whether the chosen provider is "the right one" given the founder's actual volume.
3. **Social job** — Be able to defend the choice to a co-founder or investor without it being a vibes-based answer.

## Success Metrics

- **Time-to-decision:** median time-on-page <10 minutes for founders who arrive from a Reddit/IndieHackers link.
- **Coverage:** all five major MoRs included with at least 8 comparable columns.
- **Trust:** ≥3 founder testimonials citing the page in launch posts within 90 days.

## Pricing & Monetization

The page itself is free and supported by affiliate links to MoRs (Stripe, Paddle, Lemon Squeezy have public affiliate programs). No affiliate link changes the comparison content; the page discloses affiliate relationships at the top.

## Competitive Landscape

- **SaaS pricing comparison blogs** — long-form, slow to update, no interactive filter.
- **Each MoR's own comparison page** — biased by definition.
- **G2 / Capterra** — review noise, not structured comparison.

## Risks & Open Questions

- [ ] Decide whether to add new MoRs (e.g. Polar, Trolley) or stay at the canonical five.
- [ ] Validate that the affiliate disclosure model produces real revenue or whether to keep the page purely as a credibility play.
