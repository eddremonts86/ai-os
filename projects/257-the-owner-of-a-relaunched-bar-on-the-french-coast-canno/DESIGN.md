## Design direction

The page is for a first-time visitor deciding whether to drive to a venue on the coast. The visual register is editorial, not transactional: the hero shot should make the visitor want to come, not the CTA.

Three principles drive the screen:

- **The hero image carries the page.** A wide photograph of the venue at the relevant hour (sunset for an evening bar) sits in the first viewport. The format description and opening hours live below it. A first-time visitor forms a decision from the photo, not the copy.
- **Honesty about the relaunch is structural.** The page says what the venue is now, with the new format's name and a clear "previously a different format" note. Hiding the relaunch invites the visitor to feel tricked when they arrive.
- **The next three events are above the fold on mobile.** The visitor needs to see what is on this week without scrolling past the hero. Events are the trip-planning unit, not generic "open daily" copy.

The MVP surface is functional: a venue page, a referral handout, a CMS console. There is no booking widget, no menu PDF, no e-commerce. The deliverable is the venue's introduction, not its operations.
