# Trust crisis in India's UPI payments: fear of stranger transfers hurts business

> Auto-generated product brief. Review and refine before MVP scoping.

## Value Proposition

Replaces the merchant's fear of stranger UPI transfers with a verifiable, RBI-compliant trust layer that records the payer identity at payment time and provides a structured dispute path.

## Target Users

Indian small merchants accepting UPI from new customers; Indian SMBs selling via Instagram / Meesho; Indian freelancers.

## Jobs To Be Done

When a stranger wants to pay me via UPI, I want to verify their identity within RBI rules so I am protected against chargebacks. When a dispute arises, I want a structured form with the transaction reference so the bank has something concrete to act on.

## Success Metrics

Active merchants. Verified-payment rate per merchant. Dispute-resolution success rate. Reduction in merchant-reported stranger-payment fear (survey).

## Competitive Landscape

BharatPe and Pine Labs provide merchant-side UPI infrastructure but not payer-verification at payment time. Razorpay and Cashfree are payment gateways with dispute tools, but not designed for one-off stranger transfers. No source-named direct competitor combining RBI-compliant payer verification with merchant dispute tooling.

## Risks & Open Questions

RBI compliance is the gating item — over-collecting verification data is a regulatory risk. Chargeback dispute success depends on bank relationships. Indian SMB adoption requires sustained merchant-side outreach. Need 15 merchant interviews before claims about fear reduction are credible.
