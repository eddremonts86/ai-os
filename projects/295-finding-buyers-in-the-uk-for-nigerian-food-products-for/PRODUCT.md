# Finding buyers in the UK for Nigerian food products for export

> Auto-generated product brief. Review and refine before MVP scoping.

## Value Proposition

A UK buyer orders Nigerian pantry staples from a verified Nigerian producer with UK-compliant labelling and customs-handled delivery, in one checkout, in GBP.

## Target Users

| Stakeholder | Why they care |
|---|---|
| UK diaspora household | Wants egusi, palm oil, garri delivered without the WhatsApp coordinator role. |
| UK African-Caribbean retailer | Wants a reliable supplier roster instead of one-off Instagram outreach. |
| Nigerian producer cooperative | Has the supply; needs the UK demand and the export paperwork. |

## Jobs To Be Done

1. **Functional job** — Order Nigerian pantry staples with delivery to a UK address in 7–10 days.
2. **Emotional job** — Stop the 'find a friend flying to Lagos' workaround that diaspora households currently rely on.
3. **Social job** — Share the storefront link in a WhatsApp family group instead of coordinating a personal shopper.

## Success Metrics

- Median order-to-UK-delivery ≤ 10 days.
- Order NPS ≥ 50 (measured at delivery).
- Producer onboarding — 20 verified Nigerian cooperatives within 90 days.
- Repeat buyer rate ≥ 40% within 90 days.

## Pricing & Monetization

Marketplace commission: 18% of order subtotal on first order, 12% on repeat orders (encouraging loyalty). Producer subscription for premium placement (₦40,000/month). UK-side fulfilment fee baked into shipping.

## Competitive Landscape

- African Caribbean online grocers (Totally Wild, MyFoodieBox) — UK-based retail, narrow catalogue, no direct-to-producer story.
- Etsy / eBay sellers — small, informal, no UK labelling compliance.
- Personal WhatsApp networks — the current baseline; works for some, breaks at scale.

## Risks & Open Questions

- [ ] UK import seizure on labelling non-compliance — Mitigation: per-product UK label template; FSA-aligned review before listing.
- [ ] Customs delay at Murtala Mohammed / Heathrow — Mitigation: pre-cleared export paperwork, NAFDAC where required, tracking on every shipment.
- [ ] Nigerian FX volatility — Mitigation: producer payout in NAIRA at the platform's daily mid-rate; buyer charged in GBP at the same rate.
