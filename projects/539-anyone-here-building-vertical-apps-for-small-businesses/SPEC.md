# Anyone here building vertical apps for small businesses?

## Problem
 I’ve been following the conversation around vertical software for a while, and I find myself leaning more and more in that direction. One thing I’m experimenting with is starting with distribution. Rather than picking a vertical, building something, and then figuring out how to reach people, I’m trying to build something useful that attracts a specific group of small businesses first. Then, over time, understand what problems keep coming up and build around those. So roughly: distribution → ICP → problems → products I’m curious if anyone else here is building vertical apps for small businesses and thinking about things in a similar way.
