# Terraforming with Minimal Founders – Method for Diversity from Two?

## Problem

This is an experiment based on an idea I had recently:If you wanted to seed a new world with a species do you really need to carry a large archive?
Or could a tiny founding population carry a temporary mechanism that generates population-level genetic diversity during the first few generations, then shuts itself off?I think this could be plausible for a sufficiently advanced civilization, so I started trying to model the population-genetics side of it.What do you think? Like the idea?
