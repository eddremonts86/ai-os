# B2C SaaS — finding a repeatable acquisition channel

## Problem

Hey everyone, I’m working on a B2C SaaS and the main thing I’m struggling with right now is acquisition. I can make progress on the product, but I can’t seem to find a reliable channel that consistently brings in new users. For those of you who have successfully grown a B2C SaaS: Which acquisition channels work best for you? Do you mainly use SEO, TikTok/Instagram, paid ads, influencers, affiliates, Reddit, partnerships, etc.? How did you get your first users? What helped you go from a few users to a more consistent acquisition flow? If you were starting from zero today, what would you focus on first? I’d especially love to hear real experiences, including things you tried that didn’t work. Thanks! submitted by /u/Naekoz-_- [link] [comments]

---

## Objective

Find a repeatable acquisition channel for a B2C SaaS where the founder can already ship the product but cannot find the channel that converts cold reach into consistent signups.

## Target Users

Solo or small-team B2C SaaS founders who can ship but struggle with distribution. The author is one such founder; the question is asked on r/SaaS where the same archetype is common. Implicit users are people evaluating the founder's growth playbook.

## MVP Scope

A documented acquisition experiment plan: one primary channel picked from the author's named shortlist (SEO, TikTok/Instagram, paid ads, influencers, affiliates, Reddit, partnerships), one measurable KPI, and a 6-week learning window. The deliverable is not a SaaS — it is the experiment that justifies further product investment.

## Design Direction

See `DESIGN.md` for this project's design tokens.

## Constraints

Author is constrained by their own channel-affinity profile — they did not list which they already tried. No budget number is provided. No specific ICP is named beyond 'B2C'. The plan must stay agnostic to the channel choice until the author commits.
