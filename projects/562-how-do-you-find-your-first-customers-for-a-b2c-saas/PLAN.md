# B2C SaaS — finding a repeatable acquisition channel

## Tech Stack

No product to build. Tooling: Plausible or Google Analytics for traffic, a simple spreadsheet for cost-per-acquisition, a CRM-free signup funnel. If the channel chosen is SEO: a basic CMS like Astro or Hugo.

## Architecture

Tracking-only. Source-of-truth signup flow that records (channel, signup date, conversion to paid). Optional: a referral-code system if partnerships is the chosen channel.

## Milestones

M1: pick one channel. M2: ship the first measurable artifact (blog post, ad, partnership intro). M3: 6-week learning review. M4: keep, double-down, or pivot.

## Risks

Risk: the chosen channel does not match the founder's product/ICP, so the 6-week review is uninformative. Risk: founder switches channels mid-experiment, resetting the clock.
