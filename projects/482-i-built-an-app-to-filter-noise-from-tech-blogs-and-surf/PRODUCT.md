# I built an app to filter noise from tech blogs and surface 6 quality engineering reads every day. How do I get more users?

> Auto-enriched product brief.

## Value Proposition

A tech-blog noise filter that surfaces 6 quality engineering posts per day, with a 1-line summary each.

## Target Users

- Software engineers tired of full RSS feeds
- Tech leads who want a daily digest for their team
- CTOs who want a curated engineering signal

## Jobs To Be Done

When I am tired of a full RSS feed of tech blogs, I want a daily digest of 6 posts with summaries, so I can stay current without spending an hour a day.

## Success Metrics

- At least 1,000 daily digest subscribers in pilot
- Open rate above 30%
