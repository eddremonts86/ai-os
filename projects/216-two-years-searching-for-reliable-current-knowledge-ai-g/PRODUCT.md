# Two years searching for reliable, current knowledge. AI gives generic answers, search engines thousands of low-quality links. Need a trustworthy digest in the user's domain.

> Auto-generated product brief. Review and refine before MVP scoping.

## Value Proposition

Get a trustworthy 10-minute read on your domain instead of a 2-hour search. The first 1000 subscribers pay for the product from the recovered research time in the first quarter.

## Target Users

Knowledge workers in Russia and the EU who need a current read on a specific professional domain (law, finance, AI, biotech, etc.) and have given up on search and generic AI. Secondary: teams in professional services firms (consulting, law, accounting).

## Jobs To Be Done

Functional: have a defensible current read on a specific domain. Emotional: stop the anxiety of missing a development that a colleague will mention in the next meeting. Social: be the person who always has the cite, not the one who has to look it up.

## Success Metrics

At least 60% of weekly digest items rated useful by the user after 4 weeks. Source credibility score correlation with editorial reputation above 0.7. Subscribers renewing after 3 months above 70%.

## Competitive Landscape

Search engines rank by link signal, not editorial credibility. AI assistants hide sources. RSS aggregators (Feedly, Inoreader) do not synthesise. Academic tools (Connected Papers, Litmaps) are paper-only. No mainstream service combines an explicit credibility score with a synthesised, auditable digest.

## Risks & Open Questions

Credibility score may be gamed by motivated publishers. LLM synthesis may hallucinate citations to non-existent paragraphs. Domain expertise required to validate the score per domain. May be mistaken for a news aggregator.
