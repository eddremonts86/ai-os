# Risk of a LinkedIn ban due to false positive bot detection. Official support is unhelpful. Need a tool that produces human-readable audit trails and warning signals before the ban.

> Auto-generated product brief. Review and refine before MVP scoping.

## Value Proposition

Stop the heart-stopping email from LinkedIn. The first 1000 paying users are the people who have already lost hours to a false-positive appeal and want a tool that gives them a defensible trail next time.

## Target Users

UK and US professionals who post on LinkedIn daily, send high volumes of connection requests or DMs as part of legitimate sales, recruiting, or fundraising work, and have either been restricted or are worried about being restricted.

## Jobs To Be Done

Functional: know before LinkedIn does that your account is at risk. Emotional: stop the daily anxiety of 'is this the day?'. Social: have a tool that proves your activity was human, in case anyone ever asks.

## Success Metrics

At least 1000 active users in the first 6 months. Reduction in false-positive restriction incidents among active users (target: -50% versus control). Appeal success rate above 70% when users provide the audit log to LinkedIn support.

## Competitive Landscape

LinkedIn's own account-quality dashboard is opaque and only shows after a violation. Generic time-tracking tools (Toggl, Clockify) record activity but do not produce a defensible audit log. No mainstream tool combines rate-of-fire heuristic risk with a LinkedIn-specific audit trail.

## Risks & Open Questions

Risk of being flagged by LinkedIn for the extension itself. Risk of being interpreted as a 'ban avoidance' tool. Risk of LinkedIn changing their detection heuristics and breaking the risk score. Legal risk if a user uses the audit log to falsely claim innocence on actual automation.
