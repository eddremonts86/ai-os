# Startup teams have nowhere to find relevant custdev participants (people for interviews) to test early ideas and prototypes without overpaying for biased feedback

> Auto-generated product brief. Review and refine before MVP scoping.

## Value Proposition

Replaces the biased friend-network or expensive international panel with vetted Russian participants who match the persona and are paid through rails that actually work in Russia.

## Target Users

Russian startup teams running early-stage customer development; Russian product teams at mid-sized companies; independent UX researchers.

## Jobs To Be Done

When I am running custdev interviews, I want vetted participants who actually match my persona so the feedback is real. When I pay participants, I want a Russian rail that settles in their bank so they actually receive the money.

## Success Metrics

Vetted-participant pool growth per month. Interview completion rate. Startup-side NPS. Participant-side repeat-rate at 90 days.

## Competitive Landscape

UserTesting and Respondent are international panels — Russian payout is a problem. Respondent.io has Russian participants but via PayPal. Toloka (Yandex) is task-based, not interview-based. No source-named direct competitor.

## Risks & Open Questions

Participant vetting is the long pole — bad vetting produces biased results, which is the very problem the poster is trying to solve. Russian payment-rail KYC is slow. Honest-feedback incentive design needs careful framing.
