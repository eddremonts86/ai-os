# Research: existing solutions cover only 30% of small businesses' concerns about potential legal risks. They need a different product.

> Auto-generated product brief. Review and refine before MVP scoping.

## Value Proposition

A 10–50-person business gets a plain-language risk score for the 70% of legal worries existing tools miss, plus a checklist and document drafts that close the gaps, with one-click hand-off to a vetted lawyer when the situation calls for it.

## Target Users

| Stakeholder | Why they care |
|---|---|
| SMB owners (10–50 staff) | Pay $400/hour for lawyer time they use once a year; the rest festers. |
| Fractional CFOs / bookkeepers | Want a tool to hand clients that is more useful than a generic ToS generator. |
| Vetted lawyers on the platform | Want qualified leads, not tire-kickers from generic legal directories. |

## Jobs To Be Done

1. **Functional job** — Find out which legal gaps exist in the business this quarter.
2. **Emotional job** — Stop worrying that the next contract will reveal a hole.
3. **Social job** — Look like a serious operator to investors, customers, and regulators.

## Success Metrics

- **Activation:** first scenario scored within 7 days of signup.
- **Coverage:** median business scores ≥ 4 of 10 scenarios within 30 days.
- **Document drafts:** ≥ 1 draft generated per active business per quarter.
- **Lawyer hand-off conversion:** ≥ 10% of generated drafts result in a paid engagement.

## Pricing & Monetization

$49/month per business for the full scenario library, checklists, and document drafts. Lawyer hand-off is free for the user; lawyers pay a 15% referral fee on closed engagements.

## Competitive Landscape

- **LegalZoom / Rocket Lawyer** — document-first, no scenario-driven risk scoring, no curated lawyer hand-off.
- **Generic terms-of-service generators** — one-off, not tied to the company's actual gaps.
- **A real lawyer** — high quality, low frequency; the gap between "I should ask" and "I do ask" is exactly the 70%.

## Risks & Open Questions

- [ ] Confirm at least one licensed lawyer per supported jurisdiction will review scenario content.
- [ ] Decide whether to scope v1 to US-only or include UK / EU from day one.
- [ ] Validate that the "risk score" framing does not mislead SMBs into thinking it is a probability.
