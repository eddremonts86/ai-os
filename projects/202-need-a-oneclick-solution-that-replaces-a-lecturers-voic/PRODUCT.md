# Need a one-click solution that replaces a lecturer's voice with clear English directly inside YouTube lectures.

> Auto-generated product brief. Review and refine before MVP scoping.

## Value Proposition

One click turns a Hungarian lecture into an English one. The lecturer's channel doubles its potential audience without doubling the recording budget.

## Target Users

Solo YouTube educators in non-English-speaking countries whose audience has asked for English versions. Also useful for technical bloggers who record video.

## Jobs To Be Done

Functional: produce an English track for a non-English lecture without a recording session. Emotional: stop feeling like the channel is leaving international viewers behind. Social: be the local authority whose content is also globally accessible.

## Success Metrics

Click-to-result in under 20 minutes for a 30-minute lecture. At least 60% of lecturers who try the free tier produce a paid export. Translated audio naturalness rating at least 3.5 out of 5 in blind listener tests.

## Competitive Landscape

YouTube's own multi-language audio experiment is invite-only and does not yet let creators trigger it from a URL. Rask.ai and HeyGen require the user to upload a file and offer a video editor, not a one-click URL flow. No mainstream tool combines ingestion from a YouTube URL with re-mux to a downloadable MP4.

## Risks & Open Questions

Whisper may hallucinate on heavy accents, producing a poor translation foundation. Coqui voices can sound robotic on long passages. YouTube may flag re-uploaded content as duplicate. Lecturer's slang and informal humour will not translate by default.
