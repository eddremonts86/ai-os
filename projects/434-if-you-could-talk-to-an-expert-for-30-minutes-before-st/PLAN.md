# If you could talk to an expert for 30 minutes before starting your business, what would you ask? I will not Promote

## Tech Stack

Chosen for this problem:

- Next.js
- TypeScript
- PostgreSQL
- Stripe Connect
- Resend
- Vercel

## Architecture

Next.js; Postgres for questions, claims, replies, ratings; Stripe Connect for expert payouts; Resend for notifications; Vercel.

## Milestones

- Question-board schema and tag taxonomy
- Expert claim flow with public reply
- Founder unlock + Stripe payment
- Rating system that down-weights low-rated experts

## Risks

- Expert supply risk
- Mitigated by experts being able to set their own price
