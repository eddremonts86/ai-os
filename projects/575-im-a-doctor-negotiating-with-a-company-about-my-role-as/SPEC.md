# Doctor-as-advisor — 0.5% equity offer, what to know before signing

## Problem

I got offered around 0.5% in advisory shares vesting over 3 years, with room for additional salary/equity with continued growth. But the work I’d do seems pretty essential and key to the company so that percentage seems awfully small. But it also seems to be on par with what I’m reading so my expectations are definitely off so that’s why I’m here. What should I know/discuss? submitted by /u/Lumpy-Resource-1370 [link] [comments]

---

## Objective

Help a doctor with no startup experience evaluate a 0.5% advisory-share offer (3-year vest, room for additional salary/equity) and decide what to negotiate before signing.

## Target Users

Domain experts (the author is a doctor) being recruited as advisors or fractional execs by early-stage startups. The author is the primary persona; readers in the same situation are the audience.

## MVP Scope

Not a product MVP. The deliverable is a negotiation checklist specific to advisor-equity offers: vesting terms, scope-of-work definition, IP assignment, board/observer rights, and the cash-equity trade.

## Design Direction

See `DESIGN.md` for this project's design tokens.

## Constraints

Author explicitly notes 'expectations are definitely off' — meaning the 0.5% may be fair but they don't know how to evaluate. No specific company name, valuation, or product context is provided.
