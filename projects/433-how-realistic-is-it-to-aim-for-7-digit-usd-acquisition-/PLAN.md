# How realistic is it to aim for 7 digit USD acquisition after 3-4 years of running a startup? I will not promote

## Tech Stack

Chosen for this problem:

- Next.js
- TypeScript
- PostgreSQL
- Resend
- Stripe
- Vercel

## Architecture

Next.js; Postgres for trajectory profiles and survey data; Resend for follow-up; Stripe for premium "deeper analysis" tier; Vercel.

## Milestones

- Curate 30 sourced 7-digit acquisition profiles (year, niche, team size, revenue at exit)
- Trajectory calculator with transparent assumptions
- Post-use survey that captures adjustments made
- Public methodology page that lists the dataset's limits

## Risks

- Dataset quality dominates
- A bad analogue is worse than none
