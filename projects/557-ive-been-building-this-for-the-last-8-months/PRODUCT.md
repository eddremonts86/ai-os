# I've been building this for the last 8 months — indiatrusty

> Product brief for indiatrusty, the decision-support engine scoped in the source post.

## Value Proposition

A consultation-heavy business can replace its static lead-capture form with a guided-experience widget that walks the prospect through the right decision, captures context, and delivers a much more qualified lead.

## Target Users

| Stakeholder | Why they care |
|---|---|
| Consultation-heavy businesses in India | Currently lose leads because the website is a static form. |
| B2B businesses with qualification-first sales | Need context before a human takes over. |
| Education and training businesses | Need to match prospects to the right course. |

## Jobs To Be Done

1. **Functional job** — Build a decision tree for a business's consultation flow.
2. **Functional job** — Embed a guided-experience widget on the business's site.
3. **Functional job** — Capture the qualified lead at the end of the flow.

## Success Metrics

- **Activation:** first guided experience embedded within 7 days of signup.
- **Retention:** at least 1 tree revision per active business per month.
- **Conversion:** ≥ 10% lift in qualified-lead rate vs the static form baseline.

## Pricing & Monetization

Free tier: 1 guided experience, 100 leads/month. Pro at $49/month: 10 experiences, 5,000 leads.

## Competitive Landscape

- **Typeform / Jotform** — generic form builders; no decision-tree semantics.
- **HubSpot / Salesforce** — CRMs with lead capture; no guided-experience layer.
- **Custom code** — what most consultation-heavy businesses do today.

## Risks & Open Questions

- [ ] The per-vertical templates are the differentiator; a generic tree builder is not enough.
- [ ] AI inference cost must be bounded; runaway AI is the unit-economics failure mode.
