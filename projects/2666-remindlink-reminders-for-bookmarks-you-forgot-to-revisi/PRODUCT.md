# RemindLink – reminders for bookmarks you forgot to revisit

> Auto-generated product brief. Review and refine before MVP scoping.

## Value Proposition

_Based on source brief:_ Whenever i was used to get useful article/link , I used to save Bookmarks in WhatsApp,Google Keep and even third party bookmark manager to read it later,
But when I actually had free time, I would forget to check them, and the unread links kept piling up.
There were lot of apps to manage links ,but i couldn’t find one that reminded me to get back to it.That’s the reason I built RemindLink,You can share from link any app ,select RemindLink from ShareSheet and set a Reminder.
It also supports recurring reminder (hourly , daily , weekly, monthly) with custom interval support say,once in every 2 or 3 or any duration of any recurring duration,You can also paste message containing link ,
Let’s say your friend texted you “Let’s watch www.Netflix.com/xyz tmrw at 5pm” or “Sale on www.shopping.com/sale at 18th Aug 6:00pm”
paste it to link field , remindlink understands your message and fills rest of the field for youIt also lets you create a QR code to any of your saved link to share or post anywhere.
You can also select multiple links and create QR for your link bundle instead of sharing them one by one.
