# AI assistants can't handle Feynman learning technique

> Auto-generated product brief. Review and refine before MVP scoping.

## Value Proposition

A learner can run the Feynman loop with an AI that actually pushes back on vague explanations, in Chinese, without the AI either rubber-stamping the answer or drowning the learner in jargon.

## Target Users

| Stakeholder | Why they care |
|---|---|
| Chinese university student | Studying technical subjects; needs a loop that exposes real gaps, not a chatbot that confirms everything. |
| Self-taught learner in China | Preparing for a certification or interview; wants a structured drill. |
| Tutor / study-group leader | Wants a repeatable loop to assign to mentees and review their gaps. |

## Jobs To Be Done

1. **Functional job** — Turn a topic into a teachable explanation with identified weak joints.
2. **Emotional job** — Replace the anxiety of "I read it but I don't really know it" with a measurable loop.
3. **Social job** — Be able to share a "Feynman-passed" badge for a topic with peers.

## Success Metrics

- **Loop completion:** ≥ 40% of started topics reach the "pass" state within the same week.
- **Gap-detection precision:** ≥ 70% of AI-flagged gaps are confirmed by the learner as real after the loop.
- **Jargon reduction:** the median final explanation uses ≤ 2 specialised terms per 100 Chinese characters.
- **Retention:** ≥ 30% of learners return to start a second topic within 14 days.

## Pricing & Monetization

Free tier: 3 active topics, 10 rounds per topic. Paid tier: unlimited topics + a tutor-side dashboard for gap reports (RMB/month).

## Competitive Landscape

- **Generic AI chat (ChatGPT, Claude, Doubao)** — answers well, but does not enforce the loop; learners stop after the first explanation.
- **Spaced-repetition apps (Anki, RemNote)** — strong on memorisation, weak on the "explain it back" step.
- **Human tutoring** — the gold standard, but expensive and not always available.

## Risks & Open Questions

- [ ] Confirm a China-reachable model endpoint before launch; if not, deploy a local model on a China-region VPS.
- [ ] Define the "vague phrase" detector in a way that does not over-trigger on idiomatic Chinese expressions.
- [ ] Decide whether gap notes are shareable to a tutor or kept strictly per learner.
