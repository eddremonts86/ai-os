Fast and flat. The poster needs a teammate in days, not weeks.

- Surface: skill-grid first, profile-page second, chat last.
- Color: 3-color palette + skill badges.
- Typography: sans, dense, scannable.
- No hero animation. No "find your soulmate" copy.
