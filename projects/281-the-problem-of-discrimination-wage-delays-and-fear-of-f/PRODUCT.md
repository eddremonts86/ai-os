# The problem of discrimination, wage delays, and fear of firing/deportation among immigrant construction workers in the USA

> Auto-generated product brief. Review and refine before MVP scoping.

## Value Proposition

Replaces the worker's isolated fear with a structured, confidential, multilingual support service that protects identity while providing wage tracking, legal aid, and incident reporting.

## Target Users

Immigrant construction workers in the USA; immigration and labour lawyers; worker-advocacy NGOs.

## Jobs To Be Done

When my contractor does not pay me, I want a confidential place to record what is owed so I have evidence. When I need a lawyer, I want a referral to someone who actually takes wage-claim cases. When I am afraid to call, I want an SMS hotline that does not log my number.

## Success Metrics

Active workers using the tracker per quarter. Wage claims successfully filed via referrals. Legal-aid conversion rate. SMS hotline call volume.

Worker-advocacy NGOs (e.g. Centro de los Derechos del Migrante, National Day Laborer Organizing Network) do this work in person but not at platform scale. Legal-aid directories exist but are not construction-worker-fluent. No source-named direct competitor combining anonymous wage tracking with legal-aid referrals.
