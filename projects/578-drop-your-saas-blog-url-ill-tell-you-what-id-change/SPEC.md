# Free one-priority SEO audit for SaaS blogs

## Problem

The poster is offering free, one-priority content-strategy audits for SaaS blogs. Reply with a blog URL and the poster will point to "one opportunity I'd prioritize if I were responsible for growing your organic traffic" — content gaps, search intent, BOFU opportunities, internal linking, competitor gaps, or anything else. The poster explicitly commits to no generic "write more content" advice and to backing each finding with a specific suggestion and a reason.

## Objective

Crowdsource a portfolio of SaaS blogs to audit. The deliverable is a one-priority recommendation per blog, not a full audit. The poster is positioning the offer as a way to surface a portfolio piece ("I'll tell you what I'd change") rather than as a paid audit product.

## Target Users

SaaS founders and content marketers who run a blog and want one prioritised recommendation for organic traffic growth. The post is r/SaaS, so the audience is implicitly SaaS founders with a blog.

## MVP Scope

The audit itself: one prioritised opportunity per blog, with a specific action and a reason. The categories the poster lists are content gaps, search intent, BOFU opportunities, internal linking, and competitor gaps.

See `DESIGN.md` for this project's design tokens.

## Constraints

The post is a one-time solicitation, not a product offering. The poster is providing the audit for free, with no product, no price, and no commitment of an ongoing engagement. The source does not name the poster's credentials, their agency, or their portfolio.
