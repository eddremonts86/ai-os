## Value Proposition

Handoff the shift without losing the call that mattered.

## Target Users

Public safety agencies, primarily 911 dispatch centres. A single dispatch agency is the poster's design partner; the path is "expand within an agency, then expand to sister agencies via reference".

## Jobs To Be Done

- When my shift ends, hand off everything the oncoming dispatcher needs to know, in one record.
- When a critical incident is open, flag it so the handoff is unambiguous.
- When audit comes calling, produce the chain of who knew what when.

## Success Metrics

- Active agencies (the poster's stated bottleneck).
- Hand-offs completed per dispatcher per shift.
- Time-to-confirmed-handoff at shift change.

## Pricing & Monetization

Not stated. Vertical SaaS in a regulated market typically prices per agency or per dispatcher seat with annual contracts.

## Competitive Landscape

Public safety software vendors (CentralSquare, Mark43, Tyler Technologies, Motorola Solutions) cover records and CAD; shift-handoff is typically a paper/Excel process the poster is replacing. The poster does not name competitors.

## Risks & Open Questions

- [ ] Public-safety GTM is the long pole — small market, slow sales, high trust bar.
- [ ] Compliance posture (CJIS, state-level records retention) has to be designed in early.
- [ ] The poster's open question is marketing — that is itself a real problem, possibly more addressable than the product.
