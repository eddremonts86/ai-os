# I can build the SaaS. Finding the first customer is the hard part.

> Auto-enriched product brief.

## Value Proposition

A "first customer" co-pilot for solo SaaS founders: structured weekly check-ins, ICP narrowing, and templates for the 5 most common first-customer motions.

## Target Users

- Solo SaaS founders with a working product and zero paying customers
- Solo developers who have shipped but not sold
- First-time founders who feel lost after the build phase

## Jobs To Be Done

When I have shipped a SaaS and have zero paying customers, I want a structured weekly co-pilot that asks about ICP narrowing and outreach, so I do not drift into "build more features" instead of selling.

## Success Metrics

- At least 100 founders complete 4+ weekly check-ins in 90 days
- Self-reported "landed first customer" rate above 25%
