# I’ll Take Over Your LinkedIn for 30 Days (Free)

## Problem
 I’ll Take Over Your LinkedIn for 30 Days ( Free ) And no, I don’t need your LinkedIn login. Hey everyone! I’m a freelance content writer and I’m looking to work with 1–2 SaaS founders for a 30-day LinkedIn content experiment. I’ll help turn your expertise, experiences and ideas into LinkedIn content and test what actually works with your audience. For 30 days, I’ll: - Review your current LinkedIn profile and content - Help with your founder positioning - Create 3–4 content themes based on your expertise - Write 3 LinkedIn posts per week - Test different topics, hooks and content angles - Send everything to you for approval before posting - Track things like impressions, engagement, profile views and follower growth - Compare the results at the end of the 30 days You’ll get 12 LinkedIn posts + the strategy and tracking completely free. I’ll send you the posts for approval and you can publish them yourself. I’m doing this because I’m expanding into founder LinkedIn content and want to build a case study from real work and real results. I’m looking for founders who: - Have an existing SaaS business - Know their industry well and have things worth sharing - Have a LinkedIn profile but aren't posting consistently - Are willing to collaborate and give feedback during the experiment You don't need a big following. I’m actually more interested in founders who have strong expertise but haven't figured out how to turn it into good LinkedIn content yet. I’m only taking 1–2 founders for this. If you're interested, comment or DM me your LinkedIn profile + a little about your SaaS and what you'd like to achieve on LinkedIn. I’ll take a look and get back to you if I think we’d be a good fit. submitted by /u/meowbinoo [link] [comments]
