# Calendly — what made users choose it, and what could be better?

## Problem

For those who use Calendly, what made you choose it over other scheduling tools? And what do you think could be improved or done differently on the platform? Would love to hear what you like, what annoys you, and what you wish it did better. submitted by /u/WorryOk4086 [link] [comments]

---

## Objective

Learn what made Calendly win against other scheduling tools, and where it falls short — input for a would-be competitor or a Calendly-feature roadmap.

## Target Users

Founders considering a scheduling tool, and the Calendly team monitoring community signal. The author is conducting lightweight competitive research by asking an open question on r/SaaS.

## MVP Scope

Not a product MVP. The deliverable is a synthesis of community answers into a one-page brief: switch-to-Calendly reasons and named complaints. Useful as competitive intel.

## Design Direction

See `DESIGN.md` for this project's design tokens.

## Constraints

Source is a question post, not a problem statement. Any plan written from it is constrained by what respondents actually answer.
