## Value Proposition

_Based on source brief:_ When the *BANG* of the starting gun sounds at a “Freestyle” event, swimmers are allowed to move through the water in *any* which which way they desire.

They are free to swim butterfly, breaststroke, front-crawl, backstroke, side-stoke, or crab-throw. Most choose front-crawl. Also, I made up crab-throw. But my point is this:

*The Freestyle stroke is an invitation to question convention, and to improve on what came before.*

The first iteration of front-crawl was doggy paddle, created by observing the swimming technique of nearby mammals. At a freestyle event in 1895, an English swimmer named J.H. Thayers broke the 100 yard record by using an audacious sidestroke.

You may be thinking, what does all of this have to do with MY bootstrapped side-hustle? Well I believe that every founder has an invitation to swim freestyle. When we stand toe-curled at the starting block of a new day, we tend to copy what we know and mirror what came before us. We doggy paddle, side stroke and front-crawl
