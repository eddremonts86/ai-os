# 7 MVP mistakes that cost founders time and money

> Auto-enriched product brief.

## Value Proposition

A 7-mistake MVP retrospective with worked examples, delivered as a self-audit checklist so founders see which mistake they are about to repeat.

## Target Users

- First-time founders 0-6 months into building an MVP
- Solo founders with no co-founder to argue with
- Bootcamp / accelerator participants who want a common rubric

## Jobs To Be Done

When I am building my first MVP, I want a self-audit that warns me about the 7 most common mistakes before I make them, so I stop learning the same lesson other founders already paid for.

## Success Metrics

- At least 500 audits completed in 90 days
