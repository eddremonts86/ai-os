# eBay scraper — accidental capability, looking for a product shape

> Product brief — auto-generated then authored.

## Value Proposition

**One-liner:** Package an already-built eBay scraper capability into a micro-SaaS for a single named persona — wedge-first, multi-extractor-suite later.

## Target Users

eBay power-sellers, drop-shippers, and price-intelligence researchers who currently pay for or build ad-hoc eBay scrapers.

## Jobs To Be Done

1. Functional — replace a manual script or a $100/month incumbent with a faster tool. 2. Emotional — feel less like they're reverse-engineering eBay. 3. Social — share the speed advantage with their team.

## Success Metrics

Activation: 5 paying customers within 90 days. Retention: weekly active scrapes per customer. Revenue: $50/month per customer (a common indie scraper benchmark; not in source).
