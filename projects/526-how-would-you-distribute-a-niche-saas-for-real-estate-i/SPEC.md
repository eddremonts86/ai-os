# How would you distribute a niche SaaS for real-estate investors?

## Problem

I’m building a vertical SaaS that organizes tax-sale, municipal-property, foreclosure, and related public-property data into a searchable, map-based workflow. It currently covers more than 3,500 Canadian communities, includes over five years of records, and is being expanded toward international markets. The technical product exists. My current challenge is finding the right distribution partner and a repeatable sales channel. I’m particularly interested in people who have built partnerships with professional communities, newsletters, brokerages, associations, or niche B2B audiences. I’m keeping the company name and financial information private. If you have relevant experience and want to discuss the opportunity privately, contact me. submitted by /u/pseudo_r [link] [comments]

---

## Objective

Design a GTM playbook for a niche SaaS targeting real-estate investors (a small, identifiable audience with high willingness to pay) that names the top 5 distribution channels, the cost per channel, the expected CAC, and the first 90-day experiment to run — without falling back to "build it and they will come" or generic "post on LinkedIn" advice.

## Target Users

- Primary: founder of a niche SaaS targeting real-estate investors (rental, REITs, BRRRR, flip) with a working product and no customers yet.
- Secondary: a marketing lead at an existing proptech company entering the investor segment.

## MVP Scope

- A single playbook page (Markdown, hosted as an Astro doc): channel analysis, cost ranges, CAC estimates, a 90-day experiment sequence.
- A downloadable worksheet (CSV) that maps each channel to a cost row + tracking column.
- A weekly "experiment log" template for the founder to fill in after each channel test.
- No live platform; the playbook is static.

## Design Direction

See `DESIGN.md` for this project's design tokens.

## Constraints

- Real-estate investors are a small, addressable audience; channels must be specific, not "real-estate forums" generically.
- CAC numbers must be defensible: every estimate sourced or labeled "estimate, n=X".
- Single-page playbook in v1; no community, no course.
