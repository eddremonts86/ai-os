# traffic isn't converting, tried SEO / GEO spent $37 on reddit ads - pls roast and advise

> Product brief for the trust-and-clarity audit workflow scoped in the source post.

## Value Proposition

A solo founder in a regulated-adjacent category can triage a low-converting landing page and walk away with a numbered, prioritised list of the highest-leverage fixes — focused on trust signals and audience clarity rather than design polish.

## Target Users

| Stakeholder | Why they care |
|---|---|
| Solo founders in regulated-adjacent categories | Have traffic but no sign-ups; trust and clarity are the binding constraints. |
| Indie hackers in niche SaaS | Cannot afford a CRO retainer; need a structured audit. |
| Early-stage fintech growth marketers | Need to triage low-conversion funnels before paid acquisition. |

## Jobs To Be Done

1. **Functional job** — Identify which trust or clarity dimension is binding the conversion rate.
2. **Functional job** — Get a prioritised list of the highest-leverage fixes.
3. **Emotional job** — Stop blaming the traffic source when the trust signals are the problem.

## Success Metrics

- **Activation:** first audit completed within 7 days of install.
- **Leverage:** at least 1 trust or clarity fix shipped within 14 days.

## Pricing & Monetization

Free tier: trust-signal checklist + audience-clarity checklist. Pro at $29/month: scored report + offer-fit diagnostic. Engagement at $399: founder-led audit with a written report tailored to a regulated category.

## Competitive Landscape

- **Generic CRO agencies** — not tuned to the trust-signal binding constraint of regulated categories.
- **Compliance consultancies** — focused on the legal exposure, not the conversion.

## Risks & Open Questions

- [ ] The audit must not give legal advice on the regulated category; it must stay on the conversion side of the line.
- [ ] The trust-signal rubric must be tuned per category; a one-size rubric will mislead fintech founders.
