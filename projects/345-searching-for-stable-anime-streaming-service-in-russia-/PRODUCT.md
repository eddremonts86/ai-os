# Searching for stable anime streaming service in Russia without VPN requirement

> Product brief authored from the source title and category. The poster's text was not available (source.name: manual); sections below re-state the problem and infer only what the title and category support.

## Value Proposition

A Russian anime viewer opens the app, finds a new-season simulcast and a 1990s favourite side by side, pays in RUB without a VPN, and downloads an episode for the suburban train home instead of relying on a rotating URL.

## Target Users

- Russian anime viewers aged 16-35 who currently use pirate mirrors and want a paid alternative without VPN.
- Russian-dub fans who have a small back-catalogue on free platforms and want new seasons.
- Russian diaspora viewers in CIS countries who already have Russian payment methods.

## Jobs To Be Done

1. **Functional job** - Watch current anime without a VPN and without weekly URL hunting.
2. **Emotional job** - Stop feeling that paying Russian viewers were an afterthought for the global platforms.
3. **Social job** - Recommend a Russian app to a friend, not a Telegram channel.

## Success Metrics

- **Activation:** first episode watched within 24 hours of install.
- **Retention:** >= 35% monthly active after month 2.
- **Catalogue health:** >= 90% of titles have Russian-dub or Russian-sub available.

## Competitive Landscape

- **Crunchyroll / Netflix anime** - geo-restricted or thin Russian catalogue.
- **Anime-port.ru / AniLibria / similar** - what viewers use today; rotates under takedowns.
- **Wink / More.tv / Ivi** - Russian streaming; anime is present but not the headline.
