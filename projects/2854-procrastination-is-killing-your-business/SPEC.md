# Procrastination is Killing Your Business

## Problem
 Hello fellow IndieHackers! I've been around on forum for a while, mostly in read-only learning from questions and answers. There is but one topic I want to raise and name the enemy aloud. I'm talking about the procrastination that may keep you slightly off goal every day - and result in a huge business dead end in a long-term perspective. I'm sure some of you share the pain of starting the day with the clear intention to do X and Y, but finish up doing Z and maybe a bit of Y. Ask yourself: "How many times I was distracted by the unimportant mess, or operational activities eating up to several hours of my workday? "Do I always know where exactly goes my time intended for work? Do I feel myself productivive everytime?" I'm asking you, because startups have two precious resources: money and time , both equally imortant. While spending money is usually under tight control, time also has its own budget.
