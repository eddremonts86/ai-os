# Critique my onboarding? Built a workflow tool for digital marketing managers

> Auto-generated product brief. Review and refine before MVP scoping.

## Value Proposition

A US-based digital marketing manager can produce a credible, full-channel strategy map in 10 minutes — and ship it to the team as a PDF or Notion page — without booking a planning workshop or paying a consultant.

## Target Users

| Stakeholder | Why they care |
|---|---|
| US digital marketing manager | Needs a strategy map for the next quarter but cannot spend days on it. |
| Marketing consultant | Wants a reusable planning framework to bring into client engagements. |
| Founder of a small consumer brand | Plays the marketing-manager role and needs a starting point. |

## Jobs To Be Done

1. **Functional job** — Replace a multi-day planning session with a single 10-minute flow.
2. **Emotional job** — Feel confident that the output covers paid, organic, and email together, not just one channel.
3. **Social job** — Be able to share the strategy map with leadership without it looking like a one-pager from a junior.

## Success Metrics

- **Completion rate:** ≥60% of visitors who start onboarding finish it within 12 minutes.
- **Output reuse:** ≥40% of completers export to PDF or Notion within 24 hours.
- **Validation signal:** ≥70% thumbs-up on the post-onboarding feedback card.
- **Manager verdict:** ≥5 US-based marketing managers confirm the flow "makes sense for a real workload" (the explicit ask in the source).

## Pricing & Monetization

Free during validation. If the validation holds: $19/month solo, $49/month team (up to 5 seats), $199/month agency white-label. No pricing promised in v1 because the founder is still validating the workflow.

## Competitive Landscape

- **HubSpot / CoSchedule planning templates** — static PDFs, no live generation.
- **ChatGPT / Claude prompts** — possible but no structured output or export.
- **Marketing agency workshops** — $2k-10k, multi-week, far above a manager's planning budget.
- **Notion templates** — reusable but require the manager to know what to fill in.

## Risks & Open Questions

- [ ] Confirm 5 US marketing managers actually finish the flow without confusion (the explicit ask).
- [ ] Decide whether the strategy-map output quality holds up when the manager's brand has unusual channel mix (e.g. podcast-first).
- [ ] Decide whether to keep the no-login flow or require email before export.
