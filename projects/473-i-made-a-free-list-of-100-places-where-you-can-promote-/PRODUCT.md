# i made a free list of 100 places where you can promote your app

> Auto-enriched product brief.

## Value Proposition

A curated, free list of 100 promotion channels with category, submission rules, and a personal submission tracker.

## Target Users

- Indie founders looking for free promotion channels
- Solo developers who have exhausted Product Hunt / Hacker News
- Bootcamp / accelerator participants comparing distribution channels

## Jobs To Be Done

When I have an app to promote and have exhausted the obvious channels, I want a curated list of 100 places with submission rules, so I stop re-Googling "where to promote my app."

## Success Metrics

- At least 500 founder views in 90 days
- Median channels-tried per founder above 10
