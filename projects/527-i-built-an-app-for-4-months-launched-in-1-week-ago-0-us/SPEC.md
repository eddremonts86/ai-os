# "I built an app for 4 months, launched in 1 week ago. 0 users, super polished and proven demand."

## Problem

Hey guys I built my app for around 4 months hyper perfection, it is a app that debates with you In real time. I'm broke so I overoptmized every part of it and got everything for free. I launched a week ago, im trying every method of distribution but nothing is working out Any tips? submitted by /u/PrimaryBasil7284 [link] [comments]

---

## Objective

Help a founder who has just launched (1 week ago, after 4 months of building) and has zero users run a structured first-7-days diagnosis that surfaces the most likely reason nobody is signing up — wrong audience, wrong channel, wrong onboarding, or wrong price — and prescribes one experiment per day for the next 14 days, ranked by expected impact.

## Target Users

- Primary: solo founder who built for 4 months and launched in the last week with zero signups.
- Secondary: a small team (2-3) in the same situation.

## MVP Scope

- A guided 15-minute intake that captures the product, the launch channels tried, the funnel (visitors → signups → active), and the founder's assumption about who the user is.
- A diagnosis report: ranked list of the 3 most likely root causes, each with a per-day experiment for the next 14 days.
- A daily check-in form (single question: what did you ship today?) with a 14-day streak counter.
- No accounts; the founder's email is the only persisted identifier.

## Design Direction

See `DESIGN.md` for this project's design tokens.

## Constraints

- Diagnosis must be willing to say "your audience assumption is wrong" — that's the most common root cause and the hardest one to face.
- The 14-day plan must fit inside a solo founder's working hours (<2 hours/day).
- No upsell to an agency or course.
