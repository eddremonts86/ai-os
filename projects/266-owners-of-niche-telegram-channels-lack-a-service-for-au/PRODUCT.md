# Owners of niche Telegram channels lack a service for automatically finding and organizing cross-promotion with relevant channels without spending hours on manual selection and negotiations

> Auto-generated product brief. Review and refine before MVP scoping.

## Value Proposition

Replaces the manual hunt-and-negotiate loop with a service that finds matches, drafts outreach, and tracks outcomes.

## Target Users

Owners of niche Telegram channels (1k-100k subscribers); small media companies running Telegram-first audiences.

## Jobs To Be Done

When I am ready to cross-promote, I want a short list of non-competing, audience-overlapping channels with draft outreach so I can approve and send. When a swap completes, I want to see the subscriber delta so I know which collaborations are worth repeating.

## Success Metrics

Active channels on platform. Swaps facilitated per month. Subscriber-delta attribution per swap. Paid-tier conversion.
