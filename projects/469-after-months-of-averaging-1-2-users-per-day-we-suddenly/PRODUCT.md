# After months of averaging 1-2 users per day, we suddenly got 400+ users

> Auto-enriched product brief.

## Value Proposition

A spike-analysis tool for solo founders: capture what changed the day traffic spiked, with a what-changed journal to support post-hoc attribution.

## Target Users

- Solo founders who have seen unexplained traffic spikes
- Indie hackers trying to learn from their own spikes
- Bootcamp / accelerator participants comparing "what worked" stories

## Jobs To Be Done

When my app's traffic suddenly spikes from 1-2/day to 400+, I want a tool that captures what changed that day (post, update, link, feature) so I can attribute the spike and decide whether to repeat it.

## Success Metrics

- At least 100 spike journals in 90 days
- Self-reported "I now know what worked" rate above 50%
