# Pre-revenue startup raising angel capital - what financials need to be in a pitch deck/data room? (I will not promote) — Design direction

It should look like a banker's worksheet, not a SaaS landing page.

- Surface: form + live preview side-by-side.
- Color: ink-on-paper neutrals + one warning red.
- Typography: monospace for numbers, serif for slide titles.
- No animations. No marketing overlays.
