# Moving with furniture is a weeks-long headache. No service picks up everything and pays fairly. Willing to give up to 50% commission just to get rid of this pain.

> Auto-generated product brief. Review and refine before MVP scoping.

## Value Proposition

A relocating household schedules one pickup and gets a check within five business days; the platform handles listing, negotiation, removal, and donation of anything that does not sell in 72 hours.

## Target Users

| Stakeholder | Why they care |
|---|---|
| Relocating households | Two-week Facebook Marketplace ordeal is more pain than losing half the value. |
| Downsizers / estate heirs | Cannot staff a multi-week liquidation from out of state. |
| Small landlords | Need a unit cleared between tenants in days, not weeks. |

## Jobs To Be Done

1. **Functional job** — Empty a household of its contents in 72 hours without doing it yourself.
2. **Emotional job** — Stop dreading the move; stop feeling like a seller haggling with strangers.
3. **Social job** — Hand the keys back with dignity instead of a half-empty apartment.

## Success Metrics

- **Activation:** first pickup scheduled within 14 days of signup.
- **Throughput:** ≥ 80% of items cleared within 72 hours of pickup.
- **Household NPS:** ≥ 50 after the deposit lands.
- **Crew productivity:** ≥ $4,000 net sale value per pickup, per crew, per day.

## Pricing & Monetization

Household receives 40–50% of net sale value (the platform takes 50–60% plus the pickup fee). Pickup fee is bundled into the split so the household never sees a separate line. Donations are tax-deductible receipts only, no cash.

## Competitive Landscape

- **Facebook Marketplace + self-pickup** — free to list, costs the household two weekends and several no-shows.
- **Estate-sale companies** — high-touch, three-week lead time, take 30–40% but require the household to be present.
- **1-800-GOT-JUNK** — removes everything for a flat fee but pays the household nothing.

## Risks & Open Questions

- [ ] Validate the 72-hour claim at scale — every extra day of storage kills the unit economics.
- [ ] Confirm 1099-K thresholds and per-household payout tracking.
- [ ] Decide whether to integrate with OfferUp / eBay for higher sell-through, or stay single-marketplace.
