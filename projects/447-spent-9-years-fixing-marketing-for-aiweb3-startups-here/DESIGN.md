# Spent 9 years fixing marketing for AI/web3 startups. heres the one mistake i see literally every single time — Design direction

Like an experienced consultant's worksheet, not a marketing suite.

- Surface: single-page audit + diagnostic card.
- Color: monochrome + one accent.
- Typography: serif for diagnostic titles, monospace for scores.
- No marketing copy. No testimonials carousel.
