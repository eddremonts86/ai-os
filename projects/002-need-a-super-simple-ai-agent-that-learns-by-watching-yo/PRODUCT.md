# Need a super simple AI agent that learns by watching your screen and automates routine tasks. Existing solutions are complex or expensive. Willing to pay $10–30/month.

> Auto-generated product brief. Review and refine before MVP scoping.

## Value Proposition

A solo operator can record a repetitive computer task once and replay it as a one-key automation, with no scripting and no monthly fee that exceeds the cost of the time it saves.

## Target Users

| Stakeholder | Why they care |
|---|---|
| Solo founders / operators | Spend 30+ min/day on copy-paste between SaaS tools; cannot justify enterprise RPA. |
| Small-team ops generalists | Already use Zapier; need the on-device, multi-app workflows Zapier cannot do. |
| Privacy-conscious users | Do not want screen recordings uploaded to vendor clouds. |

## Jobs To Be Done

1. **Functional job** — Turn a known repetitive workflow into a one-click button.
2. **Emotional job** — Stop feeling like a robot doing robot work; reclaim thinking time.
3. **Social job** — Look like someone who has their act together in front of clients.

## Success Metrics

- **Activation:** first automation recorded and successfully replayed within 7 days of install.
- **Time saved:** average 25 min/user/week within the first month (self-reported).
- **Retention:** at least 1 replay/week per active user after month 2.
- **NPS:** ≥ 40 among users who have 3+ saved procedures.

## Pricing & Monetization

Free tier: 3 saved procedures, local only. Pro at $19/month: unlimited procedures, cloud-sync (opt-in E2EE), JSON export. Team at $49/user/month adds a shared procedure library.

## Competitive Landscape

- **UiPath / Automation Anywhere** — enterprise-grade, priced per bot, learning curve measured in weeks.
- **Zapier / Make** — cloud-only, multi-step but tied to SaaS APIs, no on-device reach.
- **Open-source RPA (TagUI, Robot Framework)** — free, requires writing scripts in a DSL, not for non-developers.

## Risks & Open Questions

- [ ] Validate that the procedure-builder quality is good enough on real workflows (not just demos).
- [ ] Confirm the local-only positioning is a real differentiator vs. cloud-RPA's reliability wins.
- [ ] Decide whether to ship Windows in v2 or stay macOS-only for 12+ months.
