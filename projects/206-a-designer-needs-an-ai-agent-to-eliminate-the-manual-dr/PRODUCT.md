# A designer needs an AI agent to eliminate the manual drudgery of adapting designs for mobile and tablet breakpoints.

> Auto-generated product brief. Review and refine before MVP scoping.

## Value Proposition

Stop losing the first half of every week to responsive- breakpoint handwork. The first 200 design teams pay for the plugin from the recovered capacity in their first sprint.

## Target Users

In-house designers at startups and agencies in Europe and the US whose teams maintain a Figma design system and ship a mobile or tablet product. Particularly those handling marketing sites, B2B SaaS, and ecommerce.

## Jobs To Be Done

Functional: produce a mobile variant that meets the design system without manual layout. Emotional: stop resenting the part of the design job that has nothing to do with design. Social: be the designer who ships multi-device work without billing extra hours.

## Success Metrics

At least 70% of generated mobile variants pass the designer's review on the first pass. At least 30% reduction in design-to-hand-off time per screen. Plugin NPS above 40 among active users in the first 6 months.

## Competitive Landscape

Anima and Builder.io handle code export but not design-system-aware variant generation. Figma's auto layout requires manual setup per component. No mainstream Figma plugin reads the design-system library and applies its conventions to a new breakpoint.

## Risks & Open Questions

Figma's plugin API changes are common and can break the integration. Large design systems take a long time to ingest. Designers may reject the work if it doesn't match the style they imagined for that specific breakpoint.
