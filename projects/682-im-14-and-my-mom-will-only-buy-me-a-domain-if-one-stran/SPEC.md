# I'm 14 and my mom will only buy me a domain if one stranger actually pays for my app.

## Problem
 My mom thinks this is a phase or just a fad that I will get over. She just doesn't believe a stranger is going to pay her kid for software. So the deal is I get the real domain (and not a vercel domain) on her card the day one actual person buys something. One real person. Nobody has yet. The reason I built it: last term I got accused of using AI on an essay I wrote myself. I had no evidence to prove that I didn't. It records every keystroke and verifies through 25 different methods. Your teacher clicks one link and watches a full replay of you typing your essay and all the additions and corrections you made. How is it different from google doc history? Google Docs saves snapshots. It shows 200 words became 700 between 4:10 and 4:25, not how. Mine records everything and verifies everything with 25 ways to verify: the sentence you deleted and the word you misspelled, or even the twenty seconds of nothing while you figured out what comes next. Pasting from an AI doesn't work either because it tracks every paste and that sudden surge in words per minute typed. It also tracks your words typed per minute and this destroys any of the software which help you type as they all type at a constant pace. A human however varies in typing speed when they get an idea or when they stall. All these 25 methods make it very hard and tedious to try to cheat that simply doing it is more efficient. Most of the product is free and you can easily defend yourself and your academic integrity with the free plan. The $4 a month is that your records never expire and you get a full printable report you can send easily to your teacher I posted about it once and 60 people visited the site yet no one bought the product 😭 So if you have $4 and want to be the reason I get to stop using vercel.app subdomain, that'd be great. If not, just try it out and tell me where to improve. Thanks! I would be so grateful. receipts-red.vercel.app submitted by /u/Acrobatic-Owl5700 [link] [comments]
