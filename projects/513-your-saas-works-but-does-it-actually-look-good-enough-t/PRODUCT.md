# Your SaaS works. But does it actually look good enough to make people trust it?

> Auto-generated product brief. Review and refine before MVP scoping.

## Value Proposition

A solo founder with a working SaaS but a generic-feeling interface can book a fixed-scope polish engagement (landing or product UI) in under a minute, get a free 3-point critique the same day to de-risk the spend, and ship a noticeably more credible surface in 1-3 weeks.

## Target Users

| Stakeholder | Why they care |
|---|---|
| Solo SaaS/AI founder (90 days post-launch) | Product works but conversion is poor; cannot tell whether to fix copy, layout, or visual polish first. |
| Small agency (1-5 people) | One-off polish projects that don't justify a full hire; needs a predictable fixed-scope deliverable. |
| End user of the polished SaaS | Experiences a product that looks "like a real product" on first visit, raising trust before any feature is used. |

## Jobs To Be Done

1. **Functional job** — Ship a SaaS interface that converts at a higher rate without hiring a full-time designer.
2. **Emotional job** — Feel that the product is "the kind of thing people pay for" instead of a half-finished weekend build.
3. **Social job** — Be comfortable sharing the product link on socials or with investors without apologising for the UI.

## Success Metrics

- **Activation:** 5+ free critiques delivered within 7 days of landing page going live.
- **Conversion:** ≥20% of critique recipients book a paid package within 14 days.
- **Delivery:** Median package delivery time ≤10 business days; zero scope-creep re-quotes in the first 10 projects.
- **Repeat:** ≥30% of package buyers return for a second engagement within 60 days.

## Pricing & Monetization

Landing Page Polish: $1,200 flat. Product UI Polish (≤5 screens): $3,500 flat. A la carte add-ons (extra screen, copywriting pass, motion pass) priced at $400 each. No retainers in v1.

## Competitive Landscape

- **Generic freelance platforms (Upwork, Fiverr)** — variable quality, hourly billing, no SaaS-specific critique template.
- **Productized design agencies (e.g. Designjoy, Awesomic)** — subscription model, but priced for ongoing work and don't start with a free critique.
- **In-house hire** — $80-120k/year fully loaded, far above the budget of a founder who isn't yet at MRR.

## Risks & Open Questions

- [ ] Validate that a free critique actually converts at 20% — may need a stronger nurture step.
- [ ] Confirm the operator can hit a 10-business-day median without burning out.
- [ ] Define what happens when a founder's codebase is unfixable without a redesign (out of scope, refer out).
