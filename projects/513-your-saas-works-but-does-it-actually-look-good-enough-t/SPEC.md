# Your SaaS works. But does it actually look good enough to make people trust it?

## Problem

AI has made shipping functional products insanely fast. But I keep seeing the same problem: the product works, yet the interface feels generic, inconsistent, or unfinished. That's where I can help. I design + build polished SaaS interfaces from landing pages to the actual product UI. What I can help with: - Landing pages that communicate the product clearly - SaaS dashboards & product UI - UX/UI cleanup and visual polish - Responsive, production-ready implementation - Better copy + clearer conversion flow Basically: you bring the product, I make the experience feel like a real product. I'm looking to work with a few early-stage SaaS/AI founders right now. If your product currently feels more like "it works" than "holy shit, this looks good" then send me the link. I'll take a look and tell you 3 things I'd change first, for free. My previous work: Work submitted by /u/Full_Description_969 [link] [comments]

---

## Objective

Turn the free critique offer into a paid, structured polish engagement: founders send their SaaS link, get a 3-point review the same day, then choose a fixed-scope UI/UX package (landing page, dashboard, or full-product polish) delivered in 1-3 weeks.

## Target Users

- Primary: solo SaaS/AI founders who shipped a functional product in the last 90 days and can tell the interface "feels generic" but cannot fix it themselves.
- Secondary: small agencies (1-5 people) who need a contractor for one-off polish projects without hiring in-house.

## MVP Scope

- Landing page with a "free 3-point critique" intake form (link + 1-paragraph context).
- A standardised critique template the operator fills in for every submission (consistency beats depth at this stage).
- Two fixed-scope paid packages: Landing Page Polish (one page, copy + visual), and Product UI Polish (up to 5 screens).
- Stripe checkout for each package, with a Linear board handed over on payment.
- No agency features, no team accounts, no in-app messaging — email + DM is enough.

## Constraints

- Solo operator: every workflow must fit into evenings and weekends; no live chat, no SLA.
- Pricing fixed per package, no hourly billing — the goal is throughput, not billable hours.
- All deliverables in Figma + clean handoff to the founder's codebase; no lock-in to a proprietary framework.
