# ERP integral for SMBs — sales, purchasing, inventory, accounting in one platform

## Problem

The author (Spanish-speaking Reddit user, posting in r/SaaS) has spent several months building what they describe as a finished ERP system designed specifically for small and medium businesses. The pitch is a single platform that replaces the Excel sheets, paper trails, and disconnected systems currently used by SMBs, with sales, purchasing, inventory, and accounting kept in sync. The modules explicitly listed in the source are: ventas y cobranzas (sales and collections), compras y pagos (purchasing and payments), inventarios y almacenes (inventory and warehouses), tesorería y flujo de caja (treasury and cash flow), a dashboard and AI component, and document export. The author is offering a demo and is open to selling the ERP outright: "si alguien esta interesado directamente en comprar este ERP tambien lo vendo".

## Objective

Replace the patchwork of Excel, paper, and disconnected tools that small and medium businesses use to run sales, purchases, inventory, treasury and accounting, with a single always-synchronized platform. The author is positioning the product as finished and is selling it as a complete ERP, not a subscription SaaS in the source.

## Target Users

Owners and operators of small and medium businesses who currently coordinate sales, purchasing, inventory, treasury and accounting across spreadsheets, paper, and one-off tools. The language of the source (Spanish) and the SMB framing suggest a Latin American market, but the source does not name a country.

## MVP Scope

The modules listed in the source: sales and collections, purchasing and payments, inventory and warehouses, treasury and cash flow, a dashboard with an AI component, and document export. Cross-module consistency is the explicit value prop — the source emphasises that all modules live "always synchronized". The author is offering a live demo and is willing to sell the product outright, which suggests the build is already at a stage the author considers finished.
