# I can’t sell. (I will not promote) — Design direction

Anti-coaching. The poster is allergic to generic advice; the design should feel like a working document.

- Surface: minimal. Sample critique, upload form, single CTA.
- Color: dark UI with high-contrast annotations (red = lose, green = keep).
- Typography: monospace for call timestamps, sans for narrative.
- No motivational copy. No testimonials carousel.
