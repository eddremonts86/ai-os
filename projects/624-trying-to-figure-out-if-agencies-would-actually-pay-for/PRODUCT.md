# Trying to figure out if agencies would actually pay for this or if I'm solving a problem that doesn't exist

> Product brief — auto-generated then authored.

## Value Proposition

**One-liner:** A subscription that gives an agency a full multi-skill freelancer bench, white-label under NDA, with retained client knowledge across specialists and zero direct contact between the operator and the agency's end client.

## Target Users

Small and mid-sized web and digital agencies that already outsource skill gaps to freelancers. The buyer is the agency operator, not the agency's end client. Audience foil against Designjoy (the closest comparator the poster could find): Designjoy is for solo founders needing one designer; this is for agencies juggling multiple clients and multiple skill needs at once. White-label and NDA are requirements, not optional — the agency must stay visible to its own client.

## Jobs To Be Done

1. Functional — replace the recurring "find a freelancer, negotiate a quote, brief them, manage them" loop with a single subscription that drops tasks into a portal and returns delivery. 2. Functional — preserve institutional knowledge across tasks so a new specialist inherits the client file instead of starting from scratch. 3. Emotional — let the agency stop worrying that a freelancer will go around them and contact the client directly. 4. Financial — let the agency pause spend during a slow month without losing the bench.

## Success Metrics

Activation: number of agencies that submit a task through the portal within the first 14 days. Retention: monthly active submissions per agency, and pause/resume ratio (the operator's stated demand model). Revenue: monthly recurring revenue per agency and gross margin after freelancer payout. The validation gate is the poster's own first open question: at least N agencies that, when asked, would replace their existing freelancer stack with the subscription. The second open question (trial accounting) is its own validation gate: a trial structure that does not require monthly reconciliations on the operator's side.

## Pricing & Monetization

No price is stated in the source. The model the post describes is a flat monthly subscription with pause-when-slow, and a slow month means the subscription does not bill. Any specific dollar figure in this plan would be invented. The pause mechanic is itself a financial commitment — the operator takes the demand-side volatility onto their own books — which is part of why the poster asks the trial-accounting question. Trial length and structure are likewise open: free trial, paid trial, and milestone-based trial are all on the table until the poster hears back on question two.

## Competitive Landscape

Designjoy is named as the closest comparator and explicitly rejected as the right frame: it is built for solo founders needing one designer, not for agencies juggling multiple skills and clients. The poster searched for a closer match and could not find one, which is itself a market signal — there is no settled competitor in the "white-label multi-skill bench subscription for agencies" slot. Adjacent categories worth noting for context (not cited in the source): subscription creative agencies such as ManyPixels, Flocksy, Kimp, and Design Pickle all serve single-designer or multi-designer-as-needed needs for individual founders and small teams; none of them is explicitly white-label under NDA for an agency whose own client must remain unaware of the operator. The product surface Stepway claims — multi-skill, white-label, agency-as-buyer, with retained client context — is the gap the poster is pointing at.

## Risks & Open Questions

- [ ] Validate with 5+ agency owners whether they would replace an existing freelancer stack with this, or whether white-label handoff is a non-starter regardless of design.
- [ ] Decide the trial structure (free vs. paid vs. milestone-based) and the operator-side accounting method for each; this is the poster's second open question.
- [ ] Confirm compliance scope: NDA template, IP assignment under white-label, data residency for the client-file vault.
- [ ] Capacity risk: the bench is also the poster's own agency. New subscribers compete with the poster for the same specialists' hours.
- [ ] Sales-cycle risk: no reference class. The poster searched and found none closer than Designjoy, so buyers will not have a familiar product to compare against.
- [ ] Source lacked: any specific price, any specific trial length, any stated number of design partners.
