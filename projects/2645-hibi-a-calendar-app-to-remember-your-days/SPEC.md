# Hibi – a calendar app to remember your days

## Problem

Hey HN!Most calendar apps are good at showing what you have planned. They are not very good at capturing what the day was actually like.So I built Hibi, a customizable digital planner and calendar for iPhone and iPad. Every day has its own paper-like page. You can change the paper, draw on it, add text, stickers, and Washi tape, while keeping the events and reminders already on your device.I wanted something practical enough to use as my everyday calendar, but personal enough to capture more than appointments. A place to make each day feel like mine and remember it when I look back.Hibi is free to try on the App Store. I built it as a solo indie project and would love to hear what you think.https://apps.apple.com/app/id6762520622
