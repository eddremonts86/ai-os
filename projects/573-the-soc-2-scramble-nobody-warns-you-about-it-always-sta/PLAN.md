# SOC 2 fast-track for pre-Series A B2B SaaS — manual-first over tooling-first

## Tech Stack

Service-first, light tooling. Notion or Google Docs for the playbook template; a Google Sheet for control ownership; a basic landing page; Stripe for engagement payments.

## Architecture

Engagement pipeline: (1) deal-block diagnosis, (2) control inventory, (3) ownership mapping, (4) weekly progress reviews, (5) audit-ready handoff.

## Milestones

M1: package the manual-first playbook into a 6-week engagement. M2: land first paid engagement from a blocked-deal founder. M3: 5 engagements completed. M4: convert to a recurring annual model (SOC 2 cycles annually).

## Risks

Risk: founders expect the tooling-first shortcut and resist the manual approach. Risk: the audit window slips, missing the deal.
