## Objective

Replace the "build an app and it'll be passive income" myth with a single-page reality-check tool that takes an app idea, estimates realistic monthly effort (support, marketing, ops, code) and revenue, and shows the break-even time under the user's stated MRR target.

## Target Users

- Primary: solo founders 0-12 months into a SaaS who are surprised that their "passive" product still eats hours every week.
- Secondary: aspiring indie hackers deciding whether to quit a job to build an app.

## MVP Scope

- A short input form (app category, current MRR, target MRR, hours/week actually spent).
- A side-by-side comparison: hours/week vs. MRR, with industry-average effort benchmarks for support, marketing, and ops.
- A break-even chart and a one-line "your app is closer to a freelance gig than a SaaS" verdict if the metrics say so.
- No accounts; the calculation is in-page and shareable as a URL.

## Design Direction

See `DESIGN.md` for this project's design tokens.

## Constraints

- Honest framing: the verdict must be willing to say "this is a job" if the numbers justify it. The whole point is realism.
- All benchmarks must be sourced or marked "estimate based on X".
- No motivational copy. No upsell to a course.
