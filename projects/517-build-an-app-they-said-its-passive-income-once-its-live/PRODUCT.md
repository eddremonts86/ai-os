# Build an app they said it’s Passive income once it’s live.

> Auto-generated product brief. Review and refine before MVP scoping.

## Value Proposition

A solo founder can paste their real numbers (MRR, hours/week, support volume) and get an honest read on whether the product is closer to "passive SaaS" or "freelance-with-a-recurring-bill", with the underlying benchmarks visible so the verdict is defensible.

## Target Users

| Stakeholder | Why they care |
|---|---|
| Solo founder (0-12 months in) | Just discovered their "passive" app eats 20 hours/week; needs to see the gap named. |
| Aspiring indie hacker | Considering quitting a job; wants to know what "passive" actually means in MRR terms. |
| Bootstrapped SaaS at $1-3k MRR | Stuck on the plateau between solo and team; needs a reality-check on whether to hire. |

## Jobs To Be Done

1. **Functional job** — Stop overestimating how "passive" the product is.
2. **Emotional job** — Get permission to call it a job (or evidence that it isn't).
3. **Social job** — Be able to defend the time spent to a partner or co-founder with numbers, not vibes.

## Success Metrics

- **Honesty rating:** ≥70% of users who share the result report the verdict matched their lived experience.
- **Share rate:** ≥15% of users copy the shareable URL after computing.
- **Repeat use:** ≥25% of founders run the calculator more than once (e.g. quarterly).

## Pricing & Monetization

Free, supported by a single banner ad slot on the result page and an opt-in mailing list ("monthly reality check newsletter").

## Competitive Landscape

- **IndieHackers threads on passive income** — anecdotal, no tool.
- **Baremetrics / ChartMogul** — MRR dashboards, not effort benchmarks.
- **Spreadsheets** — works, but no benchmarks and no shareable URL.

## Risks & Open Questions

- [ ] Validate the benchmarks against a 50-founder survey before publishing the v1.
- [ ] Decide whether the verdict should be a single label ("job" / "SaaS" / "side project") or a spectrum.
