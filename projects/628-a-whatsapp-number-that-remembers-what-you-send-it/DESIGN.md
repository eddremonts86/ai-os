### Typography

- **Headings:** ui-serif, Georgia, Iowan Old Style, Times New Roman · 2.5rem · 700 weight · 1.15 line-height. A serif display face is the deliberate choice — most "send to self" tools reach for a sans-serif app voice; Keepr reaches for a notebook voice.
- **Body:** ui-sans-serif stack · 1.0625rem · 400 weight · 1.6 line-height.

### Components

- **Button primary:** bg #0f7a55, text #ffffff, fully rounded.
- **Button secondary:** transparent, text #131714, fully rounded, 1.5px border in #e4e1d8.
- **Card:** bg #fbfaf7, rounded 26px, soft two-layer shadow.
- **Bubble in:** bg #ffffff, rounded 16px — the user's message in the chat motif.
- **Bubble out:** bg #d8f5e4, rounded 16px — Keepr's reply in the chat motif.

### Do's

- Use the serif display face for headings; use the sans stack for body. Do not mix.
- Use `#0f7a55` for the single primary action; treat `#10b981` as the bright accent, not a second primary.
- Use the chat-bubble motif on any surface that wants to feel like the product (landing hero, empty states, onboarding).
- Match spacing to the 8 / 16 / 40 / 80 scale.

### Don'ts

- Do not add folders, tags, or organising primitives anywhere in the surface — the product replaces these, and the design should not undo that promise visually.
- Do not store or display media in the chat motif. The product promises media is never stored; the design must not imply otherwise.
- Do not introduce a third weight in headings. 700 for headings, 400 for body. Anything else is noise.
- Do not apply shadows stronger than the two-layer `--shadow-sm` recipe. Stronger shadows make the surface look like a SaaS dashboard, which is the wrong register.
