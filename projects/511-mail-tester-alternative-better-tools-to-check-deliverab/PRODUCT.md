# mail tester alternative - better tools to check deliverability?

> Auto-enriched product brief.

## Value Proposition

A deliverability-testing alternative to mail-tester.com: inbox placement + domain warmup + sender reputation at a price between free and GlockApps.

## Target Users

- Cold-email agencies testing deliverability before campaigns
- B2B SaaS teams sending outbound
- Solo founders running cold email without an agency

## Jobs To Be Done

When I am tired of mail-tester.com's 3-tests-per-day limit but not ready to pay $59/mo for GlockApps, I want a deliverability tool that gives me inbox placement, warmup tracking, and authentication checks at a fair agency price.
