# An indie hacker spends 20-30 hours manually 'cold launching' each new product in directories, Reddit, and X. Need a launching service that automates the boring parts.

> Auto-generated product brief. Review and refine before MVP scoping.

## Value Proposition

Recover 20-30 hours per launch. The first 1000 paying indie hackers pay for the service from the recovered launch time in the first quarter.

## Target Users

Indie hackers and solo founders in the UK, EU, and US who launch 2-6 products per year and want the cross-channel launch to take 2 hours, not 20-30.

## Jobs To Be Done

Functional: have a launch that actually goes out without taking the week. Emotional: stop the dread of the launch inbox. Social: be the indie hacker who always ships on Tuesday and gets the warm traffic.

## Success Metrics

At least 1000 active users in 12 months. Average launch prep time below 2 hours. Reply response time within 24 hours for at least 60% of monitored threads. Per-launch conversion rate above the indie baseline.

## Competitive Landscape

Buffer and Hypefury are social schedulers, not launch planners. Product Hunt's own launch tool is for one platform. BetaList and AppSumo are directories, not launchers. No mainstream service combines directory submission, Reddit thread participation, and X posting with a real-time dashboard.

## Risks & Open Questions

Reddit and X are hostile to automation. Each platform can change the API or the rules. Per-platform voice must be respected. Account banning risk if the automation looks bot-y.
