# The owner of a tech agency hasn't found a suitable tool for projects, domains, monitoring, and proposals. Willing to pay $100/month.

> Auto-generated product brief. Review and refine before MVP scoping.

## Value Proposition

A tech agency owner gets one dashboard that shows which clients need attention this week — domains expiring, projects slipping, monitors failing — and produces a polished proposal PDF in minutes, not hours.

## Target Users

| Stakeholder | Why they care |
|---|---|
| Agency owners (5–30 staff) | Currently stitch 4 tools together; new clients = data entry in 4 places. |
| Agency ops managers | Want a single view of the week without logging into each tool. |
| Agency clients (secondary) | Want a status page that actually updates. |

## Jobs To Be Done

1. **Functional job** — Run an agency from a single pane, not four.
2. **Emotional job** — Stop worrying that a client domain will silently expire.
3. **Social job** — Send a proposal PDF that looks hand-crafted, not templated.

## Success Metrics

- **Activation:** first client + first proposal sent within 7 days of signup.
- **Renewal reminders:** ≥ 80% of domains renewed on time within the first 6 months.
- **Proposal conversion:** ≥ 20% of proposals sent result in a paid engagement.
- **Retention:** ≥ 90% of agencies still active at month 6.

## Pricing & Monetization

$100/month flat for up to 50 active clients and 5 user seats. $200/month for 50–150 clients and 15 seats. Custom above that. Annual plans at 15% off.

## Competitive Landscape

- **Linear / Asana + a registrar + UptimeRobot + a CRM** — the current stack; works, but the seams hurt.
- **AgencyOS / Productive** — closer in shape but priced for 50+ seat agencies, well above $100.
- **Spreadsheet + willpower** — what the agency owner uses today; loses a domain renewal per quarter.

## Risks & Open Questions

- [ ] Validate the read-only integration model is acceptable (vs. writing back to Linear / Asana).
- [ ] Confirm the registrar API coverage covers Porkbun, Namecheap, and GoDaddy for the LATAM market.
- [ ] Decide whether to bundle a status page in v1 or leave that for a v2.
