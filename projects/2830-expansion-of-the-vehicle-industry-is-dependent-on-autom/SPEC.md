# Expansion of the Vehicle Industry is dependent on Automotive Connected Car Platform

## Problem
 It’s no secret that automotive cars have become widespread with their usage in and around the world. The rise of this industry is an interesting tale, and another chapter in this saga that can completely change the nature of this industry is the rise of connected car solutions, that are being worked on with the advent of the Internet of Things. With the adoption of these connected car solutions, the number of applications and avenues that these services can be used for are absolutely numerous. Here are just a few of the many applications of connected car services. Mobility Management : Moving around in a car can get infinitely easier with the advent of connected car solutions. With a large number of benefits such as the relay of live traffic information, parking assistance and optimised fuel consumption to name a few, the car can help passengers reach a destination quickly, safely, and in a cost-efficient manner.
