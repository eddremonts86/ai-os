# Building TooGoodToGo for Developers. Objective $100,000 in 30 days. Day 1

## Problem
 Hey everyone, Setting myself this challenge of generating $100,000 in 30 days. Been working as both a SaaS and Agency owner from London for a few years now and worked on some amazing projects across the years. Most of the top devs and designers I see usually have some time sitting on free time in between projects. In the UK we have Too Good To Go where you can find discounts on food surplus, what if we had the same for devs and designers? The logic is the same. An unbilled hour is gone forever and could be used for agencies and freelancers to improve their portfolio, building new client relationships and would allow buyers to find top talent at a cheaper price (only for relatively small scopes). Two things I genuinely don't know yet: Would you ever accept work below your rate card, even invisibly? Or is that a line you don't cross regardless of how the week looks? If you would, what would you need to see to feel safe doing it? Thinking about starting to collect applications with the following criteria (feel free to comment and I’ll reach out) - 10 projects worked on minimum 3 years of operating minimum 2 clients I can call. Will share any learnings along the way ! submitted by /u/Current-Payment-5403 [link] [comments]
