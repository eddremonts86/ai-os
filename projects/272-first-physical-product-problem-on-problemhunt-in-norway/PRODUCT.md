# First physical product problem on ProblemHunt: In Norway, you can't enter a building wearing shoes with spikes or roll in a bicycle with winter tires — no convenient protective pads exist

> Auto-generated product brief. Review and refine before MVP scoping.

## Value Proposition

Replaces the inconvenient shoe / tire swap with a slip-on protective pad that works in Norwegian winter and does not mark floors.

## Target Users

Norwegian residents with spike-fitted footwear or studded-tire bicycles; facility managers in commercial buildings.

## Jobs To Be Done

When I arrive at the office in my winter boots, I want to slip on a pad so I do not have to change shoes. When I roll my bicycle into the bike room, I want stud covers so the studs do not damage the floor.

## Success Metrics

Units sold per quarter. Return rate (target: under 10%). Reuse cycles per unit (target: 30+). Repeat-purchase rate.
