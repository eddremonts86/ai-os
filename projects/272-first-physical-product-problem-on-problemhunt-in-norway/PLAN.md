# First physical product problem on ProblemHunt: In Norway, you can't enter a building wearing shoes with spikes or roll in a bicycle with winter tires — no convenient protective pads exist

## Tech Stack

Industrial design in Fusion 360. Injection-moulded TPE (thermoplastic elastomer) for production, with 3D-printed prototypes for sizing iteration. Shopify storefront. Stripe checkout. Posten/Bring for Norwegian fulfilment. Simple analytics (Plausible / Shopify analytics) for funnel visibility.

## Architecture

Two physical SKUs, one Shopify storefront, one Norwegian fulfilment partner. No custom backend in MVP — Shopify handles checkout, inventory, and shipping integrations.

## Milestones

M1: Industrial design and 3D-printed prototypes. M2: First TPE production run (500 units per SKU). M3: Shopify storefront and Stripe checkout. M4: Norwegian fulfilment integration. M5: Customer feedback loop and sizing iteration.

## Risks

First production run sizing may miss; iterative sizing depends on return-rate analysis. Material performance at -10°C needs lab validation. Norwegian fulfilment costs can erode margin if not priced in.
