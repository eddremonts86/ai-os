# B2G SaaS — getting past UK local-government procurement

## Tech Stack

No product change required. Tooling: a bid-management spreadsheet, a Cyber Essentials certifier, a CRM with public-sector account templates.

## Architecture

Procurement-package pipeline: (1) certifications, (2) framework listing, (3) tender response template, (4) reference-customer outreach.

## Milestones

M1: complete baseline certifications. M2: submit framework listing. M3: respond to first tender. M4: close first signed deal.

## Risks

Risk: the founder's product does not fit the framework's category boundaries. Risk: a friendly reference is harder to recruit than the founder expects.
