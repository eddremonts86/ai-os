# B2G SaaS — getting past UK local-government procurement

## Problem

Has any one sold saas to UK local government? I have completed a number of demos and getting really positive feedback and interest. The next hurdle is the procurement stage. I am looking for someone who has done this before and could maybe offer some advice? submitted by /u/Nervous_Second_8541 [link] [comments]

---

## Objective

Move from 'completed demos with positive feedback' to a signed contract with a UK local authority — by passing the procurement gate the author has identified as the next hurdle.

## Target Users

UK local-government procurement officers (the gating buyer persona) and the SaaS founder navigating them. Secondary: UK local authority digital/IT leads who sponsor the buy.

## MVP Scope

A procurement-ready package, not a product change. Scope: G-Cloud or similar framework listing, a completed Cyber Essentials / IASME certification (typical baseline), a security questionnaire pre-filled, a pricing model that fits local-authority budgets, and a reference call list of three friendly councils.

## Design Direction

See `DESIGN.md` for this project's design tokens.

## Constraints

Author is already past demo stage — the work to do is procurement compliance, not product. Budgets in UK local government are tight; multi-year deals and procurement cycles are long (often 6–12 months). No specific framework (G-Cloud vs direct tender) is named in the source.
