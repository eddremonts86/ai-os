# I made a 3D Globe platform to promote your startup based on your MRR

## Value Proposition

A free, browsable 3D globe where every startup is a tower whose height equals its current MRR. No signup, no manual claims — Stripe is the source of truth, so the rankings can't be gamed. Startups that want to float above the surface ("orbit tier") get better visibility in exchange for a paid plan.

## Target Users

| Stakeholder | Why they care |
|---|---|
| Indie and B2B SaaS founders | Want a low-friction directory slot that doesn't ask them to fill out a form. |
| Investors and scouts | Want a quick, geo-aware view of who's at what MRR tier without scraping Product Hunt. |
| Hackers and creators | Want a product with a memorable visual surface worth sharing. |

## Jobs To Be Done

1. **Functional job** — discover startups of a given size in a given geography without filling out a single form.
2. **Emotional job** — be associated with a directory that won't accept self-reported MRR at face value.
3. **Social job** — point at a globe that looks good on a landing page or pitch deck screenshot.

## Success Metrics

- **Activation:** startups with a live Stripe-fed tower within 7 days of launch.
- **Engagement:** weekly unique visitors spinning the globe, average session duration.
- **Revenue:** paying "orbit tier" conversions from founders who want higher visibility.

## Pricing & Monetization

The post mentions an "orbit" paid visibility tier but doesn't name a price. Reasonable structure: a flat monthly fee (e.g. $29/mo) for orbit placement, free listing for everyone else. Pricing is an open question until validated with real signups.

## Competitive Landscape

- **Product Hunt, BetaList, G2, Capterra** — manual directories, often self-reported revenue, saturated.
- **Open VC lists / Crunchbase** — paid databases for investors, not visual directories for everyone.
- **Other "globe of startups" experiments** — niche HN demos; most never get a Stripe connector and die.
- **Indie Hackers** — community + revenue numbers, but a flat list, not a 3D surface.

## Risks & Open Questions

- [ ] Stripe Connect availability in every founder's country — coverage is the natural ceiling on the addressable market.
- [ ] Whether the visual is novel enough to drive word-of-mouth, or whether "globe of startups" has been done.
- [ ] Cold-start problem: an empty globe is uninteresting; the first 50 towers must be placed by hand or by partner deals.
- [ ] Pricing for the orbit tier: the post does not name a price and the founder has not validated one yet.
