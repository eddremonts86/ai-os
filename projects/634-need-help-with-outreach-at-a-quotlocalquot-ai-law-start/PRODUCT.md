The poster is a central European legal-tech founder whose SEO is converting for "lawyer AI" in the local language, who already runs a re-engagement email loop to past users, but who has no idea whether to spend on Google Ads, Facebook, or LinkedIn, and is hesitant to repeat the founder-story Reddit trick because the company is now more corporate. ## Target Users

- **The poster (SeruZ12) — central European AI-law founder** — cares because every additional lawyer who signs up is paying back the SEO + product work already done, and paid acquisition is the missing lever.
- **Solo or two-person legal-tech founder in another CEE market** — cares because the playbook is generatable across a small set of CEE languages without much localisation work.
- **Practising lawyer and paralegal** as the buyer — cares because they want a tool that fits document-heavy workflows, not a chatbot demo.
- **Local bar association** — cares because their members need trustworthy tools and a sponsorship slot is a natural paid-acquisition channel.
- **Outsourced marketing freelancer on retainer** — cares because the playbook is the brief they would otherwise spend 10 hours reconstructing from a kickoff call.

## Jobs To Be Done

- **Functional:** When I have a working legal-tech SaaS but no marketing playbook, I want a documented runbook with a paid-ads experiment scaffold, so I can test Google Ads, LinkedIn, and Facebook without hiring a marketing agency.
- **Emotional:** I want to stop wondering whether I am throwing money at the wrong channel, so I can spend on the one that actually brings lawyers who work with documents.
- **Social:** I want to be able to tell a peer founder "I have a working distribution channel", so I stop being the legal-tech founder who has traffic but no users.

## Success Metrics

- Cost per activated lawyer (signed up AND completed a document upload) — target under $25 after two weeks of testing.
- Number of bar-association or law-firm partnership intros generated from outreach — target one per week after week 4.
- Click-through rate from each paid channel's landing page variant — target over 4% for the search channel.
- Number of previous users re-engaged and re-activated through the email wave — target a 10% re-activation rate.
- Time the founder spends per week on outreach — target under 4 hours, kept consistent with the founder's solo operating model.

## Competitive Landscape

- **Harvey AI** and **Spellbook** target law firms in English-speaking markets with enterprise sales; the playbook's audience is a CEE solo founder who cannot afford that go-to-market.
- **Generic SaaS paid-acquisition playbooks** (Demand Curve, Reforge) do not address the specific ethics-of-lawyer-advertising constraint that varies by CEE bar.
- **Local SEO agencies** in CEE markets would execute the playbook for the founder, but at retainer rates the founder is explicitly trying to avoid.
- **Lemlist / Instantly** for cold email are tempting, but the poster's audience is small enough that high-volume tooling is overkill; the CRM table the playbook ships is sufficient.

## Risks & Open Questions

- Will the founder-story Reddit trick still work if re-attempted, or has the audience already priced that move in?
- How does the founder measure activated lawyers vs signed-up lawyers in a way that does not require a data team?
- The poster's reference to "throwing money at it" implies a budget they have not stated; the playbook must be runnable at $300 and $3000/month.
