## Objective

Zinley — an AI product the poster positions as (1) Cowork-style handoff for real work, (2) coding built in, (3) own phone number and email, (4) memory of you and your people, (5) covers you when double-booked. The poster's specific finding is that the word "assistant" made people open ChatGPT in their head; the longer positioning list lands better. They are still learning distribution and asking other founders of AI-that-touches-humans which phrases have converted.

## Target Users

Professionals whose work involves handing real things off to other humans (phone, email, calendar). The poster's positioning implies the user does not want a chatbot; they want an agent that other people can call or email and treat as them.

## MVP Scope

- Phone number provisioning (inbound + outbound).
- Email provisioning on a custom domain.
- Identity model: the agent acts as the user, with a clear "this is the AI" disclosure.
- People memory: persistent notes about contacts, past interactions, preferences.
- Cowork-style handoff UI for the human user to oversee and intervene.
- Calendar + double-booking handling.

## Constraints

- Positioning matters: "assistant" did not sell; the longer list did. The product cannot be named or pitched in a way that re-evokes "another chatbot".
- Identity disclosure is a legal/UX constraint in many jurisdictions; cannot be deferred.
- The poster's stated open problem is distribution ("demos > feature dumps").
