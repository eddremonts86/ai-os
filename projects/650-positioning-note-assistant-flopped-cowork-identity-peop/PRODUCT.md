## Value Proposition

An AI you hand work to — phone, email, calendar — that other people treat as you.

## Target Users

Busy professionals who want a credible stand-in for routine human-to-human touch points (calls, emails, scheduling), not a chatbot.

## Jobs To Be Done

- When someone calls, my AI picks up and handles it like I would.
- When an email needs a response, my AI drafts and (with permission) sends.
- When I am double-booked, my AI covers one side and briefs me afterwards.
- When someone asks about my work history, my AI remembers and answers.

## Success Metrics

- Handled calls/emails per user per week.
- User satisfaction with the AI's responses (the poster's stated positioning hinges on this).
- Referrals from contacts who interacted with the AI (the AI as a marketing surface).

## Pricing & Monetization

Not stated. Phone-number and email-provisioning services typically price per active line or per minute.
