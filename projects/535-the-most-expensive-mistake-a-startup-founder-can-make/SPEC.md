# The most expensive mistake a startup founder can make?

## Problem
 Scaling before it’s ready.... More traffic won’t fix poor positioning. More sales calls won’t fix weak messaging. More ad spend won’t fix an unvalidated product. Growth amplifies what’s already there, including your weaknesses. Before you hire, spend, or scale, ask: Is our positioning clear? Do we know our ICP? Is our website converting? Have we validated Product-Market Fit? Do we have a repeatable acquisition channel? If several answers are “no,” your next hire may not be the solution. Your strategy might be. That’s why I created the 50-Point Growth Readiness Checklist to help founders identify the gaps holding their growth engine back before investing more money into scaling. Don’t scale the unknown. Diagnose the gaps.
