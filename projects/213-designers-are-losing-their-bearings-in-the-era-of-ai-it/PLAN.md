# Designers are losing their bearings in the era of AI — it's unclear which direction to develop in, who reviews work, and how to keep the craft.

## Tech Stack

Next.js for the front-end and dashboard. PostgreSQL for the curriculum and review data. Meilisearch for the artefact search. Python + FastAPI for the mentor-matching logic. Stripe for the mentorship payments.

## Architecture

Designer builds a curriculum → submits artefacts → mentor is matched → review is published → designer updates plan. Public portfolio page per designer. Curriculum library is updated quarterly by a small editorial team.

## Milestones

M0 — curriculum library with 3 tracks. M1 — mentor matching with 10 mentors. M2 — review submission flow. M3 — 100 paying designers in pilot. M4 — public launch in Russia and the broader region.

## Risks

Mentor quality can drop if the vetting loosens. Designers may resist the structure and prefer the open feed. Curriculum updates must keep pace with AI tool changes. Reviews may slip into shallow feedback if quality control fails.
