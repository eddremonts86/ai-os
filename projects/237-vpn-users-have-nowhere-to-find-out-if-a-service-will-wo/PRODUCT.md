# VPN users have nowhere to find out if a service will work reliably on their network — there is no up-to-date rating based on real-time quality monitoring

> Auto-generated product brief. Review and refine before MVP scoping.

## Value Proposition

Replaces anecdote and forum-post guessing with a real-time, evidence-based signal that answers "will this work for me, right now".

## Target Users

Russian VPN users; sysadmins who maintain VPN setups for teams; journalists and researchers who depend on access to specific foreign services.

## Jobs To Be Done

When I want to watch a specific streaming service tonight, I want to see which VPN providers are currently unblocked for it so I do not waste 30 minutes trying. When my usual VPN breaks for a service, I want a Telegram alert so I can switch early.

## Success Metrics

Daily active users of the dashboard. Telegram bot opt-in rate. Time-to-detect when a service starts blocking a VPN provider (target: under 30 minutes).

## Competitive Landscape

Existing Russian-language VPN review sites are content-marketing-driven, not measurement-driven. Outernet / GreatFire test reachability from China, not Russia. Downdetector covers outages, not VPN reachability. No source-named competitor.

## Risks & Open Questions

Legal exposure of operating a measurement platform about circumvention tools inside Russia. Probe network may itself get blocked, in which case the dashboard lies. Need 3-5 advisory interviews with Russian VPN users to validate the service-list is right.
