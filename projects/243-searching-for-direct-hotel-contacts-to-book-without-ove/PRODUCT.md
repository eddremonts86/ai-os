# Searching for direct hotel contacts to book without overpaying to aggregators, which markup prices by 15-30%

> Auto-generated product brief. Review and refine before MVP scoping.

## Value Proposition

Removes the manual hunt for the direct booking channel and gives the traveller an honest estimate of what the aggregator markup costs them.

## Target Users

UK travellers who already use Booking.com / Expedia and want to avoid the 15-30% markup; travel bloggers; small travel agents.

## Jobs To Be Done

When I find a hotel on Booking.com, I want a button that takes me to its direct booking page so I can compare prices without searching. When I book direct, I want to know how much the aggregator would have charged so the savings are visible.

## Success Metrics

Browser-extension weekly active users. Direct-booking click-through rate. Verified-contact database size (UK focus).

## Competitive Landscape

Booking.com and Expedia themselves do not surface direct contacts. Direct-booking advocates (e.g. the HotelTonight positioning) exist but only on inventory they control. No source-named consumer tool that overlays direct contacts on aggregator listings.

## Risks & Open Questions

Aggregator terms-of-service risk for scraping contact data. Browser-extension distribution on Chrome Web Store has tightened. The 15-30% figure is per source and is the headline number; per-hotel verification is needed before claiming specific savings.
