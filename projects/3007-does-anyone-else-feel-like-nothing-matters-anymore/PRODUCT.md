# Does anyone else feel like nothing matters anymore?

> Auto-generated product brief. Review and refine before MVP scoping.

## Value Proposition

**One-liner:** A private, on-device voice and text journal for the cheat-code feeling — capture it, tag it, revisit it later, never have it talk back.

The product does not promise relief. It promises a place. A user who opens the app on a Tuesday because another AI launch made their side project feel silly again can record a 20-second memo, pick a tag, and close the tab. Two weeks later they can scroll the timeline and see the pattern: most entries cluster under "project" and "job," none under "learning." The value is not insight — it is the existence of the log.

## Target Users

| Stakeholder | Why they care |
|---|---|
| Tech worker feeling post-AI apathy | Wants somewhere to put the feeling that is not a public post, not therapy intake, not a chat with an AI |
| Reader of the original HN thread | Recognizes the cheat-code analogy and is curious whether logging the feeling changes how often it shows up |
| Friends/partners of someone in the demographic | Sometimes asks "are you okay?" and would like a softer channel than a direct question |

## Jobs To Be Done

1. **Functional job** — Capture a feeling in under 30 seconds and be able to find it again later by tag or date, with no account creation.
2. **Emotional job** — Feel that the feeling has been received and held somewhere, without being coached, reframed, or argued with.
3. **Social job** — Be able to say "I log this stuff privately" without disclosing the entries themselves, in a culture that treats posting about feelings as either performance or therapy.

## Success Metrics

- **Activation:** At least three entries saved within 7 days of first visit, measured locally (no telemetry in v1; this is a self-reported check during user interviews).
- **Retention:** Of users who hit activation, 40% still record at least one entry per month at the 90-day mark.
- **Engagement quality:** Median entry length is between 5 and 60 seconds of audio or 20–300 characters of text — long enough to mean something, short enough to be captured without friction.
- **Honesty check:** No metric counts "solved the feeling" — that outcome is explicitly out of scope.

## Pricing & Monetization

The MVP is free and on-device. There is no monetization path in v1. A future subscription for cross-device sync is plausible but is not assumed.

## Competitive Landscape

Source gives no competitive signal. The source post does not name comparable products. Day One, Stoic, and Reflectly exist in adjacent journaling categories, but the source does not compare to them and inventing that comparison would be a fabrication.

## Risks & Open Questions

- The source is one person's feeling; it is unknown how many others experience it strongly enough to install an app.
- A "no AI in the loop" promise is fragile — feature creep toward "weekly summary insights" could undermine the whole value proposition.
- Browser-based voice capture is unreliable across browsers; Safari and Firefox behave differently from Chrome.
- The product could attract people in genuine mental-health distress; the MVP has no escalation path and that is an open question, not a v1 surface.
