# Centralized tracking of tasks, time, and attendance for field staff. No unified dashboard, manual entry leads to errors and wasted time. Ready to invest in a solution.

> Auto-generated product brief. Review and refine before MVP scoping.

## Value Proposition

A manager of a small Andorran field-staff business replaces three sources of truth (WhatsApp, paper notebook, spreadsheet) with one mobile-and-web product whose Friday payroll export is correct the first time.

## Target Users

| Stakeholder | Why they care |
|---|---|
| Operations managers (10–200 staff) | Lose Fridays reconciling data; payroll errors damage trust. |
| Field workers | Want the check-in to take seconds, not require a typing-heavy form. |
| Payroll providers | Want a clean CSV in a known schema, not a manager's interpretation of a spreadsheet. |

## Jobs To Be Done

1. **Functional job** — Log a block of work on a phone in under 10 seconds and see it on the manager's dashboard.
2. **Emotional job** — Stop dreading Friday's reconciliation.
3. **Social job** — Look like a modern operation in front of clients and staff.

## Success Metrics

- **Activation:** first 5 check-ins per worker logged within 7 days of install.
- **Payroll accuracy:** zero manual corrections on the first month-end payroll run for an activated customer.
- **Retention:** ≥ 80% of managers still active at month 3.
- **Mobile NPS:** ≥ 35 among field workers.

## Pricing & Monetization

€8 per active worker per month, billed monthly. €6 per worker on annual plans. Onboarding fee waived for the first 20 customers.

## Competitive Landscape

- **Deputy / When I Work** — shift-focused, US-centric, no EU-only data residency.
- **Plain WhatsApp + spreadsheet** — current state; free, but loses hours of manager time per week.
- **Generic CRM field-service modules** — over-scoped; require a sales motion the SME cannot staff.

## Risks & Open Questions

- [ ] Validate that the €8/worker price is below the Andorran SME's threshold of pain for a Friday afternoon.
- [ ] Confirm offline-first sync reliability on low-end Android.
- [ ] Decide whether to bundle payroll export as a feature or partner with one provider from day one.
