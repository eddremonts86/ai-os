# UK property investors have nowhere to quickly and reliably assess refurbishment costs and find verified contractors, leading to weeks of delays and financial risks.

> Auto-generated product brief. Review and refine before MVP scoping.

## Value Proposition

A service that gives a UK property investor a credible refurbishment cost estimate within 48 hours and a short list of 2–4 verified contractors who can deliver at that estimate. The investor gets an itemised estimate with a low / mid / high band and a contractor shortlist, both with evidence the investor can compare.

## Target Users

- UK property investors (single-property and small-portfolio) needing a credible estimate and a trusted contractor.
- Buy-to-let landlords preparing a property for let.
- Property sourcers and deal analysts who need a quick "refurb cost" line item to evaluate a purchase.
- Bridging-finance and development-finance brokers who use the estimate to size a loan.

## Jobs To Be Done

- When I am evaluating a purchase, I want an itemised refurbishment estimate within 48 hours, so I can size the deal and decide whether to bid.
- When I have decided to refurbish, I want a short list of 2–4 verified contractors, so I am not cold-calling 30 tradespeople I found on Checkatrade.
- When I share my estimate with my broker, I want a single PDF that shows the scope, the band, and the contractor shortlist, so the broker can size the loan against something defensible.
- When I am a contractor and I want steady inbound work, I want to be on a verified panel the investors actually use, so my phone rings without my having to advertise.

## Success Metrics

- Number of estimates generated per month (proxy for usage breadth).
- Investor-to-contractor connection rate: percentage of estimates that lead to a contractor shortlist being opened and a contractor contacted.
- Estimate-to-completion accuracy: how often the contractor's final invoice lands inside the mid band (the MVP's honesty test).
- Contractor panel retention: percentage of verified contractors who stay on the panel after 12 months.

## Risks & Open Questions

- Validate problem with 5 UK investor interviews before MVP: confirm that the estimate-plus-shortlist framing matches what investors actually need, and that they would pay for the estimate as a service.
- Estimate honesty: a low / mid / high band is the only honest output for a refurbishment; a single number is a fiction. The MVP must publish the band, not a point estimate.
- Regional labour rates vary. The MVP's cost model must use the postcode to set a regional factor; a single UK-wide rate is dishonest.
- Verification must be honest. The MVP cannot rank contractors by a star rating the platform invents; verification must reflect real reference calls and prior-engagement evidence.
- Scope coverage is finite. The MVP is honest about which scopes it covers; it does not pretend to estimate structural work, extensions, or new builds.
