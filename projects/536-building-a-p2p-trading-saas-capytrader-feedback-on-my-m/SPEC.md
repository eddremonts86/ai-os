# Building a P2P Trading SaaS (CapyTrader) – Feedback on my model

## Problem
 Hey I've been designing a financial SaaS called CapyTrader that connects passive investors with skilled traders safely. The Problem: Investors have capital but no time; traders have talent but no large accounts. The Solution: A P2P marketplace featuring an Algorithmic Risk Firewall. Investors set a strict loss limit. If hit, the algorithm instantly freezes the account and protects the remaining funds. Monetization: A flat custody fee plus a performance fee charged strictly on profitable trades. Based on my simulations, handling volume across active users yields strong unit economics, but I want to stress-test the concept. What are the biggest regulatory or technical blind spots you see in this model? All honest feedback is welcome! submitted by /u/Own_Atmosphere5778 [link] [comments]
