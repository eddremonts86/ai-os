# New Year, New Side Project

## Problem
 The first half of last year I built my first SaaS product. Things were going great. I was able to get featured in a few notable publications and had a modest debut. Although small, the trajectory was heading in the right direction. I doubled my traffic by spending the last few months of the year working full time on my new baby. Until it failed, and well, I might be forced to pivot or quickly iterate on development once again. This was my first side project, and consequently my first failure.
