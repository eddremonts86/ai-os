# What problems do you face as a creator that a content distribution platform could solve?

> Auto-generated product brief. Review and refine before MVP scoping.

## Value Proposition

A creator can submit their current reformatting-and-re-uploading workflow once, see their pain reflected in a weekly aggregate report, and (after the validation gate) get a single tool that distributes one piece to all their platforms in under 10 minutes.

## Target Users

| Stakeholder | Why they care |
|---|---|
| Solo creator (weekly cadence) | Loses 4-8 hours per piece to reformatting; needs to know whether anyone has actually solved this. |
| Small creator team | Coordinates the same workflow across 2-4 people; needs a single source of truth. |
| Tool builder / scout | Wants to see the aggregate pain before investing in a build. |

## Jobs To Be Done

1. **Functional job** — Stop losing 4-8 hours per piece to platform-specific reformatting.
2. **Emotional job** — Feel that the workflow is sustainable at a weekly cadence.
3. **Social job** — Be able to defend the cadence to the audience ("every Tuesday") without the backend falling apart.

## Success Metrics

- **Intake volume:** ≥50 validated intakes within 60 days of the form going live.
- **Pain confirmation:** ≥70% of intakes confirm 4+ hours lost per piece.
- **Build trigger:** once 50+ intakes are in, the operator commits publicly to a build milestone.

## Pricing & Monetization

The intake + weekly report are free. The eventual distribution tool is planned as $19/month solo, $49/month team (up to 5 seats), with no pricing promised until the build is scoped.

## Competitive Landscape

- **Repurpose.io, MeetEdgar, Missinglettr** — distribution tools with overlap, but each optimizes a slice (e.g. just short-form clips).
- **Zapier / Make workflows** — DIY, fragile, no creator-specific ergonomics.
- **Manual upload** — the status quo the source explicitly wants to escape.

## Risks & Open Questions

- [ ] Validate that 50 creators report 4+ hours lost per piece (the source implies this; the gate proves it).
- [ ] Decide which platforms the eventual tool must support in v1 (YouTube + podcast + blog is a likely floor).
- [ ] Confirm whether the tool should generate platform-native assets (thumbnails, captions) or accept user-provided ones.
