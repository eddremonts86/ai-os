## Objective

A mobile app that preserves a viewer's TV Time history after TV Time shut down. The app imports the TV Time export (shows, episodes, watch dates, ratings, comments) so users keep their data instead of losing it. The poster has a small number of early Reddit-acquired users and is asking which channels to test first to reach the audience who actually needs it, without becoming spammy.

## Target Users

TV-show trackers who used TV Time and want their history preserved. The poster's audience is narrow by definition (TV Time ex-users), and the pain is one-time (the moment of shutdown).

## MVP Scope

- TV Time export import (shows, episodes, watch dates, ratings, comments).
- Continue-tracking UX so ex-TV-Time users do not need to relearn the surface.
- Mobile-first, with TV-show metadata integration (TMDb or similar).
- Optional community features (if the poster chooses to expand beyond personal tracking).

## Design Direction

See `DESIGN.md` for this project's design tokens.

## Constraints

- Audience is shrinking: every month further from the TV Time shutdown, fewer people need a one-time import.
- Solo founder; cannot staff every plausible channel.
- The poster explicitly says they want to test channels, not blast everywhere.
