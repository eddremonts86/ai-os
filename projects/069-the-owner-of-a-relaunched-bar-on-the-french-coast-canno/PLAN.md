# PLAN.md — The owner of a relaunched bar on the French coast cannot attract an audience in the evening due to the legacy of its past format (nightclub) and its isolated location

- **Backend:** Node.js API (TanStack Start)
- **DB:** SQLite con Drizzle ORM
