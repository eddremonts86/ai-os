- [ ] Write SPEC.md (this document)
- [ ] Write DESIGN.md (tokens + visual direction)
- [ ] Configure `tailwind.config.ts` with the tokens from DESIGN.md
- [ ] Set up the local development environment

## Phase 1: Core

- [ ] Build the one-page decision matrix that ranks Google Ads, Facebook, LinkedIn, and direct law-firm partnerships for the founder's shape, with a recommended first channel and budget cap
- [ ] Author the Google Ads kickoff guide covering account setup, the three keyword groups to test, conversion tracking wired to the founder's signup, and a "do not do this" list
- [ ] Draft the cold-email template for law-firm partners with three subject lines, a body template, a follow-up cadence, and a spam-filter sanity check
- [ ] Stand up the public-facing "the product is now usable" page on the founder's existing site, with a UTM-tracked CTA per channel
- [ ] Wire the weekly outreach log: a single-page Next.js form the founder fills in each Friday, summarising sends, opens, and replies
- [ ] Add the two-week stop/go rule with a documented CAC threshold and a one-paragraph rationale the founder can defend to a peer
- [ ] Include a "rules checklist" appendix that the founder's local counsel can tick before paid spend and cold-email waves go live

- [ ] Deploy the Astro playbook and the Next.js companion app to Coolify
