# Starting outreach at a local AI law startup, I will not promote

## Problem
 I am the founder of a central European AI lawyer platform. Up until now all the leads came from Google SEO "people searching lawyer AI" (in local language), or an article that blew up on a popular local news site thanks to a reddit post I made (I know inception) Ever since the article traffic has been dying down because the product was not there yet, but now it's actually usable but all the hype is gone. I would like to do outreach to get people to start using it again more. The obvious answer is to just make a reddit post bu the original one highlighted how I was a young founder and this is a free project and now we became a bit more corporate, still doable but I am putting it off. Emailing our previous users, already on the table and doing in waves. People are coming over. Other than that it's just the SEO, so I'm looking for some way to turn a dial and throw money at it and get some traffic of actual lawyers or practicioners that would be power users and work with documents a lot, I'm not expecting miracles just experiment with it and hopefully make some money. What would be the best way to reach them? Google ads? (I've never used Google Ads, do I have to select keywords or something?), Facebook ads?, LinkedIn? Thanks for reading. submitted by /u/seruZ12

## Objective

Convert the poster's confusion about where to spend the next dollar of marketing into a single, prioritised outreach plan that the founder can execute in one working week without hiring an agency. The plan is grounded in the founder's existing advantages — local-language SEO that already converts, a prior-user email list they are already re-engaging, and the credibility of a working product that the old Reddit post implied but did not prove. The deliverable is a decision matrix (Google Ads vs Facebook vs LinkedIn vs partnerships) with a recommended first channel, a budget cap, a two-week stop/go rule, and a short cold-email template for direct outreach to law firm partners.

## Target Users

- **The poster (SeruZ12) — central European legal-tech founder** — cares because the product is now usable, the SEO is humming, and the founder needs to know which paid channel is closest to a positive ROI.
- **Law-firm partner and senior associate** as the buyer — cares because they have a stack of documents to triage and a budget for tools that demonstrably save junior time.
- **Solo CEE legal-tech founder with SEO working but no paid playbook** — cares because the matrix is portable to other CEE local-language markets.
- **Marketing freelancer or content agency** the founder might hire to scale the outreach once the channel is picked.
- **University law clinic and junior associate pipeline** — the long-tail audience that the founder can reach through bar-association channels.

## MVP Scope

- A decision matrix (one page) that ranks Google Ads, Facebook, LinkedIn, and direct law-firm partnerships for the founder's specific shape, with a recommended first channel and a budget cap.
- A Google Ads kickoff guide: account setup, the three keyword groups the founder should test, and conversion tracking wired to the founder's existing signup.
- A direct cold-email template targeting law firm partners, with three subject lines and a follow-up cadence that the founder can run in waves.
- A short "I have a working product now" public-facing page the founder can use to re-anchor the original Reddit story without re-lying about being a young founder.
- A two-week measurement plan: which metric to read on day 3, day 7, day 14, and the stop/go threshold.
- A weekly outreach log the founder fills in by hand so the channel decision is data-driven, not hopeful.

## Constraints

- The poster has never used Google Ads and must be able to follow the kickoff guide without a marketing background.
- The plan assumes the founder's working product is the same one referenced in the original Reddit post; the playbook must not require a rebrand.
- No cold email at scale; the founder does individual outreach, and the playbook matches that operating model.
- The MVP covers one CEE local-language market only; multi-country expansion is out of scope.
- The plan must respect the local bar's advertising rules; the cold-email template and the public-facing page must be vetted by the founder's own counsel before shipping.
