# Chrome extension that tracks job applications on your machine

## Problem

I kept losing track of what I had submitted vs just opened, across Microsoft/Eightfold, Lever, etc.Chrome extension, load unpacked. Only reads the tab you already have open. Visit = seen, apply form = continue, "application submitted" = applied. History is IndexedDB, nothing goes to a server.Ollama is optional and only runs if you click Analyze. Opening a job does not call a model.
