# Problem of entering the AI automation market without technical experience

> Auto-generated product brief. Review and refine before MVP scoping.

## Value Proposition

A non-technical Indian operator can pick a ready-made AI automation template, configure it for a small-business client in an afternoon, deploy it with one click, and bill the client through Razorpay — without ever writing or reading code.

## Target Users

| Stakeholder | Why they care |
|---|---|
| Non-technical Indian freelancer / consultant | Wants to sell AI automation services; current path requires hiring a developer. |
| Small agency owner pivoting from SMM | Needs a faster, lower-skill path into AI services. |
| Career switcher | Moving into AI services with no engineering background. |

## Jobs To Be Done

1. **Functional job** — Deploy a paid AI automation for a client without writing code.
2. **Emotional job** — Replace the dread of "the client will ask me to change something I cannot change" with a visual config.
3. **Social job** — Have a portfolio page that proves shipped, billable work.

## Success Metrics

- **Time to first deployed client:** median ≤ 1 day from signup to first paid client deployment.
- **Template variety:** ≥ 5 templates covering the most common small-business asks.
- **Operator retention:** ≥ 50% of operators have a second paying client within 60 days.
- **Client NPS:** ≥ 30 across deployed automations.

## Pricing & Monetization

Free tier: 1 template, 1 client. Paid tier (USD or INR/month per operator): all templates, unlimited clients, marketplace profile. Template marketplace fee on third-party templates sold through the platform.

## Competitive Landscape

- **n8n / Make / Zapier** — powerful but require the operator to design flows; a non-technical user stalls at "where do I start".
- **White-label chatbot agencies** — established but expensive entry (training, retainers).
- **Generic AI chat apps** — the client can sign up themselves; the operator has nothing to wrap around them.

## Risks & Open Questions

- [ ] Confirm the 5 MVP templates with 10 non-technical operator interviews before locking the list.
- [ ] Decide whether the deployment runtime is multi-tenant (one service per operator) or single-tenant (one service per client) — single-tenant is safer for client data isolation.
- [ ] Define the "client asked me to change something" hand-off path so operators do not silently break a template.
