# It's difficult for parents of newborns in India to organize vaccination: there is no service for easy doctor search and a «turnkey» process provision.

> Auto-generated product brief. Review and refine before MVP scoping.

## Value Proposition

A booking and tracking surface for newborn vaccinations in India: a parent searches for paediatricians and clinics by city and vaccine, books a slot, and tracks the child's schedule across visits with reminders the day before each appointment and when the next vaccine is due. The clinic confirms or proposes an alternative slot via SMS or WhatsApp; the parent never has to call to chase.

## Target Users

- Parents of newborns in India booking and tracking vaccinations across the first 24 months.
- Parents relocating between cities who need to find a new paediatrician who stocks the same vaccines.
- Paediatricians and vaccination clinics wanting steady inbound bookings.
- New mothers post-delivery, often at home with limited mobility, needing a low-friction booking flow.

## Jobs To Be Done

- When my newborn is two months old and I need the next set of vaccines, I want to search by city and vaccine, so I can find a clinic near me that stocks what we need.
- When I have booked a slot, I want a reminder the day before, so I do not miss the appointment and have to restart the booking.
- When my child has had several vaccines across two cities, I want a single schedule that follows the child, so the next paediatrician knows what is already done.
- When I am a clinic, I want bookings to come in structured with the child's details, so my staff spends less time on the phone and more time on the visit.

## Success Metrics

- Number of bookings per week per active clinic (proxy for usefulness).
- Booking completion rate: share of bookings that result in a confirmed visit (proxy for friction in the booking flow).
- Schedule adherence: percentage of booked appointments kept (proxy for reminder efficacy).
- Repeat usage: percentage of parents who book a second appointment within 90 days (proxy for the multi-visit use case holding up).

## Risks & Open Questions

- Validate problem with 5 Indian parent interviews before MVP: confirm that the booking and tracking framing matches what parents actually face, and that they would use a third-party booking surface for vaccinations.
- Clinic-side onboarding is the binding constraint. The MVP must keep the clinic-side flow to SMS or WhatsApp confirmations, not require a portal login.
- Stock curation honesty: the MVP must publish "last confirmed" dates on each vaccine listing, not pretend stock is live.
- Medical scope discipline: the MVP is a booking surface, not medical advice. The IAP schedule must be clearly attributed; the doctor remains the clinical decision-maker.
- Personal data: a child's name, DOB, and vaccination history is sensitive. The MVP must define a retention policy and a parent-controlled deletion path.
