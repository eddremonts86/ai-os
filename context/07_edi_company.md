**`edi` is not an ApS and must not be written "edi ApS".** Edd asked for that originally; it was
refused because ApS is a specific Danish company form and this account is registered to a
person. Writing it would assert a legal form that does not exist, on invoices, to a regulated
payment institution. If an ApS is ever created, the Stripe account owner cannot simply be
edited — it needs KYC re-verification and a payout bank account in the company's name, which
Stripe usually handles as a new account.

**Sole trader means unlimited personal liability**, and it also means a business loss offsets
his other personal income — which an ApS would not. That second half is why it is the right
form today.

### Tax behaviour — set per price, not on the account

All five EUR prices are **`exclusive`** (`Comportamiento impositivo: No incluido`). This was set
on each price rather than on the account default, so BuilderHunt and enForma's account-wide
behaviour is untouched. BuilderHunt sells only in USD, where "Automático" is already exclusive,
so the account default was safe to leave alone.

**Why it matters:** the account default is `Automático`, which for EUR means tax **inclusive** —
€12 would have been the total, netting €9.60 at the Danish 25%, while HunterReady's pricing card
says "VAT added at checkout".

**Consequence, and this is the good part:** when the tax registration goes active, Stripe starts
adding 25% on top **by itself**. No price is edited. A `cs_live_` checkout rendered on
2026-09-06 showed `Impuesto 0,00 €` — correct, because no registration exists yet.

| API key | `hunterready-production`, **restricted** `rk_live_` |

The key holds exactly three permissions out of 158 resources, derived from what the code
actually calls — not from a guess:

```
checkout.sessions.create        → Checkout Sessions     Write
billingPortal.sessions.create   → Customer Portal       Write
charges.retrieve                → Charges and Refunds   Read   (dispute lookup only)
```

`Customers` and `Subscriptions` were considered and **rejected**: the code never reads them,
subscription data arrives inside the webhook payload. A leaked key cannot charge, refund, move
payouts or read the customer list.

**An endpoint's API version is fixed at creation** and read-only. The test endpoint sits on
`2026-06-24.dahlia`; the live one was deliberately created on the newer version so the mismatch
was not inherited.

**Proof it works:** a real `cs_live_` session was created and rendered, showing the `edi` icon,
"Suscribirse a HunterReady 12,00 € por mes", and "autorizas a edi a cobrarte". Nothing was paid.
Probe accounts were removed with the product's own `POST /api/account/delete`.

⚠️ **The webhook secret is the one thing still unproven.** It only fires on a real payment. On
the first sale, check the plan moves to `pro`; if it stays `free`, the log line
`billingOutcome=ignored` points at the signing secret.

## Danish registration — submitted, waiting

```
Case          B26-EZ-63-UR        (virk.dk, Erhvervsstyrelsen → Skattestyrelsen)
Submitted     2026-09-06
Start date    2026-04-01          (backdated so earlier expenses are deductible)
Branchekode   63.10.00 — IT-infrastruktur, databehandling, hosting
Moms          voluntary, below the 50,000 DKK threshold, quarterly
Also chosen   reklamebeskyttelse (blocks commercial use of the CVR data)
```

Not registered for: import/export (that is **physical goods outside the EU**, not digital
services — checked, and it was nearly ticked in error), lønsumsafgift, employees, ATP.

**Expect up to 14 working days**, not same-day: the case carries a moms registration, so
Skattestyrelsen has to approve it. After 14 working days with no answer: `regstatus@sktst.dk`,
quoting the case number. Registration is **free** — the ~670 kr. fee applies only to ApS/A/S.

**Two thresholds that end the voluntary period:** 50,000 DKK/year of Danish turnover makes moms
mandatory, and €10,000/year of cross-border B2C digital sales in the EU requires OSS. enForma
Enterprise at €1,000/month crosses the first with one customer in four months.

**enForma is B2B, HunterReady is B2C** — two different VAT regimes on one account. B2B
cross-border uses reverse charge and is reported through *EU-salg uden moms*; B2C cross-border
goes through OSS. This asymmetry is the strongest argument for having the registration in place
before the first sale.

*Not tax advice. Confirm with SKAT or an accountant.*

## What happens when the CVR arrives — in this order

1. **Create the company Digital Post inbox** and forward it to e-mail. The approval screen was
   explicit: once the CVR exists, the authorities write **only** there and stop sending paper
   letters. Every deadline and estimated assessment lands in a box nobody has opened. This is
   why it is first.
2. **Set expected business profit to `0`** in the forskudsopgørelse on skat.dk, or SKAT may
   estimate a profit and bill quarterly B-skat on money not earned.
3. **In Stripe** (this part is mine, minutes): the CVR into `Número de IVA (CVR)`, the Danish
   tax registration, OSS, and automatic invoices. **No price is touched.**
4. **Only then `HR_RELEASE=true`** in Coolify — Edd's decision, 2026-09-06. It is one variable
   and a restart. Until then HunterReady stays in beta and the "Get Pro" button does not exist,
   so the checkout is reachable only by API.

Then, ongoing: **file the moms return every quarter even when it is zero** (*nulangivelse*).
Not filing triggers a *foreløbig fastsættelse* with a fee each time, and that is the only thing
that costs real money while profit is zero. With expenses and no sales, moms is likely
*negative* — a refund.

## Conventions for the apps that come next

When a new product is created in Stripe, open **Opciones adicionales** — the collapsed panel
almost nobody opens — and set three things:

1. **Statement descriptor** = the app's own name.
2. **Metadata** `app: <slug>`.
3. If the price is in EUR: *Establecer manualmente*, then **uncheck** "Incluir impuestos en el
   precio".

Those three are what make it possible, a year from now, to answer how much each app billed.
